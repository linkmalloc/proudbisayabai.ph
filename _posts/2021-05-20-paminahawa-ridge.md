#### Paminahawa Ridge  


Bukidnon is a province in Mindanao known for its mountainous landscapes. It is home to some of the highest mountains and beautiful mountain ranges in the country. Part of these mountain ranges is the **Paminahawa Ridge**.   

A long narrow hilltop that offers a breathtaking view of the surrounding mountains. **Paminahawa Ridge** has an elevation of 819 Meters Above Sea Level (MASL) or around 2,687 feet. It is a famous hiking destination for many trekkers.

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/paminahawa_ridge/2.jpg)

Indeed a beauty of nature, as the sun comes up **Paminahawa Ridge** gives you a spectacular features of the landscape.


****

Hiking might be tiring but what makes it worthwhile is the amazing view it offers once you get to the top. You can camp there but be well prepared. It will get cold during the night as clouds will fog the area.

##### HOW TO GET THERE
  
🌏 Exact Location: Brgy. Impalutao, Impasug-ong, Bukidnon Philippines🇵🇭  

🗺 Via Commute: From Cagayan de Oro, ride a Bus going to Davao, tell the driver to drop you off to (Impalutao Integrated School), much better to plan your trip together with a tour guide from Bukidnon to prepare your itinerary and schedule.   
  
🗺 Via Waze: Navigate with Waze or Google Maps and pin your location to “Impalutao Integrated School”. Contact your guide to avail free parking.
