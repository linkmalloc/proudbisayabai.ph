###### Talima Beach Villas and Dive Resort
  
#VisitOlangoIsland: A tropical hide-away that offers you serenity and privacy with a scenic panoramic ocean view. 

Talima Beach Villas and Dive Resort is a small, peaceful beach resort on Olango Island's northern shore. Their coral rock beach fronts, 14-hectare Talima Fish Sanctuary, and house reef offers a paradise for snorkellers.

There is a small swimming pool in a attractive garden and anyone can swim out to the reef. Everything on this resort is just perfect without worrying. They assure you total rest and relaxation in their secluded and private location on a coral rock cliff. Talima Resort is perfect for couples, groups, divers, snorkellers and people who want to get away from the hustle and bustle of the city life.

###### How to get there?   

🌏 Located at Olango Island, Cebu 🇵🇭  
  
🗺 Via Commute: If you are commuting, from Cebu City you can ride a bus or any jeepneys going Mactan Newtown, then hop on a tricycle going to Angasil port. They can organize a boat trip from the Hilton pier beside Mövenpick Hotel straight to our island resort.  
  
However, one can also take the public ferry boats departing from either Hilton pier or Angasil pier on Mactan Island to Santa Rosa pier on Olango Island. From St. Rosa to the Talima Resort, it takes a 10 to 15 minutes tricyle ride only.
