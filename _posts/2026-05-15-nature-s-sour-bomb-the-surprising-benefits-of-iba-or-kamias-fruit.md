The iba fruit, scientifically known as Averrhoa bilimbi, is a small, green, elongated tropical fruit commonly called “kamias” in the Philippines. Known for its intensely sour flavor, the fruit is often described as nature’s sour bomb because of its sharp tangy taste and juicy, crisp texture similar to a combination of calamansi and green mango.



Widely found in many Filipino backyards and rural communities, kamias has long been used in traditional cooking, home remedies, and even natural cleaning practices. Its strong sourness makes it a favorite ingredient in dishes such as sinigang, paksiw, and local fish recipes.



Did you know?



Aside from being a flavorful ingredient, kamias also offers several health benefits.



Rich in Vitamin C

Kamias contains high levels of Vitamin C which may help strengthen the immune system and protect the body against common illnesses.



Supports Digestion

Its natural acidity may help improve digestion and stimulate appetite when consumed in moderation.



Low in Calories

The fruit is naturally low in calories, making it a healthy addition to balanced diets.



Contains Antioxidants

Kamias contains antioxidants that may help fight free radicals and support overall wellness.



Traditional Herbal Uses

In some communities, kamias has traditionally been used as a natural remedy for cough, skin itchiness, and high blood pressure, although experts still advise proper medical consultation for treatment.



Beyond health benefits, kamias is also popularly used as a natural stain remover and metal cleaner because of its acidic properties.



Despite its extremely sour taste, the humble iba or kamias fruit remains one of the Philippines’ most unique tropical fruits, proving that even the sourest fruits can offer sweet benefits for health and daily life.
