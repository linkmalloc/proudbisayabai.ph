###### EVO Nature Camp  
Gimingaw naka ug Camping with the Barkadas sah? 

Breathe in the fresh air of a peaceful place to rekindle your soul!

EVO Nature Camp is tucked away in the mountains, making it easy to escape the city's hectic pace. You can get to Evo Nature Camp in only a few minutes by car or public transportation from Cebu's downtown area. It's perched above the ridge, providing you the greatest view of Cebu's mountain ranges. It's so close to the horizon that it's practically touching the clouds. You may take a leisurely stroll across its landscaped garden or go into the surrounding woods. As you gaze at the lush greenery, don't forget to take a moment to appreciate the gentle breeze.

###### HOW TO GET THERE?  
🗺 Via Commute: It's 45 mins straight drive from JY Square thru Transcentral Highway. Turn left after passing the Police training camp in Gaas, Balamban. Plenty of signs when you reach the Welcome Balamban sign. You won’t miss it!
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze or Google Map.

###### R A T E S

- Entrance fee: 
  - P75 Inclusive of Environmental Fee
  - Free entrance for kids 5 years old & below
  - USAGE OF GAZEBO: P100/hour
  - TREKKING: P2,500 (1-5pax) / P500 (for every excess pax)
  - CAMPING: Usage of area with own tent: P300 (1-4pax) / P200 (5 pax up)
- Tent rental with inflatable beds:
  - P300 (good for 2)
  - P500 (good for 4)
  - P700 (good for 6)

****

###### Does not include beddings. Please bring your own.
