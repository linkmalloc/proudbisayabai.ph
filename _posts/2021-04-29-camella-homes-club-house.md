#### Camella Homes Club House Talisay City
<center>Chill lang ta diri.💦🍃😍</center>
****

Camella Homes Club House Talisay City is a perfect place to chill. The place offers a swimming pool with clean water and a good, overlooking view of the city. The fees are pretty cheap, especially because the parking area is free to the public. If you wanted to exclusively enjoy the place, come here during weekdays because more likely there are only a few people during these days.

****
![](/images/posts/camella_homes_club_house/13.jpg)

##### RATES  

🕐 Operating hours: 8AM - 5PM daily

Entrance is free unless you plan to swim
- P100 for adults
- P80 for kids

##### HOW TO GET THERE  

🌎 Exact Location: Camella Homes Club House Lawan 1, Talisay City  
  
🗺 Via Commute: From the highway South Bus Terminal or Emall, Take any jeepney or bus going to Lawaan. Then tell the driver to drop you at Yahay food park, Fare range (20-30 pesos) Estimated Travel time (highway to lawaan yahay food park): (30-45 minutes)  
  
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a Bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
