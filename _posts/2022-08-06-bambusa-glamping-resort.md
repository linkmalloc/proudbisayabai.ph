##### Bambusa Glamping Resort  
Indonesia feels? Sa Moalboal rani! Andam na ba for your next Laag? A Must-Visit Bambusa Glamping Resort in Moalboal Cebu!   

"Surrounded by lush tropical gardens and a beautiful natural stone pool, our unique bamboo houses are the perfect adventure for travelers and nature lovers who want to be completely immersed in their surroundings and experience tranquil provincial life with a touch of luxury."  

Guests will discover rustic, yet elegant, spacious and comfortable rooms. The two bamboo houses have been designed with nature in mind to give you a truly unique getaway.  

Dili na kinahanglan mo layo kay halos naa na tanan sa Cebu. That's why we love Cebu!   

###### Exact Location  
Road to Basdako White Beach, Brgy. Saavedra, 6032 Moalboal 🇵🇭  

###### How to get there?   

###### Via Commute:
From Cebu South Bus terminal, ride a Ceres bus going to Moalboal and tell the driver to drop you off at Brgy. Saavedra. In your arrival you can ask the locals about the Bambusa Glaping Resort for your guidance.   

###### Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze: 📍Google Map Link: https://g.co/kgs/MYqDMb
