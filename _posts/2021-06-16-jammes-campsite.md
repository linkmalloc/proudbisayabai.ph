##### Jamme's Campsite  

A newly opened attraction in Busay, Cebu City. Jamme's Campsite is an exclusive foggy home with a cozy natural setting that gives you the feeling of being at home but away from home. The place is ideal for those who are looking for a quick escape from the busy city.

They are located in Sitio Lupa (Lower Cantipla) Brgy. Sudlon 2, Cebu City. They have one cottage with 1 room and will be exclusively used for your group. They will not accept other guests on that day once your booking is confirmed. They have Superb customer service and very accomodating staff Jamme Mabini Leuman. The place is very accessible, child-friendly and what's more, is you can bring your furbabies.

##### AMENITIES  
- Cottage 
  - 1 Room for 2 person
  - Extra Bed for 2
- Camp Area
- With Electricity
- Comfort Room
- With LTE Signal both for Smart and Globe Networks.
- Cookware and Kitchen utensils is available to use for free
- Free Drinking Water

****
> Note: This is a farm area and they don't have connection from water district, only rain water is available for toilet and bath, though they provide mineral water for drinking for free.  

****
##### STRICTLY NO WALK-INS 
Check-in Time : 2pm  
Check-out Time: 12 noon  

****
##### RATES:   
P1,500 good for 4 persons and the place is yours. (Yup, exclusive sya ninyo)  
350/ head in excess of 4 pax, MAX OF 6 PAX
  
****
##### NO CORKAGE FEE
You can bring your own food with NO CORKAGE fee

They only accept overnight camping, no day use, but most of your camping needs are available in the campsite. They have HONESTY STORE inside the cottage (canned goods, noodles, rice, eggs, condiments and wines are available)

****
##### OTHER INFO
They need atleast 50% downpayment to confirm your booking via gcash or fund transfer.  
For further inquiries and booking, please call or text [09608195085](tel:09608195085) and look for TC.  

Visit their facebook page at [Jamme's Campsite](https://www.facebook.com/pages/Jamme's%20Campsite/106789274230586/)

##### HOW TO GET THERE?
  
🌏 Location: Trancentral Highway, mu left turn ka diha sa may strawberry farm, Sitio Lupa, brgy sudlon 2, Cebu City, Just a 40-minute drive from Cebu City.  
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
