##### Dalaguete Beach Park 

Dalaguete is a municipality located in the southern part of Cebu Province. The municipality is well-known as the Summer Capital and the Vegetable Basket of Cebu. Besides having really cold weather in the mountainous part, Dalaguete has many other tourist attractions to offer. One of those is this beautiful white sand beach resort, Dalaguete Beach Park.

If you want a quick get away from a tiring week, tara na mga bai sa Dalaguete Beach Park! Dagat is waving! 🌊

##### HOW TO GET THERE?  

🌎 Exact Location: Brgy. Casay, Dalaguete, Southern Cebu  
  
🗺 Via Commute: From South Bus Terminal, ride a bus going to dalaguete, Cebu, Then just tell the driver to drop you by near Dalaguete Beach Park. Travel time is about 2-3 hours depending on traffic movement.   
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
