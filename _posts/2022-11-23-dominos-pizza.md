“You can’t buy happiness but you can buy Pizza and that’s kind of the same thing.”  

That famous tag line of Domino’s Pizza tickles us and makes us order some slice of happiness. And if you are a pizza-lover, and an adventure-seeker who wants to try something new in Cebu, this is a sign. Because the world’s favourite Pizza is finally here.   

Last November 12, 2022, Domino’s Pizza opened its first store in Cebu located at Pacific Mall Mandaue U.N. Ave, Mandaue City, Cebu. The store is the 88th in the Philippines. Following its opening in Mandaue City, it also opened its second store located at the Ground Floor of Horizon 101 Condominium, General Maxilom Ave., Cebu City last November 18. With its aggressive expansion, the brand will open its third store in Cebu at IT Park, 
Apas, Cebu City this December.

##### About Domino’s  

Domino’s Pizza Inc (Domino’s) is a pizza restaurant chain. It provides pizza delivery and carryout services as well as dine-in services through its restaurants. It offers a wide range of pizza products with varying crusts and sizes with several toppings, pasta-based dishes, chicken wings, boneless chicken, oven-baked sandwiches, bread side items, softdrinks and desserts. The company operates through a network of company-owned and franchise stores in US and in international markets including the Philippines.  

The first Domino’s Pizza branch opened in 1960 when brothers Tom and James Monaghan purchased a pizza store known as “Dominicks” in Michigan. The premise that inspired them was simple: Tom decided that whoever ordered a pizza was probably hungry and to satisfy fickle customers, they had to promise quick delivery. The brand built its success with that premise and it became a hit allowing Domino’s to aggressively expand its network.

###### Store Exact Locations  

1st Store: Ground Floor, Pacific Mall Mandaue, U.N. Avenue, Mandaue City, Cebu  

###### How to Get There?  

Via Commute: From Colon Street, ride a jeepney “01K” going to Parkmall. Then from Parkmall, ride a bus going to Consolacion and tell the driver to drop you off at Pacific Mall.   

2nd Store: Ground Floor, Horizons 101 Condominium, General Maxilom Avenue, Cebu City.

###### How to Get There?
Via Commute: From Colon Street, ride a jeepney “12L”, “04C” going to Mango Avenue. Tell the driver to drop you off at Horizons 101.
You can also navigate through Google Maps or Waze.  

That fast delivery guarantee is an enticing deal, so why not order a hot Domino’s Pizza to share with your family and friends?  You can order Domino’s Pizza on Grab Food, Food Panda, and thru its official app—Domino’s Pizza Philippines.
