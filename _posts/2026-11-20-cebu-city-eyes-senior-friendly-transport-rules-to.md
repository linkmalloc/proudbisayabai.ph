Cebu City is considering new transport standards that would make it easier for senior citizens to board public utility vehicles, wait at terminals and seek assistance while commuting.

  

Councilor Winston Pepito has proposed the Senior-Friendly Transport and Mobility Ordinance of 2026, which aims to address difficulties older passengers encounter, including long queues, inadequate waiting facilities and limited assistance when boarding vehicles.

  

Under the proposal, the Cebu City Transportation Office (CCTO), in coordination with other city offices and concerned barangays, would identify and designate senior-friendly loading and unloading areas near hospitals, health centers, public markets, government offices and major transport terminals.

  

Where feasible, these designated areas must be located within 100 meters of the main entrance of the facilities they serve.

  

  

The loading zones would need level, nonslip surfaces, curb ramps and accessible paths connecting them to sidewalks and nearby establishments. Clear signs and pavement markings would also be required.

  

  

The proposed ordinance also outlines minimum requirements for passenger waiting areas, including roofing or shade, seats with backrests, adequate lighting, nonslip flooring and proper drainage. 

  

Route and service information must be displayed in large print in both Cebuano and English.

  

  

At terminals, senior citizens and other passengers needing assistance would receive priority through designated queues or boarding procedures. 

  

City-regulated terminals would also be required to assign trained personnel to assist with queuing, ticketing, baggage and boarding, without collecting additional fees.

  

  

The priority assistance provisions would extend beyond senior citizens to persons with disabilities, pregnant women, passengers carrying infants or young children, and individuals who are injured, visibly ill or have difficulty standing, walking or boarding.

  

The measure also seeks to improve passenger safety by requiring public utility vehicle drivers to stop completely and as close to the curb as safely possible.
