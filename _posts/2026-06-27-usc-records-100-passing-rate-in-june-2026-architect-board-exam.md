**CEBU CITY** — The University of San Carlos (USC) achieved a perfect 100% passing rate in the June 2026 Licensure Examination for Architects after all 51 of its examinees successfully passed the board exam.



With the outstanding performance, USC was also recognized as the only school from Cebu to be included in the list of the country’s Top Performing Schools. The university joined two other institutions nationwide that likewise posted a 100% passing rate in the latest licensure examination.



The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) released the official results on Friday, June 26, reporting that 2,799 out of 3,290 examinees nationwide passed the June 2026 Architect Licensure Examination, for an overall national passing rate of 85.08%.



USC’s latest achievement further strengthens its reputation for producing highly qualified architecture graduates and maintaining strong performance in professional licensure examinations.



The university extended its congratulations to its 51 newly licensed architects, celebrating their hard work, dedication, and commitment to excellence.



The June 2026 Architect Licensure Examination was administered by the PRC through the Board of Architecture.
