Are you seeking a luxurious retreat where you can relax and unwind in the truest sense of nature? You have come to the opportune spot!   

Even the most ardent travelers require rest. Summer's not yet over. It is always around you. If you are looking for a serene place to stay in Southern Cebu, look no further!  

Taking long drives around the city's outskirts is one of the most pleasant things to do while singing along the entire journey. Admirable musical voices would fill the air, creating an ethereal atmosphere that is difficult to explain. Words cannot adequately express the sentiments and emotions associated with such drive with lover or barkada to the South.  

The Parrot Resort Moalboal is a 5-minute walk from Panagsama Beach and features air conditioning, satellite television, free coffee, tea, and drinking water. Enjoy cooling down in the pool with their luxurious poolside mini bar and get up close personally with their variety of adorable PARROTS. Not just that!  

Laughably fighting talkative thoughts held captive in a little jail yearning for freedom. Soak in the tranquility of the water near it, with its reflections stretching into the horizon. As the sun sets in the evening, watch it rise in the morning for another magnificent beginning.  

This is an ultimate destination for reminiscing and letting go of worries. Now is the time to pack your belongings and explore what Parrot Resort Moalboal has to offer!  

Nindota ba. #VisitMoalboal: Perfect jud diri for vacation!

FRONT desk hours: 7 AM- 7 PM daily  

Exact Location: Parrot Resort Moalboal Panagsama Road Basdiot Molboal  

###### How to get there?  
Via Commute: Ride a bus from South Bus Terminal via Moalboal and tell the driver to drop you off at 7/11 Moalboal. That is the corner also going to Panagsama beach. Ride a Tricycle and tell the driver to drop you off at Parrot Resort Moalboal. The resort is only 5 mins. away from the Panagsama beach.  

Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
