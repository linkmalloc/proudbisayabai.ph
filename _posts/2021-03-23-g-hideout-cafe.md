To all coffee monsters out there... Check this out! 🤩☕  
  
G na dayon! mo adto sa Bacolod aron mangape lang, Saan aabot ang pagiging addict mo sa KAPE? 😂  
  
🌎 Granada Alangilan Road, Bacolod City (front Charito Heights)   
  
🕔 Open from 6am - 8pm  
  
G Hideout Cafe is a great place to visit. It is one of the most peaceful cafés I've ever seen. They have an outdoor dining area where you can enjoy the fresh air and cozy atmosphere.  
  
The place may look simple but I am confident that you will enjoy returning because of the tranquility it provides. They also offer reasonably priced food and beverages that you will undoubtedly enjoy.
