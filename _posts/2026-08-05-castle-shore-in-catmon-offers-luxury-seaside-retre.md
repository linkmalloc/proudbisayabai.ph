Travelers planning a group getaway in northern Cebu can find both comfort and privacy at Castle Shore, a beachfront property designed to accommodate large families, corporate teams, and groups of friends looking for a relaxing seaside escape.

  

Recommended for 20 to 30 guests, Castle Shore combines spacious accommodations with resort-style amenities, making it an ideal venue for reunions, celebrations, team-building activities, and weekend vacations.

  

Situated along the coast of Catmon, the property features a main house and a separate seaview villa, offering guests ample space while maintaining an intimate and tranquil atmosphere. The main house houses four air-conditioned bedrooms, each with its own en-suite bathroom, along with a living room, dining area, and fully equipped kitchens. The seaview villa includes a private bedroom, en-suite bathroom, outdoor dining area, kitchen, and its own sun deck overlooking the sea.

  

Guests can enjoy a variety of amenities, including a private saltwater mini pool, direct beach access, a grilling area for outdoor dining, kayaks, snorkeling gear, and a sun deck perfect for taking in panoramic ocean views. The property also provides cooking essentials, dining equipment, and beach necessities to ensure a convenient and comfortable stay.

  

Castle Shore can accommodate up to 30 guests, with an additional headcount fee applied beyond the standard 20-person capacity. Hair dryers, extra blankets, and wine glasses are also available upon request through the on-site staff.

  

Pet owners are welcome to bring up to two trained pets, subject to house policies and corresponding pet fees. Guests may also avail themselves of optional butler services for dishwashing and light housekeeping to further enhance their stay.

  

Blending the comforts of a private vacation home with the charm of Cebu’s northern coastline, Castle Shore offers visitors a peaceful retreat where families and groups can unwind, celebrate special occasions, and create lasting memories by the sea.
