\#VisitPinamungajan: Weekend Chill, we know the perfect place!  

A Must-Visit new exclusive resort in West Cebu, **HANDUMANAN-borra beach house** in Pinamungajan!  

The Beach House - **HANDUMANAN** flourishes inside a small community of fisher folk, and everything is done with extra love.
You will experience the life in **Tajao**. You will enjoy the unlimited peace and the charm of dark sand romance.

###### How to get there?

🗺 Via Commute: If you are commuting, from Cebu City you can ride a bus at Southbus Terminal bound for Pinamungajan, Cebu. Estimated Fare is P120. Then tell the driver to drop you off at Tajao Elementary School. From there, you can ask local vendors and habal-habal drivers going to the location. About 2-3 hours travel time from Cebu City.  

🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze / G Map. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze / GMap.
