A former mayor in Cagayan is now facing a possible lifetime driving ban after allegedly fleeing a gasoline station without paying, forcing a gas attendant to chase his vehicle along a busy road.



The incident happened on April 20, 2026, and quickly circulated online, showing the attendant running after the moving car in an attempt to stop the driver and settle the unpaid fuel. The situation sparked public outrage, with many condemning the act as both irresponsible and dangerous, especially given the risk posed to the worker and other motorists.



Authorities confirmed that the former local official has been identified and is now under investigation. Charges related to theft, reckless driving, and endangerment may be filed, depending on the outcome of the inquiry. The Land Transportation Office (LTO) is also reviewing the case and is considering imposing a lifetime revocation of the individual’s driver’s license if proven guilty.



In a “just in” development, the LTO has issued a show-cause order against the driver, who is also reportedly a vlogger, in connection with the incident in Allacapan. The agency has also suspended the driver’s license and placed the vehicle under alarm status.



A viral video shows a person laughing while a gas station attendant runs after the vehicle to collect payment for a full tank, further fueling public anger.



Online, netizens shared mixed reactions. Some expressed anger, with one commenting, “dasurv makulong,” while others questioned the motive behind the incident, asking if it was done “for the content.”



Officials emphasized that fleeing without paying for fuel is a criminal offense and warned motorists that such behavior will not be tolerated. They also reiterated the importance of road safety and accountability, regardless of one’s social or political status.



Meanwhile, labor groups have raised concerns over the safety of gas station workers, urging stricter enforcement of protections and protocols to prevent similar incidents in the future.



The case remains under investigation as authorities gather more evidence and testimonies.
