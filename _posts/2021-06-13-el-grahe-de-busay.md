#Kaonsabukid: El-Grahe de Busay is finally open to the public! 🍽😍😮  

It’s a one-stop shop with a variety of food stalls plus for a budget friendly price. All you have to do is order and dine while enjoying the panoramic view of the city lights.   

##### LOCATION

🌎 Exact location: Poblacion Sitio Garaje, Barangay Busay Cebu
