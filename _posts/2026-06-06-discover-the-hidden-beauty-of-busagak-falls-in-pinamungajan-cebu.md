Looking for a refreshing escape from the city heat? Hidden in the peaceful mountain barangay of Busay in Pinamungajan, Cebu, Busagak Falls offers a cool and exciting retreat for nature lovers, hikers, and adventure seekers.

This remains one of South Cebu’s quiet natural gems waiting to be explored, with NO ENTRANCE FEE. Its raw beauty and relaxing atmosphere make it a perfect destination for anyone wanting to disconnect from the noise of everyday life and reconnect with nature.

The journey itself is part of the adventure. Visitors will trek through scenic trails, pass through rocky pathways, and cross flowing streams before reaching the majestic falls. After a 20–30 minute walk, you'll finally be rewarded with the sight of crystal-clear waters cascading into cool natural pools — a perfect spot for a refreshing swim after the hike.

Whether you are seeking a thrilling outdoor experience or simply a sweet escape with friends and family, Busagak Falls offers a memorable adventure worth the trip.

**How to Get There**

***Via Commute:***
From Cebu City, ride a bus at the South Bus Terminal bound for Pinamungajan, Cebu. The bus fare is approximately ₱120. Upon arriving at Pinamungajan Public Market (Lungsod), ride a habal-habal going to Barangay Busay. From there, prepare for a 20–30 minute trek before reaching the falls.

***Via Private Vehicle:***
Busagak Falls can be navigated through Waze, making travel easier for private vehicles. Since the location is quite far from the town proper, having a local friend or guide familiar with the area is highly recommended.
