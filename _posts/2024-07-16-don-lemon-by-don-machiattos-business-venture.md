Cebu's beverage scene just got a zestier twist with the grand opening of Don Lemon Philippines in Tisa. Launched by Don Macchiatos, this new venture promises to quench your thirst like never before. Adding to the company’s array of successful businesses, including the first 39 Coffee, Don Macchiatos, and Kuku, Don Lemon Philippines is set to become the go-to spot for refreshing lemonade drinks.

###### A Grand Opening to Remember  

The opening was a spectacular event, attended by the executive management of Don Macchiatos, Barangay Tisa officials, influencers, and enthusiastic supporters. Special guests Joseph Sabello “Ungart”, Al Moralde, Bite King, and host Melvin Protacio who revealed the flavors and its otherings.  

###### Lemonade Starting at Just ₱29  

Don Lemon Philippines offers an array of tantalizing drinks starting at an unbelievably affordable price of ₱29. Here’s a sneak peek at the refreshing menu:  

Pure Lemon - ₱29: Enjoy the refreshing taste of Don Lemon (Plain), made with 100% pure lemon extract. This drink offers a crisp, tangy flavor perfect for any occasion.  

Strawberry - ₱29: Experience the vibrant taste of Lemon Strawberry, featuring lemon extract mixed with pure strawberry flavor. This delightful beverage is both refreshing and satisfying.  

Passion Fruit - ₱29: Savor the exotic blend of lemon extract and pure passion fruit flavor. Lemon Passion Fruit combines tangy and sweet notes for a refreshing tropical treat.  

Mango - ₱29: Indulge in Lemon Mango, where the zesty lemon extract meets the sweet, rich flavor of pure mango. This drink provides a smooth and deliciously refreshing experience.  

###### Customize Your Drink  

For an additional ₱10 each, you can jazz up your drink with these delicious add-ons: Chia Seeds, Nata De Coco, Rainbow Jelly, Popping Boba, and Honey.

###### How to Get There  

Located near Sol’s and the famous siomai sa Tisa, Don Lemon Philippines is easily accessible. Simply ride 12L from Ayala Terminal or book a ride to Don Macchiatos Tisa Branch.  

###### Operating Hours: 9am - 10pm  

Don't miss out on the excitement and the chance to enjoy the delightful drinks of Don Lemon Philippines today and experience the zest!  

Don Lemon Philippines is set to become a favorite among Cebu's beverage enthusiasts. Be sure to drop by and quench your thirst with the best and most affordable lemon-based drinks in town!
