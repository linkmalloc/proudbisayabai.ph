When was the last time you allowed yourself to let go of thoughts centered around emails and work or the “things” you want or “tasks” that must get done? Tara mangatkat ‘ta!  

Disconnect to reconnect with nature as you leave the hustle and bustle behind. Embrace the serenity of the great outdoors with camping and experience the wonderful mountain view of Kang-irag Peak in Barangay Sirao, Cebu City!  

It is time to unwind and take a break. Be in love at first sight with the emerald green forests and slopes that consist of an enticing beauty tucked away like a hidden treasure waiting to be discovered, together with the breathtaking sky-high craggy peaks.  

It provides various beautiful panoramas overlooking parts of Cebu City and the nearby municipalities of Mandaue, Lapu-Lapu, Consolacion, and Liloan. A mountain in the district of Sirao, Cebu, called Mt. Kang-irag, is sometimes referred to as Sirao Peak and frequently pronounced as Mt. Kan-irag by local climbers. It has a former golf course. This mountain has a height of 735 meters above sea level. It provides a noteworthy perspective of the city and surrounding mountains. As you take in the vista, be genuine and connect with the version of yourself that seemed the most real. Be in the moment and recognize the beauty of the little things.
Additionally, there are several flower farms in the neighborhood, and locals use a man-made pond to irrigate their farms. The widely recognized Budlaan Falls and several other tiny waterfalls along the route offer swimming opportunities preceding the camping. It would be an ideal plan to gaze at the stars here at night while you reminisce on your life. You can also make a bonfire while enjoying some toasted marshmallows with your friends.

Wanderers have always been in awe of and mesmerized as the vicinity offers a wide variety of fascinating and fun activities, including hiking, camping, and more. Everyone needs to experience the area's natural culture before going back to reality.  

Exact Location: Kang-Irag Peak, Langob-Sirao, Cebu City

###### How to get there?  

Via Commute: From Cebu City, ride any jeepney, Beep bus, or Ceres bus going to JY Mall in Lahug, Cebu City. From there, ride a habal-habal (motorcycle) going to the foot of Kang-irag Peak. Most of the drivers already know or familiar with the place. Average fare is P70-80 per head per way (minimum of 2 persons).  
Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.  

Entrance Fee: P70.00  
(Very Safe with free comfort room)  
Suggested trail: Budlaan trail to Sirao Peak
