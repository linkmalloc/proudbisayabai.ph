Whether you’re craving a refreshing drink, a sweet frozen dessert, or simply a cozy place to unwind, Don Lemon Philippines and KUKU Frozen Yogurt offer the perfect destination along P. Del Rosario Street, beside the Don Macchiatos USC branch and near Sto. Rosario Church in Cebu City.

  

The branch features a spacious, modern, and aesthetically designed dine-in area, creating a welcoming space for students, professionals, families, and friends to enjoy great food and meaningful moments together. Whether you’re taking a study break, catching up with friends, or cooling down after a busy day, the inviting atmosphere makes every visit more enjoyable.

  

Don Lemon serves its signature freshly made lemonade in a variety of refreshing flavors, with prices starting at just ₱29 for a Small and ₱39 for a Large—making it an affordable choice for anyone looking to beat the heat.

  

For dessert lovers, KUKU Frozen Yogurt offers creamy frozen yogurt with options to suit every craving. Enjoy the Plain for ₱59, the Combo with sauce for ₱79, or indulge in the Overload for ₱149, complete with sauce and your choice of delicious toppings.

  

With refreshing beverages, creamy frozen yogurt, budget-friendly prices, and a comfortable dine-in experience, Don Lemon Philippines and KUKU Frozen Yogurt continue to provide customers with a refreshing destination in the heart of Cebu City.

  

The branch is open daily from 9:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., ready to serve your favorite refreshments from morning until night.
