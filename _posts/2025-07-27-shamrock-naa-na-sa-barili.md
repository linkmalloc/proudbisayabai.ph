From a humble bakeshop in Cebu City in the 1960s, **Shamrock** has become a household name known for its signature Otap, broas, and proudly local delicacies. Decades later, it continues to bring the comforting flavors of Cebu to more communities — and now in Barili!

**Shamrock Express Barili is now open — with DRIVE-THRU convenience!**
Located right in the heart of Poblacion, this newest branch serves your all-time favorites and hearty meals, all freshly baked and made with love from Cebu.

*Open daily: 7AM–6PM*

**OPENING PROMO ALERT!**

Grab a Combo Box of 4 Otap Puff and get a FREE 12oz Hot Americano — perfect for pairing with your favorite bite.

Promo runs until July 31, 2025 only!

So, bai, if you’re in Barili, make this your new go-to tambayan for coffee, carbs, and classic comfort food.

Kita kits sa Shamrock Express Barili!
