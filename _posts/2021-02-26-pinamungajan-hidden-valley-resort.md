****
****
#### How to get there?  
<hr style="border-color: #c5c5c552 !important;">

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/proudbisayabaii/image/upload/v1614303428/pinamungajan/152662209_298799735249035_8991497469882219945_o_jof8qy.jpg)

****
##### Exact Location:  
Barangay Lamac, Municipality of Pinamungajan.    

****
##### 🗺 Via commute:
- Ride a bus sa Cebu South Bus Terminal going to Lutopan, then Toledo City. Inform the driver na to drop you off sa Eskina Bunga for Hidden Valley. Then and Bus fare is ₱40.00 pesos per head, while travel time is about 1 to 2 hours depende sa traffic.   
- From Lutopan, take a “habal-habal” going to Hidden Valley Wave Pool. The fare each is  ₱60.00 pesos (one way lang na sya) If ikaw ra isa you need  to pay ₱120.00 pesos for the fare. Travel time is another 20 minutes to 30 minutes for 14km. (Depende padagan sa driver)  

****
##### 🗺 Via private vehicle:  
If you have your own car just use Google map or waze then search “Hidden Valley Resort”.
