A mother kept her composure after discovering a snake inside her child’s motorcycle helmet while they were traveling to Tagbilaran City, Bohol.

  

According to Emma Ongcal, she noticed the snake inside her child’s helmet during their trip. Instead of reacting immediately, she chose to remain calm to avoid causing panic that could have endangered them while they were on the road.

  

They were eventually able to stop safely, and the snake was removed without any reported injuries.

  

The incident has drawn attention online and serves as a reminder for motorcycle riders to carefully inspect their helmets before wearing them, particularly if the helmet has been left unattended or stored for an extended period.

  

Authorities and road safety advocates continue to encourage riders to check their safety gear before every trip to help prevent unexpected incidents.
