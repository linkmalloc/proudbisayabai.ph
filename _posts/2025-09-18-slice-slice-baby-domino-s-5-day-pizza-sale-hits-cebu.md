Cebuanos, get ready for a pizza feast! From **September 17 to 21**, Domino’s Pizza is rolling out its **5-Day Pizza Sale** with deals you won’t want to miss.

Buy any **Finest or Specialty Pizza** for as low as **₱469** and enjoy a **FREE Pizza** — available in both **Regular** and **Family sizes**. Perfect for sharing with friends, family, or just indulging yourself.

Celebrate the week with cheesy goodness at these participating Domino’s Cebu branches:

* Pacific Mall Mandaue
* Mango Avenue
* I.T. Park
* SM Seaside Cebu
* Paseo Arcenas Banawa

Don’t wait — head to your nearest Domino’s and join the pizza party while it lasts!
