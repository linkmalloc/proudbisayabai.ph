#### THE CAMP, Cebu  
  
YES to Pabukid nasad ta bai! Visit The Camp Cebu and get lost with nature away from buzzling City life! 🏔🏕😯  
  
🌍 Exact location: Sitio Bandila, Barangay Bonbon, Cebu City, Philippines  
  
“The Camp” is perfect for a night away or for having a special event. Both large and small groups can be accommodated.   
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: From the city about 1hr travel to the starting point by private vehicle. The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.   

****
##### 📌Activities:  
- Trekking  
- Camping  
- Mountain Climbing  
- MTB trailing  

****
##### 📌 Fees:  
- Php 850 per head with free dinner and breakfast next morning.  
- Php 550 per head bring and cook your own food.  

****

>FREE!   
- Use of kit utensils  
- 1.5 gal of mineral water  
- Stove  
- Griller w/ free charcoal  

****
For more details please visit their facebook page: [https://www.cebumountainbike.com/the-camp-in-cebu-philippines/](https://www.cebumountainbike.com/the-camp-in-cebu-philippines/)
