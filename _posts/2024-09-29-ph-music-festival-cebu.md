Cebu! Get ready for a musical experience like no other! BIGWIN29 is excited to bring you **FUSION: THE PHILIPPINE MUSIC FESTIVAL** on October 5, 2024, live at City Di Mare. This spectacular event promises a celebration filled with exceptional performances, high-energy beats, and surprises that will leave you wanting more.  

###### Headlined by some of the biggest stars in OPM  

Prepare to be enchanted by heartfelt anthems from December Avenue and get all the feels while vibing to Zack Tabudlo’s greatest hits. Rising star Maki and P-Pop sensation Kaia will bring the heat on the stage and keep the crowds on their feet with their dynamic music too!  

Adding a local touch to the festival, Cebu's very own Karaoke World Championships (KWC) Philippine Winner, Francis Alinsonorin, will take the stage, together Cebu finalists Jovie Loon and Nica Jañola.  

Cebu’s finest DJs, DJ Victor Jao, DJ Matt, and DJ Via Chan will light up the night with their beats – turning Citi Di Mare into a dance floor. The energy and beats of their sets is expected keep the vibe kicking!  

###### Special appearance of El Presidente, Nadine Lustre  

To top off the evening, BIGWIN29’s muse, Nadine Lustre, will captivate the audience as she graces the evening. BIGWIN29 will prepare a grand production number that promises to be the festival's crowning highlight.  

And that’s not all! BIGWIN29 will be giving away tons of prizes and giveaways, ensuring that every attendee has a chance to go home a winner.
FUSION is more than just a festival; it’s a celebration of music, culture, and community crafted especially for Cebuanos. With a stellar lineup and unforgettable experiences, BIGWIN29 invites everyone to come together and play as one.  

The New Channel or TNC, an alternative new media platform supports this event as its flagship project this year called Karaoke World Championships (KWC) Philippines.
