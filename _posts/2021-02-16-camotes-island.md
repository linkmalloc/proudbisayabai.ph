#### Camotes Island

Looking for a place where you can chill and relax? Kanindot diri! Ari ta bai!  

**How to get there?**  

**Via Commute:**  
If you are commuting, from Cebu City you can ride a bus or jeepney going Danao Port and ride a boat to Consuelo Port (fare is Php220/head).  

**Via Private Vehicle:**  
If using a private utility and want to bring it in the Island, you can ride a roro in the same port, fare is around Php680 for motorcycle including the rider. Travel time is around 2 hours.  


Barge Schedule pls refer to [Jomalia Shipping Lines FB page](https://www.facebook.com/JomaliaShippingCorp).  

The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.

Ceres bus from Mactan to Danao to Consuelo port vice versa rate. Estimated rate see below.
- Student : 150.00 - 180.00 
- Adult : 220.00 - 250.00 
- Señor Citizen : 150.00 - 160.00 
- Environmental & Terminal fee 10.00 - 20.00
