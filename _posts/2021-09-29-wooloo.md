##### WOOLO

\#LaagAmihanan: Escape the busy City life and plan a mountain retreat today! 🥺🍃

Experience our private sanctuary in the highlands of Garing, Consolacion that offers exclusivity, breathtaking views, perfectly manicured gardens, sumptuous culinary selections and more.
Woolo! A unique take on boutique accommodation using sustainable materials. 

Wooloo weaves the vibrant traditions of Cebu into the most intricate detail of its eclectic design and concept. Experience this one of a kind getaway when you go for a drive to the mountains, paved by lush greeneries and breathtaking scenes.

🌍 Exact Location: Barangay Garing, Consolacion Cebu 🇵🇭

🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu Northbus terminal which is located now in SM City Cebu, ride a bus bound for Consolacion. Then tell the driver to drop you-off at Brgy. Garing, from there you can ask some locals on where to ride going to the exact location. 

🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze or Google Map. From the Town proper - Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze/ GMap.
