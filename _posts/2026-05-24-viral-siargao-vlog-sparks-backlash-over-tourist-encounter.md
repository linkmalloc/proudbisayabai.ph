A vlog filmed in Siargao is drawing criticism online after netizens called out the behavior shown toward an American tourist in a now-viral video.



In the clip, content creator $aintjohn approached a foreign woman and asked if he could take a small sip of her soft drink. However, viewers claimed he ended up finishing the entire drink before improperly throwing away the empty cup instead of disposing it in a garbage bin.



Many viewers were further disappointed after noticing that the American tourist herself reportedly reminded him to throw the trash properly.



The incident quickly sparked reactions online, with several users expressing embarrassment and frustration over the behavior shown in the video, especially since it happened in one of the country’s most visited tourist destinations.



“Not funny. Kahiya hiya!!!” one commenter wrote, while another said, “He thought it was funny!”



The viral clip also reignited discussions about responsible content creation, with many online users reminding vloggers that gaining views and engagement should still come with respect, discipline, and proper conduct toward others.



Some netizens also criticized the content creator’s responses to online backlash, claiming he often reacts with sarcastic remarks whenever people call out his behavior online.
