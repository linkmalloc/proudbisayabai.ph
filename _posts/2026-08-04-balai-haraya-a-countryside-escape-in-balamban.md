Hidden amid the rolling landscapes of Nangka, Balamban, Balai Haraya Exclusive Farmstay offers travelers a refreshing escape from the city’s fast-paced lifestyle. Just about an hour’s drive from Cebu City and only two kilometers from the national road, this private farmstay blends comfort, simplicity, and nature into one memorable countryside retreat.

  

Unlike commercial resorts, Balai Haraya carries a more personal story. It is the family’s own resthouse—a place built for quiet weekends, meaningful gatherings, and moments of relaxation. Today, its doors are open to guests who wish to experience the same peaceful sanctuary.

  

Inspired by the tropical charm of Bali, the property features a warm, laid-back ambiance that complements its lush natural surroundings. Rather than luxurious extravagance, Balai Haraya embraces simplicity, proving that true relaxation doesn’t have to come with a hefty price tag. It is thoughtfully designed for couples, families, and small groups looking for an intimate getaway close to nature.

  

Guests enjoy exclusive access to the entire farmhouse, complete with a private 3-by-4-meter dipping pool (4 feet deep), perfect for cooling off after a leisurely afternoon. The house comfortably accommodates guests with one queen-sized bed, one queen-sized tatami bed, one double mattress, two bathrooms, a fully equipped kitchen, a television, and spacious outdoor areas ideal for dining, relaxing, or simply taking in the fresh mountain air.

  

Beyond its amenities, the true highlight of Balai Haraya is the experience it offers. Mornings begin with crisp countryside air and panoramic farm views, while evenings are filled with the soothing chorus of crickets under a blanket of stars. Surrounded by open fields and fresh farm produce, guests are invited to slow down, disconnect from the demands of daily life, and rediscover the beauty of simple living.

  

Accessibility is another advantage. Despite its tranquil location, the farmstay is easily reached by both cars and motorcycles via a flat, well-maintained road. Free on-site parking is also available for all guests.

  

For added peace of mind, outdoor security cameras are strategically installed around the property, covering the pool area, guest entrance, vegetable farm, and the rear portion of the house. No cameras are installed inside the home or in private guest spaces, ensuring both safety and privacy throughout every stay.

  

Whether you’re planning a romantic weekend, a family staycation, or a quiet retreat with friends, Balai Haraya Exclusive Farmstay offers more than just accommodation—it provides an opportunity to embrace the slower pace of countryside life, surrounded by nature, comfort, and genuine Filipino hospitality.
