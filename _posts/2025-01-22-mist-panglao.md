Imagine dining in a restaurant that feels like a hidden forest paradise—a serene escape where world-class dining meets breathtaking nature. Welcome to MIST, a unique 3-tier treehouse restaurant nestled in the island paradise of Panglao, Bohol!  

###### A Feast for the Senses

MIST offers more than just a meal; it’s an experience. From the moment you step in, the forest-inspired ambiance takes you away from the hustle of everyday life. The lush greenery, wooden accents, and cozy treehouse setting make for a magical atmosphere, perfect for romantic dates, family getaways, or even solo soul-searching adventures.  

Their menu? A masterpiece of Filipino gourmet cuisine with a creative twist, each dish artfully plated by their talented chef. Whether you’re craving savory classics or adventurous flavors, every bite is a testament to MIST’s dedication to culinary excellence.  

###### Why Visit MIST?
- Unparalleled Ambiance: Dining amidst nature on a treehouse is a once-in-a-lifetime experience.
- Filipino Gourmet Cuisine: A mix of tradition and innovation, offering dishes that are both comforting and exciting.
- Exceptional Service: The staff makes you feel right at home, adding a personal touch to your visit.
