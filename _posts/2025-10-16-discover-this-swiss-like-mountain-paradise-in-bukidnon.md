Tired of the relentless city heat and noise? Imagine trading the cacophony of traffic for a profound silence so deep you can hear your own heartbeat, and swapping humid air for a cool, gentle mist that kisses your face. This isn’t a dream of the Alps; this is the breathtaking reality waiting for you at **Katihan Eco Park** in Malaybalay City, Bukidnon. Dubbed as a ***"Swiss-like"*** haven right in the heart of Mindanao, this destination is quickly captivating the hearts of travelers and photographers alike. It offers a soul-stirring escape into nature, where the legendary landscapes of Bukidnon unfold in a spectacular theater of endless rolling hills and majestic, mist-covered peaks that stretch as far as the eye can see.

The true magic of Katihan Eco Park lies in its panoramic, ever-changing canvas. As the sun begins its ascent, the sky erupts in hues of orange, pink, and gold, casting a soft, ethereal glow over the luscious greenery. During sunset, the landscape is bathed in a warm, golden hour light, creating a truly magical atmosphere that feels almost otherworldly. But the experience isn't just passive sightseeing. The park's design thoughtfully incorporates the natural topography, featuring walking trails that meander through various ecological zones. Adventurers can embark on a journey through grassland areas, breathe in the fresh, pine-scented air, and explore forest patches that harbor a diverse collection of local flora and fauna. It’s a living, breathing ecosystem where you can truly connect with the untouched beauty of the Philippine highlands.

Ready to answer the call of the mountains? Planning your visit is straightforward. Katihan Eco Park is located in Malaybalay City, Bukidnon. 

Make sure to plan your trip accordingly! Pack your jacket, charge your camera, and prepare to be awestruck by this unparalleled mountain sanctuary.

📍Location: Katihan Eco Park, Malaybalay City, Bukidnon

🕒 Opening Hours:  Mon-Sun except Sat, 7 AM-6 PM
