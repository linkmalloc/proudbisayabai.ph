A popular product sold in Korea is a whole chicken called Tongdak. This first appeared in Korea in the late&nbsp;70s when Koreans started selling full-roasted chicken in Myeongdong, Seoul. In fact, it was the first chicken that was brought to the said polity. Get the gang together and prepare to enjoy some seriously exemplary chicken and other Korean cuisines that will make your night unforgettable. Either way, the band will undoubtedly get you in the mood for enjoyment or maoy.  

The newest place to satisfy your appetite for chicken guilt-free.&nbsp;To achieve that delicate wood earth flavor, TongDak is grilled&nbsp;over specialized firewood. Grabe ka lami, samot na if naay shat ug beer. Ari na dayon ta kauban ang barkada, bai!

###### How to get there? 

Exact Location: P. Remedio St. A.S Fortuna Pinetree Mandaue City 

Via Commute: From Cebu City, sakay lang ka ug any jeepney padung Talamban (13C or 62C) then naog ka sa Gaisano Country Mall. Where you can take a jeepney bound for Consolacion or Liloan.  

Via Taxi: Ride a taxi and tell the driver to drop you off at Jollibee AS Fortuna. Right side Jollibee in P. Remedio street just straight ahead and look for TongDak sign board. 
The place can be navigated on Waze / Google Map. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
