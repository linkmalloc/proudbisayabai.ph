Get that delightful experience of artisanal coffee and scrumptious pastries in the newest yet also the biggest cafe of Abaca Baking Company situated on the 4th floor of Ayala Center Cebu.   

From a scratch bakery slash café concept, they offer the finest all-butter croissants, baked goods, and pastries in the metro. All-day breakfast together with comfort classics are also available creating a pleasant, bittersweet blend.  

A few of these that you must try are 3 cheese tomato, Four Egg Scramble, Campfire Latte, Bloody Mary Brunch Cocktail, and Roasted Tomato Soup.  

Abaca Baking Company provides premium coffees, relaxing teas, fresh juices, and smoothies that anyone can love and appreciate. This is a sign for you to explore their delectable meals and beverages with pastries. It will firmly improve your experience to the next level!
