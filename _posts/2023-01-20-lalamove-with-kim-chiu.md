Cebuana actress Kim Chiu recently fulfilled her dream of owning a business when she launched House of Little Bunny, an online shop that sells fashionable and dainty tote bags and crossbody bags to complete one’s wardrobe, accommodating local consumers and even those abroad.   

As Kim makes strides in business, she has tapped 24/7 same day, on-demand delivery platform Lalamove as her partner in nationwide delivery for her bestselling handbags, catering to customers from Manila and her hometown in Cebu and marking her Lala-lovely beginning as the platform’s newest ambassador.  

Here are three reasons why Lalamove is Kim’s chosen partner courier:  

###### Affordable delivery services  

An expert in Affordelivery, Lalamove offers entrepreneurs like Kim, budget-friendly and professional delivery services. Kim uses Lalamove’s affordable services in Cebu. Apart from motorcycle deliveries, Lalamove also offers sedan deliveries that start at PHP 102 and PHP 120 for MPV, while the first 40KMs starts at PHP 310 for Small Trucks and PHP 1,460 for Big Trucks that can accommodate bulk orders with ease.  

###### Same-day truck delivery  
With Lalamove, Kim can deliver her arm candies in bulk, and products get to customers as fast as possible with its same-day delivery service within the province. “With Lalamove, our customers stay satisfied because of quick, efficient, and reliable service. Their purchases are safely transported from the House of Little Bunny, into their homes,” Kim shared.  

Lalamove’s same-day truck delivery services has unrivaled island-wide coverage in Cebu – from its northernmost part Daanbantayan all the way down to Santander.  

###### Guaranteed delivery during peak season  

Handcrafted and made of high-quality materials, the handbags are handled with care by Lalamove, and customers receive their items in pristine condition. With Lalamove’s fleet of vehicles, entrepreneurs like Kim also enjoy hassle-free booking even during the rush hour and peak season.   

“Here at Lalamove, we ensure each delivery is done efficiently and safely, at an affordable price. We prioritize our customers and partners’ experience, addressing peak season demands to empower SMEs and businesses in Cebu and nationwide,” explained Lalamove Cebu City Manager Nelson Judaya.
