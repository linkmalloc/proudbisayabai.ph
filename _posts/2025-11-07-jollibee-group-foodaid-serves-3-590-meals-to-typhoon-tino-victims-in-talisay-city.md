The[ Jollibee](https://www.facebook.com/JollibeePhilippines?__cft__[0]=AZXJrsLt6d_69huMq3JCZ7VWcRsU4Ntw0gH9jvRxYEGvODTJaFvvPwqF0ibpxDN6ig24zi9w1XlWgYuGlqnw4RbBg8ZrP5IKKoVjogz-i3VFcDZU0Vg3hMp_leJ9Q_M40nslJo8ewCwIqLbH13q5Xee8Qibrpd0bSO9wG87BGLHV7A&__tn__=-]K-R) Group FoodAID extended support to families affected by Typhoon Tino, distributing a total of 3,590 Chickenjoy meals across 10 evacuation centers in Talisay City.

The initiative provided comfort and relief to evacuees who lost their homes and are still recovering from the severe flooding. In Talisay City, Cebu, on November 7, 2025, many families remain resilient and hopeful, drawing strength from the support of community partners.

Through this effort, Jollibee Group FoodAID continues to demonstrate its commitment to uplifting communities in times of need, offering nourishment and encouragement during difficult times.
