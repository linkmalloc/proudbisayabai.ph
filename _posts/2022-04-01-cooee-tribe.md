Are you craving a delicious meal but are on a strict diet and are weight and health-conscious? Or do you
want to start living a healthier lifestyle but don't know where to start? Trust me, we’ve all been there. Dili
mabalaka, bai, Cooee Tribe is just in town and they know how to get your weight-loss goals off to a
delicious start.  

Summer na! Ready na ba ka? Healthy diets and lifestyles have been at the cutting edge of new life in the
latest days, with social media influencers introducing healthy Instagramming for their endorsements
showcasing their perfect body-yaddy. The question is, what really works for you? What is going to be of
real gain to your bodies? Here, we take you through the best healthy snacks available in Cooee Tribe, just
for you, all of which are natural. There are no miracle pills or glowing green consumable energy gels
here; only healthy, natural elements that should be part of your diet. Kids will love most of these, too!

The same passion, experience, and drive for quality management that drives their coffee and foodies also
drives their healthy smoothie options. Looking for a quick and handful way to get your daily servings of
fruits and vegetables? Cooee Tribe’s smoothies will provide you with the extra boost you deserve! The
smoothie of your dreams! It is not only rich and creamy, but it's also gluten-free. Their smoothies
combine the best of both worlds.  

###### Operating Hours:  
8AM to 10PM daily   

###### Exact Location:  
G/F Antara Retail Strip, Antara Residences, Lawaan, Talisay City    

###### How to get there?  

Via commute: From Colon, ride a jeepney going Jollibee Bulacao and ride another jeepney
heading Minglanilla and tell the driver to drop you off at Yahay Food Park Talisay. From there, ride
a tricycle/habal2 then tell the driver to drop you off at Antara Retail Strip, Antara Residences.  

Via private vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze/ Google Map. Whether you are riding a
bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze / G Map: Cooee Tribe
