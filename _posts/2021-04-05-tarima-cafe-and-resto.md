#### Tarima Café and Resto   
  
Eat and chat with friends and experience calm and serenity in this seaview reastaurant as you indulge yourself with delicious and distinctive dishes at Tarima Café and Resto in Catmon, Cebu!   
☕️🍃💭  
  
🌍 Exact Location: Binongkalan, Catmon Cebu, near binongkalan bus stop.  
  
🚶🚘 How to get there?  
  
🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu City have a ride in north bus terminal going Catmon. The place is at the left west side 200 meters from km 47/hinagdanan resort, nearby binongkalan bus stop.   
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
