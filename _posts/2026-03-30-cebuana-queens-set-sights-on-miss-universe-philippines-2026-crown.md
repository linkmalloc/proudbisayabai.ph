Pride runs high in Cebu as two homegrown beauty queens, Apriel Smith of Cebu City and Nicole Borromeo of Cebu Province, take the national stage in the upcoming Miss Universe Philippines 2026 pageant.



The prestigious competition is set to take place on May 1 at the SM Mall of Asia Arena, where candidates from across the country will vie for the coveted crown and the chance to represent the Philippines on the global Miss Universe stage.



Apriel Smith, representing Cebu City, is steadily gaining attention for her elegance, confidence, and strong stage presence. Known for her passion for self-expression and empowerment, she brings with her the vibrant spirit of Cebu City and aims to inspire through her journey in pageantry.



Meanwhile, Nicole Borromeo, representing Cebu Province, is no stranger to the spotlight. She rose to prominence after being crowned Binibining Cebu 2022 and later earned the title of Miss Universe Philippines Tourism 2023. Nicole is widely recognized for her poise, intelligence, and advocacy-driven platform, making her one of the strong contenders in this year’s competition.



As both candidates carry the pride of Cebu, supporters are encouraged to rally behind them as they compete on the national stage. Their journey reflects not only beauty and grace but also the strength and determination of Cebuana women.
