The Department of Health (DOH) has opened an investigation into reported child deaths following the Phase 3 rollout of its Measles-Rubella Supplemental Immunization Activity (MR-SIA).

  

In a statement Friday, the DOH expressed condolences to the families of the children who died and said it is coordinating with local government units and other relevant agencies as the cases are reviewed.

  

“The DOH assures the affected families and the public that these cases are being taken seriously,” the agency said.

  

The investigation will examine the children’s clinical records, vaccination histories, laboratory test results and other relevant information as authorities determine the cause of death.

  

The DOH said the review forms part of its investigation into Adverse Events Following Immunization, which covers serious or unusual health events reported after vaccination.

  

The agency stressed that an illness or death occurring after vaccination does not by itself establish that the vaccine caused the event.

  

“The DOH emphasizes that reported serious or unusual events following vaccination are not automatically linked to the vaccine and must undergo thorough investigation by experts,” it said.

  

The health agency urged the public to refrain from speculation and sharing unverified information while the investigation continues.

  

It also maintained that vaccines remain safe and effective in protecting people against vaccine-preventable diseases, including measles and rubella.
