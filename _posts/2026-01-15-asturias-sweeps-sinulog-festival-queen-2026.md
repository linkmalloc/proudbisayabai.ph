Stefanie Collamar Przewodnik of the Municipality of Asturias dominated the stage at the Cebu City Sports Complex on January 16, winning the Sinulog Festival Queen 2026 crown and multiple major, special, and minor awards.



Judges placed Mary Yasmin Fabian of Carcar City as 1st Runner-Up, Kyra Rei Hopkins of Tribu Septimo, Dumanjug as 2nd Runner-Up, Clarissa Marie Angelina Gambalan Westram of Cotabato as 3rd Runner-Up, and Chanel Frenzie O. Catao of Inayawan Talents Guild as 4th Runner-Up.&#x20;



The winners impressed audiences and judges with their poise, creativity, and vibrant celebration of the Sinulog spirit.



Asturias also won the Major Awards, claiming Best in Production Number, Best in Festival Costume, and Best in Solo Performance.&#x20;



Carcar City earned Best in Group Production and Best in Instrumentation, while Inayawan received Best Festival Costume Designer.



In the Special Awards, Stefanie Collamar Przewodnik of Asturias took home Chow-Festival Queen, Hiyas ng Sugbo, Smart Choice Award, Miss Palawan, Miss Magnolia Chicken Timplados 2026, and Miss Photogenic.&#x20;



Dumanjug’s Kyra Rei Hopkins won Ms. GSM 2026 and Miss Bellshayce Foods, while Britney Felecio of Mandaue City received Coins.ph Sinulog Queen and Miss Regasco.



Asturias dominated the Minor Awards, winning Miss Bingoplus Festival Queen 2026, Miss DITO 2026, Miss Coca-Cola 2026, and Miss Manila Bankers Assurance 2026.&#x20;



Cotabato’s Clarissa Westram earned Miss MyTV 2026, while Dumanjug’s Kyra Hopkins claimed Miss Cebu Landmasters 2026, McDonald Sinulog Festival Queen 2026, and Miss MYRA 2026.



Other candidates showcased talent and creativity on stage, including Candy Urna (Hugpong Mananayaw ng Bayan, University of the Philippines Cebu), Ashley Dainne Locson Fernandez (Maria Clara L.
