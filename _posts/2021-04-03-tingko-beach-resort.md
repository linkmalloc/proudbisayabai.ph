#### Tingko Beach Resort 
  
BISDAK LAAGAN CHECK! Unsay plano sa next Laag Bai? Naka drawing na ba Tanan Aw! Pack your bags and put some colors to those drawings, Arats nah! 💦😯🌴  
  
🌎 Exact Location: Alcoy, South of Cebu 🇵🇭  
  
A public beach resort that nestled specifically in Baranggay Daan. It is just 3 km away from the public market, so running out of something is never a problem. It's long streched shoreline, fine white sands and shallow blue waters are good for family and friend activities.
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu City, You can go to  Cebu South Bus Terminal and ride any bus bound for Alcoy South Cebu.Then tell the driver to drop you off at Baranggay Daan (estimate distance 3km away from public market or lungsod. You can also ask some locals to guide you going to the exact location.   
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.  
  
📌 Fees:  
Entrance fee:   
- Adult: P60.00  
- Kuds: P30.00 6years old below
