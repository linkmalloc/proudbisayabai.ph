#### La Vue Cabine
<center>Want to escape from buzzling City life with friends? Rekindle your soul by taking in good air in a relaxing place! 😯🍃</center>

****
La Vue Cabine is an ideal and super-exclusive place to relax and chill with the people you love. It is located in Brgy. Pangamihan, Toledo City, Cebu. Being surrounded by mountains in the center of Cebu, the place offers beautiful, serene, quiet, and verdant scenery. The service and amenities they offer are commendable. The Staffs are very accommodating and requests are openly accepted. It's a perfect place for finding your inner peace in luxury living.

****

##### Activities  
- Strolling or hiking  
- Board Games and Binoculars  
- Mini-kitchen   
- Small Swimming Pool   
- Moon Watching using the astronomical telescope  

****
##### Rates   
- ₱20,000 per night for **7-12 guests**  
  So if e divide by 12 person possible each person ₱1,666 pesos.  
- ₱16,000 per night for **5-6 guests**  
  if e divide by 6 person possible 2,666 per person   
- ₱6,000 pax per night for 1-2 guests (Mondays to Thursdays only)  
- ₱12,000 pax per night for 3-4 guests (Mondays to Thursdays only)  
  if divide by 4 = 3,000 per person.  

****  
Rates are inclusive of dinner and breakfast (dagko kaayog servings) you also have the whole place to yourselvesss, a bonfire, pwede sad mo mag videoke kung ganahan mo and of course the mini pool which is by the way a hot spring water and they replace it after every guest so its safe guys 😉

#### HOW TO GET THERE

##### 🌍 Location:  
Sitio Babag 2, Brgy. Pangamihan, Toledo City  
	
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
From Ayala Center Mall ride a V-hire Terminal to Cantipla corner Transcentral Hi-way. Drop off (Strawberry de Cantipla eco farm) P150. From there, Sakay ka habal-habal eskina Cantipla to La Vue Cabine. P60-80.   

****
##### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorcycle or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
