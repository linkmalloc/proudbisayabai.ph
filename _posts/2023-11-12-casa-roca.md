Nestled in the heart of Talisay City, Cebu, Philippines, Casa Roca-Private Pool Villa offers an oasis of tranquility and luxury for discerning travelers seeking an exceptional vacation experience. This two-story villa, boasting a private pool, is a haven of comfort and style, catering to the needs of couples, families, or groups seeking intimate gatherings.  

📍Location: Casa Roca- Private Pool Villa, Talisay Cebu \| https://g.co/kgs/Q169jh Villa Estellita Subdivision, Bulacao, Talisay, Cebu (3-5 minutes from the highway)

Accommodation  
• Exclusive use of the entire main villa for groups with less than 18 overnight staying guests.  
• Exclusive use of both the main and extension villas for groups with more than 18 overnight staying guests.  

Main Villa  
• Poolside Bedroom: 2 double-sized beds, 1 toilet and bath with hot & cold shower  
• Entire 2nd floor: Spacious dorm-type room with a balcony, 3 double-sized bed bunks, 1 double-sized floor mattress, 2 toilets and baths (one with hot & cold shower)  

Extension Villa  
• Main Room: Spacious dorm-type room, 2 double-sized bed bunks, 1 double-sized floor mattress, 1 toilet and bath with hot & cold shower  
• Balcony Room: Spacious dorm-type room with a balcony, 2 single-sized bed bunks, 1 single-sized floor mattress, 1 toilet and bath with hot & cold shower  

Amenities   

• Parking for up to 2 carsPrivate and exclusive swimming pool (4ft deep)  
• Kiddie pool (2ft deep)  
• Kitchen and dining area with appliances and utensils  
• Outdoor tables and chairs  
• Outdoor swing  
• Outdoor shower  
• Recreational area with billiards and dartboard  
• Outdoor charcoal grilling area (charcoal not provided)  
• High-speed fiber internet (600mbps)  
• Air-conditioned, soundproof, and spacious digital karaoke room (available from 8pm to 2am)  

Rates  
• Monday to Thursday: ₱14,800 for 1 to 15 people. Additional guests: ₱500 per head.  
• Friday to Sunday and Holidays: ₱15,800 for 1 to 15 people. Additional guests: ₱600 per head.  

Payment Policy  
• 50% downpayment + ₱2,000 security deposit (refundable upon check-out)  
• Full payment required at least 1 day before the check-in date  
• No card payments, only cash, gcash, and bank transfers for now  

To secure reservation, please message Casa Roca through their Facebook page or instagram or contact us at +63 939 923 6615 and kindly provide your preferred date for us to verify availability.
