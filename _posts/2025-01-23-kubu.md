If you’re looking for a dining spot that combines delicious flavors with stunning ambiance, Kubu Restaurant + Bar at Garden Bloc, IT Park, Cebu City, is the perfect destination. Whether you’re visiting during the radiant daylight or under the enchanting night sky, every moment at Kubu feels like a sensory treat.  

Kubu blends nature and artistry, offering a unique fusion of flavors that cater to every craving. From hearty meals to creative beverages, their menu is crafted to satisfy both your taste buds and your aesthetic side. It’s an ideal spot for catching up with friends, enjoying a romantic dinner, or even sparking inspiration for your next big idea.  

Open from 4 PM to 12 AM on weekdays and extending until 2 AM on weekends, Kubu sets the perfect tone for laid-back afternoons and vibrant evenings. Whether you’re a foodie or a casual diner, this restaurant and bar will surely leave you with unforgettable memories.  

So, what are you waiting for? Mangaon ta diri!  

📍 Location: Garden Bloc, IT Park, Cebu City  
🕣 Operating Hours: 4 PM - 12 AM (Weekdays), 4 PM - 2 AM (Weekends)
