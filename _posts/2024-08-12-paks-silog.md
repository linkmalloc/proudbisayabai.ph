Looking for a place that serves delicious, budget-friendly meals at any time of the day? Look no further than Pak’s Silogan in Guadalupe, Cebu City! Now open 24/7, this popular eatery is perfect for satisfying your cravings, whether it’s breakfast, lunch, dinner, or even a late-night snack. Located conveniently on J. Labra Street, right in front of the Our Lady of Guadalupe Parish Church, Pak's Silogan has quickly become a go-to spot for locals and visitors alike.  


At Pak’s Silogan, you can enjoy a variety of flavorful silog meals starting at just 60 pesos. The menu features a wide array of options, including classic favorites like Tosilog, Porksilog, Cornsilog, Hamsilog, Hotsilog, Longganisa Silog, Lumpia Silog, Spam Silog, and Hungarian Silog. For those who love chicken, their Chicken is a must-try! And if you’re in the mood for a little extra, you can add on sides like Pancit Canton for 25 pesos, noodles for 20 pesos, noodles with egg for 35 pesos, or a scrambled egg for just 15 pesos.  

Whether you’re a student on a budget, a family looking for a quick and satisfying meal, or simply a food lover seeking out Cebu’s best silog, Pak’s Silogan has something for everyone. The combination of hearty meals, affordable prices, and round-the-clock service makes it an unbeatable choice for those seeking comfort food at any hour. So, what are you waiting for? Tara, ngaon ta at Pak’s Silogan!
