 ****
 Samgyeopsal craze has been unstoppable among Filipinos. Its popularity continues to grow with little to no signs of slowing down. Filipinos, especially the younger ones, fully embraced the Korean culture. Korean cuisine is definitely a hit to their taste buds. No wonder, Korean restaurants are popping up anywhere not just in the metro, but also in the provinces. There are a lot of Korean and Japanese restaurants to choose from to satisfy your cravings. But the one that stands out is MEATSUMO CEBU and here’s why.  
 
###### QUALITY AND VARIETY  
MeatSumo is a famous Korean and Japanese Fusion unlimited barbeque in Manila. It is derived from the words MEAT: Korean Meat and SUMO: Japanese Sumo Wrestler. Because of its success in other branches, the vibe was brought here in Cebu. It is the newest samgyeopsal find in town. It brings both Korean and Japanese flavours to your table with both unlimited BBQ and side dishes. It has a vibe where Seoul meets Tokyo.

Quality and variety often determines whether diners come back for a second visit. At Meatsumo Cebu, only the finest ingredients are being served and the wide variety of choices is impressive.   

###### Friendly and Modern Ambiance  

Once you enter the restaurant, you can’t help but fall in love with the spacious, comfy, and colourful ambiance. It is also a pet-friendly place. The staff are friendly and accommodating. They are always ready to assist you for you to enjoy your dining experience.  

So, what are you waiting for? The table-top grills are all fired up and waiting for you at Meatsumo Cebu.
