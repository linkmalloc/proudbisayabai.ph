Four Taiwanese nationals allegedly involved in cybercrime activities were arrested by Bureau of Immigration (BI) operatives at a workplace in Mandaue City, bringing to six the number of foreign nationals apprehended in separate operations in Cebu and other parts of the country.

The BI Fugitive Search Unit (FSU) arrested the four in Barangay Pagsabungan on Oct. 29 while they were allegedly operating online platforms from their respective workstations.

In a statement on Sunday, FSU acting chief Rendel Ryan Sy identified the suspects as Chan Feng Jui, 23; Yu Hsin Yu, 28; Wu Ching Yen, 25; and Jian Cheng Jia, 24.

The four are wanted under arrest warrants issued by the Taiwan New Taipei District Prosecutors Office on Aug. 20 in connection with alleged cybercrime activities.

They are currently detained at the BI detention facility in Taguig City while awaiting deportation proceedings.

In a separate operation on Nov. 4, BI operatives arrested two Chinese nationals wanted by authorities in China for alleged involvement in illegal online gambling and fraud.

The FSU arrested Yang Jiazhi, 31, in Parañaque City for allegedly operating an illegal online gambling syndicate. He is wanted under an arrest warrant issued by authorities in Sichuan Province and is reportedly the subject of an Interpol Red Notice.

Yang allegedly helped run a network of 360 illegal gambling websites and handled the syndicate's international financial transactions.

The other Chinese national, Cheng Kun, 41, was arrested in Cagayan over allegations that he defrauded a compatriot through a fraudulent overseas employment scheme.

Chinese authorities in Fuxin issued a detention warrant against Cheng in 2024. He allegedly collected 35,800 yuan from a compatriot by making false promises of overseas employment and other services.

Both Chinese nationals are detained at the BI Warden Facility in Taguig City pending deportation proceedings.
