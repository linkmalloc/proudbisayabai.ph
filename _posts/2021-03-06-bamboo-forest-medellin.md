#### BAMBOO FOREST MEDELLIN  
  
Naol naka ari! 😯😍 A slice of Kyoto, Japan's famous bamboo forest in the middle of a sugarcane field in Medellin, Cebu. 🎋🍃  
  
A sprawling bamboo plantation in northern Cebu is becoming a favorite hangout.  
  

📍 Bamboo Forest, Luy-a Medillin, Cebu.  

****
****
#### HOW TO GET THERE?
  
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
From the North Bus Terminal in which located at SM City Cebu, ride any bus bound for Kawit, Medellin. Bus fare is P180 per person. Then tell the driver to dropped off at the town proper (lungsod). From there ride a tricycle going to the Bamboo Farm.  
  
##### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.  

****
****
##### 📌 Fees:   
- Entrance fee: P20.00 only (with welcome drink)   
- Rent a costume: P100.00   

****
