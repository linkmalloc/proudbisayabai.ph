##### Bluewater Sumilon Island Resort

#VisitSumilon: Soothe Your Soul in a Perfect Island Staycation  

In the municipality of Oslob, there is a very well-known island called Sumilon, which will immediately strike you as an extraordinary getaway. It is a wonderful place to stay where you can release all your cares instantly.  


At Bluewater Sumilon Island Resort, you can find rocks of all shapes and sizes, polished and worn away by years of crashing waves. It is not simply known for having the heavenly and clearest water in Cebu, but surely the waves will bob you like fishing corks and steer you up so high till your feet leave the sandy bottom where you can feel the cool breeze caressing your body.

The management supports local farmers and fisherfolks. Undoubtedly, they will serve you fresh tropical foods in their kitchen that will bring you delight, comfort, and contentment. It will make you feel in the guise of being in a new region than Cebu and still experience Filipino culture to satisfy your Binisaya food cravings because there are other cuisines you can explore to eat.  

If you are striving for something a little more exciting? You may go snorkeling and diving. After a long journey on the trail and delving into the panoramic view of the island, you may even feel the serenity of their natural lagoon.

Not just that! Aiming for a very romantic couple date? The resort organizes a perfect romantic dinner with a glass of sparkling wine as well as a very lovely decoration where you can say "love is in the air". It is hassle-free and, they will set up everything to make it noteworthy!

Nindota sad diri uy! Breath nature’s fresh air and dip in Sumilon Island’s clear turquoise waters to welcome a break from the rigors of distance learning, tired mind, and weary soul.  

Wake up with a picturesque view. Feel the island breeze, listen to the humming of the birds and the melody of the waves. Ari na dayon, bai!  

Exact Location: Sumilon, 6025 Oslob   

###### HOW TO GET THERE?

Via commute: From Cebu: Take a taxi cab going to South Bus Terminal and ride a Ceres Bus going to Bato Oslob. You may just inform the driver to drop you off in Bancogon Oslob at Bluewater Sumilon Island Resort's port. Time Travel is 4-5 hours land travel and 7-10 minutes boat ride going to Sumilon Island.  

Via private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
