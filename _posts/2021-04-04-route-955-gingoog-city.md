#### Route 955 Gingoog City-Claveria
LOOK: Baguio FEELS? Route 955 Gingoog City-Claveria, Misamis Oriental in a Day: The Little Baguio City of Mindanao.   
  
Cafés \| Sceneries \| Foods \| Nature  
  
A very cool and refreshing place to visit  to ease your stress. Definitely, you'll enjoy much along the road while viewing nature and mountainous sceneries. Don’t forget to stop over to their cafés which offers great foods and hot drinks. ❤  
  
Soon when this pandemic will end, add Claveria into your bucket list. Surely, you’ll enjoy  the relaxing view.   
  
Cafés and Places to Visit: ☕️🍽🍂  
- Claveria View Deck  
- San Vills Cafe  
- Europe Cuisine  
- Guzmana  
- Claveria Municipal Hall  
- Cold Spring  
- Don Narciso
