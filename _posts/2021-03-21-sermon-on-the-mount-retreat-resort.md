#### Sermon on the Mount Retreat Resort
  
LOOK: Perfect Sceneries in a Rustic Getaway only at Sermon on the Mount Retreat Resort! 💭😯😍  
  
Stay fresh during the summer with the cool breeze and fresh crisp air.  🍃🍂   
  
🌍 Exact Location: Trancentral High Way, Gaas, Balamban, Cebu  
  
###### 📌 How to get there?   
   
###### 🗺Via Commute:  
There is a Van for Hire in Ayala Terminal mosakay ka diha going to Balamban but only stop at Transcentral Highway. From Transcentral highway ask some locals were to ride going to the exact location.   
  
###### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorcycle (habal-habal) or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.   

****
###### 📌 RATES:  
- 1 Big Room (12 pax): P12,500.00  
- 2 Rooms (17 pax): P17,500.00   
- 2 Big Rooms (24 pax): P25,000.00   
- Room (4 pax): P5,000.00 - 4 pax   
  
****
###### 📌AMENITIES:   
-  Pool   
- Sauna   
- Karaoke   
- Trekking  
- Massage  
- Fun night Marshmallows Bonfire  
- Forest Bathing at viewing deck   
  
****
> NOTE:  
- Complete cooking and dining utensils   
- w/ fridge &amp; bbq griller  
- clean as you go
