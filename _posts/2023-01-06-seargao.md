SIARGAO IN ARGAO, SEARGAO!  

Seargao Seaview Café is a newly opened cafe offers delicious food and stunning views of the sea. It has large gardens, Seaview decks, a boardwalk with sea villas, and pavilions where you can grab those aesthetic photos like you are in Siargao! Besides that, they also have exciting activities like the seawater net pool, water adventures, crystal kayaks, and more! You can hold special events like birthdays, weddings, and dates with your loved ones too.  )
