Yura Highland
  
Vacation home with overlooking Sea of Cloud, a peaceful stay away from the buzz of the city life. No neighbors, no noise, just birds singing! 🦜🦅🍃  
  
🌎 Exact Location: Datu Salumay Marilog District Davao City. (Before Hillsview)   

****
📌 Rates:   
- Villa Rate: 12,000 good for 15pax (Introductory Price)  
- Exclusive Whole Villa Good for 15 pax   
- Excess fee per person is 500 pesos  
- Maximum of 20 persons including children more than 7 yrs old.   
- Pwede sa pud siya 800 pesos each if group kayo since good for 15 pax siya .  
  
🏔 Newest Modern Minimalist Vacation Home.  

****
📌 Schedule:  
OVERNIGHT STAY ONLY!  
- Check-in starts at 2pm  
- Check-out before 11am   

****
📌 Inclusion:  
- Videoke until 10pm   
- Grill area  
- Gas stove   
- Utensils (20 person)  
- kitchen cookware  
- Available drinking water  
- Bath, shower and comfort room   

****
Notes:  
- Deposit 50% for reservation.   
- Reservation fee is not refundable   
- No room service   
- Pets not allowed  
- No Corkage   

****
Sea of clouds depends on the weather.  
Less clouds when raining, windy or full moon.  
  
NO SIGNAL. NO WIFI.  
Day Tour not allowed.  
  
📌 What to bring?  
- Bath Towels  
- Toiletries  
- Foods  
- Cooking Ingredients
