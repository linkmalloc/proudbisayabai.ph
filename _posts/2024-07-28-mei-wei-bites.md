Exciting News, Cebu!  

Mei Wei Bites Lapu-Lapu, renowned for offering the most affordable and authentic Chinese dumplings in the Philippines, has officially opened its doors. This new dining spot promises to be a haven for dumpling enthusiasts, bringing a taste of China to the heart of Lapu-Lapu. Located at Dhelz Foodpark, near Birhen sa Regla and right beside Poblacion Barangay Hall, Mei Wei Bites is set to become a favorite destination for locals and tourists alike.  

****
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/meiweibites/p7.jpg)


Operating from 10 AM to 8 PM, Mei Wei Bites invites everyone to indulge in their delectable range of dumplings and other Chinese delicacies. The eatery prides itself on its commitment to quality and authenticity, ensuring each bite transports you to the bustling streets of China. With its convenient location and affordable prices, Mei Wei Bites aims to provide a unique and memorable dining experience.  


Don't miss out on this mouthwatering opportunity to savor the best Chinese dumplings in Cebu. Visit Mei Wei Bites today and join the growing community of satisfied customers. For more updates and mouthwatering photos, check out their Facebook page. #MeiWeiBites #ChineseDumplings #ProudBisayaBai
