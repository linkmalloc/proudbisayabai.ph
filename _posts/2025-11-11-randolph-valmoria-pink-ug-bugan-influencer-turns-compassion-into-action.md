Known for her vibrant pink “Ug Bugan” persona, influencer and content creator Randolph Valmoria is proving that style and heart can go hand in hand. Following the devastation caused by Typhoon Tino in Cebu, Randolph spearheaded a relief campaign that raised ₱638,078 for affected families, a testament to her unwavering commitment to helping those in need.



Randolph’s philanthropic spirit is not new. She has consistently organized donation drives, including support for earthquake victims, and she continues to rally her friends, celebrity allies, and brand partners to amplify her efforts. Among the generous sponsors were



Rowel Divina Donation Drive – ₱100,000

Momshie Melai Cantiveros – ₱50,000

Madi Telma – ₱20,000

Christine Mae Worada (TikTok Shop) – ₱50,000

Megasoft Hygienic Products – ₱30,000 plus hygiene kits worth ₱200,000

Abegail Cariquitan (partner of Elias J. TV) – ₱25,000

Tosen Foods Inc. – 50 boxes (5,000 cans) of Señorita Sardines

Bang Smile Doctor Dental Clinic – dental kits valued at ₱300,000



Together with volunteers, Randolph’s team prepared 1,000 relief packs and 350 hygiene kits, with plans for 3,000 more packed meals and bottled water to reach even more families. Sharing her gratitude, Randolph said, “Di gyud nako ni mabuhat ug ako ra. Daghang salamat sa mga volunteers nga nagtabang karon.”



In addition, volunteers recently descended from the mountainous areas of Balamban, Cebu, particularly Brgy. Cabagdalan. On November 11, Tuesday, they reported casualties and still-missing residents due to the flooding caused by Typhoon Tino, highlighting the ongoing need for rescue operations in remote areas. Randolph’s team extends thanks to Balamban & Asturias volunteers and the BONAL ROBEL Team for their tireless support.



Through her hands-on approach and relentless advocacy, Randolph demonstrates how influencers can make a tangible difference, turning visibility into meaningful action. Her work is not just about raising funds; it’s about building community, hope, and resilience in the face of disaster.
