###### Paharuhay Campsite  

An Exclusive Use of Camp site

\#BukidGoals: Maka pahuway jud tag tarong diri! Need a new place to chill with your barkadas and your family? Try this new campsite in Caduawan Tabogon Cebu, Just a 10-15 min trekk to the camp and you will reach this beautiful 360 degree view!  

****
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/paharuhay_campsite/5.jpg)
<small>Source: Mark Cuyos | (No copyright infringement intended)</small>  

The owners of Paharuhay Campsite, Manolo and Maria, first intended to establish this location to relax, spend time with family, and appreciate nature. They went to have a look at the breadth of the family's land one day and discovered this lovely mountain while walking.

This mountain offers panoramic views to the north, south, east, and west. From there, you can even see the tip of the Mayon volcano. They then decide to build a little cottage with a 360-degree view of the mountains and sea, ideal for sunrise and sunset. ​

They realized, however, that it would be a pity not to share this with everyone. As a result, they wanted to share this lovely location with the rest of the world.

>Paharuhay means to let go of worries and appreciate the present time.

###### How to get there?  

🌏 Exact Location: Upper Caduawan, Tabogon, Cebu 🇵🇭  

🕑 Check in time: 2PM
🕛 Check out: 12NN (the next day)

🗺 Via Commute: Take a bus ride going to BOGO or San Remegio and just let them drop you off at tabogon caduawan inbetween km83-km84. The exact location is inbetween km83 to km84.. right before reaching km84 in caduawan tabogon. 
Cellphone Signal is accessible in the area and the campsite it self.  

🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.

###### Rates:  
Php 2500 (maximum of 10 pax)
For overnight camp stay  

###### What to Enjoy? 
-  Free breakfast 
- 360deg view of mountains and sea; perfect view for sunrise and sunset
- Fully Equipped kitchen free of butane stove, rice cooker, refrigerator, pots and pans, plates and bowls, utensils, glasses, water dispenser and 1 gallon of water.  
- Free use of pool and tree house  
- Bbq grill is provided   
- No corkage fee for food and drinks (you can bring your own food, groceries and beverages)  
- Dining tables with chairs
