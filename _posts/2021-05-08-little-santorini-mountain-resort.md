#### Little Santorini Mountain Resort   

Looking for Instagrammable view with picturesque mountain background for your memorable summer staycations? Then, Little Santorini in Liloan, Cebu is PERFECT for you!  
A Mountain Resort with amazing view of the mountains and a secluded place to relax and chill. This greek-inspired resort is located at the beautiful town of Liloan, Cebu.

****
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/little_santorini_mountain_resort/2.jpg)  
<small>Source: Lyndon Bridge</small>  

The place has 3 swimming pools, a big one for adult, a kiddie pool and a jacuzzi.  
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/little_santorini_mountain_resort/5.jpg)  

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/little_santorini_mountain_resort/4.jpg)  

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/little_santorini_mountain_resort/13.jpg)  

The place is built at the side of a hill. So when going to the resort be ready to climb the sloopy hill.

At the top of the hill, you can setup camping tents. It is where the restaurant is also located.

The place resides at the side of a hill so be ready to climb some stairs.

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/little_santorini_mountain_resort/13.jpg)  

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/little_santorini_mountain_resort/13.jpg)  



FEEL the vacation with family and friends at Little Santorini Mountain Resort in Liloan, Cebu.


Be ready to climb some hills! 

🕑 Operating hours: 
Day use 9am to 5pm 
Night Use 3pm to 10pm

##### HOW TO GET THERE    

🌏 Exact Location:  Sandayung 2, Brgy. San Roque, Lilo-an, Cebu  
  
🗺 Via COMMUTE: From SM City Cebu ride any bus or v-hire going to Lilo-an. Drop off at Yati intersection, near Yati Elementary School. From there, ride a habal-habal (motorbike) going to Little Santorini Mountain Resort. Fare is only P70.00 each good for 2 persons and P80.00 if only one. approx. 1 hour away from Cebu City, 15 minutes away from the main road (going uphill).  
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
