With the heat rising these days, everyone deserves a place to cool down and relax. If you are looking for a refreshing natural escape in Cebu, Mantawihan Spring is a destination worth visiting.



Located in Cogon, this beautiful cold spring offers crystal clear waters and a peaceful atmosphere, perfect for a quick getaway. One of its best features is that there is no entrance fee, making it an affordable destination for families, friends, and travelers looking to beat the summer heat.



A Hidden Nature Escape



Mantawihan Spring is surrounded by towering cliffs, lush plants, and trees that create a relaxing and scenic environment. Cemented pathways make the area accessible, while nipa palms add a tropical charm to the surroundings. On one side of the spring, visitors will notice a formation that resembles a small hidden cave, adding a unique and tranquil feel to the place.



Because of its cool water and serene setting, the spring has become a favorite spot for locals and tourists who want to unwind, swim, and enjoy nature away from the busy city.



How to get there?&#x20;



Via Commute

Travelers heading to Tuburan have several transportation options from Cebu City:



• Take a V-hire from Cebu IT Park to Tuburan

• Ride a Ceres Bus from SM City Cebu to Tuburan

• Take a Corominas Bus from Cebu South Bus Terminal



The estimated fare is around ₱180 one way.



Via Private Vehicle

For those driving, the destination can easily be found using Waze. Simply search for Mantawihan Spring for accurate directions.



Why Visit Mantawihan Spring



Mantawihan Spring offers a refreshing retreat from the heat, surrounded by natural beauty and peaceful scenery. Whether you want to swim in cold spring water, enjoy a quiet moment in nature, or simply explore another hidden gem in Cebu, this destination provides a perfect escape.
