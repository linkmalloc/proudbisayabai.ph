#### Camotes Island  
 
Camotes Islands supplies the perfect mix of tranquility and entertainment in the exciting city of Cebu. Combined area is 236.36 square kilometres. It is 34 nautical miles away from Cebu City and is part of Cebu. Camotes Islands comprises three major islands namely, Pacijan Island, Poro Island and Ponson Island. It also has a small Islet, Tulang island. There are 4 municipalities in Camotes. Poro and Tudela municipalites are located in Poro Island. Pacijan Island and the islet of Tulang are parts of the municapality of San Franciso. Ponson Island's sole municipality is Pilar.
  
🌎 Exact location: Camotes Islands, Cebu

#### Tourist Spots

****
###### Lake Danao Park
If you want to chill and just be close with nature Lake Danao is a great choice to go. There's a lot of activities you can do in the place such as Zip line across the other side of the lake, Kayaking, Pedal boat for two or for four people. There are also alot of restaurant in the place for an affordable price.

- Entrance Fee : 50.00 pesos

###### Timubo Cave
Explore the beautiful caves in Camotes. One of these is Timubo cave which was rich in history that provides water to the residents back then. Going there is so easy. The freshwater in the cave is so cold and refreshing. There is also a grotto inside.  

Entrance Fee: 50.00 pesos

###### Papa Tasie Family Park and Resort
Papa Tasie Family Park and Resort is Lakeside Hotel and Resort located in Lake Danao, Camotes Islands. They also have Paddle Boat Rental, Adventure Zipline and Motorcycle (Scooter) Rentals.

Facebook Page - [https://www.facebook.com/PapaTasieResortOfficial](https://www.facebook.com/PapaTasieResortOfficial)

- Entrance Fee : 100.00 pesos  


📌 How to get there?  
  
🗺 From Cebu City: From North Bus terminal (SM City Cebu) ride a bus passing thru Danao Port. Fare is around Php 40.00 - 60.00. From Danao Port ride a passenger boat bound for Consuelo Port in Camotes. Fare is Php220 + terminal fee of Php 5.00. Travel time is around 1 hr upto 2hrs. Once you reach Consuelo Port, there are a lot of public utility motorcycles that you can rent to roam around Camotes Island.  
  
📂 Travel Requirements: just bring valid ID   
  
📌 Rates:  
☀️ Motorcycle rental fee: php 500 for 24hrs  
Motorbangka fee from mainland Camotes   
  
☀️ Island to Tulang Diot Island: We were able to book a special trip for pho 400 for the six of us.   
  
☀️ Tent space fee in Tulang Diot Island: php 150 for overnight stay. They also have tents for rent for php 300 including the tent space.  
  
☀️ Paraiso Cave Entrance fee: php 100.00  
Mangodlong Rock Entrance fee: php 20.00
