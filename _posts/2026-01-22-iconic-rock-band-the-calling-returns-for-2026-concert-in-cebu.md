Get ready to sing your hearts out, Cebu! The early 2000s nostalgia is hitting the Queen City of the South hard as the iconic American rock band, The Calling, brings their "Before The World Turns To Dust" Tour to the Philippines.

Mark your calendars for Friday, February 20, 2026, because the Waterfront Cebu City Hotel and Casino is about to transform into a massive rock arena. Known globally for their mega-hit anthem "Wherever You Will Go," the band is set to deliver a night of raw emotion, gritty vocals, and the alternative rock sound that defined a generation.

Presented by EKG Productions, this concert promises to be a journey through the band's discography, featuring fan favorites like "Adrienne," "Could It Be Any Harder," and "Our Lives." Whether you were there during their peak MTV days or you discovered their timeless sound on Spotify, this is a musical event you cannot afford to miss.

**Secure Your Spot: Ticket Prices and Seat Plan**

The official seat plan has been released, offering a variety of options for every type of concert-goer, from the die-hard fans who want to be up close to the stage to those who just want to vibe with the crowd.

Here is the breakdown of the ticket tiers:

• VIP (Reserved Seating): PHP 5,000 

• GOLD (Reserved Seating): PHP 4,000  

• SILVER (Free Seating): PHP 3,000 

• BRONZE (Free Seating): PHP 2,000 

• BALCONY (Free Seating): PHP 1,500 

• GEN AD (Standing): PHP 1,200 

The show starts at 8:00 PM. With a lineup of hits that have stood the test of time, tickets are expected to sell out fast. 

Gather your friends, memorize the lyrics, and prepare for a night where "The Calling" finally comes home to Cebu.

For inquiries and ticket reservations, you may contact 0939 969 9786.
