Lapu-Lapu City has added two more barangays to its list of communities certified as drug-cleared, bringing the total to 11 as local officials continue their campaign against illegal drugs.

Barangays Punta Engaño and Pusok received the recognition during the observance of Drug Abuse Prevention and Control (DAPC) Week 2026 on Monday, Nov. 16.

The City of Lapu-Lapu Office for Substance Abuse Prevention (CLOSAP) organized the activity with the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA) Region 7, Lapu-Lapu City Police Office (LCPO) and Department of Education (DepEd).

The two barangays join Subabasbas, Calawisan, Bankal, Pajo, Pajac, Babag, Ibo, Buaya and Agus, which had previously secured drug-cleared status.

To qualify as drug-cleared, a barangay must have previously been identified as drug-affected but must meet requirements showing that it has addressed illegal drug activities. These include having no identified drug users and pushers, implementing active anti-drug programs through the Sangguniang Barangay and maintaining a functional Barangay Anti-Drug Abuse Council (BADAC).

The barangay must also undergo validation and deliberation by the PDEA.

Barangay Caohagan, meanwhile, is classified as drug-free because it has never been identified as affected by illegal drugs since the validation process began. Drug-free barangays have no recorded cases involving drug users, pushers or drug dens and have sustained their anti-drug programs.

CLOSAP Executive Director Gary Lao said the campaign requires the participation of families, schools, workplaces and communities, not only law enforcement agencies.

He stressed the need for collective action to protect young people and maintain safe communities.

City Councilor Emilio Galaroza, who delivered the message of Mayor Ma. Cynthia “Cindi” King-Chan, said the city's response should extend beyond law enforcement.

He said the local government must strengthen education and prevention initiatives, provide opportunities for young people and ensure that individuals who need assistance receive proper guidance and support.

LCPO Director Police Col. Agosto Daskeo said illegal drugs were linked to other criminal activities, adding that nearly 80 percent of people detained in local jails were involved in cases directly related to illegal drugs.

He said police could not address the problem alone and called for stronger cooperation among government agencies and communities.

Department of the Interior and Local Government Lapu-Lapu City Director Neila Aquino also emphasized the importance of community participation in sustaining anti-drug efforts.

PDEA Region 7 Acting Assistant Regional Director Thessa Tiuzen, who represented Regional Director Joel B. Plaza, said the goal of a drug-free city must begin at the barangay and family levels.

The observance carried the theme “Nagkakaisang Pamilya at Pamayanan Laban sa Illegal na Droga Para sa Ligtas at Malusog na Kinabukasan.”

The activity ended with city officials, partner agencies and community stakeholders signing a pledge of commitment to sustain their efforts against illegal drugs.
