Cebu City Hall employees may receive up to P25,000 in year-end incentives, but the proposed additional P20,000 payout remains subject to national government guidelines and the city's approval of the funding.

  

Mayor Nestor Archival Sr. said the city is working to release the incentives before Christmas, provided it meets the requirements for their grant.

  

The potential P25,000 benefit consists of a P5,000 Productivity Enhancement Incentive (PEI) and a proposed P20,000 Service Recognition Incentive (SRI).

  

Councilor David Tumulak, chairman of the Committee on Budget and Finance, said the P5,000 PEI is already included in the city's 2026 annual budget. Regular, casual and job-order (JO) employees are covered by this incentive.

  

The proposed SRI, however, has yet to receive final approval.

  

Tumulak said the city had allocated a proposed P61 million for the SRI under Supplemental Budget No. 2, with funding expected to come from unutilized city funds.

  

The proposal still requires a review of its funding source and confirmation of the Department of Budget and Management's (DBM) guidelines for the incentive's release.

  

“Wa pa na siyay guidelines. Nagpaabot pa ta sa guidelines,” Tumulak said in an interview with reporters on Friday, referring to the SRI.

  

He said the Committee on Budget and Finance was also checking the proposed funding before acting on the measure.

  

Archival earlier mentioned a possible P5,000 to P7,000 Christmas bonus for JO workers. Tumulak clarified, however, that the amount likely referred to the P5,000 PEI already provided in the annual budget.

  

The city has no separate appropriation for an additional Christmas bonus for JO workers, he said.

  

Under the current proposal, JO employees are covered by the P5,000 PEI but are not included in the proposed SRI, which is intended for regular and casual employees.

  

Tumulak stressed that employees should not yet consider the additional P20,000 SRI a guaranteed payout while the city awaits DBM guidance and completes its review of the proposed funds.
