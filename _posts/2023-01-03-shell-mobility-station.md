Shell Robinsons Mobility Station is the first gallery-themed Shell Mobility Destination in the world. In addition to the museum of art, it primarily provides high-quality fuels and environmentally friendly features including solar panels, green walls, and rainwater catchment. About 30% of its power consumption is supplied by the sun’s power. The area's center of attention is the staircase. This is not your average stairway because, with the other locations, it is encircled by 84 paintings. These include works by Lamberto Acyatan, Raul Agas, and Antonio Ylanan, three highly regarded master painters from Cebu. Their masterpiece shows the daily life in the Philippines. You'll be surprised when you get here, for sure!  

Furthermore, 50 staff are employed by the company's fuel operations, fast repairs, convenience stores, bakeries, restaurants, and snack bars. Local items from various MSMEs in Cebu were featured on this station whilst also, pursuant to the DTI's One Town One Product initiative.

The restrooms are intriguing enough to cause you to pause because they are a very improbable area for you to wind down.  The main hall has a variety of Filipino-made goods and even game boards that have a distinctive and appealing ambiance. You and your pals can also utilize the unique conference room for meetings or merely for leisure. Bai, this is something you must not forget to put on your bucket list!
