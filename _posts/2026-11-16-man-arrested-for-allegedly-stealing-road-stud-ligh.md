A man was arrested after he was allegedly caught removing road stud lights, commonly known as Cat’s Eye, along Ouano Avenue in Barangay Tipolo, Mandaue City.

The Mandaue Safety Maintenance Area Response Team (Mandaue-SMART) apprehended the suspect in the early hours of the morning and turned him over to the Subangdaku Police Station on Nov. 6.

He is facing theft charges and remained in police custody as of the latest report.

Mandaue-SMART team leader Gerard Glenn Soon Gobalani said the suspect allegedly took about three road stud lights, including their cables.

Gobalani said the suspect used tools he brought with him, including a hammer, to pry the devices from the pavement. He allegedly continued removing the lights even while they were still illuminated.

“Nakita to nato kay naay nisumbong sa atoa,” Gobalani said, adding that a concerned individual reported the incident to the team.

The suspect allegedly placed the devices inside a sack after removing them. Gobalani said authorities believe the materials may have been intended for sale by weight because the lights are made of aluminum.

He also claimed that proceeds from the sale were supposedly intended to buy illegal drugs.

Gobalani said the team's records showed that the suspect had allegedly been apprehended by police on previous occasions for theft-related incidents.

“Atong na-establish nga in and out na ni sila, theft gihapon... badlungon na gyud ba,” he said.

Mandaue-SMART continues to monitor the area to prevent similar incidents and protect public infrastructure.

Gobalani thanked residents and motorists who report suspicious activities and urged the public to help safeguard government property by informing authorities about possible theft or vandalism.
