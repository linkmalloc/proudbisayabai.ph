Several Cebu City barangays will experience scheduled power interruptions from Nov. 24 to 29 as the power distributor conducts transformer upgrades, line maintenance and rerouting works across the city.

The interruptions will affect residential areas, subdivisions, commercial establishments and parts of the Carbon Public Market, with some outages lasting up to eight hours and an overnight interruption scheduled downtown.

On Tuesday, Nov. 24, portions of Barangays Buhisan, Pamutan, Punta Princesa and Tisa will have no electricity from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. along parts of Buhisan Road, Nazareth Street and Tinubdan Road for a distribution transformer upgrade.

Parts of Tres de Abril Street and Tres de Abril Extension in Barangay San Nicolas Proper will also be affected from 9 a.m. to 2:40 p.m. while crews tap a service entrance wire.

In the mountain barangay of Guba, portions of Guba Road will experience an interruption from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. for voltage correction. A section of Eduardo Aboitiz Street in Barangay Tinago will also be affected from 9 a.m. to noon for primary line reliability work.

On Wednesday, Nov. 25, power will be interrupted from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. in portions of Barangay Bulacao along Flordeliz and Gumamela streets as crews relocate a primary pole at a customer's request to prevent future interruptions caused by an obstruction.

On Thursday, Nov. 26, portions of Barangays Bacayan and Pit-os will have no electricity from 8 a.m. to 11 a.m. for primary line maintenance. Affected areas include A. Miñoza Street, Daffodil Street, Jerusalem Street, Bacayan Barangay Road and Fressia Street, including Metropolis Phase 2 Subdivision.

Parts of the road leading to Hillsview Village in Barangay Tisa will also be affected from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. as crews install secondary lines.

In Barangay Talamban, portions of Sunny Hills Subdivision along Sunvalley Drive will experience an outage from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. for the installation of a distribution transformer and upgrading of secondary lines.

On Saturday, Nov. 28, portions of Barangay Lahug will be without power from 9 a.m. to noon while crews tap a service entrance wire. The affected areas include parts of Ecotech Road, Sunset Drive and Gorordo Avenue, as well as portions of Sitios Avocado and San Jose, Ecotech Hills, Sudlon Compound, Sunset Village and Top Hills.

A separate 10-hour interruption will affect portions of Barangays Ermita, Kalubihan and Pahina Central from 8 p.m. on Nov. 28 until 6 a.m. on Nov. 29 as crews reroute primary lines.

The overnight work will cover parts of Quezon Boulevard, F. Calderon Street Extension, Leon Kilat Street, M. Escaño Street, Magallanes Street and MC Briones Street, including portions of Sitios Bato and Warwick Barracks and the Carbon Public Market.

Vendors and stallholders at Carbon Public Market are advised to prepare for the overnight interruption, particularly those handling perishable goods that require refrigeration.

Residents in affected areas are urged to charge electronic devices, store water and unplug appliances before the scheduled outages.

The power distributor also advised consumers to treat all power lines as live throughout the announced interruption periods because electricity may be restored earlier than scheduled once work is completed.

Households are also encouraged to check on senior citizens, persons with disabilities and residents who depend on electrically powered medical equipment, and arrange backup power where necessary.
