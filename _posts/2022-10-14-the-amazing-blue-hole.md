##### AMAZING BLUE HOLE TUBURAN  

Consider visiting the Blue Hole Spring at Barangay San Juan, Tuburan town, in northern Cebu.  

This spring has been one of many attractions frequently visited by both locals and tourists in this peaceful town of Tuburan. It is about 46 kilometers north of Cebu City, or about an hour to an hour and a half journey from the Queen City of the South.  

There are many things to do in Tuburan, but let's learn more about the famous Blue Hole Spring.

Many people assume that the spring was called solely for its color. The bluish tint of the spring indicates that it is fairly deep. “Wala pay diver naka abot sa kina ilawman niini”, according to locals.
There is no fee to enter this spring's pool. But this does not give us license to exploit the wonderful gifts of nature.  

Around the spring, there are several tables and chairs that may be utilized for dining or just relaxing with family or friends. Bamboo rafts are also available, allowing visitors to unwind while floating on Blue Hole Spring's clear waters.   

You may take a brief plunge or remain a bit longer and marvel at nature's best, but remember to leave no trace behind.  

###### How to get there?

Exact Location: San Juan, Tuburan Cebu

Via Private Vehicle: Located at the western part of Cebu — 2 hours away from the City. The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
