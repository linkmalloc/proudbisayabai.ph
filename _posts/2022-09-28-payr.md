Sige PAYR!

Dine while waiting for your car to be washed! Suwayi ug tilawi ang ilang bag-o nga mga lamian na pagkaon. Kung nangita mo ug Lami and affordable na kan-anan, Naa diri imong gipangita!  

PAYR is an open-air dining concept with a local, rustic, carwash concept. Some nice greenery adds to the relaxing vibe of the place. If you want to eat, chill, or drink outdoors, you may do it here. It’s quite nice to dine here whether day time or at night.  

###### How to get there ?  
Exact Location: General Echavez Street, near DOLE7 Cebu City 🇵🇭  

🗺 Via commute: From Ayala Mall, ride a Jeep or Beep bus (13C) going to colon then tell the driver to drop-off at Dole in front of Jollibee Gorordo. From dole just a walking distance going to the exact location.

🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze: 8W53+V8X, Cebu City, Cebu
