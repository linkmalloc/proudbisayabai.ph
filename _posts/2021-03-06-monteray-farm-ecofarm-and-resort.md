##### HOW TO GET THERE?

****
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
From South Bus Terminal, ride a bus going to Pinamungajan. Tell the driver to drop you off Pinamungjan Public Market or "lungsod". From there ride a habal-habal going to Dakit Poblacion ask the driver where is Monteray Farm most of them know's the place already.   
  
##### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated thru Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle.  

**** 
##### 📌 Rates:   
- Good for 2 standard room - P1,500/night  
- Good for 2 superior room - P2,000/night  
- Good for 6 - P3,000/night
