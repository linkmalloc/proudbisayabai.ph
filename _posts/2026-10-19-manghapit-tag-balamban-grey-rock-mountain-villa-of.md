### How to get to Grey Rock Mountain Villa

  

Grey Rock Mountain Villa is located in the mountainous area of Balamban, Cebu. Travelers coming from Cebu City can consider traveling by private vehicle or public transportation.

  

### By private vehicle

  

From Cebu City, take the Cebu–Transcentral Highway toward Balamban. Follow the route provided by the property host to reach the exact location.

  

Travelers should prepare for winding mountain roads and changing weather conditions. Fog and rain may affect visibility, so drive carefully and allow additional travel time.

  

### By public transportation

  

Travelers may take a van or other available public transportation from Cebu City toward Balamban. Depending on the drop-off point, an additional motorcycle ride or privately arranged transfer may be needed to reach the property.

  

Because the villa is located in a mountain area, it is best to coordinate with the host before departure for the exact location, recommended route, and available transportation arrangements.

  

### Estimated transportation fare

  

For travelers taking public transportation, the estimated van fare from Cebu City to Balamban is around ₱160 per person one way, or approximately ₱320 for a round trip. However, travelers should prepare an additional budget for transportation from Balamban town proper to Grey Rock Mountain Villa, as the final leg may require a locally arranged motorcycle ride or private transfer.

  

Those traveling by private vehicle should also account for fuel expenses, which will depend on their vehicle type, fuel consumption, and starting point.

  

Since fares and transportation availability may change, guests are advised to contact the property host before their trip to confirm current rates, arrange a pickup if necessary, and obtain the exact directions to the villa.

  

**Travel tip: For families and larger groups, arranging a private vehicle or group transfer in advance may offer greater convenience, particularly when traveling with children or carrying luggage.**
