Thousands of Cebuanos filled the streets this Sunday, November 30, for SuPaKK: Sugboanong Pakigbisog Kontra Korapsyon.

In a powerful show of unity, Catholic devotees, students, and workers marched together from the Basilica Minore del Sto. Niño to Fuente Osmeña Circle.

A Call for Change

The march was peaceful but firm. The crowd gathered to demand real accountability and an end to corruption. It was a walk of hope, proving that the people of Cebu are ready to stand together for what is right.

“A symbolic act of solidarity, humility, and hope."
