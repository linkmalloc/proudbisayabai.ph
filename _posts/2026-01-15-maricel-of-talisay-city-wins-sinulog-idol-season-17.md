Besting 12 other hopefuls, Maricel Bingga of Talisay City emerged as the Grand Champion of Sinulog Idol Season 17 during the finals night held at the Fuente Osmeña Circle, January 15.

2nd: Francis Alinsonorin - Naga City

3rd: Porsche Malingin - Carcar City

4th: Martin Lausa - Toledo City

5th: Evan Villacastin - Carcar City
