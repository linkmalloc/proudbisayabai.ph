Ever wondered which senatorial candidate shares your views the most? Instead of guessing based on campaign promises or social media posts, this online quiz lets your answers do the work.

  

With the option to choose any Philippine dialect, 2025.nosibalasi.org is a quick and accessible quiz that helps determine which politician aligns with your views and beliefs. 

  

The quiz asks 13 straightforward questions on major political and social issues, from divorce and the death penalty to the West Philippine Sea and the International Criminal Court (ICC). Simply answer whether you’re in favor, against, or would rather skip a question, and the site generates a ranked list of senatorial candidates whose publicly stated positions most closely match your own.

  

The fun part? You can click on each candidate to see exactly where your opinions matched and clashed with each candidate, complete with the sources used to determine their positions. It’s an easy way to compare platforms, explore different viewpoints, and see how your own beliefs stack up against different political figures.

  

Originally made for the 2025 elections, the quiz is still a fun little rabbit hole to fall into if you’re curious about your own political views. It’s not meant to tell you who to vote for, but it is a good way to see which candidates actually share your beliefs. Whether you’re just killing time, settling a debate with friends, or simply wondering, “Kinsa kaha akong ka-vibes?” this one’s worth a click.
