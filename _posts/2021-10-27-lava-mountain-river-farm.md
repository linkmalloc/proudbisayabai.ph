##### Lava Mountain River Farm  

\#Chillvibes: This weekend na bah? Imagine going to a place where you can unwind and recharge after an exhausting work week. Why not pamper yourself with a rejuvenating weekend staycation at Lava Mountain River  Farm!  

**Lava Mountain River Farm** is an eco-resort that offers serenity, tranquillity, and, above all, exclusivity.
The 4-hectare property, located alongside the Mananga River in the highlands of Busay, is thick with trees and lovely environment, providing an abundance of fresh air and a peaceful atmosphere. Our infinity pool is fed by constant running water, keeping it fresh and rejuvenating, making it a genuine one-of-a-kind experience!

###### Inclusions: 
- Exclusive Use of Riverside Hut, and Riverside Villa Grounds.
- Shared use of swimming pool.
- Use of open areas and access to river.
- Drinking Water Provided.
- With Grilling area - free 1kg of charcoal.
- With Refrigerator.
- No corkage fees.
- No bringing of electrical appliances for cooking.
