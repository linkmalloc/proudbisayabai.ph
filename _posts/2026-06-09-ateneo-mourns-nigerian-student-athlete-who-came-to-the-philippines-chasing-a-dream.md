The basketball community is mourning the loss of a young Nigerian student-athlete, Divine Adili, whose dream of building a better future through sports came to a tragic end.

On June 8, 2026, Ateneo de Manila University confirmed the death of one of its men’s basketball players following an alleged drowning incident during a team-building activity in Dipaculao, Aurora.

He came to the Philippines carrying more than just a basketball. He carried the hopes of his family and dreams of a brighter future.

Like many young athletes who leave their homeland in pursuit of opportunity, he traveled thousands of miles from Nigeria with aspirations of obtaining an education, excelling in the sport he loved, and creating a better life for those waiting for him back home.

Every training session, every game, and every sacrifice reflected his determination to succeed. Beyond the court, he was a son, a friend, and a young man driven by faith, perseverance, and a desire to make his family proud.

His journey was never solely about basketball. It was about creating opportunities, overcoming challenges, and turning dreams into reality.

Now, that journey has come to a heartbreaking end.

Instead of returning home with stories of success and achievement, he returns to Nigeria in a coffin, leaving behind grieving loved ones, teammates, coaches, and supporters who are struggling to process the tragedy.

His passing serves as a painful reminder that behind every athlete is a person with hopes, responsibilities, and a family that loves them deeply. Behind every jersey is a story of sacrifice and ambition.

Today, many mourn not only the loss of a promising basketball player but also the loss of a young life filled with potential.

Though his time was cut short, the impact he made on those around him will not be forgotten. His courage, determination, and love for his family remain part of the legacy he leaves behind.

Gone too soon, but never forgotten.

May he rest in peace.
