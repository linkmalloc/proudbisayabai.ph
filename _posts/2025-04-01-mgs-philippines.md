MGS Philippines has officially launched in Cebu! Offering 20-in-1 comprehensive features, their top-of-the-line product, the MGS Elite Pro Smart Lock, redefines security and convenience.  

Under the esteemed Don Macchiatos Group of Companies, MGS Philippines has proudly inaugurated its first-ever branch in SM City Cebu, marking a significant milestone in its expansion. With this new location, the company continues to strengthen its presence in the Philippines, offering high-quality products and exceptional customer service. This launch reflects the Group’s commitment to growth and bringing innovative experiences to more communities, particularly in the bustling city of Cebu, where residents and visitors can now enjoy the brand’s offerings in a prime, accessible location.  

###### Redefining Smart Security
The MGS Elite Pro Smart Lock is designed to provide homeowners with unparalleled security and ease of access. Its advanced 7-way unlocking system ensures multiple entry options tailored to users’ needs. Key features include:
- 3D Facial Recognition for seamless, hands-free access
- App Management for remote monitoring and control
- Fingerprint Unlock for quick and secure entry
- RFID Card Unlock for added convenience
- 6-12 Digit Password Unlock for customizable security
- Mechanical Key and Emergency Unlock as backup solutions
- Video Recording & Voice Intercom for enhanced surveillance
- Built-in Doorbell for an all-in-one home security experience

<br>

###### Smart Investment in Security  
Originally priced at ₱60,000, the MGS Elite Pro Smart Lock is now available for only ₱29,999, with an installment option of ₱2,499 per month at 0% interest. Customers also benefit from a 2-year warranty, lifetime technical support, and a product trusted by celebrities and security experts alike.   

###### A Milestone for MGS Philippines Cebu
The grand opening of MGS Philippines Cebu was attended by company executives, members of the media, and special guests, marking a significant step in making state-of-the-art security technology more accessible to Filipino households.
For those looking to experience this innovation firsthand, MGS Philippines Cebu’s kiosk is now open at SM City Cebu, 1st Floor (in front of BDO).
