CEBU CITY, Philippines — A new destination for baking enthusiasts, home-based businesses, and aspiring food entrepreneurs has officially opened in Cebu City, as Cebu Baking and Packaging Depot welcomed customers on November 16, 2026, at 234 Manalili Street.

The store provides a range of baking ingredients, essential supplies, tools, and packaging materials designed to support home bakers, small business owners, and established food entrepreneurs.

### **Making baking essentials more accessible**

With the growing interest in home baking and food-based businesses, Cebu Baking and Packaging Depot brings together essential baking and packaging supplies in one convenient location.

Whether customers are preparing homemade treats, fulfilling online orders, or managing a growing food business, the store offers products to support their everyday baking and business needs.

By making these essentials more accessible, the company aims to help customers spend less time sourcing materials and more time creating, baking, and growing their businesses.

### **Supporting small businesses and bigger dreams**

Beyond providing baking materials, Cebu Baking and Packaging Depot recognizes the potential of small businesses and the individuals behind them.

Many successful food businesses begin in home kitchens, driven by passion, creativity, and the determination to turn simple recipes into sustainable sources of income.

Through its products and services, the store hopes to become a reliable partner for home bakers, microentrepreneurs, and established food businesses.

“We believe that every growing business deserves access to the right materials and opportunities. Whether someone is baking for the first time or fulfilling orders for a growing customer base, we want Cebu Baking and Packaging Depot to be part of that journey,” the CBPD Management said.

### **Part of the HMC Group**

Cebu Baking and Packaging Depot is part of the HMC Group, alongside sister companies HMC, HWJ, Shopwell, and other businesses under the group.

Its opening reflects the group’s continuing commitment to business development, entrepreneurship, and creating opportunities that support local communities.

Through this new venture, the group aims to strengthen its presence in Cebu’s baking and food business sector by providing practical solutions for aspiring and established entrepreneurs.

### **A new destination for Cebu’s baking community**

Now welcoming customers, Cebu Baking and Packaging Depot invites home bakers, food business owners, and baking enthusiasts to explore its selection of baking ingredients, tools, and packaging essentials.

Store Details:

\* Store: Cebu Baking and Packaging Depot

\* Address: 234 Manalili Street, Cebu City

\* Official Opening: November 16, 2026

\* Store Updates: Cebu Baking and Packaging Depot on Facebook

With its doors now open, the store looks forward to supporting Cebu’s growing baking community and helping transform creative ideas into business opportunities.
