CEBU CITY — Two motorcycles collided at the intersection of M.J. Cuenco Avenue and T. Padilla Street at around 7:00 p.m. on July 15, 2026, prompting a response from traffic authorities.

  

Based on initial information, one of the motorcycles was making a left turn while the other was traveling straight through the intersection when the collision occurred.

  

Personnel from the Cebu City Transportation Office (CCTO) immediately responded to the scene to manage traffic, assist those involved, and ensure the safe flow of vehicles in the area.

  

As of this writing, authorities have not yet released information on the condition of the riders. An investigation is ongoing to determine the exact circumstances surrounding the crash.

  

Motorists were advised to exercise caution and expect temporary traffic congestion while the scene was being cleared.

  

Source: Cebu City Transportation Office (CCTO).
