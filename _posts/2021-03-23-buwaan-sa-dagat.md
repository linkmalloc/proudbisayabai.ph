#### BUWAAN SA DAGAT  
  
TAN-AWA mga bai! Tuslob buwa kilid sa DAGAT?  Ari na mo bai mangaon ta samtang nag lingaw ug tan aw sa bawod ug ka nindot sa dagat! 😍  
  
🕙 Operating hours: 1PM - 9PM   
  
🌎 Exact Location: Talisay City, Pooc Sitio Maharlika  
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu City, you can ride any jeepney or bus Bound to Talisay City and tell the driver to drop you at Tabunok Talisay and ride a tricycle bound to pooc and tell the driver to drop you at the "kapilya" near to Pooc Sitio maharlika, From Sitio maharlika, you can ask people about buwaan sa dagat for directions.  
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.  
  
📌 MENU:   
- Lumpia shanghai   
- sisig   
- sardines   
- halang - halang   
- lumpia taugi   
- lumpia dynamite
