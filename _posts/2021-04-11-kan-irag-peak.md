#### Kan-Irag Peak  
  
Experience the stunning mountain view at Kan-irag Peak in Barangay Sirao, Cebu City! 🏕⛰  

If you want to be away from the busy city, be in Kan-irag! You can chill and have a view of the greenery mountain, perfect for relaxation.

###### Tips and Guidelines
1. RESPECT other campers, avoid unnecessary noises especially in the middle of the night, people go there to relax.
2. BRING your trash back, don’t just throw it anywhere else. 
3. No VANDALIZING
4. BE READY and prepare necessary camping gears to protect your from rain or intense heat.
5. RESPECT Nature

****
🌎 Exact Location: Kan-Irag Peak, Langob-Sirao, Cebu City.  
  
📌 Entrance Fee: P30.00  
(Very Safe with free comfort room)
