The Sinulog Festival thrives through the support of local brands that celebrate Filipino culture and community. Let’s dive into what makes each sponsor a proud part of the Sinulog 2025 celebration:  


<br>
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/sinulog-2025/jobee1.jpg)

###### Jollibee  
As you celebrate the fiesta of Sto. Niño, bring your families together in the most iconic Filipino fast food chain.

Known for spreading joy to Filipinos worldwide, Jollibee continues its legacy as a Sinulog supporter. With its beloved menu, including Chickenjoy and Jolly Spaghetti, Jollibee symbolizes family gatherings and Filipino pride, making it a perfect partner for this cultural celebration.

<br>
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/sinulog-2025/donmac1.jpg)

###### KUKU by Don Macchiatos
Cool down with a frozen treat that’s as fun and unique as the celebration itself as you celebrate the vibrant Sinulog festival.

Bringing an affordable twist to frozen yogurt, KUKU by Don Macchiatos has made high-quality treats accessible to everyone. With its focus on personalization and quality, it’s the go-to dessert for festival-goers who want something refreshing amidst the festivities.


###### Don Macchiatos
Celebrate the fiesta of Sto. Niño with a cup of premium coffee that fuels your energy and warms your heart for the week’s festivities.

As a prominent coffee brand, Don Macchiatos takes pride in offering premium brewed coffee and signature espresso drinks. Cebu’s first 39er’s coffee, they focus purely on delivering authentic coffee experiences. Their Sinulog sponsorship underscores their dedication to quality and local community support.

###### Don Lemon Philippines
Quench your thirst and stay refreshed during Sinulog with a lemonade that energizes your festive spirit.

Refreshing, zesty, and invigorating—Don Lemon Philippines offers a unique take on lemonade. Their flavorful beverages provide a delightful way to cool off and stay hydrated during the Sinulog festivities.

<br>
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/sinulog-2025/lucas1.jpg)

###### Cusina Lucas
Savor the taste of tradition and share a meal with your loved ones, making Sinulog even more meaningful with Cusina Lucas.

A gem in Cebu’s food scene, Cusina Lucas serves hearty Filipino dishes that warm the soul. Known for their modern take on traditional meals, they connect generations of food lovers and add flavor to the Sinulog celebration.

<br>
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/sinulog-2025/athome1.jpg)


###### At Home Massage and Spa Cebu
Amidst the colorful crowd of Sinulog, find time to relax and rejuvenate with a luxurious home spa experience.

Relaxation and rejuvenation come to life with At Home Massage and Spa Cebu. Their top-notch home service allows you to recharge after a long day of festivities. Their sponsorship ensures that even amidst the energy of Sinulog, self-care is just a call away.

<br>
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/sinulog-2025/8flix1.jpg)


###### 8flix&Chill Cebu City
Celebrate Sinulog with the comfort of local flavors and the joy of sharing a meal with your favorite people.

Renowned for the best Tuslob Buwa in Cebu, 8flix&Chill Cebu City offers an authentic local dining experience. Festival-goers can indulge in this iconic Cebuano dish while enjoying a cozy environment, making it a must-visit spot during Sinulog.

<br>
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/sinulog-2025/jeju1.jpg)

###### Jeju Samgyupsal
Make your Sinulog celebration memorable with an unlimited feast that brings family and friends together.

A haven for Korean BBQ enthusiasts, Jeju Samgyupsal serves unlimited samgyupsal with premium ingredients and authentic marinades. It’s the perfect spot for a communal dining experience that matches the celebratory spirit of Sinulog.

<br>
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/sinulog-2025/kulafu.jpg)

###### Vino Kulafu
Raise your glasses and toast to the spirit of togetherness as we celebrate the vibrant Sinulog festival!  

A beloved classic in Filipino culture, Vino Kulafu has been a staple at family gatherings and celebrations for generations. This Sinulog, let Vino Kulafu add warmth to your festivities, celebrating camaraderie, gratitude, and the vibrant traditions that bring us together.
