**Feel like you’re in Japan—right here in Cebu!** 

Step into a slice of Japan at Sachiko’s Little Kyoto, an authentic Japanese restaurant and tourist attraction nestled in the scenic hills of Babag Uno, Cebu City.

Enjoy outdoor Japanese dining, stunning Instagrammable spots, and a peaceful Kyoto-inspired vibe.

* &#x20;Location: Sitio Lamacan, Babag Uno, Cebu City
* &#x20;Operating Hours: 3PM – 9PM (Tuesday to Sunday)

From Cebu City South Bus Terminal, ride a bus heading to Babag Uno Sitio Lamacan.

Once at Lamacan, take a tricycle or habal-habal to Sachiko’s Little Kyoto. Just 3km from Temple of Leah about a 10–15 minute ride straight ahead. You’ll spot us on the left!

Easily navigable on Waze perfect for cars or motorbikes. Just type in: Sachiko’s Little Kyoto.

Entrance Fee:

Day Use:

* ₱120 per adult
* ₱30 per child
* Children below 4ft – FREE

Come for the food, stay for the view. Experience Japan without leaving Cebu—only at Sachiko’s Little Kyoto!&#x20;
