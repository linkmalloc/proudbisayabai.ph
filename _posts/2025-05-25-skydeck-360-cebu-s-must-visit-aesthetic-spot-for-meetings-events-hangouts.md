If you’re on the hunt for the perfect place to meet, unwind, and celebrate with friends or colleagues in Cebu City, look no further than Skydeck 360 — an elevated rooftop haven offering panoramic views, irresistible food, and a vibe that’s both chill and classy. &#x20;

<br />

<img width="800" src="https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/510/4754621748209785-2.jpg" />

#### Why Skydeck 360 Stands Out

Perched on the roof deck of Grand Residences Tower A, right beside 88th Avenue on Roxas Street, Skydeck 360 isn’t just a restaurant — it’s an experience. Whether you’re hosting a corporate meeting, planning a romantic dinner, or catching up with your aesthetic circle over cocktails, this venue ticks all the boxes. &#x20;

<br />

<img width="800" src="https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/510/4754621748209785-2.jpg" />

#### Ambiance That Inspires

From sunrise to starlight, Skydeck 360 delivers a 360-degree view of Cebu’s skyline, making it the ideal backdrop for every occasion. By day, enjoy the golden glow of the morning sun while sipping fresh brew; by night, bask in the city lights with acoustic music gently playing in the background. &#x20;

<br />

<img width="800" src="https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/510/a782311748209853-6.jpg" />

#### Culinary Delights

Skydeck 360 doesn’t just offer views — it satisfies every craving with a curated menu that blends local flavors and international flair. From all-day favorites to dinner classics, there’s something for every palate. &#x20;

Don’t miss out on their signature Unli Breakfast Buffet! &#x20;
Price: ₱549.&#x20;
Time: 6:00 AM – 10:00 AM daily.  &#x20;

Perfect for early risers, business meetings, or simply starting your day on a high note.

Operating Hours:
&#x9;•	Sunday to Thursday: 6:00 AM – 11:00 PM
&#x9;•	Friday to Saturday: 6:00 AM – 1:00 AM (featuring live acoustic performances).&#x20;

Whether you’re closing deals, celebrating milestones, or just catching up with your crew, Skydeck 360 offers the atmosphere and service to make it memorable.

Location:

Roof Deck, Grand Residences Tower A
Roxas Street, Cebu City (beside 88th Avenue)
