TAN-AWA: BENLIW  PANORAMA  IN UBAY, BOHOL 🏕⛰🍃☀️When looking to unwind, breathe some fresh air and become one with nature, the Benliw Panorma is the place to be.   
  
The Breathtaking landscape of Benliw Panorama is located at Barangay Benliw, Municipality of Ubay Bohol.   
  
Benliw is one of the 44 barangays of the municipality of Ubay, in the province of Bohol, Philippines. The barangay's total land area is 589 hectares.  
  
*“Soaking up Ubay’s strikingly surreal beauty”*

#### How to get there?  
  
##### 🗺 Via Commute:

****
###### Cebu City to Ubay  by boat: 
###### M/V J&amp;N Carrier  
10am Monday-Saturday, 12am Sunday

###### M/V J&amp;N Ferry
10pm Sunday-Friday, no Saturday trip

###### Ubay Port Going to Barangay Benliw
- Ride Motor-Hired /Habal-Habal going to Benliw  (sabot lang mo sa Driver)  

****
###### Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
