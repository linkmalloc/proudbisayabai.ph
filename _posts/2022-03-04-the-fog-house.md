###### The Fog House: Instagram-worthy Panoramic View at the Top of Cebu  

If you've been thinking about rewarding yourself by taking a break; driving away to a
destination; escaping for a while from the stressful demands of life, and choosing either to spend
quality time with your loved ones where you can also loosen up a bit or have your own personal
space, heading to somewhere that can give you a sense of peace and solitude; with less air
pollutants and away from loud car horns of the city; has untouched wonders of nature with yet
every corner being Instagram-worthy that doesn’t require you to travel outside Cebu, but still not
making it happen for you can’t seem to find the perfect ‘dream place’ you have in mind. But
since these are the exact descriptions of the villa, The Fog House will make your vision into a
reality!  

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/the_fog_house/5.jpg)

Though The Fog House is isolated from the rest of Cebu, just having a look out the window
while sipping your warm cup of coffee while the cold foggy breeze touches your skin will make
you appreciate what life has to offer and make you feel as if you own the whole world for a
moment. This is an ideal stress-relieving environment that can help you gain a greater
appreciation for connections.  

<br>
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/the_fog_house/7.jpg)

****
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/the_fog_house/12.jpg)


Whether you want to get away from the hustle and bustle of the city or have your own one-of-akind exclusive house party experience, The Fog House has you covered.  

- Infinity pool with pressure jets
- Karaoke with great sound system
- Cable TV
- H/C shower
- 2 huge living rooms
- 2 kitchens each with electric stoves and refrigerators
- 3 Bedrooms
- H/C water dispenser
- Spa bed with in-house massage therapist  

<br>
Aside from its spacious setting, which can accommodate up to 30 guests, The Fog House has a
cleaning protocol in place, which is extremely for any event setting looking to book exclusive
groups gatherings, whether for business or any personal events. This isn't just having policies
that's important; it's also about ensuring your safety as a guest, since safety and convenience
while seizing the day are always the top priorities at The Fog House.

###### Rates
- P25k good for 20 pax (Monday-Thursday)
- P30k good for 20 pax (Friday-Sunday)

<br>

Check out: 12NN   

****
###### The Fog House Amenities:
- Drinking water is provided
- An infinity pool to swim
- Towel for each person is provided
- Karaoke is free to use
- 3-in-1 bath essentials is provided (shampoo, conditioner, and body wash)
- Pets are allowed
- No air condition (as the setting is already very cold, especially at night)
- Parking space can fit to 10 cars – no parking fee
- Sound systems with bluetooth connection is provided
- There are 3 rooms in total. The 2 rooms can accommodate up to 6 guests each while
the other room can fit up to 8 guests.
- Guests are free to use the kitchen and its kitchen equipments (grill, dining utensils,
microwave, fridge, small oven toaster, XL size rice cooker)
- Wi-fi powered by 4G/LTE

****
###### The Fog House Rules + Information
- No corkage fee.
- There are flight of stairs and ramps before reaching the villa.
-Does not offer room accommodations.
- Villa is exclusive to only one (1) group of guests.
- Only 1st and 2nd floor is allowed for guests, 3rd floor is exclusive for the owner.
- Dining utensils are available, but may bring disposables if you don’t want to clean
the dishes. Uncleaned utensils will be charged; other damages and stains will be
charged accordingly.  

****
###### How to get there?

Exact Location: Taptap, Busay, Cebu City  

Via Commute: Via Commute: Ride a v-hire going to Busay, Cebu from the Ayala
Center Cebu Terminal. Ask the driver to drop-off at The Fog House Taptap, Busay.
Estimated fare is P130  
Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a
bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze: [https://g.co/kgs/aiuvL8](https://g.co/kgs/aiuvL8)
