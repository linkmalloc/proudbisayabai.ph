#### Acacia Mercado  
  
BAG-ONG lugar to dine and chill for your weekend mga Bai! Imong plano nga laag plus simply satisfying your cravings all in one with this relaxing ambiance embracing nature in Acia Mercado 💯 🍩🧋  
  
🕑 OPEN DAILY 11AM - 10PM  
  
🌍 Exact Location: Poblacion Alcantara Cebu   
  
📌 How to get there?  
  
🗺 Via Commute: (Landmark: Alcantara public market beside fish section) Ride a Bus from South Bus Terminal (Barili via Bato) tell the driver to drop you off at Alcantara Public Market (Town before mag Moalboal)  
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
