###### Highland Mango Ecofarm

LOOK: Next Laag Alert! Witness and experience panoramic view of the whole City and the great mountain view at Highland Mango Ecofarm in Consolacion Cebu!  

It boasts a beautiful garden, an onsite cafe, City view and an outdoor pool for your comfort and convenience.  

One thing that made you excited here is the igloo-con-hobbit type house accommodation near the infinity pool and the two cabin rooms for big groups located on the ground floor of North Peak Cafe.  

🌏 Exact Location: Purok Yellowbell, Brgy. Panoypoy, Consolacion, North Cebu  

📍 Google Map Link: [https://goo.gl/maps/HWRyH4WwX7t5kPWL9](https://goo.gl/maps/HWRyH4WwX7t5kPWL9)

###### How to get there? 

🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu City you can  take jeepney routes 62B/B going Pit-Os. Flying V Gasoline Station is their last stop for this route. From there, you can haggle a Habal-habal from Php100-150 per person. Parking space is available  

🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze or Google Map. Whether you are riding a motorcycle or your own vehicle, it is best to navigate via Waze / G Map. 
The residents of the area became our human Waze to locate the place. As they always said, before you go to a heavenly place, you've to pass a narrow rough road. It was a challenge in a way, but at the same time, fun going to sites that you haven't been there before.

You can also contact Kuya Junjun at [0926-935-1184](tel:0926-935-1184) who can guide and drive you going to Highland Mango Ecofarm.
