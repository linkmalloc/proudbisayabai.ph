If you’re searching for a café that offers more than just aesthetic interiors, MR. BABOO Cafe is steadily becoming a favorite among Cebuanos looking for affordable drinks, comforting meals, and a relaxing tambayan atmosphere.



Known for its milk tea starting at only ₱59, the café has caught the attention of students, barkadas, families, and even travelers passing through the area. But beyond the budget-friendly drinks, customers are also coming back for the café’s growing selection of satisfying comfort food. From spicy noodles and sandwiches to frappes and hearty rice meals, MR. BABOO offers a menu designed for both quick cravings and full meals.



One of the café’s biggest charms is its warm and welcoming ambiance. With cozy interiors and a laid-back vibe, it has become a go-to hangout spot for casual meetups, study sessions, coffee dates, and late afternoon food trips. The relaxing atmosphere gives customers a break from the usual busy café experience, making every visit feel more personal and comfortable.



MR. BABOO Cafe continues to expand its reach across Cebu with branches located in Bonbon Diot, Poblacion Pinamungajan and Luray 1, Poblacion, Toledo City, Cebu. Open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, the café offers dine-in, takeout, and delivery options for customers looking to enjoy their favorites anytime.



For those planning their next café stop, MR. BABOO Cafe offers a simple but memorable experience — good food, affordable drinks, and a cozy space where people can slow down, relax, and enjoy every bite and sip.
