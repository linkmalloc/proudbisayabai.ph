If you are looking for a peaceful retreat away from the city heat and daily stress, Forest Camp Resort is one of the must visit destinations near Dumaguete City.



Located in the mountain town of Valencia, the resort is only around 20 minutes or approximately 11.5 kilometers from Dumaguete City, making it a convenient nature getaway for both locals and tourists.



Known for its cold spring water pools, relaxing atmosphere, and lush forest surroundings, the resort offers a refreshing break from the tropical heat. Guests can enjoy swimming in crystal clear spring pools, exploring hanging bridges, and relaxing under towering trees surrounded by nature.



One of the highlights of Forest Camp Resort is its naturally flowing mountain spring water that keeps the pools cool even during hot summer days. The peaceful environment and scenic mountain setting also make it ideal for family outings, barkada trips, team buildings, and weekend escapes.



Rates and Entrance Fees



Based on recent visitor updates and resort posts, entrance fees are approximately:



• Adults — around ₱200

• Children (2 to 8 years old) — around ₱100 to ₱170

• Tent pitching — around ₱300

• Cottage and overnight accommodation rates vary depending on room type and capacity.



Rates may change during peak seasons and holidays, so visitors are encouraged to check the resort’s official page before traveling.



How to Get There



From , travelers can reach  through several transportation options:



• Ride a jeepney bound for Valencia Public Market, then take a habal habal or tricycle going to Forest Camp

• Hire a tricycle directly from Dumaguete City for a more convenient trip

• Rent a motorcycle or private vehicle and follow the Valencia road toward Apolong, Valencia



The mountain resort continues to attract visitors looking for a cool and relaxing nature escape in Negros Oriental, especially during warm weather and holiday seasons.
