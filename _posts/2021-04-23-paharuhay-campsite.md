#### Paharuhay Campsite  
  
Need a new place to chill with your barkadas and your family? Try this new campsite in Caduawan Tabogon Cebu, Just a 10-15 min trek to the camp and you will reach this beautiful 360 degree view. 🏕😯🌄  


You can enjoy the camlness of nature in the morning, challenge yourself with a little bit of hiking and gaze through the stars at night. These are just a few of many things that can do at Paharuhay Campsite.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/pbb1/image/upload/v1619197554/paharuhay_campsite/dkcfc0xi6vyzzghxfvoq.jpg)
<small>Source: Kodakeros De Cebu</small>  

****

🕑 Check in time: 2PM  
🕛 Check out: 12NN (the next day)  

****
##### WHAT TO ENJOY   
  
- Free breakfast   
- 360 degrees view of the mountains and the sea, perfect view for sunrise and sunset  
- Fully Equipped kitchen with free butane stove, rice cooker, refrigerator, pots and pans, plates and bowls, utensils, glasses, water dispenser and 1 gallon of water.  
- Free use of pool and tree house  
- Bbq grill is provided   
- No corkage fee for food and drinks (you can bring your own food, groceries and beverages)  
- Dining tables with chairs

##### HOW TO GET THERE

Exact Location: Upper Caduawan, Tabogon, Cebu  
   
  
🗺 Via Commute: Take a bus ride going to BOGO or San Remegio and just let them drop you off at tabogon caduawan inbetween km83-km84. The exact location is inbetween km83 to km84.. right before reaching km84 in caduawan tabogon.   
  
Cellphone Signal is accessible in the area and the campsite it self.  
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
