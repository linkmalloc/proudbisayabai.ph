Six in 10 Filipinos are dissatisfied with President Ferdinand Marcos Jr.'s performance and distrust him, placing him at the bottom of the satisfaction and trust rankings among five top national officials in a November nationwide survey by Tangere.

  

The Nov. 16 to 18 survey found that 60 percent of respondents were dissatisfied with Marcos, while only 25 percent expressed satisfaction, resulting in a net satisfaction rating of minus 35.

  

An equal 60 percent said they distrusted the President, compared with 27 percent who expressed trust, giving him a net trust rating of minus 33.

  

Marcos posted the lowest ratings among the five officials covered by the survey, which also measured public sentiment toward Vice President Sara Duterte, Chief Justice Rodil Vaquilar Zalameda, Senate President Sherwin Gatchalian and House Speaker Faustino De Guzman Dy III.

  

Duterte remained the highest-rated official, although her satisfaction and trust ratings also fell below the majority threshold.

  

The Vice President received 48 percent satisfaction and 46 percent trust, while 34 percent expressed dissatisfaction and 35 percent distrust. Her public awareness stood at 100 percent.

  

Tangere said Duterte's latest figures marked a departure from previous tracking surveys, when her satisfaction and trust ratings had consistently remained above 50 percent.

  

Chief Justice Zalameda ranked second behind Duterte, with 39 percent satisfaction and 39 percent trust. His public awareness rating was 82 percent.

  

Gatchalian posted 33 percent satisfaction and 32 percent trust, while Dy received 27 percent satisfaction and 29 percent trust.

  

Among Cabinet members, Interior and Local Government Secretary Jonvic Remulla recorded the lowest satisfaction rating at 36 percent, with 39 percent expressing dissatisfaction.

  

Remulla also received 37 percent trust against 39 percent distrust, making him the only Cabinet official in the survey with a negative net trust rating of minus 2.

  

Social Welfare Secretary Rex Gatchalian led Cabinet satisfaction ratings with 51 percent, followed by Education Secretary Juan Edgardo “Sonny” Angara at 50 percent.

  

Agrarian Reform Secretary Conrado Estrella III and Science and Technology Secretary Renato Solidum Jr. each registered 49 percent satisfaction, while Defense Secretary Gilberto “Gibo” Teodoro Jr., Transportation Secretary Vince Dizon and Labor Secretary Francis Tolentino each received 47 percent.

  

In trust ratings, National Anti-Poverty Commission head Manny Pacquiao led Cabinet officials with 51 percent trust and 17 percent distrust, resulting in a net trust rating of 34.

  

Rex Gatchalian followed with 50 percent trust and 24 percent distrust, while Angara recorded 49 percent trust and 24 percent distrust.

  

Teodoro posted 46 percent trust and 25 percent distrust, while Dizon and Tolentino each received 45 percent trust.

  

The non-commissioned survey covered 1,500 respondents nationwide from Nov. 16 to 18 through a mobile-based respondent application.

  

It used stratified random sampling with quota-based sampling, with participants distributed across the National Capital Region, Northern Luzon, Southern Luzon, the Visayas and Mindanao.

  

Tangere reported a margin of error of plus or minus 2.40 percentage points at a 95 percent confidence level.
