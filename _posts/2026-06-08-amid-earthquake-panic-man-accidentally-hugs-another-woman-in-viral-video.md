MINDANAO, Philippines — While the powerful magnitude 7.8 earthquake that struck parts of Mindanao on June 8, 2026, left communities shaken and caused significant damage in several areas, one unexpected moment captured during the chaos has brought a touch of humor to social media.



A video shared by Cristine Barbie Mascariñas shows people scrambling to safety as the ground shook during the strong tremor. Amid the panic, a man was seen accidentally hugging another woman while trying to react to the unfolding situation, creating a lighthearted scene that quickly caught the attention of online viewers.



The uploader humorously captioned the video: “Panic already but hugged others. It’s okay because everyone panicked, but I’m already down.”



The clip quickly gained reactions from netizens, many of whom found humor in the unintended embrace while acknowledging the fear and confusion people experience during emergencies. Others noted that moments like these are natural during disasters, when instinct often takes over and people seek safety amid uncertainty.



The earthquake, one of the strongest to hit Mindanao in recent years, triggered emergency response operations across several provinces and cities. Authorities continue to assess damage, monitor aftershocks, and assist affected communities.



While the viral video provided a brief moment of laughter, it also serves as a reminder of the importance of remaining calm and following safety protocols during earthquakes and other emergencies.



As recovery efforts continue in affected areas, many online users expressed gratitude that no serious injuries were reported in the incident captured on camera, turning what could have been a frightening memory into a story that brought smiles amid a challenging day.
