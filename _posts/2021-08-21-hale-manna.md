###### Hale Manna Beach Resort and Coastal Garden  

Hale Manna Beach Resort and Coastal Garden - the House of Good energy. A distant resort from the shore of Bas Daku Moalboal that gives the peace and tranquility you need. It is a one-of-a-kind vacation resort where the concepts and best practices of sustainable coastal tourism give visitors with a unique and unforgettable experience of what it is like to live an ecologically friendly lifestyle in a tropical setting.

On the one hand, the entire atmosphere of Hale Manna is one of exquisite peace amidst an expanse of open sea, and on the other, the comfort of green grass and the lush surroundings of a tropical coastal garden paradise. Such an atmosphere will be maintained through environmentally responsible water, solar electricity, drainage, and sanitation systems. The structures are located inland, while the beachfront portion of the property is mostly open space.

###### HOW TO GET THERE?  

🕑 Check-in time: 2:00 PM  
🕑 Check-out time: 12:00 NOON   
  
🌏 Exact location: Brgy. Bas Daku, Saavedra, Moalboal, Cebu 🇵🇭  
  
🗺 Via Commute: From the South Bus Terminal, take a bus going to Moalboal. Then ask to be dropped off at Hale Manna Resort, near Tunga Elementary School before Gaisano Grand Mall Moalboal.  
  
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
