Discover the hidden greenery and take in the extraordinary succulents. This is it! The most relaxing thing you can do if you need a break from everything that's stressful in your life. Learn more about the vegetation while savoring a picnic on the grounds and strolling the adjacent trails. Buwakan ni Alejandra gives the impression that you are on another side of the world.  

The garden is perfectly situated an hour from the center of Cebu City on the Transcentral Highway. It has about 200 different species of flora and fauna, including many different flowering plants. Buwakan ni Alejandra's new designs will transport you to the Garden of Eden or the alluring settings of your favorite movies. It will let you experience the feeling of being in a celestial realm.

This tourist destination bears Eleuterio Gentap's mother-in-law’s name. Gentap works as the landscaper for the flower garden in addition to being a councilman in the barangay. The most widely used and spoken language in the Visayas is Cebuano, where the word "buwak" translates to "flowers." As a result, "buwakan" now refers to a beautiful landscape. It is obvious from the name that it is a paradise for flower petals. The majority of plantitos and plantitas will consider this to be heaven because the plants are all in good health.

It has gorgeous scenery with lots of vibrant blooms. Alejandra includes a Bali-inspired installation, colorful stairs, and a steel bridge with a beautiful mountain in the distance. This Instagram-worthy locale is the ideal backdrop for a pre-debut or engagement photo shoot. With bright sunshine and clear skies, the vista is still lovely even in the colder or rainier seasons with the fog engulfing the area!   

Spend a good hour there just snapping shots of the flowers and the mountain skyline.  

Operating Hours:  
7AM to 6PM, Mondays to Sundays if there’s a storm, they will close the premises to protect the flower garden.  

Exact location: Brgy. Gaas, Sitio Bunga Balamban, Cebu  

###### How to get there?

Via Commute: Take a V-hire from the Ayala Center Terminal going to Balamban via Transcentral highway route. Fare is P120 per person (one-way). Inform the driver to drop you off at Buwakan ni Alejandra.

Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a van or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze: Buwakan ni Alejandra 0921 812 9664
