Swimming alongside whale sharks in Oslob, Cebu, is an unforgettable experience for nature and wildlife enthusiasts. Known as the largest fish species in the world, these gentle giants offer visitors a chance to witness marine life up close in their natural habitat.



Visiting Hours



The whale shark interaction site in Tan-awan, Oslob is open daily from 6:00 AM to 12:00 NN, providing ample time for early risers and day-trippers to enjoy this activity.



Admission Rates (2025)

&#x9;•	Filipino tourists: ₱500 per person

&#x9;•	Foreign tourists: ₱1,000 per person

&#x9;•	GoPro Rental: ₱550



Note: Rates are for the official whale shark watching activity only. Other services such as guided tours or transport packages are separate.



How to Get There



By Commute:

From the South Bus Terminal in Cebu City, take a Sunrays or Ceres bus heading to Oslob and request to be dropped off at Tan-awan. The fare is approximately ₱275 one-way (air-conditioned).



By Private Vehicle:

Travelers using private vehicles or motorbikes can navigate easily using Waze. Parking is available near the site.



Overnight Accommodation



For those wishing to stay overnight, several resorts and guesthouses are available nearby. Rates typically range from ₱800 to ₱3,000 per night, depending on the type of accommodation and amenities.



Important Reminders

&#x9;•	Online booking is required prior to arrival to manage visitor capacity.

&#x9;•	Walk-ins are allowed, and LGU staff will assist in completing online registration forms on-site.

&#x9;•	Visitors are advised to follow safety guidelines during interactions to ensure the well-being of both the whale sharks and themselves.



Why Visit Oslob Whale Sharks?



Oslob Whale Shark Watching provides a rare opportunity to interact with the ocean’s largest fish in a responsible and regulated environment. It is ideal for families, couples, and solo travelers seeking an educational and immersive wildlife experience in Cebu.
