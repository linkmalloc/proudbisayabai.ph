AJ Thai Street Food is a newly opened restaurant in Cebu City that offers authentic Thai cuisine in a casual and affordable setting. With two branches located in Ayala Center Cebu and Parkmall, AJ Thai Street Food has become a go-to spot for locals and tourists alike who are craving a taste of Thailand.  

Come and experience the authentic flavors of Thai street food without having to leave Cebu City. AJ Thai Street Food menu features a wide variety of popular Thai dishes, all made with fresh, high-quality ingredients namely Pad Woosen, Kra Paw Mix, Som Tam, Phla Mamuang, Yum Egg, Kao Pad Goong, Khong Khali, Moo Namprik, and many more!   

One of the things that makes AJ Thai Street Food so popular is its affordable prices. Most of the dishes on the menu are under ₱300, making it a great option for budget-minded diners. AJ Thai Street Food's restaurants have a casual and relaxing atmosphere, making it a great place to enjoy a meal with friends or family.  

📍 Unit A4 - 201 & 202, Alfresco 4, Second Floor, Parkmall, Ouano Avenue Mandaue City  

Whether you're a fan of Thai food or not, we have something for everyone and you will definitely like it! And with our convenient location in Parkmall, you can easily enjoy a delicious Thai meal before or after shopping.  
 
Opens 10am-9pm daily.  

Visit us today and try some of the best Thai food in Cebu City!
