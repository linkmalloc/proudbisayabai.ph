##### 🕑 Operating hours:  
11AM - 9PM

##### 🌏 Exact Location:  
Mactan, Lapu-Lapu City (near Mactan Elementary School)  

##### 🚶🚘 How to get there?

##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
The quickest way to get from Cebu City to Lapu-Lapu City is to take a bus. Then tell the driver to drop you off at Mactan Elem. School. Or ride a v-hire from SM City Cebu going to Mactan, Lapu-Lapu City.  

🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorcycle or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
