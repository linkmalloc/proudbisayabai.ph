Every single component in this sugbahan house has its own meaning!  

The Coco Sugba Logo and Wall of Art, for example, are drawn by student artists who have laid out the patriotic desire of the Owner.

The place has been used for individual blogging and private celebrations such as christening, birthdays, and anniversaries and also for rustic/fiesta themed debut.
