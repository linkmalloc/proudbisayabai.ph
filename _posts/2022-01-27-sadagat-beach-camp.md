###### Sadagat Beach Camp 

A campsite with lapping waves or tidal features, and a sunset scenery. Di ka magmahay, Bai!  Park your car, pitch your tent, hang your hammocks and camp in the countryside. Have a dazzling getaway by driving to Sadagat Beach Camp.

Relieve your stress and let the sun kiss your skin. Get your phone or camera and take good pictures with an Instagram-worthy scene. Walk in the sand with your loved one as if you are in a romantic movie because there are many things that you can enjoy during your stay and your camping moment. Your spirit will be soothed by the sound of the waves, and you will fall in love with the splendor of the setting sun. As you glance up at the sky, grin at the blinking stars, and bask in the glow of the moon, you'll be awestruck by the silence in the night. You can bring your chairs, tables, and other items and seize every moment at the beach. Invite your friends, have a good time, and discover about the many diverse aspects of a shoreline.

###### HOW TO GET THERE?

🌏 Exact Location: KM128 Sitio Tambo Kawit, Medellin, 6012 Medellin, 🇵🇭

🗺 Via Commute: From North Bus Terminal in which situated at SM City Cebu. Look for a Ceres Bus going to Kawit Medellin. Then tell the driver to drop you off sa merkado then unahan going to Sitio Tambo you will find Sadagat Beach Camp. 
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze or Google Map. From the Town proper - Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze/ GMap: [https://g.co/kgs/qPKmYZ](https://g.co/kgs/qPKmYZ)

###### To maintain the camp vibe, They have 3 basic Rules: 
1. No loud noise/music and no karaoke
2. Clean as you go
3. Respect privacy of other campers  

NOTE: Pets are allowed if they are always kept on leash and will not scare children which we always have at the camp. Also, please bring your own pet poop bags, no burying in sand.
