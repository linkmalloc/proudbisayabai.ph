For travelers seeking an upscale private retreat, Casa Teresita offers an exclusive luxury property in Cebu designed for families, friends, and corporate groups looking to unwind in a serene natural setting.

Blending modern comforts with a tranquil, zen-inspired atmosphere, the property features three private villas—Casa Uno, Casa Dos, and Casa Tres—surrounded by lush greenery and anchored by a spacious swimming pool. Its peaceful environment provides the perfect escape from the hustle and bustle of city life while offering ample space for relaxation and celebrations.

The published overnight rate accommodates up to 20 guests, with exclusive access to the entire compound. Casa Teresita can host up to 30 guests, with additional headcount fees of ₱1,000 per night for guests aged 13 and above and ₱500 per night for children aged 4 to 12.

Guests can enjoy a range of amenities, including an adult swimming pool, kiddie pool, indoor and outdoor kitchens, outdoor dining and lounge areas, a sun deck, landscaped gardens, an outdoor bar, water dispensers, Wi-Fi, and parking for up to 10 vehicles.

Check-in starts at 3:00 p.m., while check-out is at 11:00 a.m. Early check-in and late check-out requests are subject to availability and applicable fees. To preserve its peaceful ambiance, the property observes quiet hours from 9:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m.

Offering privacy, comfort, and resort-style amenities, Casa Teresita provides an ideal setting for luxury staycations, family reunions, milestone celebrations, and memorable group escapes in Cebu.
