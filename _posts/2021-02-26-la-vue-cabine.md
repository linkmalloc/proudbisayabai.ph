Want to escape from buzzling City life with friends? Rekindle your soul by taking in good air in a relaxing place! 😯🍃   

****
****
What's more amazing in this cabin is that they are eco friendly. They use wooden spoon and forks, coconut shell bowls and bamboo straws.   
  
****
****
#### How to get there?  
<hr style="border-color: #c5c5c552 !important;">
##### 🌍 Location:  
Sitio Babag 2, Brgy. Pangamihan, Toledo City  
	
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
From Ayala Center Mall ride a V-hire Terminal to Cantipla corner Transcentral Hi-way. Drop off (Strawberry de Cantipla eco farm) P150. From there, Sakay ka habal-habal eskina Cantipla to La Vue Cabine. P60-80.   

****
##### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorcycle or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
