#### CRAB PRO
<center>TRENDING: “CRAB PRO” PALUTO ON THE SPOT IN RAMOS! 🤤🦀🦞  </center>
  
****
No matter where in Cebu, you won’t have any trouble finding seafood options because tasty seafoods aren’t far away!   
  
CRAB PRO offers Live Mud Crabs, Prawns Lobsters, Salmon and Tuna. They are a Dampa Style Express Paluto “ PALUTO ON THE SPOT”. So you buy seafoods and they cook it your way! Fresh stocks arrival almost everyday from Visayas and Mindanao Farms. Additional PHP 150.00 only for cooking charge!   
  
🕙 Operating hours: 9AM - 8PM daily   
  
🌎 Exact Location: F. RAMOS CEBU CITY (near Velez College)  
  
📌  How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: From Colon, you can either take a taxi or a Beep bus. Should you choose a taxi, tell the driver to drop you off at Crabpro Ramos near Ramos Brgy. Hall and Gym.   
  
 🗺 For more information on how to get there Please refer to the Map provided below.
