During the Don Macchiatos press conference at NUSTAR today, November 25, 2025, brand ambassador Daniel Padilla reflected on his contract with the brand and its fast-growing community, calling the journey nothing short of a success. He also commended Don Macchiatos for its swift response to Cebu communities affected by recent disasters, expressing full support for the brand’s ongoing relief efforts.

Daniel spoke warmly about his relationship with the founders and executives, describing their connection as “parang magkakaibigan,” a bond built on trust, camaraderie, and genuine respect. Company executives echoed this sentiment, saying Daniel’s endorsement goes beyond a typical partnership, evolving into a family-like relationship.

For the holidays, Daniel teased the brand’s new campaign, **“Your Holiday Wish, Granted,”** which aims to spread joy and grant simple wishes, especially for those impacted by recent calamities.

When asked which Don Macchiatos drink best represents him, Daniel chose the iced caramel macchiato, “para suave,” he said with a smile.

Don Macchiatos also announced its expansion plans in Dubai with its partners, marking another milestone for the brand. They further teased a lot more to look forward to in the coming year — exciting developments, bigger projects, and surprises that fans and customers should definitely watch out for.
