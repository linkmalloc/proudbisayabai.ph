CEBU CITY, Philippines — Rice prices remain stable at Carbon Public Market as of Tuesday, April 21, 2026, following the government’s implementation of a price freeze on basic commodities.

Market monitoring shows that despite recent concerns over supply and inflation, rice vendors continue to maintain steady pricing across various varieties. This development aligns with the assurance from the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) that essential goods will remain at regulated prices until April 30, 2026.

The price freeze aims to protect consumers from sudden spikes, especially in the aftermath of supply chain pressures and environmental factors affecting production. Vendors at Carbon Public Market also expressed cooperation with authorities, ensuring compliance while maintaining sufficient stock for daily buyers.

Consumers are encouraged to remain vigilant and report any irregularities in pricing, as government agencies continue to monitor markets closely to uphold fair trade and consumer protection.
