The Philippine National Police (PNP) will intensify operations against the illegal manufacture, sale, distribution and use of fireworks and firecrackers this holiday season following an incident in Misamis Occidental that injured at least 18 people.

PNP chief Gen. Jose Melencio C. Nartatez Jr. ordered police units to strengthen monitoring and enforcement after a fireworks display exploded toward spectators in Tangub City during the grand opening of the Christmas Festival of Lights 2026.

Local police have launched an investigation to determine what caused the mishap and identify those who may be held liable, Nartatez said.

“As we welcome the holiday season, we want to underscore the importance of responsible celebration through strict regulation of fireworks and pyrotechnic devices,” he said in a statement.

Nartatez directed police units at all administrative levels to tighten operations as holiday gatherings and celebrations increase toward the end of the year.

“Festive traditions must never come at the expense of public safety and must not be measured with loud firecrackers but by the absence of injuries,” he said.

The PNP's directive comes as authorities reported a separate enforcement operation in Manila, where six people were arrested over an alleged unauthorized firecracker assembly operation.

The Manila Police District dismantled the operation inside a residential compound in Tondo on Nov. 22 and seized illegal pyrotechnics estimated to be worth P500,000.

Police arrested six adults aged 24 to 41 and rescued a 17-year-old minor who was found working at the site.

National Capital Region Police Office acting regional director Brig. Gen. Christopher N. Abrahano said the operation reflects the police force's intensified campaign against illegal pyrotechnics ahead of the Christmas and New Year holidays.
