Some of the best celebrations don't need grand occasions—they simply need good food and great company.

  

This September, Caja Kitchen Cebu is turning every weekend buffet into an even more rewarding experience as it joins Bayfront Hotel Cebu's anniversary celebration. Whether it's a long-overdue family lunch, a dinner catch-up with friends, or a simple gathering with colleagues after a busy week, every shared meal comes with the exciting chance to dine for FREE.

  

From **September 1 to 30, 2026**, groups of at least four paying adults can enjoy Caja Kitchen Cebu's signature buffet and receive a mystery rolled paper for a chance to have part—or even all—of their meal on the house.

  

Lucky diners may instantly win:

*   FREE Buffet for 2 Persons
*   FREE Buffet for 3 Persons
*   FREE Buffet for 4 Persons
*   FREE Buffet for 5 Persons
*     
    

The best part? The prize is applied immediately to the same dining bill, making every visit a celebration worth remembering.

Available during Caja Kitchen Cebu's weekend buffet schedule, guests can indulge in an abundant spread of local favorites and international dishes for **₱688 per person**, with every qualified group getting one mystery rolled paper upon payment.

  

After all, the best memories are often made around the table—over shared stories, second servings, and laughter that lasts long after dessert.

  

So gather your favorite people and make your weekend buffet a little more exciting. You might just leave with a full stomach and a dining bill that's much lighter than expected.

  

### **Promo Details**

  

**Promo Period:** September 1–30, 2026

**Buffet Rate:** ₱688 per person

**Applicable Buffet Schedule:**

*   Friday Dinner Buffet
*   Saturday Dinner Buffet
*   Sunday Lunch Buffet

  

### **Promo Mechanics**

1.  The promo is open to groups of at least four (4) paying adults dining in a single transaction.
2.  Every qualified group will receive one (1) mystery rolled paper upon settlement of the bill.
3.  Unroll the mystery rolled paper and redeem one (1) instant prize:

*   FREE Buffet for 2 Persons
*   FREE Buffet for 3 Persons
*   FREE Buffet for 4 Persons
*   FREE Buffet for 5 Persons

4\. The prize will be deducted from the current dining bill only.

  

  

### **Terms and Conditions**

*   The promo is valid from **September 1–30, 2026**, during Caja Kitchen Cebu's scheduled buffet services only.
*   Applicable buffet schedules are:
*   Friday Dinner Buffet
*   Saturday Dinner Buffet
*   Sunday Lunch Buffet
*   Only groups of at least four (4) paying adults dining in a single transaction are eligible.
*   Each qualified group is entitled to one (1) raffle draw only.
*   The prize won is valid only for the current dining transaction and cannot be deferred or redeemed on another date.
*   If the prize covers more guests than are present, the unused complimentary buffet slots will be forfeited and are not transferable, convertible to cash, vouchers, or future dining credits.
*   Example: If a group of four (4) wins a FREE Buffet for 5 Persons, all four guests dine for free. The remaining one (1) complimentary buffet slot is automatically forfeited.
*   If the prize covers fewer guests than are in the group, only the corresponding number of guests will dine for free. The remaining guests will be charged the regular buffet rate of **₱688 per person**.
*   Prizes are non-transferable, non-refundable, and not convertible to cash or any other product or service.
*   This promo cannot be combined with other promotional offers, discounts, vouchers, or privileges, except those required by law.
*   Bayfront Hotel Cebu reserves the right to verify eligibility and amend the promo mechanics and terms and conditions without prior notice.
