Step into a realm of unparalleled affordable luxury and exploration as LB Pietz Pension House Hotel beckons with open arms. Inspired by the wanderlust adventures of the Pietz family, this boutique retreat promises an unforgettable escape, blending affordability with opulence in perfect harmony.  

As the esteemed Mayor Cesar E. Suan graces the occasion, marking the dawn of a new era in hospitality, anticipation fills the air. With its modern-tropical ambiance, LB Pietz Pension House Hotel offers the perfect sanctuary for travelers seeking comfort and style. Dazzling floral arrangements from Kolorful Creations adorn the ribbon-cutting ceremony, orchestrated in collaboration with Kreative Corner Productions Co., setting the stage for the opulence that awaits within.  

Located within close proximity to Cebu's newest and longest bridge, the CCLEX, the journey to this haven of relaxation is as seamless as it is picturesque. Marvel at the cool view that unfolds before you, setting the stage for an unforgettable experience.

Upon arrival, indulge in a feast for the senses as you explore the vibrant surroundings. From the sprawling SM Seaside to the enchanting Ocean Park and the charm of Il Corso, each destination invites you to immerse yourself in its unique allure.  

And let's not forget NuSTAR, a beacon of innovation and progress, where the pulse of the city beats strongly and of course, no visit to this picturesque locale would be complete without a trip to the famous 10,000 Roses and the island-hopping experience in Oro. Just a stone's throw away from LB Pietz Pension House Hotel, this enchanting attraction captivates visitors with its mesmerizing beauty, offering a serene escape amidst a sea of delicate blooms. With LB Pietz Pension House Hotel as your starting point, every excursion becomes a journey of discovery, promising moments of serenity and bliss.  

Indulge in delectable dining options, starting your day with a sumptuous breakfast spread that tantalizes the taste buds. For those seeking relaxation and recreation, the hotel offers an array of amenities, including a refreshing pool, state-of-the-art gym facilities, and a 24-hour front office ensuring round-the-clock assistance.  

Stay tuned as we unveil more glimpses into this haven of relaxation and wallet-friendly luxury, beckoning you to embark on a journey of unforgettable experiences.
