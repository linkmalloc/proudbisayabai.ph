****
###### Jelly's Haven Resort 

\#VisitBantayan: Instagrammable beach resort in Bantayan?  

If you're looking for a beautiful getaway on a stunning white sand beach with crystal clear water, Jelly's Haven Resort is the place for you.  

About 4 hours travel away from Cebu City, Jelly's Haven Resort is located at Sta. Fe, Bantayan Island, Cebu. It is a beach spot where you may  unwind while listening to the sound of rustling leaves and splashing waves. Aside from the natural beauty that the beach provides. This resort also has colorful beach decorations that are incredibly Instagrammable.

The resort offers a variety of accommodation options, ranging from the Superior Room to the Jelly's Grand Suite. This accommodation comes with a complimentary breakfast for two persons. Tents appropriate for a couple or a family are also available.

##### Room Rates

****
###### Tents:
- COUPLE TENT: P500 pesos per night, good for 2 person
- FAMILY TENT: P1,000 pesos per night, good for 4 person

****
###### Superior Rooms:
- Good for 2 person
- 2 Single Beds / 1 Queen-sized
- Complimentary breakfast for 2 person
- P5,200 pesos per night

****
###### Deluxe Room:
- Good for 2-4 person
- Complimentary breakfast for 2 person
- P6,800 pesos per night

****
###### Executive Suite:
- Good for 2-4 person
- Complimentary breakfast for 2 person
- P7,500 pesos per night

****
###### Jelly’s Grand Suite:
- Good for 2 person
- Complimentary breakfast for 2 person, basket of fruits, 1 bottle of red wine
- P12,000 pesos per night

##### How to get there?

Exact Location: Sta. Fe, Bantayan Island, Cebu (4 hours away from the City) 🇵🇭

🗺 Via commute: From North Bus Terminal Ride a bus going to Hagnaya. Travel time is 3 to 4hours. From Hagnaya take the Express Ferry Boat to Sta. Fe, Bantayan Island then the travel time is 45mins to 1hour ride to Sta. Fe, Bantayan Island. Bus fare is P170 pesos per way then P340 pesos roundtrip na. Naay discount (20% discount for students, just present School ID) Then while sa Ferry &amp; Terminal Fee P110 pesos only per way / P220 pesos roundtrip na.

🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
