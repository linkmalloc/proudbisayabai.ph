Naka ari na ba ka bai?

##### HOW TO GET THERE  

Water Terraces Falls is exactly located in Catang, Argao, Cebu. It is 72km away from Cebu City and 7km away from the main highway. From Argao town proper it takes 10 to 15 minutes to reach barangay Catang. You will hike for another 10 to 15 minutes from the parking area to reach this beautiful place.    

🌍 Exact Location: Brgy Catang Argao \| approx. 10mins to 15mins from proper Argao  

🕓 Opening hours: 8:00AM - 5:00PM  
  
🗺 Via commute: Fom Cebu City South Bus Terminal just ride a bus bound to Argao Bus stop. Then ride a motorcycle/habal-habal going Argao Public Market. Then sakay nasad kag laing motorcycle padung sa Brgy. Catang, tell the driver to drop you off at Terraces Falls.  
  
🗺 Via private vehicle : The place can be navigated through waze/google map.
