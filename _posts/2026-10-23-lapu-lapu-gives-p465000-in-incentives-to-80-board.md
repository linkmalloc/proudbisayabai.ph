The Lapu-Lapu City Government has awarded P465,000 in cash incentives to 80 residents who passed various licensure examinations, including a physician board exam topnotcher who received P70,000.

  

  

The incentives were distributed during a recognition ceremony at the City Hall Atrium on Oct. 19, 2026.

  

  

Of the 80 recipients, 79 received P5,000 each, while the topnotcher received P70,000 in recognition of his performance in the licensure examination.

  

  

Among the awardees was Fedel Dane Caracut, a Cebu Institute of Medicine graduate who placed fourth in the March 2026 Physician Licensure Examination with a rating of 89.58 percent.

  

  

Mayor Ma. Cynthia “Cindi” King-Chan congratulated the new professionals, commending their dedication and commitment to their respective fields.

  

  

She said their achievements could inspire other Oponganons to pursue their educational and professional goals.

  

  

Residents applying for the city’s board passer cash incentive must submit a barangay residency certification, a request letter addressed to the mayor, a voter’s certificate, a photocopy of a valid identification card and a certificate of passing from the Professional Regulation Commission.

  

  

Bar passers must also submit a certificate of membership in the Philippine Bar, while topnotchers must provide a certificate of ranking.
