##### Pine Tree Mountain Resort  

Cebu offers a variety of resorts that cater to the requirements of locals and visitors alike. Some of these resorts are on Metro Cebu, but most may be found in the island's northern and southern municipalities. There are other resorts perched on Cebu's mountains. The Pine Tree Mountain Resort, located in Barangay Gaas, Balamban Cebu, is one of these resorts.

A paradise lying on a mountaintop, PineTree Mountain Resort is a first of its kind. It was initially designed as a rest home for the owner. Then the owner wanted to welcome visitors to his mountain home other than his family and friends and therefore created more rooms for this purpose.

###### HOW TO GET THERE?  

🌍 Exact Location: Gaas Balamban, Cebu  

🗺 Via commute: Ride a Vhire from Ayala heading Balamban and tell the driver to drop you off at Pine Tree Mountain Resort or ride a Habal-habal from JY sqauare.  Before Adventure Cafe. Just a 40-45 mins. away from JY square Lahug.

🗺 Via Private Vehicle: Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze or Google maps. Parking space is available.

###### RATES  

- Weekend day trip 
	- P500 entrance fee (with breakfast; from 6AM-10AM) or P700 entrance fee (with plated lunch or dinner, Note: dinner is only available on Saturdays)
- Day-use 
	- P 1,500 per person (Inclusive of free lunch, and use of infinity pool; from 8AM-5PM)  
- Weekend overnight stay 
	- P5,000 per room per night (2 pax with free breakfast; check-in 3PM and check-out at 1PM) Extra person -P2,000
