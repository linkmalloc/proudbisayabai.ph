Whether it’s the first cup of coffee in the morning or the foundation of a new café venture, having the right equipment and skills can make all the difference. Kapemart Philippines is helping coffee enthusiasts, home baristas, and aspiring entrepreneurs turn their passion for coffee into better brews and thriving businesses.

  

As one of the country’s largest distributors of coffee equipment, Kapemart provides Filipinos with access to quality brewing technology and equipment for both home and commercial use. Its Cebu branch, located at FLC along Hernan Cortes in Mandaue City, serves as a one-stop destination where customers can explore and experience coffee machines and equipment suited to their needs.

  

### **A Complete Coffee Equipment Destination**

  

From beginners setting up their first home coffee station to entrepreneurs preparing to open a café, Kapemart offers solutions for different experience levels and budgets.

  

Its product lineup includes trusted brands such as **Gemilai, Milesto, Breville, Lelit, Hambach, and Barista Premium**, providing customers with options ranging from startup-friendly machines to high-end commercial equipment.

  

Beyond supplying machines, Kapemart helps customers find equipment suited to their brewing preferences, business requirements, and budget.

###   

### **Developing Skills From Bean to Cup**

  

For Kapemart, great coffee is not only about having the right equipment—it is also about knowing how to use it.

  

The company offers a **1-Day Barista 101 Training**, designed for beginners and aspiring coffee professionals who want to develop foundational coffee knowledge and practical brewing skills. The training is priced at **₱6,000 and includes free lunch**.

Participants learn coffee fundamentals, including its history, classification, roasting methods, and grind sizes. The hands-on program also introduces different brewing methods, including **French Press, V60, Aeropress, Moka Pot, Chemex, and espresso machines**.

The training also covers espresso preparation, milk frothing, basic latte art and stencil art, as well as popular café beverages such as **Americanos, Lattes, Cappuccinos, Mochas, and Frappes**.

###   

### **Supporting Coffee Entrepreneurs Every Step of the Way**

  

For aspiring café owners, the training goes beyond beverage preparation. Participants are introduced to essential business concepts, including **machine maintenance, costing, and profit-margin calculations**.

  

By combining coffee equipment with hands-on training, Kapemart Philippines aims to support customers throughout their coffee journey—from setting up a home brewing station to preparing for a café business.

  

Customers can visit **Kapemart Philippines Cebu** at **FLC, Hernan Cortes, Mandaue City, Cebu** to explore its range of coffee equipment, learn more about its brands, and inquire about its Barista 101 Training.

  

For coffee enthusiasts and entrepreneurs looking to take their passion further, Kapemart Philippines provides the tools and knowledge to help turn every cup into an opportunity.
