Mic Check' Tara, Karaoke as a 'Friend'

After years of being isolated in this pandemic, we surely deserve to spend and be with our barkadas and family na! And as Filipinos, what could be the perfect way to reunite with them? KARAOKE! (With or without tagay!) Where is the perfect place to be? Of course. At Mic Check! Located at, Escario St. Corner Juana Osmena St., Barangay Kamputhaw, Cebu City!   

They are open 24/7! Kaya kahit pa wanto-sawa ang kantahan, sayawan, at tugtugan, pwede yan sa Mic Check!   

When you reach there, here's what I suggest you should do!   

- Chill at the al fresco for some easy listening music. (Yes, po. Opo. May outdoors sila!) 
- Vent out with a friend while listening to the al fresco music. (Pero no to, Maoy ha! 😏) 
- Bring your gang at the airconditioned rooms at Mic Check and sing your hearts out. (Walang iyakan ha...)
- Order some food and get dinner while singing your hearts out.
