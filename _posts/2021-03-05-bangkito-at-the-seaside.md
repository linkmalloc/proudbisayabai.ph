BOODLE FIGHT with Barkadas? Worries no more kay naa ra diri sa Dumanjug South Cebu! Arats na Bai sa BANGKITO AT THE SEASIDE!!! 🍱🥙🥗  

****
****
##### 🕒 Opening hours:  
3PM - 10PM  

****
##### 📍 Exact Location:  
Poblacion Looc Dumanjug Cebu.  

****
![](http://res.cloudinary.com/proudbisayabaii/image/upload/v1614950791/bangkito_at_the_seaside/udcfzcbbumjbyh3bkvjr.jpg)  
<small>Source: Michael Sagonoy | Vivi Moore</small>  

****
#### HOW TO GET THERE?  

##### 🗺 Via commute :  
Just ride a bus sa South Bus Terminal pa Dumanjug then tell the driver to drop you off sa Dumanjug Looc then after ask someone asa ang Barangay Looc then likod ana naa diha ang “Bangkito at the Seaside”.  

****
##### 🗺 Via private vehicle :  
You can used waze or google map then search “Dumanjug Looc”  

****
