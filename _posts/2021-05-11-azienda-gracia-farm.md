#### Azienda Gracia Farm  

Looking for a relaxing and peaceful place to chill with your special someone? Well, **Azienda Gracia** is waving for you!

**Azienda Gracia** is an organic farm in the highlands of Barangay Guba, Cebu City. The farm has a big treehouse with open living space on the main floor, bedrooms on the attic, and a kitchen on the lower ground. The spacious main floor is a good place for guests to bond, do workshops and seminars, or chill and relax while surrounded by nature.

Azienda Gracia is a working farm with farm animals and livestock, vegetable gardens, fruit-bearing trees, native tree collections, stingless bees, and many more. It is a complete experience to farm life and nature.

##### HOW TO GET THERE?

🌎 Exact Location: Azienda Gracia Farm, Sitio Cambilog, Brgy. Guba, Cebu City  
  
🗺 Via Commute: From Colon St. ride 13C Beep Bus or Jeepney going to Talamban, Cebu (fare is P20-30 pesos).Then ride habal-habal going to Brgy. Guba. Usually habal-habal from Talamban or Sirao. Since Brgy. Guba is next to Brgy. Sirao so they can pass either routes Talamban or sa TCH thru Sirao.  
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
