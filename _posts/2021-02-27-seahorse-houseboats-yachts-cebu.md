Experience this perfect dating place with your boo! In just P2,000 both of you will experience these:  
  
📍MAKASAKAY KA UG YACHT  
📍MAKAJAM UG LIVE BAND  
📍MAKAKAON UG FOOD BUFFET

Oh diba bongga kaayo mga bai! 😍😱  
  
🕑 Operating hours: 8:00AM-7:30PM   
  
🌎 Exact Location: Arterra Hotel,Punta Engaño,Lapulapu City  

****
#### How to get there?  
  
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
Ride a bus going to Mactan Newtown then naog naog mo ara then sakay jeep para punta enganño  
  
##### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
