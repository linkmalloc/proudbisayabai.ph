CEBU CITY, Philippines – A confrontation erupted between protesters and members of the Philippine National Police (PNP) after authorities intervened during the burning of a small piñata at a rally opposing US President Donald Trump’s visit and the proposed Pax Silica agreement on Monday, November 16.

  

The incident occurred in downtown Cebu City, where demonstrators had gathered to express their opposition to Trump’s visit to the Philippines and the proposed agreement.

  

Tensions escalated when police personnel intervened as protesters set fire to the piñata during the demonstration. The situation later turned chaotic as police attempted to arrest several protesters, triggering further confrontation between the two sides.

  

What began as a peaceful gathering subsequently developed into a tense encounter between demonstrators and law enforcement personnel.

  

The rally was part of the protesters’ expression of opposition to the proposed Pax Silica agreement and the US president’s visit, reflecting concerns over US involvement in the Philippines and its implications for the country’s national interests.
