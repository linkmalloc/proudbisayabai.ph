CEBU, Philippines — One of the popular singer-actresses, Sandara Park, is among the lined-up Korean stars to perform at the concert that will take place in the City of Cebu, particularly at the City di Mare Events Grounds on September 23.

This event is headlined by "AWAKE: A New Beginning," organized by Mark Entertainment and Events.

Joining Sandara in this concert are K-pop groups such as AB6IX, VIVIZ, and Younite, as well as South Korean dance groups 1MILLION and MB Crew.

The ticket prices are P8,000 (SVIP), P6,000 (Gold), P4,000 (Silver), and P2,000 (General Admission).

The organizers are confident that they will receive strong support from the Cebuano community for this concert.

Furthermore, after this concert, there will be another phase featuring Korean performers who will also come to Cebu.
