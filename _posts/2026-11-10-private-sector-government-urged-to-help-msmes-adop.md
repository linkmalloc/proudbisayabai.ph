Small businesses need more than access to technology to benefit from artificial intelligence (AI) and other digital innovations. They also need guidance on how to use these tools and affordable ways to integrate them into their operations.

Angeline Po, Globe Business vice president for product management, said limited knowledge and technical expertise remain among the major barriers preventing micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) from adopting technologies such as AI and the Internet of Things (IoT).

“They're not sure how to start. And of course, there's also the payment,” Po said in an interview on Monday, Nov. 9, 2026, on the sidelines of Globe Telecom's quarterly event in Taguig City.

To address these concerns, Globe Business, the telecommunications company's enterprise and SME segment, has partnered with the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) to conduct in-person and online seminars for business owners interested in using AI to promote their products and services and improve their operations.

Po said these technologies could help businesses automate some of their processes, while government support and partnerships with technology providers could help MSMEs adjust to changes in the business environment.

“I know they have some initiatives on AI. And that cuts across small, medium and large enterprises. So, it's very critical for the small businesses,” she said.

Globe Business also offers 30-day promotions that allow MSMEs to try its products and services before deciding whether these suit their business needs.

Po said trial offers could help address concerns about the additional expense of adopting new technologies, particularly IoT, by allowing business owners to see their potential benefits before committing to the investment.

“(These) can help boost and accelerate the adoption (of IoT) because sometimes, that becomes a barrier. Like, I have to pay for it. It's an additional cost. But sometimes, when they try it and they realize the value of it. That's when they realize that it's a small investment,” she added.
