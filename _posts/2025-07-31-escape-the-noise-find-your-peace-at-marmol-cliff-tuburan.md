Tucked deep within the lush landscapes of Tuburan, Cebu, Marmol Cliff is a serene escape carved naturally by time and water. This hidden gem offers the perfect retreat for those seeking to unwind, recharge, and reconnect with nature.

Far from the bustle of the city, Marmol Cliff invites you to traverse its cool, clear waters, surrounded by towering rock formations and tranquil greenery. The journey is part of the adventure—one that rewards visitors with stillness, breathtaking scenery, and a renewed sense of calm.

📍 Marmol Cliff, Tuburan, Cebu

Whether you’re craving solitude, serenity, or a slice of natural wonder, ari na mo diri—Marmol Cliff is waiting.

How to Get There

* From Cebu City:

Take a Ceres bus or van bound for Tuburan at the Cebu North Bus Terminal. The trip takes approximately 3 to 4 hours.

* Once in Tuburan proper:

You can hire a local habal-habal (motorbike) to take you to Marmol Cliff. The ride is scenic but rugged, so be prepared for a bit of off-road adventure.

Travel Tips:

1. Visit during dry season for better road access.
2. Wear proper footwear as the trail may be slippery.
3. Bring water and snacks there are limited stores nearby.
4. Coordinate with locals or the tourism office for safety and guidance.
