#### Ermi Beach Resort
Who’s excited for the next weekend? Isa’ng kamot! 🙋🏻‍♀️   
  
If you’re planning for a weekend getaway with your family or friends, unya nangita mo ug ka stayhan.  Ari na mo sa ERMI BEACH Resort!   

##### 🕐 Operating hours:   
- Check-in: 2:00 PM   
- Check-out: 12:00 NN  

****
☎️ Contact Number #: [0925-864-5924 ](tel:0925-864-5924 )  
🔗FB: [https://www.facebook.com/ERMI-BEACH-Resort-957216004367454/](https://www.facebook.com/ERMI-BEACH-Resort-957216004367454/)  
  
🌎 Exact Location: Gawi, Oslob – approx. 3.5 hours away from Cebu City  

Just after the boundary of Boljoon and Oslob. A peaceful place with a peaceful scenery.

****
****

##### 📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: From South Bus Terminal, take a bus bound for Bato-Oslob. Tell the driver to drop you off in the border of Boljoon and Oslob.   
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.   
  
##### 📌 Rates:   
🛌 Standard Room (Double): ₱1,750 per night, good for 2 persons  
🛌 Standard Room (Triple): ₱2,300 per night, good for 2-3 persons    
🛌 Deluxe Room (Double): ₱2,200 per night, good for 2 persons  
🛌 Deluxe Room (for 5): ₱4,000 per night, good for 5 persons  
🛌 Deluxe Room (for 7): ₱5,000 per night, good for 7 persons  
  
##### 📌 Add-ons:  
- Additional Persons: P200/ person per night  
- Additional Beds: P200 per bed  

****
