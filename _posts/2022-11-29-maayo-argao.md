South Cebu is home to some of the best beaches and resorts that offer a relaxing escape from the urban jungle. It has become the go-to weekend or quick get-away destination for city dwellers who want to lounge on the beach, have a family trip or commune with nature.  

One on the list of the best places to visit in the Southern province of Cebu is the town of Argao. It is a quiet town rich in history and culture. Since its founding in 1608, Argao has grown from a sleepy fishing and farming town to a tourist destination today with many promises.  

One of its prides is The Maayo Argao, the newest haven for travellers and adventure-seekers alike. Greeted by a symmetrical mini courtyard and the colonial style touch at the main reception, Maayo Argao is Moroccan-inspired boutique resort, reflected by the arches and pillars of the Moroccan veranda.

It is Cebu’s most tasteful and most instragrammable vacation spot in the South. Its standard and family rooms, 3 and 4 bedroom villas, the coastal themed Asul Café and the stylish reception area and restaurant—altogether offers a casual yet refreshing escape in a Moroccan-inspired atmosphere with local tropical vibe and sophistication.  

Owned and operated by Maayo Hotel under Primary Group of Builders, resort guests are sure to be pampered and well-taken care of. The nature of Maayo Argao lies not just in its attentive staff, luscious green spaces, or exquisite décor, but in the quiet splendour of the surrounds. For a hotel of its size and grandeur, it retains a delightful intimacy, giving you time for relaxation and contemplation before you’re ready to go back to the bustling city. Maayo Argao lets you enjoy modern comforts at reasonable rates.

###### How to get there?

Via Commute: From South Bus Terminal, ride a bus going to Argao. Tell the conductor to stop at Petron in Poblacion, Argao. Then ride a tricycle going to the resort. Just tell the driver that you’re heading to Maayo Stay Argao.

Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated via Waze or Google Maps. (Spacious Parking Space Available)
