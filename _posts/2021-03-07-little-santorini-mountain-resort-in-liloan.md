Santorini Greece? Nope! It's Little Santorini Mountain Resort in Liloan.  If you're looking for affordable place and instagrammable view then, mao nani ang tubag 😍  

****
##### Operating hours:   
- Day use 9am to 5pm   
- Night Use 3pm to 10pm  
  
📍 Exact Location:  Sandayung 2, Brgy. San Roque, Lilo-an, Cebu  
  
****
##### HOW TO GET THERE?
  
****
##### 🗺 Via COMMUTE:  
From SM City Cebu ride any bus or v-hire going to Lilo-an. Drop off at Yati intersection, near Yati Elementary School. From there, ride a habal-habal (motorbike) going to Little Santorini Mountain Resort. Fare is only P70.00 each good for 2 persons and P80.00 if only one. approx. 1 hour away from Cebu City, 15 minutes away from the main road (going uphill).  
  
****
##### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.  
  
****
##### 📌RATES:   

****

- WEEKDAY PROMO:  150 for adult &amp; child  
- WEEKENDS PROMO:  Adult 250 Child 150

****
##### SPECIAL PRIVATE AREAS:  
- Pavilion 1 (20-30 pax) = Php 3,500.00  
- Pavilion 2 (20-30 pax)  = Php 3,000.00  
- Springbok bar (15-20 pax)  = Php 2,500.00  
- Pump boat (15-20 pax)  = Pho 2,500.00  
  
****
##### 📌NOTE:  
- 40% discount in all special private areas (save 1000 &amp; more)

- No Corkage for Food but NO DRINKS ALLOWED
