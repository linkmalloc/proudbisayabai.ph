Located within the vibrant Norkis Cyberpark in Mandaue City, TWB Cafe, also known as The Whyte Bhox Cafe. With its inviting ambiance and commitment to quality, TWB Cafe promises an unforgettable experience for both coffee lovers and food enthusiasts!  

Step into TWB Cafe and be greeted by their tempting aroma of freshly brewed coffee. From velvety lattes to robust espressos, every cup is meticulously crafted by skilled baristas using the finest coffee beans. Discover a diverse selection of signature drinks like the Hot Matcha Espresso and the indulgent Mocha Frapesso, each promising a symphony of flavors to delight the senses.  

Beyond its stellar coffee offerings, TWB Cafe boasts a delectable menu to satisfy every palate. Indulge in modern twists on Filipino classics such as the Crispy Palabok, or savor the freshness of the Salmon and Crabsticks. For the ultimate culinary experience, don't miss out on their ultimate specialyy: the mouthwatering US Rib Eye Steak, cooked to perfection and served with a medley of delectable sides.  

Whether you're craving a perfectly brewed cup of coffee or seeking a memorable dining experience, TWB Cafe invites you to indulge in a journey of flavor and discovery. Located just very near The Sentral Club in Norkis Cyberpark, TWB Cafe promises more than just a meal and coffee—it offers an escape where coffee culture meets culinary excellence.  

Mao nang adto na ta!
