Escape into nature at Terrazas de Flores, a serene destination along the Cebu Transcentral Highway where cascading flower terraces create a peaceful retreat from the city. Designed as a sanctuary for families, friends, couples, and travelers of all ages, this blooming landscape offers a calm and refreshing experience surrounded by natural beauty.

Location: Cebu Transcentral Highway, Malubog, Cebu City

Open daily: 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM

Terrazas de Flores also features a restaurant known for crowd favorites such as Back Ribs, Chicken Francaise, Meaty Carbonara, and Chicken Teriyaki, among many others.

**How to get there:**

From Cebu City proper, take the Cebu Transcentral Highway heading toward Balamban. Terrazas de Flores is located in Barangay Malubog and is accessible by private vehicle or motorcycle. Travel time is approximately 40–60 minutes, depending on traffic.

Whether you are seeking quiet moments in nature or a relaxing dining experience with scenic views, Terrazas de Flores is a must-visit destination in Cebu.
