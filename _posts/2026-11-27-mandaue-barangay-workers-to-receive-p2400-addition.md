Barangay Health and Nutrition Workers and Clean and Green program personnel in Mandaue City are set to receive an additional P2,400 in honorarium covering three months, following the City Council's approval of a temporary increase in their compensation.

  

The adjustment covers qualified workers assigned across the city's 27 barangays, with an additional P800 for each month from September to November 2026.

  

Councilor Joel Seno, who authored the measure, said the additional payment recognizes the workers' contributions while providing financial relief amid rising living costs.

  

"We are giving them an opportunity sad nga maka-enjoy and recognizing their efforts since they are city-paid," Seno said in an interview on November 26.

  

Seno said the council would work toward releasing the additional honorarium within December.

  

The approval followed the recommendation of the Local Finance Committee under Resolution No. 2026-11-015, approved on November 6, which sought Mayor Thadeo Jovito "Jonkie" Ouano's approval of the temporary increase.

  

Under the measure, only workers who remain in service as of November 30 will qualify for the additional payment.

  

Their monthly honorarium will return to its original rate after the three-month coverage, as the adjustment is not intended to be permanent.

  

The Local Finance Committee determined that the increase was financially feasible based on the city's financial position and would not affect the delivery of other essential public services.

  

Funding will come from the city's available appropriations, subject to fund certification and applicable budgeting, accounting and auditing rules.

  

The measure will take effect upon the mayor's approval.
