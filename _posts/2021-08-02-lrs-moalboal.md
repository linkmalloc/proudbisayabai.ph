#VisitMoalboal: Usahay mangandoy sad tag pahuway unya laag kuyog atong mga hinigugma, Nagplano mo nga molaag? Ari ta Bai! For only 1,600 PESOS Good TWO Already! 💦🍹😮

This resort is perfect for those looking for a quite place to unwind. If you’re looking for luxury at a reasonable price and an excellent place to enjoy some peace and quiet time, check out LRS Moalboal Cebu in Basdiot Moalboal. 

CHECK IN TIME 2:00PM 
CHECK OUT TIME 11:30AM

Moalboal, South Cebu 🇵🇭

Exact Loation: LRS, Talisay Road, Moalboal, 6032 Cebu
