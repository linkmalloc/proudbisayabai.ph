###### Kawasan Falls

Kawasan Falls is a multi-layered waterfall system found in the town of Badian, Cebu, near Barangay Matutinao. It is, without a doubt, the most popular tourist attraction in Badian.

Kawasan Falls, famous for its magnificent blue water, is located at the foot of the Mantalongon Mountain Range, about one kilometer from the national road of Badian and at least four hours of hiking from Dalaguete's Osmea Peak. Its cool, pure water flows over tiers of waterfalls from Kandayvic Spring before reaching the Matutinao River and the Taon Strait.

###### How to get there?  

🌏 Exact Location: Kawasan Falls, Badian, Cebu  

🗺 Via Commute: From the South Bus Terminal, take a bus going to Bato/Barili. Tell the driver that you are going to Kawasan Falls (150 pesos). From the drop-off point, take a habal-habal to the entrance and pay the fee of only 40 (foreigners) pesos per head and 20 for the locals.

🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
