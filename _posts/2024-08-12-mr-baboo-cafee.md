If you haven't yet discovered the delightful world of MR. BABOO Cafe, now's the perfect time! Nestled in serene locations, this cozy café has quickly become a must-visit destination for locals and tourists alike. With its signature offerings, including delicious milk tea starting at just 59 pesos, MR. BABOO brings a blend of comfort and flavor that’s hard to resist. Whether you're craving a refreshing drink, a hearty meal, or a quick snack, this charming café has something for everyone.  

Step into MR. BABOO and you'll be greeted by a warm, homey atmosphere that feels just like your favorite comfort spot in the province. Open daily from 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m., MR. BABOO invites you to breathe in the fresh air while indulging in their wide array of offerings. From spicy noodles to comforting sandwiches, and a variety of classic milk teas and frappuccinos, each visit promises a satisfying experience. Don’t forget to try their hearty rice meals, which have become a crowd favorite, keeping patrons coming back for more.  

With branches conveniently located in Bonbon Diot, Poblacion Pinamungajan, and Toledo City, Cebu, MR. BABOO Cafe offers a perfect spot for a quick bite or a leisurely meal. Whether you prefer to dine in, take out, or have your meal delivered, MR. BABOO is ready to serve you with a smile. Stay connected and updated by visiting their Facebook page, where you can explore more about what they have to offer. Experience the magic of MR. BABOO Cafe—where every visit feels like a warm embrace in the heart of Cebu.
