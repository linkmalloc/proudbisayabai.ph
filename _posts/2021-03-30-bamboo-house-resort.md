#### Bamboo House Resort
Basdaku White Beach, Moalboal on your bucket list for summer? Tara na mga Bai!!! sa Bamboo House Resort. 🏕🌊🌴🌞  
  
🌍 Exact Location: Saavedra, Moalboal, Cebu 15 minutes lang by foot from Basdaku Beach Resort.  
  
📌How to get there?  
  
🗺 Via commute : Ride a Bus sa Cebu South Bus Terminal kanang bus that’s bound for Moalboal via Barili/Badian. Then ask the driver to drop you off at Gaisano Grand Mall Moalboal or at the town proper. If dropping off at the town proper, you may arrange a service with the resort beforehand to pick you up from there. Otherwise, ask around for directions.  
  
🗺 Via private vehicle : You can use wazed or google map then search “Moalboal Bamboo House Saavedra Basdaku”.  
  
📌 Room Rates  
  
###### Good for 2 person - 500.00 pesos.  
The cottage is divided into 2 bedrooms which is good for double dates or a group of 4.  
  
###### Good for 2 pax - 650.00 pesos.  
It is a single-detached house where you can enjoy your privacy.  
  
###### Good for 2 pax - 800.00 pesos.  
It is also a single-detached house with your own sala where you can enjoy some chitchat, food mukbang, and more.  
  
###### Good for 3 pax - 800.00 pesos.  
Cozy and modern Filipino Bahay Kubo with a queen-sized bed.  
  
###### Good for 4 pax - 1,200.00 pesos.  
This one is spacious and good for a barkada overnight stay-in.
