#### Lambug Beach Badian

****
Asa man ta this weekend? No need to travel outside Cebu and spend thousands. With a pocket-friendly adventure to South, your feet will surely bring you to the Little Boracay of Southern Cebu!!! 🏖☀️😍  
  
🌎 Exact Location: Lambug Beach Badian, South Cebu  
  
📌 How to get there?  
  
🗺 From the South Bus Terminal, take a bus going to Badian or Oslob. Fare is ₱140. Tell the bus driver to drop you off at the town center or "lungsod" of Lambug Badian. Travel time is approximately 3 hours.  
  
- From the town proper, ride a tricycle going to Lambug Beach. Fare is ₱30 per person. Travel time is 20-30 minutes.  
  
🗺 Via private vehicle : If you have your own car just use Google map or waze then search “Lambug Beach Badian”.  
Scroll down to get the map location.

📌 Entrance fee: P45.00  
  
📌 Fees  
- Parking fee: P100.00   
- Tent charge: ₱50 /head on weekdays, ₱150 /head on weekends you should bring your own tent.   
- Cottage: ₱500 per day  
- Bus fare: ₱140 /head  
- Tricycle: ₱30 /head  

****
P.S. Pwede raka mag hapin, para wala'y bayad and mas makatipid. Just make sure to be responsible with your own trashes lang jud.
