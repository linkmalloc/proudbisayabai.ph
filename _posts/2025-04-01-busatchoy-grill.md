If you're looking for a perfect place to enjoy great food while basking in the breathtaking views of Busay, look no further than Busatchoy Grill—the first-ever Batchoy spot in the area! Combining the rich flavors of Batchoy with the cool mountain breeze, this newly opened eatery is a must-visit for both locals and tourists alike.

###### The Birth of Busatchoy (Busay + Batchoy)
Busatchoy Grill was born from a simple yet thoughtful observation. Owner Veronica Castillo, after living in Busay for a year, realized that something was missing—a comforting, warm dish that suits the cool climate. Thus, Busay Batchoy was created. More than just a restaurant, Busatchoy Grill was built with the vision of addressing market demand while also creating job opportunities for the local community.

“I know that the more businesses I build, the more I can give back to my people through employment,” the owner shared. True to this belief, Busatchoy Grill is not just about good food; it’s about fostering growth and community.

###### What’s on the Menu?
The star of the show is their Batchoy Overload, a generously portioned bowl of hearty, flavorful goodness that can serve 3-4 people for just ₱150! Whether you're visiting with family or friends, this dish is perfect for sharing and warming up in the cool Busay weather.

Here’s a quick look at their pricing:
- Batchoy - ₱150
- Unod - ₱35
- Isaw - ₱25
- Tambok - ₱20
- Paa - ₱95
- Backbone - ₱65
- Longganisa - ₱40
- Skinless - ₱30
- Chorizo - ₱50
- Hotdog - ₱25
- Hotdog Balls - ₱25

Why Visit Busatchoy Grill?
Aside from the mouthwatering food, Busatchoy Grill boasts several perks that make it a must-visit dining spot:

✅ Generous Servings – Perfect for sharing without breaking the bank.  
✅ Spacious Parking – No need to worry about finding a spot for your car.  
✅ Fast & Friendly Service – You’ll be welcomed with warm smiles and prompt service.  
✅ Cozy Ambiance – Enjoy the cool mountain breeze while indulging in flavorful dishes.  


###### Plan Your Visit
📍 Location: Veterans Drive, Lower Busay, Cebu City  
⏰ Operating Hours: 3 PM to 3 AM daily   

Whether you’re a Batchoy lover or just someone looking for a new dining experience with stunning views, Busatchoy Grill is the place to be. So, gather your friends, drive up to Busay, and savor a bowl of warm, comforting goodness today!
