If you’re craving an authentic coastal food experience in Sugbo, make your way to Cordova’s Street Food Plaza at the Roro Port. As the sun begins to set and the sea breeze rolls in, this lively strip transforms into a vibrant haven for food lovers seeking bold flavors without the hefty price tag.

**Where the Sea Meets the Street**

Set right by the water, the plaza offers more than just a meal. It delivers atmosphere. The scent of charcoal grilled barbecue fills the air, vendors call out their daily specials, and diners gather around simple tables to enjoy freshly prepared seafood. The setting is casual, welcoming, and unmistakably local.

Expect a generous spread of street side favorites
Freshly grilled fish straight from nearby waters
Saang shellfish cooked to savory perfection
Juicy pork and chicken barbecue skewers
A variety of classic Filipino street foods that are both nostalgic and satisfying

Everything is affordable, flavorful, and served with genuine Bisaya hospitality.

**A Gathering Place for Everyone**

What makes Cordova’s Street Food Plaza special is its community feel. Families share seafood platters, barkadas laugh over rounds of barbecue, and travelers discover the simple joy of eating by the sea. The cool evening breeze and the gentle sound of waves provide a refreshing contrast to the bustle of the grills.

Whether you’re a dedicated foodie or simply looking for a relaxed dinner spot after exploring Cebu, this seaside food hub delivers an experience that feels both grounded and memorable.

**Plan Your Visit**

Roro Road Port, Cordova Cebu
Open daily from 4:00 PM to 11:00 PM

Arrive hungry, come early for the freshest selections, and support the hardworking local vendors who keep this culinary gem thriving.

In Cordova, food is shared, savored, and celebrated by the sea.
