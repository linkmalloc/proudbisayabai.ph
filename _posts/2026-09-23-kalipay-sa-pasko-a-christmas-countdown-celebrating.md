Christmas has a way of making ordinary moments feel extraordinary.

A familiar song playing in the background. A meal shared around the table. A thoughtful gift. A cup of coffee during a busy December morning. A trip home. A simple message from someone we have not heard from in a while.

These are the moments that make the Christmas season meaningful.

![](https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/2996/18b1231790141547-2.jpeg)

_Photo credits: John Togonon, Proud Bisaya Bai Photographer_

This year, Proud Bisaya Bai presents **KALIPAY SA PASKO,** a Christmas countdown campaign celebrating the wishes, moments, experiences, and simple joys that make the holiday season special. More than a countdown to Christmas, KALIPAY SA PASKO is an invitation to pause, appreciate what matters, and celebrate the little things that bring happiness during the holiday season.

### **Celebrating the simple joys**

The Christmas season does not always have to be grand. Sometimes, happiness can be found in the simplest things: enjoying your favorite food, sharing a refreshing drink, discovering a new place, preparing for a journey, spending time with family, or simply finding a moment to appreciate the season.

KALIPAY SA PASKO celebrates these everyday experiences and the stories behind them.

Through the campaign, Proud Bisaya Bai creates a space where the community can share Christmas wishes, traditions, favorite moments, and the things that bring them joy. Because every Christmas story is different, but every story is worth celebrating.

### **A Christmas countdown for everyone**

As the days draw closer to Christmas, the countdown becomes more than just a number. It becomes a reminder to make time for the people and experiences that matter. For some, Christmas means going home. For others, it means starting a new tradition, enjoying a favorite meal, giving a thoughtful gift, or simply finding a reason to smile.

KALIPAY SA PASKO brings these stories together through the Proud Bisaya Bai community, celebrating the many ways Filipinos experience the Christmas season.

### **Celebrating local brands, local stories**

At the heart of the campaign are businesses and brands that are part of everyday Filipino experiences.

Our Proud Brand Partners represent different industries, products, and experiences, but they share one thing in common: they are part of the communities we live, work, celebrate, and grow in.

**MATLEX RUBBER & CONSTRUCTION SUPPLIER PHILIPPINES**

Behind every growing community are businesses that help build and support it. Matlex Rubber & Construction Supplier Philippines represents the importance of construction and supply businesses in keeping projects moving and communities developing. Its partnership with Proud Bisaya Bai adds another story to our celebration of local enterprise and the people behind the businesses that help build communities.

**DON MACCHIATOS PHILIPPINES**

For many Filipinos, coffee is more than a beverage. It is part of the daily routine and often a reason to pause, connect, and enjoy a moment. Don Macchiatos Philippines brings its coffee experience closer to communities, becoming part of everyday coffee moments shared by customers.

This Christmas, those simple coffee moments become part of the stories celebrated through KALIPAY SA PASKO.

**DON LEMON PHILIPPINES**

A refreshing drink can turn an ordinary break into a small moment of joy. Don Lemon Philippines brings a refreshing beverage experience to customers through its selection of lemonade offerings. As part of KALIPAY SA PASKO, Don Lemon Philippines joins the celebration of simple experiences that bring a little more brightness to the holiday season.

**ALVO POWER SYSTEMS**

Reliable power plays an important role in homes, businesses, and communities.ALVO Power Systems offers power solutions designed to support different energy and backup requirements, including advanced generators and solar panel systems.

From backup power for businesses and establishments to solar-based energy solutions, ALVO provides options for customers looking for practical ways to support their power needs.

As a Proud Brand Partner, ALVO Power Systems joins KALIPAY SA PASKO in celebrating innovation, reliable solutions, and businesses helping communities stay powered.

**MR. PURE ICE**

From everyday refreshments to holiday celebrations, ice is a small but essential part of many Filipino gatherings. Mr. Pure Ice serves customers and businesses that rely on ice for their daily needs.During a season filled with gatherings and celebrations, the brand becomes part of the everyday details that help make shared moments possible.

**PADAYON CROISSANT**

The word “Padayon” carries a special meaning in Cebuano: to continue, persevere, and move forward. Padayon Croissant brings together the experience of croissants and coffee while carrying a name that reflects the spirit of moving forward. For a Christmas season filled with reflection and new beginnings, the meaning behind Padayon resonates with the spirit of KALIPAY SA PASKO.

**KUKU’S FROZEN YOGURT**

Sometimes, happiness is simply enjoying something sweet. KUKU’s Frozen Yogurt adds a refreshing and creamy experience to the local dessert scene, offering customers another way to enjoy a simple treat. As part of our Christmas campaign, KUKU’s celebrates the little pleasures that make ordinary days a little brighter.

**MARSTEK PHILIPPINES - CEBU**

Technology continues to influence how people manage energy and power in modern homes and businesses. Marstek Philippines - Cebu is part of the growing energy technology sector, bringing energy-related solutions closer to the local market. Its partnership with Proud Bisaya Bai reflects the campaign’s celebration of innovation, entrepreneurship, and businesses shaping the future.

**SOROTEC PHILIPPINES**

As interest in renewable energy and energy storage continues to grow, Sorotec Philippines brings solar-powered solutions closer to Filipino customers. Among its offerings are solar batteries and energy storage solutions, designed to help customers make better use of solar energy and support their power needs.

With technology playing an increasingly important role in modern energy management, Sorotec Philippines represents innovation in the growing renewable energy space. As a Proud Brand Partner, Sorotec Philippines joins KALIPAY SA PASKO in celebrating innovation, sustainability, and the possibilities of a more energy-conscious future.

**JET WHEEL PHILIPPINES**

Travel meets technology. Christmas is also a season of travel. Families head home, friends plan getaways, and travelers prepare for new adventures. Jet Wheel Philippines, featuring Airwheel electric luggage, brings technology into the travel experience by combining luggage functionality with electric mobility.For travelers looking for a modern travel companion, Airwheel electric luggage offers a different approach to moving through airports, terminals, and travel destinations.

As a Proud Brand Partner, Jet Wheel Philippines becomes part of KALIPAY SA PASKO, celebrating journeys, adventures, and the excitement of where the road ahead may lead.

Travel smarter. Move easier. Go farther.

### **More than a countdown**

  

![](https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/2996/08df531790142096-3.jpeg)

Christmas is measured in days, but its meaning is found in moments.KALIPAY SA PASKO is Proud Bisaya Bai’s way of celebrating those moments: the wishes we make, the memories we create, the traditions we keep, the businesses we support, and the simple joys that make the season special. As Christmas draws closer, we invite our community to share what makes this season meaningful to you.

Unsa imong wish karong Pasko, mga bai?

Let us count down to Christmas together, one story, one wish, and one joyful moment at a time.
