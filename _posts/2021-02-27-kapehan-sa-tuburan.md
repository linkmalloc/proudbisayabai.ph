Come and Relax here at Kapehan Sa Tuburan in brgy. Orong Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental! 🏕🍃😯  
  
****
A nice cafe by the nature with native type ambiance, perfect for relaxation away from the city.. you can also stay here overnight or just walk in dine-in, foods and drinks here are really good, Promise!!!😋
