\#WhatsNewCebu: Prepping your summer trip now is a great idea because summertime is literally right around the corner! 

Experience pleasurable activities to partake in during your break. Feel a cozy vibe while enjoying delicious foods at Dayún Glampsite! It is ideal for romantic getaways and is family-friendly.

On the estate, there are various options available to unwind and rejuvenate. For leisure, you can go hiking, go for a walk, or watch birds. Definitely a natural refuge where visitors can go to truly unplug from the stress of daily life. Guests can soak in nature while surrounded by untouched foliage and emerge feeling revitalized.

Dayún Glampsite highlights its fantastic glass glamping tent with its native, rustic, tropical concept. Some nice greenery adds to the spot’s relaxing vibe. Soft and mellow music welcomes the guests, which is so homey!

Lahi rajod ang feeling kung naa ka sa bukid, bai!

CHECK-IN / OUT TIME  

Check-in: 2PM   
Check-out: 11AM   

Note: They strictly do not allow late check-outs to give ample time for our cleaners to prepare the area for the next booking.

Exact Location: Purok Hillsite, Lantawan, Brgy. Greenhills, San Fernando, Cebu - Just an hour drive from Cebu City or 5 mins away from San Fernando highway

###### How to get there? 

You may use Waze or Google Maps, just search Dayun Glampsite: https://g.co/kgs/PPTWfT (Nearest landmark from the highway is Maiquis Bakeshop and Sangat Barangay Hall and Gym) 

Just enter the road between Maiquis Bakeshop and Sangat Barangay Hall and Gym and follow the road going to Sitio Hillsite, Lantawan, Brgy. Greenhills, San Fernando, Cebu.
