###### Serenity Farm and Resort: A Haven of Beauty and Rest

A steep ascent with gravel embedded in the ground leads off the Malubog Busay highway straight into the gates of Serenity Farm and Resort! 
The scene you will see after minutes of traveling through woodlands out of the bustling city streets where there is a presence of dwellings is an unexpected godsend: flowers bloom over the premises with color popping up in the afternoon sun amidst bushes of fresh green leaves. While driving along the road, take in the wonders of nature and alpine scenery.

###### How to get there?
Via Commute: From Metro Colon, ride any Jeepneys bound to IT Park or JY Square Mall. Then for a habal-habal around the Mall and tell the driver to drop you off at Serenity Farm and Resort. It's 45 mins straight drive from JY Square. Estimated fare is P150-200 ( need good negotiation) one way and can accommodate 2pax per habal-habal. 

Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze or Google Map. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze or Google Map: Sitio Tiguib, Brgy., Cebu City, Cebu 0917 726 0775 [https://g.co/kgs/6fC6Gf](https://g.co/kgs/6fC6Gf)
