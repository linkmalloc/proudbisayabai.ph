For adventure seekers and hiking enthusiasts, Mt. Lanaya in Brgy. Legaspi, Alegria, Cebu offers one of the most challenging climbs in the province. Towering at around 700 meters above sea level, this mountain is known for its steep slopes, narrow ridges, and almost 90-degree ascents, making it a must-try for thrill-seekers looking to level up their hiking experience.&#x20;

The climb rewards hikers with breathtaking panoramic views of surrounding mountains and the ocean below. On a clear day, you can even catch sight of the jagged peaks of Osmeña Peak in the distance, creating a truly unforgettable vista.&#x20;

Location: Mt. Lanaya, Brgy. Legaspi, Alegria, Cebu

**How to get there:**&#x20;

From Cebu City, head to the Cebu South Bus Terminal and ride a Ceres bus bound for Bato via Barili. Inform the conductor that you will be dropped off at Legaspi, Alegria. Travel time is approximately 4–5 hours depending on traffic, with a fare of ₱155.

Upon arrival at the Barangay Hall of Legaspi, hikers must register and pay the following fees:
&#x9;•	Guide Fee: ₱500 per group of 5
&#x9;•	Environmental Fee: ₱50 per person

The area is accessible via Waze, whether you’re commuting by bus or driving your own vehicle, making navigation straightforward for first-time visitors.

Mt. Lanaya is ideal for those seeking adrenaline-pumping adventure, stunning landscapes, and a rewarding challenge. Make sure to prepare properly, wear the right hiking gear, and bring enough water and snacks for the climb. This hidden gem in Alegria promises an unforgettable experience for every mountaineer.
