LOVE IS IN THE AIR in Daku Island, Siargao! ❤️💕
The BIGGEST island among the 3 islands in Tri-island tour where you can have a mouth-watering Boodle Fight lunch and see the beautiful white sand beach. 🌴🇵🇭  
📍 Daku Island, Siargao  

**Tourist Requirements:**  
**Entering via Sayak Airport and Surigao Airport:**  
1. E Health Pass with Valid ID
2. Negative RT-PCR test result taken within 48 hrs of departure from point of origin. 
3. 5 Day confirmed booking from an accommodation establishment with CAO or PCAO
4. Roundtrip ticket / travel details (if applicable)
5. If the tourist presents a negative RT-PCR test result taken more 48 hrs from point of origin, a negative antigen swab test will be required on the day of arrival. 
6. Travel Itinerary. Book your Tri-Island Your with [Gotmarked Tours](https://www.facebook.com/gotmarkedtours/) 💙
