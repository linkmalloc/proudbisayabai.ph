More than 300 security personnel will be deployed for Mandaue City's Christmas lighting ceremony on Monday evening, Nov. 23, as authorities prepare for an estimated 12,000 to 15,000 spectators.

Mandaue City Police Office (MCPO) Director Police Colonel Cirilo Acosta Jr. said the police are completing preparations for the event in coordination with Mayor Thadeo Jovito “Jonkie” Ouano.

“We are expecting about 12 to 15 thousand spectators nga manguban sa Christmas lighting, which is a tradition in Mandaue City,” (We are expecting about 12,000 to 15,000 spectators to join the Christmas lighting, which is a tradition in Mandaue City) Acosta said.

The MCPO will provide around 180 police personnel, while other security agencies and city offices will account for the remaining members of the deployment. Reserve personnel will also be on standby for additional security needs.

Among the agencies expected to assist are the Bureau of Fire Protection, Armed Forces of the Philippines and Philippine Coast Guard, along with the city's Traffic Enforcement Agency of Mandaue (TEAM), Barangay Public Safety Office and Civil Security Unit.

Police preparations will also cover the Food Park in front of City Hall, which will open from Dec. 7 to 17.

Acosta said police personnel already maintain a regular presence in the area because it serves as a gathering place and houses the city government.

“Everyday naa tay deployment. Even if wala pa ang holiday season, naa tay regular deployment,” (We have personnel deployed every day. Even before the holiday season, we maintain a regular deployment) he said.

However, police will strengthen security measures in December as more visitors are expected in the area.

Acosta said police intelligence units at the stations and headquarters would monitor individuals, particularly repeat offenders, who might take advantage of crowded events to commit theft and pickpocketing.

“All stations have their own intelligence units, as well as our headquarters,” he said.

Authorities will also prohibit people under the influence of alcohol from entering the event area to prevent disturbances.

“Bawalon nato ang mga nakainom because kasagaran mahimong cause sa problema,” (We will prohibit those who have been drinking because they are often a cause of problems) Acosta said.

Smoking will also be prohibited to prevent accidental burns in crowded areas. Police reminded spectators not to bring sharp objects or firearms to the festivities.

Meanwhile, TEAM head Hyll Retuya said traffic adjustments would be implemented during Monday's ceremony.

“Kahibaw man ta nga puno kaayo na dinha, but we are not closing the area for our motorists nga makaagi lang,” (We know the area will be very crowded, but we will not close it off to motorists who simply need to pass through) Retuya said.
