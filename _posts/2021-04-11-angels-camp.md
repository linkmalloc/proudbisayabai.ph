#### Angel's Camp, Sea of Clouds
Experience the beauty of nature! 😯💨🧺  
  
Angel's Camp is the Newest Tourist Spot in Bansalan,Davao del Sur. Perfect place to unwind, hike and connect with Nature. They also offer Batchoy which is literally good for the cold weather.   

🕑 Open Hours: 8AM - 6PM daily   
(Check in 4PM | Check out 7AM)   
  
🌎 Exact Location: Purok Venus.Bagong Silang Balutakay, Bansalan Davao del Sur  
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Kung gikan Bansalan, unahan lang sa holyland. Kung gikan Kapatagan, unahan lng sa Montefrio. The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.   
  
OVERNIGHT reservation is needed.  
  
DAYTOUR Rate: P55.00   
OVERNIGHT Entrance: P100.00  
  
###### ⛺Tri-angel's Native Hut: P1,500.00 Pesos  
- with simple beddings.  
- Good for 2 to 3 persons.  
excess person-add 100 / person  
No free breakfast   
- 1000.00 php. If you provide your own beddings
