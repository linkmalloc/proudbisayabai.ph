#### Skip's  Beach Resort
  
TAN-AWA: Any plans for your next staycation? Get unwind with family and friends for a very affordable Resort at Skip's  Beach Resort in Daanbantayan, Cebu! 🏖☀️💦  
  
🕧 Operating hours: 6:30AM - 11PM   
  
🌍 Exact Location: Agujo, Daanbantayan, Cebu   
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: From North Bus Terminal which is located at SM City Cebu, ride a Ceres Bus bound for Maya Bagay Daanbantayan, Cebu. From there ride a Tricycle going to the exact location. You can ask also some locals on how to get there.   
   
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.   
  
📌 Rates:   
- Entrance fee: P5.00 per person   
- Parking fee: P20.00   
- Tables and chairs: P100 no maximum limit  
  
👉🏻  Click the Full details here: [https://m.facebook.com/SkipsBeachResort/](https://m.facebook.com/SkipsBeachResort/)
