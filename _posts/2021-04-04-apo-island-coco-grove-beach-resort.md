#### Apo Island Coco Grove Beach Resort
Relax, Dive and Swim with turtles at Apo Island Coco Grove Beach Resort. 🏝  
  
How to get there?  
📍Apo Island can be accessed via Negros. To reach the island, ride a ferry or book a flight bound for Dumaguete.    
  
📍When you arrive in Dumaguete, take a private transportation to Malatapay, the jump-off point. Travel time is around 50 minutes.    
  
📍If you want a budget-friendly commute, opt for public transportation. However, the travel time will be more longer. Take a trike to the bus terminal in Dumaguete. Ride any southbound bus or jeepney bound for Zamboanguita and tell the driver that you are going to Apo Island.    
  
📍You'll be dropped off in Malatapay. Look for the large sign that reads “Apo Island”, follow the sign and take a walk for 15-20 minutes through the market area to the boat jetty.   
  
📍Upon arrival in Malatapay, ride a boat bound for Apo Island Negros. Make sure to put your belongings in a waterproof bag because the sea swells are strong, you and your gadgets might get wet.   
  
📍You can either stay in Coco Grove Resort or in other Villas within the island.
