Marco Polo Plaza Cebu is redefining the weekday lunch break, proving that a premium hotel dining experience doesn't have to break the bank. Cafe Marco has officially launched its **Semi-Buffet Weekday Lunch**, a culinary offering designed for the modern Cebuano lifestyle—whether you are rushing for a quick bite, hosting a casual business meeting, or simply catching up with friends.

### **A Curated Culinary Experience**

The concept is simple yet brilliant: guests enjoy unlimited access to an extensive spread of appetizers and soups, followed by a choice of a freshly prepared main course.

The semi-buffet station is a treasure trove of flavors, featuring fresh salads, local street food favorites, and rotating chef’s specials. A standout among the small plates is the savory **Pepperoni Crostini**—a perfect bite-sized starter to whet your appetite.

### **Main Course Highlights**

Each guest selects one individually plated main course from five categories: Pasta, BBQ, Fried Delights, Gourmet Sandwiches, Filipino Flavor Collection. Served with Sides such as garlic bread, fries, rice, mashed potatoes, or seasonal vegetables depending on the selection.

A highly recommended choice for those craving continental flavors is the **Chicken Schnitzel**. Crispy on the outside and tender on the inside, it is served with a zesty **lemon caper sauce** and paired with creamy **mashed potatoes**, offering a comforting yet sophisticated midday meal.

### **A Sweet Ending**

No lunch at Cafe Marco is complete without visiting the dedicated dessert station. Alongside seasonal fruits and local delicacies, the pastry team has rolled out some decadent treats.

Dessert lovers should not miss the rich and indulgent **Midnight Truffle Cake**, a chocolate lover's dream. For those who prefer a balance of sweet and salty, the **Caramel Cheesecake** is a must-try, ensuring your lunch break ends on a perfectly sweet note.

### **Exceptional Value for Every Diner**

Available **Mondays to Thursdays**, this dining experience is tailored to fit different needs and budgets:

* **Standard Rate:** PHP 980 nett per person.
* **Online Exclusive:** PHP 880 nett per person when booked via the [<u>Marco Polo Plaza Cebu Online Store</u>](https://store-cebu.marcopolohotels.com/en/cafemarco/).
* **Group Rate:** PHP 780 nett per person for groups of 20 or more.

Whether it’s a quick corporate lunch or a relaxed gathering, Cafe Marco offers a polished setting with food that hits the spot every time.
