A lone bettor from Camarines Sur won the P25.1 million jackpot in Tuesday’s Super Lotto 6/49 draw, marking the second consecutive day that a player from the province claimed a major lottery prize.

  

The Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO) announced Wednesday that the bettor correctly guessed the winning combination of 11-20-12-36-44-26 to claim the P25,187,181.56 jackpot.

  

The winner has one year to claim the prize at the PCSO main office in Mandaluyong City. The amount will be subject to a 20 percent tax under the Tax Reform for Acceleration and Inclusion law, which applies to lotto winnings exceeding P10,000.

  

Meanwhile, no bettor won the Ultra Lotto 6/58 jackpot, which reached P182.43 million. Its winning combination was 35-14-07-13-46-55.

  

The PCSO said 11 players won P118,181.81 each after matching five of the six winning numbers in the Super Lotto draw. Another 507 bettors won P2,366.86 each for getting four correct numbers, while 11,260 players received P50 each for matching three numbers.

  

The Super Lotto 6/49 draw is held every Tuesday, Thursday, and Sunday.

  

PCSO General Manager Melquiades Robles urged the public to continue supporting the agency’s gaming products, saying revenues generated from its operations help fund health programs, medical assistance, and other charitable initiatives.
