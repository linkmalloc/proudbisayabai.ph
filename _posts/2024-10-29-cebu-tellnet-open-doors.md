Founded in Japan, Tele-Net has been an industry leader in providing high-quality contact center services for 25 years, with operations in China, the USA, and the Philippines. With over 1,500 employees globally, Cebu Tele-Net’s service portfolio includes specialized customer support, IT solutions, and cost-effective BPO services. The company remains committed to strengthening its relationships with clients by combining innovative solutions with a unique strategy to customer interaction.  

****
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/cebu-telenet/p1.jpg)

Cebu Tele-Net, an integral part of the Tokyo-based Tele-Net Group, is actively seeking Customer Service Representatives (CSRs) for its Cebu City site. With a quick hiring process, no previous BPO experience required, and a range of employee benefits, Cebu Tele-Net offers an excellent opportunity for those eager to enter the customer service industry.  


###### Why Choose Cebu Tele-Net? 
###### 1. Quick Hiring and No BPO Experience Needed:  
   For those new to the BPO sector, Cebu Tele-Net’s comprehensive training ensures that applicants can confidently transition into the customer service field. With the entire hiring process completed within one to two days, candidates can quickly secure a rewarding role without prolonged waiting.
###### 2. Fixed Weekend Rest Days:  
   Cebu Tele-Net understands the importance of **work-life balance**, and with fixed weekend rest days, employees have the opportunity to recharge and focus on personal growth.  
###### 3. Health and Wellness Benefits:  
   The company prioritizes the well-being of its workforce by offering free **HMO coverage**, which includes benefits for dependents. This focus on employee health extends beyond the job, promoting an inclusive perspective to work and personal life.
###### 4. Work Environment:  
   All roles are onsite, ensuring that employees can engage directly with peers and supervisors, fostering strong rapport within the workplace.

###### How Cebu Tele-Net Stands Out in the Customer Service Industry  
Cebu Tele-Net’s roots in Japan bring an unparalleled commitment to quality and customer care, anchored in the Japanese philosophy of Omotenashi, which loosely translates to “heart-to-heart service.” It seeks to connect people and cultivate trust, placing heartfelt communication at the core of every service interaction. This ethos has shaped Cebu Tele-Net’s client relationships and is central to the company’s identity. By focusing on lean operations and hiring skilled individuals dedicated to high-quality service, Cebu Tele-Net offers clients cost-efficient, reliable, and premium customer service solutions. The result? Reduced costs, rapid response times, and service that consistently exceeds expectations.  

###### Commitment to Client Success and Customer Satisfaction  
Cebu Tele-Net’s approach to customer service emphasizes building genuine, trust-based relationships with clients and end-users alike. Understanding the goals of its clients is key to Cebu Tele-Net’s service model. Whether the objective is to drive profitability, enhance customer support, or increase operational efficiency, Cebu Tele-Net shapes its services to meet client needs. This integrated approach ensures that Cebu Tele-Net doesn’t just fulfill client requirements; it helps clients build and strengthen relationships with their customers.
