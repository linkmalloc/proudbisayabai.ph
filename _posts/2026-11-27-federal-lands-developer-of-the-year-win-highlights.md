Federal Land Inc. is completing its Marco Polo Residences development in Cebu with the construction of its fifth and final tower, following strong buyer demand for the project that recently earned recognition at the 2026 Philippine Real Estate Awards.

  

The developer’s Marco Polo Residences was named Best Investment Property in Metro Cebu in September, while Federal Land received the Developer of the Year award at the same industry event.

  

Federal Land President Jose Mari Banzon said the recognitions reflected the company's work and the confidence of its partners and customers.

  

“We are grateful for these recognitions, as they reflect the hard work of our people and the trust our partners and customers have placed in us over the years,” Banzon said.

  

 “For us, these awards are also a reminder to keep doing what we do best: creating quality developments where people can build meaningful experiences and lasting memories.”

  

Banzon shared the company's updates and plans during a media gathering on Nov. 26 at Marco Polo Plaza Cebu, which Federal Land owns and developed. The event coincided with the hotel's annual Christmas tree lighting ceremony.

  

He said the company's developments aim to provide communities with homes, hospitality services and amenities that support residents' lifestyles.

  

“Our goal has always been to create more than just buildings,” Banzon said. “We want to develop communities where people can enjoy a complete lifestyle, whether through quality homes, hospitality, or convenient amenities. Marco Polo Residences is a good example of this. We've seen the community grow over the years, and we're happy to welcome even more families as we complete the development with Marco Polo Parkplace, our fifth and final tower.”

  

Marco Polo Parkplace is a 20-storey tower within the Nivel Hills community. The development topped off this year, with limited units remaining, according to Federal Land.

  

The project completes the Marco Polo Residences master plan, which is located beside Marco Polo Plaza Cebu.

  

The hotel, meanwhile, marked the start of the holiday season with its Christmas tree lighting, attended by hotel and Federal Land executives, employees, special guests and members of the Cebu media.

  

Children from Candulawan Elementary School also joined the celebration through the GMA Kapuso Foundation. 

  

They placed handwritten wishes on the hotel's Wall of Wishes before the Christmas tree was illuminated.

  

The event also featured a visit from Santa and Mrs. Claus and a holiday spread prepared by Executive Chef Patrick Chew and the hotel's culinary team.

  

Marco Polo Plaza Cebu's new General Manager Laurent Bourgeois and Director Arthur Gindap were also present. 

  

The hotel is preparing for its 20th anniversary next year.

  

Federal Land is the property arm of GT Capital Holdings and has more than 54 years of experience in the real estate industry.
