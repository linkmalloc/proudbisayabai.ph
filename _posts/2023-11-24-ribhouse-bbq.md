Ribhouse Barbecue is a must-try for anyone looking for delicious and affordable barbecue in Cebu City. Located in Gaisano Country Mall, Ribhouse offers a variety of grilled meats, including pork belly, back ribs, chorizo, and chicken. All of their dishes are served with unlimited rice, making it the perfect spot for a hearty meal.  

In addition to their great food, Ribhouse Barbecue also has a cozy and inviting atmosphere. The restaurant is decorated with vintage black and white tiles, warm mood lighting, and an interior design that integrates greenery with teal couches. This creates a relaxed and comfortable setting that is perfect for enjoying a meal with friends or family.  

If you're looking for a delicious and affordable barbecue restaurant in Cebu City, Ribhouse Barbecue is definitely worth checking out.
