There is something comforting about finding a place that naturally fits into your day. Sometimes it's where you answer emails before lunch, catch up with friends after class, finish a deadline over coffee, or simply pause for a quiet afternoon with a slice of cake in hand.

For the past year, Bayfront CoLive+ Café+ has quietly become one of those places.

As Bayfront CoLive+ marks its first anniversary this October, Café+ celebrates not just twelve months of serving coffee and cakes, but a year of becoming part of the everyday routines of students, professionals, digital nomads, travelers, and neighborhood regulars who have found comfort in its welcoming space.

Located within Bayfront CoLive+ along Gov. M. Roa Street in Cebu City, Café+ was created to complement the rhythm of modern living. It isn't just a café attached to a co-living space—it's an extension of the community itself, where productivity, conversations, and well-deserved breaks naturally come together.

Whether it's a quick coffee between meetings, an afternoon study session, or an unplanned catch-up with friends, Café+ offers a relaxed environment where every visit feels effortless.

To thank guests who have made the past year meaningful, Bayfront CoLive+ Café+ is celebrating with a simple yet delightful anniversary treat.

From **October 1 to 31, 2026**, guests who purchase any slice of cake can enjoy **one hot or iced coffee for just ₱1**, available daily from **11:00 AM to 3:00 PM**.

It's an invitation to slow down for a while—to pair a favorite dessert with freshly brewed coffee, discover a new afternoon habit, or simply give yourself a well-deserved break in the middle of a busy day.

The anniversary offer reflects what Café+ has always been about: making everyday moments feel a little more rewarding without asking for much in return.

Guests can choose any available cake slice and pair it with their preferred hot or iced coffee, with one ₱1 coffee available for every cake slice purchased. The offer is exclusively available during the anniversary period and cannot be combined with other ongoing promotions, discounts, or vouchers.

Over the past year, Café+ has become more than just a convenient coffee stop. It has served as a meeting place for new friendships, collaborative work sessions, casual celebrations, and quiet personal moments. Every cup poured and every conversation shared has helped shape the welcoming atmosphere that defines the space today.

As Bayfront CoLive+ enters another exciting year, Café+ remains committed to creating experiences that go beyond coffee and cake—offering a space where guests can work comfortably, connect with others, recharge between schedules, or simply enjoy life's slower moments.

Because sometimes, the best celebrations aren't the grandest ones.

Sometimes, they're as simple as sharing a slice of cake, enjoying coffee over good conversation, and appreciating the everyday moments that make a place worth coming back to.
