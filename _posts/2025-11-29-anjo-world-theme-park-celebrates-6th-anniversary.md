Anjo World Theme Park marked its 6th anniversary on November 30, 2025 with a full-day celebration highlighting Filipino culture, music, and entertainment for families and guests. The milestone event brought together performers, visitors, and partners in a lively program that showcased the park’s commitment to delivering world-class family experiences.

One of the key highlights of the celebration was the Anjo Story Performance, a musical and cultural showcase featuring traditional Filipino folk dances, live music, and magical storytelling. The performance paid tribute to beloved cultural pieces, including well-loved folk traditions such as Tinikling and other hometown-inspired dances.

The festivities opened with a Grand Parade led by Anjo and friends, setting an energetic tone for the day. The program continued at the Anjo World Studio Stage with a welcome message, followed by a special performance from The Sisters of Mary Boys Town Handbell Choir, whose musical presentation captivated the audience. Adding to the excitement were the performances of the Anjo Star Search finalists, who showcased their talent before the announcement of the top winners.

During the program, Anjo World also acknowledged its valued sponsors and presented the Belmont Group AVP, highlighting the theme park’s achievements, expansions, and memorable moments over the past six years. The event concluded with the awarding of the Top 3 Anjo Star Search winners, celebrating rising talents within the community.

As Anjo World continues to grow, the park remains dedicated to offering memorable attractions, cultural showcases, and family-friendly experiences for both locals and tourists. The 6th anniversary celebration reflects its ongoing mission to bring joy, creativity, and world-class entertainment to Cebu.

Six years of fun and stories at Anjo World — and many more to come.

📍 Belmont One South Road Upper, Minglanilla, Cebu
