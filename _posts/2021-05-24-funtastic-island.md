#### Funtastic Island   

A place of fun and great adventure. **Funtastic Island** is a place to relax and enjoy the ocean breeze that will surely captivate your hearts. The place is worth experiencing with family and friends. The main highlight of **Funtastic Island** is the cliff jumping that gives you the adrenaline rush and the beautiful cottages on top of the boulders. The view that it offers is really amazing.

##### HOW TO GET THERE?  

🌍 Exact Location: Gibitngil Island, Medellin, Cebu  

The place is located at Gibitngil Island, Medellin, Northern Cebu,about 3-4 hours travel time. From Cebu City ride Ceres going to Medellin or Via Kawit Medellin, then tell the driver to drop-off at  Kawit Medellin. When you arrived their ask some locals or any habal2 drivers, where to ride going to Fantastic Island or Gibitngil island. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
