###### 150 Peakway Mountain Resort  

\#LaagSouth: Experience the fullness of Dome-living in Cebu. Embrace your next staycation at the 150 Peakway Dome!  

Seek the place where you breathe fresher air,  where you're surrounded by endless possibilities of creating nature-inspired memories.   

They promise you serenity and safety at 150 Peakway Mountain Resort. Make a good decision for yourself! Get inside now with these comfy and spacious camping domes with reduced light pollution and eco-friendly light spectrums that will protect your privacy, allow you to read calmly, and let you to stargaze at night as if you were in another world.
