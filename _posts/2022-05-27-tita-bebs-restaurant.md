#####  Tita Beb’s Specialty

Would you like to have some decent food porn or a delicious meal to satisfy your hunger pangs? Why not go somewhere or order something that will give you a healthy, tasty, and satisfying meal?  

Tita Beb's Specialty and Dimsum are here to serve alongside some of your favorite Pinoy dishes! TBS promotes Asian fusion cuisine with distinct flavors derived from Pinoy and Chinese delicacies.  

Tita Bebs Specialty plans to set up food carts across Cebu to cater to the demands of those who enjoy home-cooked meals. It began as an online food company in July 2020, in the midst of Cebu's government's rigorous intensified community quarantine. Their objective is to help all Cebuanos suffering by getting out to buy food and those on the front-line people protecting and serving others.  

Tita Bebs Specialty would love to share the flavor of homemade dishes with those who are far away from home, showing them that it is just a taste away. They share not just incredible food but also the sensation of happiness during the Christmas season, despite the present epidemic, and therefore our battle cry has been activated. The hashtag #parabationkagpasko has been created.  

Nothing surpasses a heartfelt homemade meal precisely. One of the secrets to living a happy and healthier life is eating healthily. Check it all out and learn how to eat to feel well. After trying TBS entrees, you want to grab right more of it!  

Dimsum lover, nakatilaw naka diri? Lami kaayo! Dimsum-time Will Be the New Me-time When You See Tita Bebs Specialty Menu.
