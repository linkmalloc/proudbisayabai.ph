Easter Sunday is the most important celebration in the Christian calendar, marking the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead. It comes after Holy Saturday and concludes the Sacred Triduum, symbolizing victory over sin and death and the fulfillment of God’s promise of salvation.



This day celebrates new life, hope, and renewal, as the resurrection of Christ affirms the foundation of Christian faith. Churches around the world hold joyful services, filled with thanksgiving, music, and the proclamation of “He is risen.”



In the Philippines, Easter Sunday is often welcomed with the Salubong, a traditional pre-dawn ritual reenacting the meeting of the risen Christ and His mother, Mary. It is a moment of joy that contrasts the sorrow of the previous days, as communities come together to celebrate hope and redemption.



As the Lenten season concludes, Easter Sunday reminds the faithful that after suffering comes new life, and after darkness comes light—bringing a message of enduring faith, love, and salvation for all.
