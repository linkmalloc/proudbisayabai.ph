Looking for the perfect spot to unwind, catch up with friends, or simply enjoy a good cup of coffee? &#x20;

Cebu is home to a thriving café culture, offering everything from cozy hideaways to Instagram-worthy spots and specialty coffee havens.&#x20;

Whether you’re a local or a tourist, these must-visit cafés and coffee shops in Cebu promise great brews, delightful pastries, and spaces that capture the city’s vibrant charm. Here are some of our top picks you shouldn't miss!

*Disclaimer: Information are based on the latest available details from each establishment and are subject to change without prior notice.*

A dessert haven specializing in French pastries, cakes, and sweet treats perfect for any craving.

Location: G/F Streetscape Mall, Paseo Saturnino, Banilad, Cebu City&#x20;
