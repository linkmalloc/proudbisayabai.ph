Cebu Normal University (CNU) ended 2025 with a rewarding flourish as 44 of its students from Cebu City, Mandaue, and Talisay City claimed top spots in the Licensure Examination for Teachers (LET)—and walked away with cash prizes totaling millions of pesos. 

Families and loved ones cheered the winners during the awarding ceremony at CNU on Tuesday, December 23.

Of the top 44, 15 excelled at the Elementary Level and 29 at the Secondary Level. CNU President Dr. Daniel Ariaso, Sr. highlighted the school’s record-breaking performance. 

"It is a great honor to have you here for the first time in 123 years, earning 44 top 10. You make us very proud and happy," he said. 

CNU ranked first nationwide for the Secondary Level with a passing rate of ninety-seven point five seven percent and fourth for Elementary Level with ninety-three point five three percent, according to the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC).

Cash incentives were handed out to all topnotchers: first placers received P100,000; second and third placers P70,000; fourth and fifth P50,000; and sixth to tenth P30,000 each.
