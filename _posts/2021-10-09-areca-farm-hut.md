##### Areca Palm Hut 
LOOK: Bali in Northern Cebu? Add on your Bucketlist! Enjoy the rustic vibe at Areca Palm Hut. Just a two-hour drive from Cebu City! Areca Palm Hut is a modern bahay kubo situated in the highlands of Catmon. 

Sometimes, we owe ourselves the relaxation and recreation we need most, especially when we have passed a tedious work week. So don't hesitate and deprive yourself of that because you really deserved that! Visit the Areca Palm Hut, just enjoy and relax.

🌏 Exact Location: Cabungaan, Catmon Cebu 

###### How to get there? 

📍Google Map Link: [https://g.co/kgs/Cf3iiC](https://g.co/kgs/Cf3iiC)

🗺 You can locate via Waze and Google Map Apps. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze/ G Map.  
🗺 Nearest landmark from the highway is Recuerdo Beach Resort. Opposite side there is a road going uphill. It is situated 3km away from the national highway.

###### Amenities: 
- exclusive use of the entire property
- private spring-fed pool
- floating breakfast
- wide garden for picnics
- outdoor shower &amp; dining
-  bonfire &amp; smores
- wide attic room
- fully equipped kitchen
- AND MY FAVORITE: Pet friendly!

###### What to expect:
- ZERO network which is really good for us, for anybody who wants to disconnect and relax. (They offer wifi for a minimal fee) 
- You can bring your own food, no corkage fees. You can cook or you can directly order from their menu. They also provide basic condiments in the kitchen. 
- It's 3km+ away from the main road (the road itself isn't too convenient, it's narrow, lowered vehicles not advised)

****
###### Rates: As of last week of Sept - Oct 2021
- 3,500 for 2 w/o breakfast (promo)
- 4,000 for 2 with floating breakfast
- 5,000 for 4 with breakfast 
- additional 750php per head  

****
###### Note: 
They only accept a maximum of 6 adults and 2 kids per stay.  
Rates may change without prior notice. Kindly contact their page for bookings &amp; other inquiries.
