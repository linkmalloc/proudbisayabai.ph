Cebu has a rich culinary landscape, and if you want to experience one of its most iconic street food offerings, you need to stop by Shomai sa Tisa. This humble food stall has become a local institution, known for its flavorful and affordable meals. For just 50 pesos, you can enjoy a satisfying meal that includes your choice of shomai, a serving of rice, and a refreshing drink. Whether you’re a Cebu local or a first-time visitor, Shomai sa Tisa is a must-try experience that showcases the heart of Cebu’s street food culture.  

The menu at Shomai sa Tisa is simple yet packed with variety. At its core, you’ll find the classic shomai, served hot and juicy, with a choice of two varieties: the original shomai, which is traditional and packed with savory goodness, or the Japanese-style shomai, which adds a unique twist with its slightly lighter flavor and seasoning. Each dumpling is perfectly seasoned and paired with a generous serving of fluffy rice. But that’s not all—if you’re craving more, you can add a side of dynamite, a spicy, deep-fried snack, crispy fried chicken, or ngohiong (a Filipino fried spring roll with a savory filling). These options provide an additional burst of flavors, making your meal even more satisfying.  

The charm of Shomai sa Tisa isn’t just in the food—it’s in the entire experience. Located along Katipunan Street in Tisa, Cebu City, the stall is often buzzing with activity, with locals and visitors alike enjoying their meals together. The casual, no-frills setup adds to the authenticity of the experience, and it’s the perfect place to grab a quick and affordable bite. The 50 pesos price tag is unbeatable, especially when you consider the generous portion sizes and the fact that you’re getting a delicious, well-loved local dish. It’s no wonder Shomai sa Tisa is a go-to spot for anyone craving a quick, flavorful, and budget-friendly meal in Cebu.  

Whether you’re grabbing a quick snack after a busy day or enjoying a leisurely meal with friends, Shomai sa Tisa offers something for everyone. With its rich history, local charm, and unbeatable prices, it’s clear why this street food spot has become a beloved staple in Cebu’s vibrant food scene. Don’t miss out on this iconic Cebuano experience—make sure to swing by and taste the best of Cebu’s street food culture at Shomai sa Tisa!  

Location: Katipunan Street, Tisa, Cebu City  

Price: Starting at 50 pesos for a meal with shomai, rice, and drink  
Must-Try: Original and Japanese-style shomai, dynamite, fried chicken, ngohiong
