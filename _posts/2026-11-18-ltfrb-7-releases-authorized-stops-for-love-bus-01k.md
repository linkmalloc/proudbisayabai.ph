Commuters taking the government's Love Bus Libreng Sakay 01K route should use only designated loading and unloading areas, the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board Central Visayas (LTFRB 7) reminded the public following complaints that some modernized jeepneys allegedly passed waiting passengers without stopping.

In an announcement, the agency published the official list of authorized stops for both directions of the route connecting Urgello Street in Cebu City and Parkmall in Mandaue City.

LTFRB 7 said passengers must board and alight only at these designated areas to help ensure safe, orderly and efficient trips under the free ride program.

For the Urgello-to-Parkmall direction, the authorized stops begin at Urgello Street near Southwestern University Coliseum, followed by San Vicente Ferrer Church, Urgello Strip and Residences, and Southwestern University Medical Building.

The route continues along Leon Kilat Street, immediately after TMS Lodge, as well as Happymart, Mercury Drug Store, M. Lhuillier/Colonade Supermarket and Gaisano Main along Colon Street.

Passengers may also board or alight at Cebu Bolt Center along M.J. Cuenco Avenue, Carreta Elementary School and the A. Soriano Avenue bus stops near G Mall.

Other designated stops on this direction are F. Cabahug Street, immediately after SM City Cebu, SM Hypermarket, M. Logarte Avenue, UC Med and Chong Hua Hospital.

For the Parkmall-to-Urgello direction, the listed stops include Mantawe Drive across Chong Hua Hospital, MO2, East Paris KTV at APM Centrale and Mabolo Fire Station.

The route also covers A. Soriano Avenue across G Mall, Benedicto College and M.J. Cuenco Avenue, followed by Shamrock and Vicente Gullas Street behind Gaisano Main.

The remaining stops are along Legazpi Street behind Colonade Supermarket, Sanciangco Street across UC Main, GSIS, ACT New Building, Southwestern University Medical Building and Urgello Street near Southwestern University Coliseum.

“Ang inyong kooperasyon dakong tabang aron mas maayo ang serbisyo sa Love Bus Libreng Sakay,” the agency said, appealing to commuters to follow the designated loading and unloading areas.

The reminder followed complaints that some modernized jeepneys operating under the 01K route allegedly refused to pick up passengers waiting at authorized stops.

On Oct. 31, LTFRB 7 issued show cause orders against JP and Sons Transport Group Inc. and Aleja Fua Rent a Car Corp. over reports involving their modernized jeepneys.

The agency's action followed a report by The Freeman, which featured video footage of commuters waiting at a designated stop in front of a shopping mall in Barangay Mabolo during the evening rush hour. The footage showed 01K modernized jeepneys allegedly passing the waiting passengers without stopping.

LTFRB 7 said refusing to transport passengers without a justifiable reason may constitute refusal to render public service or refusal to convey passengers under existing regulations and Joint Administrative Order No. 2014-01.

Both transport operators were directed to submit verified written explanations and supporting documents in response to the allegations.

The 01K route connects Urgello Street in Cebu City and Parkmall in Mandaue City, serving commuters traveling between the two locations.

The free ride service operates on weekdays during two periods: from 6 a.m. to 9 a.m. and from 5 p.m. to 8 p.m.

The government launched the Libreng Sakay pilot program along the route on July 23, 2025. The initiative was later branded as the Love Bus and expanded to include a second route connecting Anjo World Theme Park in Minglanilla to SM Seaside City Cebu.
