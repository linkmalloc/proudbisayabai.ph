###### Durhan White Beach Resort: A Pristine Beach Every Traveller Shouldn’t Miss

North Cebu has always been a go-to destination for a fun get away of some travellers. Whether it’s a daytrip or a weeklong travel, there are plenty of beautiful beaches in North Cebu that would make you forget the hustle and bustle of the city life. One of the best beaches in North Cebu can be found in Tabuelan. Even if you are not an island lover, trust us, you cannot surely ignore the beauty of Durhan White Beach Resort. Its turquoise water will entice you to put on your swimsuits and dip right in the water. It is a safe and secure haven, a perfect place for relaxation with great guest service that will give you a seamless and remarkable stay.

###### HOW TO GET THERE?  

Exact Location: Brgy. Tabunok, Tabuelan, Cebu—approximately 2 hours away from the city.

🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu City ride a jeep or Bus going to SM City Cebu where the new Cebu North Bus Terminal situated and ride a bus/v-hire bound for Tabuelan or Victoria. Bus fare is P115-120 (one-way). Ask the driver to drop you off at Durhan White Beach Resort.

🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a van or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
