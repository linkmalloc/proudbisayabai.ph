The Holiday Inn Cebu City celebrated its 3rd anniversary with a night of Filipino creativity and cultural pride in **"SAULOG: An Anniversary Fashion Show”** which took place last June 11, 2025. The fashion show attracted a crowd of fashionistas, artists, and VIP guests for a night to remember.

It started with cocktails, with the guests chatting over a carefully curated assortment of dishes influenced by Filipino cuisine, with every bite being a humble bow to local flavors with a contemporary twist. The lively ambiance created the mood for the rest of the night: elegance blended with cultural respect.

As guests settled, the Philippine National Anthem was played, filling the room with a sense of solidarity and nationalistic pride. This was followed by a warm and motivational welcome from Holiday Inn Cebu City's General Manager, Gibeth Gloria, who thanked attendees and partners for being part of the hotel's ongoing success.

The fashion show officially began with a stunning collection of pieces by Classique by AudieAE and Nardie Presa. The show was directed by the esteemed Audie Espino, whose creative guidance brought every collection to life with poise and purpose. From contemporary takes on classic silhouettes to strong, statement pieces rooted in Filipino heritage, every piece had a story of culture and innovation.

At the end of the show, the crowd was in awe, moved by the artistry and cultural tribute showcased on the runway. The event was not just a fashion show but a movement that celebrated the creativity, identity, and resilient spirit of the Filipinos.

As Holiday Inn Cebu City expands, it is events like **SAULOG** that reflect its dedication to celebrating local talent, culture, and community in style .
