###### 10,000 ROSES IS BACK!

Nindot jod diri labi nag gabii. One of the great tourist spot in cebu is now back! Walk and enjoy the view of 10,000 Roses while you indulge the fresh air of Cordova, Cebu!

These artificial roses are made out of synthetic and plastic petals that are powered by LED light. When it is powered on at night, it illuminates and glows making the place look magical. A night out with your lover, friends, and family would be worth it. Walking and roaming around the place gives you a surreal feeling. 

The café is located on the roof deck. The view from above allows you to take a moment, and appreciate the mesmerizing ambiance. It is alfresco dining that offers a variety of appetizers to choose from like pasta, sandwiches, pastries, etc. They also serve coffee and other drinks. 

Looking for a place to chill and unwind while enjoying the night view? This place would be one on the list.

Exact Location: Day-as Barangay Rd, Cordova, 6017 Cebu

Operating Hours: 2PM - 10PM daily

Via Commute: From Sm Cebu, Ride a V-hire going to Cordova. Then tell the driver to drop you off at Cordova Grandmall. Once you arrive, you can ask some locals about the 10,000 Roses for guidance.  
Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze / GMap. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze
