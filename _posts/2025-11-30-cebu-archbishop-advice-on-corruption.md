“𝐋𝐢𝐧𝐠𝐢𝐚 𝐬𝐚 𝐢𝐧𝐲𝐨𝐧𝐠 𝐭𝐚𝐩𝐚𝐝. 𝐔𝐧𝐲𝐚 𝐢𝐧𝐠𝐧𝐚 𝐧𝐠𝐚 𝐤𝐮𝐧𝐠 𝐰𝐚𝐥𝐚 𝐩𝐚𝐲 𝐤𝐨𝐫𝐚𝐩𝐬𝐲𝐨𝐧, 𝐫𝐞𝐥𝐚𝐱-𝐫𝐞𝐥𝐚𝐱 𝐫𝐚 𝐮𝐧𝐭𝐚 𝐭𝐚 𝐤𝐚𝐫𝐨𝐧.”

(“Look at the person beside you. Then tell them that if there were no corruption, we could all be relaxed right now.”)

— Cebu Archbishop Alberto Uy

Archbishop Uy led the program for SuPaKK: Sugboanong Pakigbisog Kontra Korapsyon on Sunday night at Fuente Osmeña Circle, Cebu City, Nov. 30. He also joined the protest march attended by thousands of Cebuanos, who walked from the Basilica Minore del Sto. Niño de Cebu in a united call for accountability and systemic reform.

During the program, participants also signed the Unified People’s Declaration on the Corruption and Flood-Control / Infrastructure Crisis in the Philippines a common analysis and commitment for integrity, justice, and ecological renewal.
