Three barangays in Mandaue City are set to receive an additional 2,000 cubic meters of water daily following the commissioning of a new Metropolitan Cebu Water District (MCWD) tapping point in Barangay Umapad.

The facility along Batillier Road is expected to benefit around 2,000 households in Umapad, Opao and Ibabao-Estancia, particularly residents who have experienced weak water pressure and insufficient supply.

MCWD formally opened the new connection on Nov. 9 as part of efforts to improve water distribution ahead of the anticipated effects of Super El Niño.

MCWD General Manager Atty. John Dx Lapid said the tapping point would provide a more direct route for water to reach the three communities, reducing the need for supplies to pass through distant areas.

“Sauna, mutuyok pa ang supply nato gikan didto sa layo-layo gyud nga lugar, but right now, direkta na ang supply nila,” Lapid said.

He said the connection serves as a temporary intervention while MCWD prepares to renew pipelines in the area to further improve water delivery.

The project also forms part of the water district's efforts to address supply concerns in other Mandaue communities following discussions with Mayor Thadeo “Jonkie” Ouano.

Ouano said the additional volume would help more households access water as the city prepares for possible supply pressures during the expected dry conditions.

“Everyone deserves clean and sufficient nga tubig,” the mayor said, thanking MCWD for the project.

He also pledged the city's continued support for the water district's infrastructure projects and other efforts to improve services in communities.

“Mag tinabangay lang jud ta,” Ouano said, underscoring the need for cooperation between the city government and MCWD.

Barangay captains Reb “Biboy” Cortes of Umapad and Nixon “Jojo” Diaz of Opao joined city and MCWD officials during the opening.

The anticipated El Niño, which could bring prolonged dry conditions and put pressure on available water resources, has prompted local governments and water providers to strengthen their preparations.
