Cebu City, Philippines — A foul-smelling and visibly unsanitary footbridge along Osmeña Boulevard, near the Social Security System office, has drawn concern from commuters and pedestrians after reports surfaced on March 27.



People passing through the area are often forced to cover their noses due to the strong odor, with many choosing to avoid the footbridge altogether and instead cross the busy road below. This situation raises safety concerns, as pedestrians risk accidents by bypassing the designated crossing structure.



Observations in the area indicate the presence of accumulated human waste and garbage, pointing to a persistent sanitation issue that has yet to be properly addressed. The condition of the footbridge not only affects public comfort but also highlights broader challenges in maintaining urban cleanliness and hygiene in high-traffic areas.



Footbridges are intended to provide safe passage for pedestrians, especially along major thoroughfares like Osmeña Boulevard. However, when such facilities become neglected, they can discourage proper usage and compromise public safety.



Local authorities are expected to assess the situation and implement measures to restore cleanliness and ensure that the structure remains safe and usable for the public.
