###### Kalanggaman Island  

\#BUCKETLISTCHECK! Andam na ba Tanan mo LAAG padung Leyte? 

Experience the pure white sand and crystal clear waters of Kalanggaman Island!

Kalanggaman Island, Derived from the word “langgam,” which translates to “bird” in the Cebuano dialect, Kalanggaman Island got its name because of its peculiar shape. When viewed from above, the island looks much like a bird in flight. Apart from its unique shape, the island has been slowly turning into a beach destination thanks to its turquoise blue waters and long sandbars from both sides of the island.

###### HOW TO GET THERE?  

🌎 Kalanggaman Island, Palompon Leyte  

✈️ Via Plane: Flying to Ormoc City is the fastest and the most comfortable way to get to Kalanggaman Island, but You can also fly to Tacloban and take a 4- to 5-hour van ride to the town of Palompon, Leyte.  

🛳 Via Cebu by boat. You may also travel by sea to Palompon from Cebu.
