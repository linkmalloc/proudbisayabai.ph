#### Zip 'N Sip
  
TRENDING: Looking for something unique? TILAWI Bai! The BEST fruit shake with a TWIST😱 🤤  
  
Legit jud! smoothie w/ Ice Cream &amp; Pearl at Zip 'N Sip in Sugbo Mercado IT Park, Cebu!  
  
🕑 Operating hours: 3PM - 11PM    
  
🌎 Exact Location: Garden Bloc, l. Villa St, Apas Cebu City IT Park, Cebu   
  
📌 How to get there?  
  
🗺 Via Commute: Ride a bus / Beep going to Itpark Cebu, then drop-off at IT Park Bus Terminal. (Unahan ra diha gamay sa right side. Just walk few steps and you will find the exact location. Fare is 20-30 pesos.   
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
