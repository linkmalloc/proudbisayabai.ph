##### El Mar Resort  
 
\#LaagAmihanan: Want a quiet and chill place for your next vacation? EL MAR Resrot and Spa in Sogod, Cebu is PERFECT for you! 💦🌊🏝
Experience the pure white sands, Deep turquoise seas. Endless blue skies. Your luxe beach getaway in Sogod, Cebu! 

🌏 Exact Location: Calumboyan, Sogod, Cebu 🇵🇭

###### How to get there?

Google Map Link: Q2XM+F35, Borbon, Cebu(032) 266 1608 [https://g.co/kgs/LdPxKG](https://g.co/kgs/LdPxKG)
