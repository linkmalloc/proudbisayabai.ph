Gazini Ganados is a Filipino-Palestinian beauty queen, model, and public figure best known for winning the title of Binibining Pilipinas 2019 and representing the Philippines at the prestigious Miss Universe 2019 held in Atlanta, Georgia, USA.

Born on December 26, 1995, in Dapitan City, Zamboanga del Norte, Gazini later grew up in Cebu where she became widely admired not only for her beauty but also for her humility, intelligence, and strong connection to her Bisaya roots. Her rise to fame inspired many young Filipinas, especially aspiring beauty queens from the Visayas and Mindanao.

Before entering the world of pageantry, Gazini pursued Architecture studies at the University of San Jose–Recoletos in Cebu City. She first joined local pageants before eventually making her breakthrough on the national stage. Her elegance, confidence, and powerful stage presence helped her stand out during the Binibining Pilipinas competition, where she earned the opportunity to wear the Philippine sash on the global Miss Universe stage.

During her reign, Gazini became known for promoting causes related to elderly care and women empowerment. She often emphasized the importance of compassion, family values, and using one’s platform to inspire others. Her warm personality and genuine interactions with supporters also made her one of the most beloved titleholders of her generation.

This 2026, Gazini Ganados once again captured attention after joining the Miss Grand International All Stars 2026 alongside fellow Filipina queens. Her strong stage presence immediately stood out and wowed pageant fans around the world, especially during the introduction segment where she confidently carried herself with elegance and power. Many viewers were also captivated by her stunning gold colored swimsuit performance, which highlighted her beauty, confidence, and undeniable queenly aura on stage.

As a proud Cebuana, Gazini continues to bring pride to the Bisaya community. Many fans admire her for remaining grounded despite her success in the entertainment and fashion industries. Beyond pageantry, she has also ventured into hosting, modeling, brand endorsements, and television appearances.

Today, Gazini Ganados remains an influential figure in Philippine pop culture and beauty pageantry, symbolizing grace, resilience, and the modern Filipina spirit that shines on both local and international stages.
