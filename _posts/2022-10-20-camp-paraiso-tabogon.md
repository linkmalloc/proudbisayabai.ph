###### CAMP Paradisio Tabogon  

Looking for a place to escape and unwind? A Must-Visit glamping haven in Northern Cebu CAMP Paradisio Tabogon, Cebu!  

Imagine going to a place where you can unwind and recharge after an exhausting work week. Grabeh ingon ani nga view kuyog imong favorite person and talking about life.   

At first glance, Tabogon might be relatively unknown as a tourism hotspot in Cebu. In fact, some Cebuanos do not even know that such town.   

Tabogon, officially the Municipality of Tabogon, is a 3rd class municipality in the province of Cebu, Philippines. According to the 2020 census, it has a population of 41,432 people.   

Exact Exaction: Sitio Pasil, Camoboan, Tabogon, Cebu (On the way to Salag Hanging Bridge)

###### How to get there? 

Via Commute: From Cebu City, Take a yellow bus going to Tabogon Poblacion fare is P150 person / per way. From Tabogon terminal ride a habal - habal going tp Camp Paradisio about 10mins. travel P70/person. 

The place can be navigated on Waze / Google Map. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate waze pin the location "Camp Paradisio" on Google Maps or Waze.
