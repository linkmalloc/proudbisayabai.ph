In the Serenity Farm and Resort complex, just approximately 30 minutes from the city, lies Liel's Kitchen, a restaurant serving contemporary Japanese food. They offer you the most in-depth exploration of Japanese culture and daring food trips with a panoramic view of mountain ranges as one of the things to overcome the emotional-mental condition of melancholy, feeling smothered, and living in a bizarre sanctuary.  

Make your stomach churn, showcasing magnificent meals and intricate recipes from a nation with a long history of culinary customs. Classic sushi is available, as well as fusion variations on Japanese delicacies like ramen, among other mouthwatering options that will have your heart whimpering in anticipation!

Liel’s Kitchen also provides smoothie bowls and Japanese drinks like Strong Zero by Suntory, in addition to their delectable interpretation of modern cuisine.  
Grabe mga bai, indeed it is something that you must try!  

Exact Location: Sitio Tiguib Brgy. Malubog Busay, Cebu City. PH

###### How to get there?  
Via Commute:  
From Metro Colon, ride any Jeepneys bound to IT Park or JY Square Mall. Then for a habal-habal around the Mall and tell the driver to drop you off at Serenity Farm and Resort. It's 45 mins straight drive from JY Square. Estimated fare is P150-200 ( need good negotiation) one way and can accommodate 2pax per habal-habal.   

Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze or Google Map. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze or Google Map: Sitio Tiguib, Brgy., Cebu City, Cebu 0917 726 0775 [https://g.co/kgs/6fC6Gf](https://g.co/kgs/6fC6Gf)  

NOTE: Liel’s Kitchen accepts events and parties exclusive or non exclusive.
