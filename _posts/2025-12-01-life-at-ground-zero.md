Nearly a month after Typhoon Tino, this is Tamiao 2, Compostela’s Ground Zero, photographed on Nov. 30. Residents continue to ask for support with essentials such as cooking pans, pots, laundry soap, and even underwear — simple items that restore comfort and dignity.

Compostela recorded the province’s highest death toll at 37, with 24 people still reported missing, the PDRRMC said on Nov. 24, 2025.
