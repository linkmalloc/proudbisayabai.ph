###### Enchanted Mountain Cebu  

Drive Your Way to Wonderland: An Escape to Enchanted Mountain Cebu

Do you want to have a magical morning walk in the woods? Oops! Look, and you might fall. Not like what happened to Alice in Wonderland but to the beauty of the view and discover various types of enchanted rooms available for you!

No need to be concerned, fella! If you want to feel like a princess in a palace or a Disney character you admire. Well, you will find a mountain that even, without a spell, the whole place will justify you to remain there. It is a three-hour journey away from the city. Surely, fairytales can come true in the Dalaguete municipality. Believe, and you will see At Enchanted Mountain Cebu.

If you are hungry after a long stroll on the trails and breathing in the fresh air of nature, or when you have just swum in a splendid pool? Abracadabra! You can eat at any of the benches around the lawn, and they have food stalls.

Just like Cinderella, Enchanted Mountain offers different kinds of rooms. Even without a speck of pixie dust, you can enchantingly choose a perfect castle for yourself. Explore with several sorts of lights and ambiances, which all follow a walking trail that takes you through the park, past the castle, and then back to a very magical setting. Be an enchanted spirit’s friend, share nature’s promises, and walk throughout the place peacefully!


Experience Breathtaking views and take your mind to places where you see yourself being whoever you want to be at ENCHANTED MOUNTAIN CEBU!

###### HOW TO GET THERE?

🌏 Exact Location: Lumbang, Dalaguete, Cebu - approx. 3hours away from the City  


Via Commute: Take a bus bound for Dalaguete, Cebu. Then, ask the conductor or bus driver to drop you off at Dalaguete Proper. Fare is around P120/head. Look for a motorbike driver who will take you to the Enchanted Mountain Resort in Brgy. Lumbang, Dalaguete.   

Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
