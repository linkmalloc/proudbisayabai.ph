Nestled in the highlands of Samboan, Cebu, Lantaw de Samboan offers visitors a peaceful retreat surrounded by breathtaking natural scenery. This emerging destination has become a favored spot for those seeking relaxation away from the busy city life, where fresh mountain air, lush greenery, and panoramic views take center stage.



Located in Upper Basak, Samboan, Cebu, the site is open daily from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. With an affordable entrance fee of only ₱40, guests are given access to a refreshing escape that showcases the beauty of the province’s countryside.



How to Get There



From the South Bus Terminal in Cebu City, travelers may take a bus bound for Samboan with an estimated fare of ₱150–₱200. Passengers are advised to alight at Samboan Municipal Hall or Boluntaryo. From there, a habal-habal ride going to Lantaw de Samboan is available, typically costing around ₱100. For convenience, navigation apps such as Waze may also be used for easier travel guidance.



Lantaw de Samboan continues to attract both local and visiting travelers who wish to experience a calm and scenic environment, making it a noteworthy addition to Cebu’s growing list of eco-tourism destinations.
