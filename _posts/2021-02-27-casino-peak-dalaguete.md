#### Casino Peak, Dalaguete
  
Katkat goals with Barkadas SOON at Casino Peak, Dalaguete, Cebu and experience Sunrise 1013 meters above sea level🏔🏕  
  
##### 📌 What to Enjoy?   
- Trekking  
- Camping   
- Hiking   
- Photoshoot   
- etc.  
  
##### 📌 Fees:  
- Entrance fee: P50 per head  
- Camping fee: P50 per head (bring your own tent)  
- Guide fee: P200-300 per group (optional)  
( Note: price as of last year )
