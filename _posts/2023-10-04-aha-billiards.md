A.H.A Billiards and Restobar offers a variety of billiards tables, as well as a full bar and restaurant. The menu features a wide variety of dishes, from Filipino classics to international favorites. There is something for everyone to enjoy at A.H.A Billiards and Restobar.  

Location: L/1 Ayala Malls Central Bloc, Padriga St., IT Park , Cebu ( beside SEDA HOTEL )  

Operating hours: 10 AM to 2 AM  

Contact: 09569978380/09614173036 or follow them on Facebook at @aha.billiard.restobar   

###### Promo Rates:  

1 Hour = Php 500 (Inclusions: 300 per hour + 200 consumable food/beer)  
2 Hours = Php 1000 (Inclusions: 600 per 2 hours + 400 consumable food/beer)  
3 Hours = Php 1400 (Inclusions: 900 per 3 hours + 500 consumable food/beer)  
4 Hours = Php 1800 (Inclusions: 1200 per 4 hours + 600 consumable food/beer)  

The longer you play, the more discounts!  

Whether you are a seasoned billiards player or just starting out, A.H.A Billiards and Restobar is the perfect place to have fun and relax. With its friendly staff, great atmosphere, and affordable prices, A.H.A Billiards and Restobar is sure to become your new favorite spot to play billiards.
