##### Coal Mountain Resort
  
Looking for a place to Chill, kanang mawala imong stress sa life? Murag diri ang Tubag!🏝💦🌄  
  
Bisita na diri mga Bai!! Swimming Pool nga gipalibutan ug bukid, OMG! maka 😍  
  
🌍 Exact Location: Linut-od, Argao, Cebu 🇵🇭  
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: Gikan Cebu South Bus Terminal, sakay ka ug Bus bound for south, bisan unsa nga bus basta moagi sa Argao, look for the sign. Then tell the driver to drop you at the bus stop, fare is P147. Then sakay ka ug tricycle and tell the driver pahunong sa parkingan pasaka sa bukid (exact location: infront of Argao Complex). Then pangita ug habal-habal driver, and tell the driver to drop you at Coal Mountain Resort (Brgy. Linut-od), fare is P150 if duha ang sakay sa habal-habal, 200 if usa lang.   
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Google Maps. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Google Maps.  
  
📌 Entrance Fee: P150   
  
💻 Facebook Page: https://www.facebook.com/CoalMountainResort16/   
  
♻️🗑LEAVE NO TRACE! (Gasa sa kina-iyahan nga angay ampingan, Matag usa kanato makatabang sa pag amping sa atong kalikopan sama sa pag punit sa basura ug paglabay niini sa saktong labayanan)
