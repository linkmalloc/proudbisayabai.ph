###### The Rustic Food Corner  
  
#KaonsaToledo: Enjoy the Best of Cebu Unli Wings for only Php 249 and fall in love at first bite! 🤤  
  
Rustic Food Corner in Toledo is their second branch and they are known for their big serving and flavorful unlimited wings &amp; rice for only PHP 249! They have four variants of flavors too!

###### HOW TO GET THERE?  

🕓Operating hours: 11 AM - 9 PM Daily  
  
🌍 Located at Maxi - J bldg., Dumlog, Toledo City  
  
🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu SouthBus terminal ride a bus going to Pinamungaja or Toledo Cebu. Then tell the kundoktor to drop you off at eskina Sangi, From there ride a tricycle going to the location. It is also a walking distance from Grandmall (Fare is 10.00 ) You can ask some locals and street vendors along the way.   
   
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze or Google maps.
