Fast-food giant Jollibee has introduced a new Super Meal that combines some of its most popular menu items into one affordable and satisfying bundle, offering customers more value for their money.

The Super Meal includes the brand’s iconic Chickenjoy, a serving of half Jolly Spaghetti, steamed rice, a refreshing drink, and a Yumburger—all for only ₱185. This all-in-one combo is designed for customers who want a complete and filling meal without the need to order items separately.

Known for its signature crispy and juicy fried chicken, sweet-style spaghetti, and classic burgers, Jollibee continues to expand its offerings to meet the growing demand for budget-friendly yet hearty meals. The new Super Meal caters especially to students, workers, and families looking for convenient dining options that don’t compromise on taste and quality.

The release of this value meal comes as part of the company’s ongoing efforts to provide sulit deals while maintaining the flavors that Filipinos have come to love. With rising food costs, bundled meals like this offer a practical option for consumers seeking both variety and affordability in one order.

The Super Meal is now available in participating Jollibee branches nationwide for dine-in, takeout, and delivery.
