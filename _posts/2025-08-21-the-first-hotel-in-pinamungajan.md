Experience a new level of comfort and elegance at PERC Hotel, the first-ever hotel in Pinamungajan, setting the standard for luxury and convenience in the heart of the town. Whether you’re here for business, leisure, or a quick escape, PERC Hotel offers the perfect stay for every kind of traveler.

*Room Categories*

* Double Deluxe
* Family Room
* Barkada Room
* Triple & Quadruple Rooms
* All rooms are well-appointed and include complimentary breakfast. Book now via Agoda or the ⁦Booking.com⁩ app for seamless reservations.

*Hotel Amenities*

* Glass Elevator with panoramic views
* Rooftop Bar – unwind with a scenic view
* In-house Convenience Store
* Restaurant serving local and international cuisine
* Conference Rooms for business and events
* 25 Luxury Rooms
* Rooftop Pool – Coming Soon!

*Dining Hours*

Ala Carte Restaurant: 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM

*Hotel Hours:* 

Check-in: 2:00 PM

Check-out: 12:00 Noon

*How to get there?* 

From Cebu City, head south via Naga-Uling Road or Toledo City route. Once in Pinamungajan proper, PERC Hotel is easily accessible along the town’s main road. You can reach the hotel by private car, bus, or V-hire from Cebu South Bus Terminal. It’s approximately a 2.5 to 3-hour drive, offering scenic countryside and coastal views along the way.

**Whether you’re a local exploring a staycation or a traveler discovering Pinamungajan for the first time, PERC Hotel offers a premium experience you won’t forget.**

Come stay where comfort meets class only at PERC Hotel.
