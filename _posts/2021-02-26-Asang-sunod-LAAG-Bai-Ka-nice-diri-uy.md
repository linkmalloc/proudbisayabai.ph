Asang sunod LAAG,Bai? Ka nice diri uy! Do you want to escape from the stress that the city provides? Let’s try something new and exciting! Climb and experience the breathtaking view at Little Tagaytay of Cebu! 

Location: Tagaytay, Pulangbato, Cebu City 

How to get there?

Via Commute: From Cebu City ride a Ceres bus or Beep bus bound for Talamban or Pit-os. Hire a motorcycle(habal-habal) to Sitio Burgos, Pulangbato. Then start trekking. Estimated trekking time is 30minutes to 1 hour. 

The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.

Note: This place is privately-owned, but as of now, it is open to public who likes to enjoy some exciting activities. Please be a responsible traveller and leave no trace. 

What to enjoy?
- Trekking
- Camping
- Photoshoot
- Picnic
- many more 

Entrance Fee: None
