###### Why Choose CREST Senior High School?  

****
###### HIGH-Quality Education:
- Comprehensive academic program: Choose from academic and maritime strands aligned with your interests.
- Dedicated teachers & small class sizes: Get personalized attention & support to ensure your success.

****
###### FREE Tuition & Fees:
- CREST is committed to making education accessible. Enjoy free tuition and fees for all students.

****
###### PROVEN Track Record
- CREST students excel! We boast achievements in both local and international competitions.
- "From Education to Employment" program: Get career guidance and job placement assistance after graduation.

****
###### WIDE Network of Partnered Companies
- Network with leading maritime companies through our extensive partnerships.
- Chart your career path, whether you choose immediate work or further studies.

****
###### EXCLUSIVE Benefits:
- ₱500 cash gift to jumpstart your academic journey.
- FREE uniform for a hassle-free school experience.
- Air-conditioned classrooms for comfortable learning.
- World-class deck and engine simulators for hands-on training.
- Olympic-size pool and beach facility for trainings and recreation.
