Carbon Market vendors, workers, consumers and other concerned Cebuanos marched from Carbon Market to Cebu City Hall on Tuesday, August 25, in a mobilization led by the **Save Carbon Public Market Movement (SCPMM)**.

  

The group called for the **termination or rescission of the Carbon Market Joint Venture Agreement (JVA)** between the Cebu City Government and Megawide/Cebu2World, citing concerns raised in the **Commission on Audit’s 2025 Annual Audit Report**.

  

SCPMM clarified that its campaign is **not against modernization**, but seeks to ensure that Carbon remains a public market, vendors’ livelihoods are protected, and public assets are used with accountability.
