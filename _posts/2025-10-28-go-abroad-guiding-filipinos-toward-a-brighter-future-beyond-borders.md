Every big move begins with the decision of taking the leap.



Whether it’s for education, work, or a fresh start abroad, navigating visa requirements can be daunting. That’s where GO Abroad Visa Consultancy steps in: guiding Filipinos with expertise, transparency, and heart.



Founded and led by Canadian CEO Sylvain Brousseau, GO Abroad has built a strong reputation for reliable and people-first visa assistance. With a Philippines-based team of experienced professionals, the consultancy offers personalized support for those pursuing opportunities in Canada, Australia, New Zealand, and other destinations.



"What we do is more than visa work," says Brousseau. "We help Filipinos prepare for success abroad — with the right mindset, documents, and direction. Because when they succeed, we succeed too."



At GO Abroad, every client’s journey is personal. Applications are handled with care and integrity, ensuring that each step, from consultation to approval, is smooth and stress-free. This people-centered approach, combined with international expertise, has made GO Abroad a trusted partner for thousands of Filipinos ready to start a new chapter overseas.



Beyond processing papers, the company’s mission is to empower Filipinos to dream boldly and live confidently abroad. With honesty, guidance, and genuine care at its core, GO Abroad continues to open doors — one visa, one success story at a time.
