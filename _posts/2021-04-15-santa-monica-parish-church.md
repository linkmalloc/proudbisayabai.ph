#### Santa Monica Parish Church
  
<center>Captivating Sta. Monica Parish Church in Pinamungajan, Cebu! 💒😯</center>  
  
Garbo sa mga Pinamungajanons!  
 
  
Santa Monica Parish Church. It used to be under Barili, until in 1850 when the area under the Sta. Monica Church was declared parish.  
  
Standing tall over a century as a symbol of the Pinamungajanons’ deep faith in God, the parish was first served by a certain Fr. Feliciano Fuentes.  
  
The church, made of coral stones during the Spanish period, houses the image of Santa Monica and four church bells with Spanish inscriptions.
