Don Macchiatos Philippines has officially introduced a bold new addition to its menu, the Iced Barako, launched nationwide on November 21. The newest flavor brings the iconic strength of Barako coffee into a refreshing iced drink, crafted for those who crave a caffeine kick without the heaviness of a hot brew.



Known for its distinct aroma and strong character, Barako coffee has long been a Filipino favorite. Don Macchiatos’ latest creation transforms this classic into a chilled blend designed to satisfy both long-time coffee lovers and curious first-timers.



Adding to the excitement, the Iced Barako also debuts with Don Macchiatos’ new strawless lid, an eco-friendly upgrade that makes sipping smoother while reducing plastic waste.



Just in time for the holiday season, the Iced Barako delivers a smooth yet powerful flavor profile that stands out from the usual iced coffee options. Whether you’re on the go or simply looking for a budget-friendly pick-me-up, this new drink fits the bill.



The Iced Barako is now available in all Don Macchiatos branches nationwide for ₱39.



A bold new flavor for a bold season. Grab yours today!
