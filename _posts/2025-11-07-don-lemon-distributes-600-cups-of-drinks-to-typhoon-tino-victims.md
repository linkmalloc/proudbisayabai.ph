Don Lemon, a brand under Don Macchiatos Franchising Corp., distributed a total of 600 cups of free drinks to Typhoon Tino victims on November 6 and 7 at Mohon Elementary School and Barangay Bacayan Gymnasium, both serving as temporary shelters in Cebu, as part of its relief initiative.

Through this initiative, Don Lemon aims to bring relief amid the challenges brought by the extreme heat that followed the aftermath of Typhoon Tino in Cebu. Moreover, this effort reflects the company’s commitment to giving back to the community that helped shape its growth and to fostering a sense of solidarity amid the series of calamities that have affected Cebu since the recent earthquake.
