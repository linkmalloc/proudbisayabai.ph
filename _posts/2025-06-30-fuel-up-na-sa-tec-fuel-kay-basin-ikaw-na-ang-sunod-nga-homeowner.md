Pwede sad ka maka daug ani na minor prizes:

• 2 Yamaha Mio 125 Motorcycles

Paspas ang serbisyo, sakto ang presyo, limpyo ang stations og CRs, nya naa pa’y VIP discounts og rebates. SPEEDY, SAKTO, SULIT — TEC Fuel jud ang best bai!

Promo runs from May 2, 2025 to April 18, 2026.

**Mechanics:**

1. The raffle is exclusively open to TEC Fuel VIP Card holders
2. The raffle runs from May 2,2025 to April, 18 2026
3. 1 raffle entry is equivalent to 10 VIP points
4. 1 VIP point is equivalent to 4 liters purchase of any TEC Fuel fuel product purchased between May 2,2025 to April 18, 2026
5. All points incurred during the promo period (whether spent or saved) can be converted into raffle entries
6. VIP Card Points must be converted into physical stubs and filled up with complete details in order to be valid
7. You can claim and drop your physical stubs at any TEC Fuel station of your choice from April 18, 2026 to April 26, 2026.
8. VIP Card points will no longer be converted into physical stubs after April 26, 2026
9. All physical stubs must be dropped in the lottery drum at your TEC Fuel station of choice on or before April 26, 2026 to enter the raffle
10. Raffle draw results will be posted and livestreamed on all TEC Fuel Facebook accounts on May 2, 2026
