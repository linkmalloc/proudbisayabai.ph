##### NAKA-STARBUKS NA BA ANG TANAN?  

The mountain is calling, mga bai! Approximately just a 45-minute hike from Paseo Arcenas in Banawa, the famous Starbuks trail offers hikers a spectacular view of the sunrise over the city of Cebu.

 The trail is a popular choice for both seasoned hikers and beginners looking for a quick and rewarding escape into nature. Along the way, you’ll encounter lush greenery and peaceful surroundings, making the journey as enjoyable as the destination. Upon reaching the campsite, the view of the sunrise casting its golden glow over Cebu is truly breathtaking and well worth the early wake-up call.

While the phone signal and data reception are strong and stable, it's important to note that there is no electricity at the campsite. Make sure to charge your devices fully before setting out and bring along power banks to keep your gadgets running. To ensure a hassle-free and enjoyable experience, I highly recommend ordering food from the local area. There are several vendors and small eateries that provide delicious meals, perfect for refueling after the hike. This adventure is a must-try with your friends, offering a unique combination of natural beauty, convenience, and a touch of adventure.

###### IMPORTANTE NGA PAHINUMDOM:
Please take responsibility for your trash, Laagan. It’s crucial to adhere to the #LeaveNoTrace principles to preserve the natural beauty of this trail. Dispose of your waste properly and respect the environment to ensure that future hikers can enjoy the same pristine experience. Keeping our natural sites clean and undisturbed is essential for maintaining their beauty and ecological balance. Enjoy the adventure and do your part to protect and preserve nature!
