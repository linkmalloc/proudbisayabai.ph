While air quality in Cebu City remains in the unhealthy range, the northernmost coastal town of Santa Fe offers a refreshing contrast—clean sea breeze, open skies, and pristine shorelines.



Captured this afternoon, April 20, 2026, these aerial views highlight the natural beauty of Bantayan Island, long known as one of Cebu’s top island getaways. With its powdery white sand beaches, calm turquoise waters, and laid-back island life, Santa Fe continues to attract both local and international travelers seeking rest and fresh air away from the city.



A Glimpse of Santa Fe, Bantayan Island

Santa Fe sits at the northern tip of Bantayan Island and serves as the main gateway for visitors arriving from mainland Cebu. Over the years, it has grown into a vibrant yet peaceful destination, balancing tourism with its natural charm. From beach resorts and sandbars to seafood dining and bike-friendly roads, the town offers a relaxing coastal experience that highlights the best of northern Cebu living.



As Metro Cebu deals with haze and rising pollution levels, destinations like Santa Fe remind us of the importance of preserving clean environments and supporting sustainable tourism.
