Most would argue that breakfast is the most important meal of the day. One would opt for a quick bite and some would want to indulge in a full feast.

  

At Marco Polo Plaza Cebu, your first meal of the day is beyond elevated with its diverse spread of breakfast selection. With Café Marco currently undergoing renovations, the hotel’s breakfast buffet is relocated to Hai Shin Lou restaurant. 

  

With good food, a warm ambiance, and top-tier service, Marco Polo Plaza’s signature breakfast buffet offers a delightful culinary journey to start the day. Featuring a diverse spread of Filipino and international breakfast favorites, guests can indulge in freshly baked pastries, made-to-order eggs, seasonal fruits, cereals, and wholesome specialties, all expertly crafted by the hotel’s talented in-house chefs.

Bring your family and experience the breakfast buffet with Marco Polo’s 2+1 Rise and Dine promo for direct reservations, with two guests paying for P1,088 each and the third guest dines for free!

  

You may also avail an online promo for P988 per person through Marco Polo Plaza Cebu Online Store, and experience the buffet’s top-notch menu.

These breakfast promos are available starting July 1, 2026, until the completion of Cafe Marco’s renovation.

  

Unsa pa’y imong gihuwat, bai? Don’t miss out on these exceptional breakfast promos and start your day with a scrumptious breakfast!
