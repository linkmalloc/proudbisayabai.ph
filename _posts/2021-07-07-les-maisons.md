##### Les Maisons d'Itac
A splendid little corner of paradise with luxurious houses, sea view, and with swimming pool and wifi in the Island of Cebu.

Les Maisons d'Itac is located in a fishing town in a secluded section of Cebu Island, and its quiet ambiance provides the ideal chance to unwind and immerse in magnificent sunsets. It's a friendly and intimate setting where you may rest in luxury with a panoramic view of the sea and its gardens. Two hours' drive from Cebu City, on Cebu Island's west coast, Philippines. Your journey to Les Maisons d'Itac will be one of peace and leisure.

##### HOW TO GET THERE?  

🌍 Exact Location: Patricks Road, Brgy. Santa Cruz Ronda 🇵🇭  

🗺 Via Commute / Private Vehicle: If your getting a bus you'll need to go to the south bus terminal direction Moalboal. If you're by car you can use google maps just search for Les Maisons D'Itac. Just want to inform you that they have a no entry no reservation policy for their guest privacy. If you want to come and have a occular visit please let them know before so they tell you if it's possible or not.

##### Amenities: 
🛌 SUITES - Two rooms, air-conditioned, sea view, king size bed (2x2m), bathrooms and separated toilet. One kid room, air-conditoned, two single beds, bathroom and toilet. Big terrace, dining room, private swimming pool, sea view, safety box, minibar, wifi. (For COVID time a small cooking area has been place)

🛌 NEPTUNE FOR 4 - 2 bedrooms, king size bed (2x2meter) with mosquito net and mattress raised for the best ocean view. Traditional nippa ceiling room, with dressing space, ceiling fan,  fridge and water dispenser. Bathroom with Italian shower and 2 lavabos. Water closet separate from the bathroom with ocean view, common swimming pool

🛌 STUDIO - Air-conditioned room, sea view, king size bed (2x2meter), with bathroom, terrace and transat, desk, possibility of 2 extra bed, common swimming pool, wifi.

🛌 BAMBOO ROOM- Traditional bamboo house, with ceiling fan, sea view, kingsize bed (2x2meter),bathroom, common swimming pool, private lounge and dining area at the ground floor, wifi.
