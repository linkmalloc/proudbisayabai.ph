#### Cebu Zip Pool House    
  
Cebu Zip Pool House is a vacation house with 5 rooms, a pool, and other amenities that are ideal for staying with your family and friends.

![](/images/posts/cebu_zip_pool_house/1.jpg)  

A perfect place for a staycation. The place is located in Soong, Mactan, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu. They are accepting daily rentals, scroll down below for more details on the rates.

![](/images/posts/cebu_zip_pool_house/9.jpg)  

For bookings and inquiry please contact there facebook page at [Cebu Zip Pool House](https://www.facebook.com/Cebuziprealty/)  or you can contact them at ([09178816506](tel:09178816506)).  

![](/images/posts/cebu_zip_pool_house/12.jpg)  

![](/images/posts/cebu_zip_pool_house/8.jpg)  

##### RATES   
4 Rooms (10 pax)

###### Weekdays 
= Php 7,000.00 per night  
  
###### Weekends (Friday-Sunday) 
= Php 8,000 per night  

###### Additional fees if more than 10 pax:  
- Adult = Php500.00   
- Kids = Php300.00   

****
##### INCLUSIONS  
- Wifi Access  
- Unlimited Karaoke  
- Kitchen where you can bring your own food and cook  
- Pool  
- Arcade  
- Barbecue   
- Grill  
- Water  

****

##### TAKE NOTE:  
- Php 1,000 for reservation fee per night to confirm your booking.

****
##### HOW TO GET THERE

🌍 Exact Location:  Da Costa Ville, Casia-Soong Rd., Mactan,  Lapu-Lapu City,  Cebu  
  
🗺 Via COMMUTE: From Parkmall mall ride a jeepney (MI -02B) going to Mactan Oasis Garden. Then, ride a tricycle going to Da costa ville.  
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
