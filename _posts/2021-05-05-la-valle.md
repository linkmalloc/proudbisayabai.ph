#### La Valle in Sogod, Northern Cebu  
<center>A place that feels like home.</center>

****
Experience a great modern province life at La Valle in Sogod, Northern Cebu. It is a vacation house in the middle of nature, a perfect place for a relaxing staycation. 

**La Valle** offers a space where you can disconnect from all the hustle and bustle of your daily busy life and reconnect with your loved ones, nature, and yourself.  

![](/images/posts/la_valle/4.jpg)  


![](/images/posts/la_valle/6.jpg)  

There's a lot that you can do at **La Valle**. You can camp in their spacious camping area or just stay and sleep in their relaxing amakan houses. The "amakan" wall or the woven bamboo walls gives the room a very comforting ventilation and the interior arrangements makes you feel at ease with its minimalistic design.  

![](/images/posts/la_valle/3.jpg)  

In front of the Amakan houses, you can take a dip in the infinity pool where you can enjoy the stunning view of the surroundings or indulge yourself in a night swim.

##### HOW TO GET THERE  

La Valle is exactly located at barangay Pansoy, Sogod, Norther Cebu. To get here, it will take roughly 2 hours drive from Cebu City and it is approximately 15 minutes drive from the Poblacion, Sogod, Cebu.

🗺 Via Commute: From North Bus Terminal-Sogod Cebu (Bus Fare 100-150, travel time 2-3hrs) Sogod Gym Ride a Motorcycle Taxi (Habal-Habal) To Pansoy Sogod (Motorcycle Fare 50-100php per head)  
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
