If you want to feel and see the best view of the city don’t miss to visit these instagrammable places:      
    
****    
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/asa-maglaag-sa-sugbo-2/tile1.jpg)    
###### Osmeña Peak       
Standing as the highest peak in Cebu, Osmeña Peak in Dalaguete offers a breathtaking view of jagged cliffs and the surrounding islands. The trek to the summit is relatively easy, making it accessible even for beginner hikers. The peak, known for its stunning sunrise and sunset views, offers an unparalleled experience of seeing Cebu from above.     
    
Visitors should try to catch the sunrise, as the early morning light paints the landscape in a golden hue, offering a serene and awe-inspiring start to the day.    
    
****    
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/asa-maglaag-sa-sugbo-2/tile5.jpg)    
###### [Tops of Cebu](https://proudbisayabai.ph/destination/2024/08/15/kai-enters-pinoy-big-brother)        
Tops of Cebu, located in Busay, is the go-to place for those who want to see the city’s skyline sparkle at night. This popular hangout spot provides a 360-degree view of Cebu and its neighboring islands, making it perfect for a romantic evening or a casual night out with friends. The cool mountain air and the stunning view of the city lights below create a magical ambiance.     
    
Be sure to bring a jacket and enjoy a warm cup of coffee while watching the city come alive under the stars.    
    
****    
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/asa-maglaag-sa-sugbo-2/tile3.jpg)    
###### Temple of Leah        
Often dubbed as Cebu’s version of the Taj Mahal, the Temple of Leah is a grand structure built as a symbol of undying love. Located in Busay, this Roman-inspired temple offers more than just a love story; it provides a spectacular view of the city and the sea beyond. The temple, with its towering columns, statues, and expansive grounds, is a perfect spot for photography enthusiasts.     
    
Don’t miss exploring the inside, where you’ll find a gallery of antique items and memorabilia that add to the temple’s allure.
