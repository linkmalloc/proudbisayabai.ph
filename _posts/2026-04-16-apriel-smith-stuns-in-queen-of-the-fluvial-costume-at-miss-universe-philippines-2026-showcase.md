Cebu City’s pride, Apriel Smith, captivates with her stunning “Queen of the Fluvial” national costume for the Miss Universe Philippines 2026 National Costume Showcase.



Apriel Smith drew inspiration from Cebu’s deep-rooted faith and rich cultural heritage, highlighting the enduring devotion of Cebuanos to the Santo Niño de Cebu. Her ensemble beautifully reflects the city’s identity, shaped by sacred waters and centuries-old traditions, symbolizing both spirituality and cultural pride.



The intricate design pays homage to Cebu’s iconic fluvial procession, blending elegance, symbolism, and storytelling on the national stage—making it a standout piece in this year’s competition.



The National Costume Showcase is set to take place today, April 16, 2026, at 7:00 p.m., where candidates from across the country will present costumes inspired by their respective local cultures and stories.
