#### Hobbit House of Mt. Kitanglad Agro-Eco Farm
<center>Nature | Our kind of luxury. 🍄😯🍃</center>  
<center>Hobbit House of Mt. Kitanglad Agro-Eco Farm, where heaven meets earth. </center>
 
****
You don’t have to go to New Zealand if you’re a fan of the Lord of the Rings films. In Mount Kitanglad Agro-Eco Farm in Malaybalay City, Bukidnon, there are Hobbit-inspired houses that was commissioned by the site’s co-owner, Benjamin Torregosa Maputi Jr. The houses have been there since 2018.

🌍 Exact Location: Brgy. Sinaburan, Imbayao, Malaybalay City, Bukidnon.   
   
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: From Cagayan de Oro City, take a bus bound for Malaybalay City. Once in Malaybalay, take a tricycle to Casisang Landing market.  There you will find a habal-habal terminal. Tell the driver that you are going to the hobbit house Agro-Eco Farm in Barangay Imbayao. Fare is 100/head each way.  
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: If you are bringing a car coming from Cagayan de Oro, take the Malaybalay diversion road. You can use waze or google maps.   
  
###### 📌 Amenities:  
- Hobbit House  
- Tree house  
- Glamping Tent  
- Camp site  
- Bonfire area  
- Bird watching and Eco Trail  
- Unlimited brewed coffee  

****
###### 📌 Entrance Fee:  
- Php 50/person with free local brewed coffee, farm tour and forest trek.   

****
###### 💡 REMINDERS:  
- Ask your habal-habal driver to wait for you if you are staying for a few hours only.  
- Only motorcycles and four-wheel drive vehicle are advisable because of the rough road  
- If you are bringing a car, make sure that it is a 4x4/4WD. If not, just take the habal-habal from Malaybalay.  
- Be prepared for a bumpy ride  
- Agro-Eco farm is about 14km from Sayre highway in Malaybalay city
