##### Guitar Wood House

Let's bring your music experience to the next level!     

When music and arts go together, they create an abstract portrait that expresses something that cannot be put into words. It reaches your soul in a deep way. 

The vibe you can feel at Guitar Wood House in Panglao Bohol hits different together with your life sentiments. You can sing out loud as if you are taking a shower, more specifically, when you're by yourself, playing a tune along with the guitar. When paired with art, you create your own internal movies or scenes to accompany the music. No lyrics are telling you how to feel.

The song pierces your bones whether you are thrilled or in pain. Indeed, it is a home for arts and music. Guitar Wood House is a perfect all-wood music venue with a bar and restaurant that provides a snuggly and incredible experience for music enthusiasts. This fantastic establishment has a top-notch music system with a spectacular menu of drinks and cuisine.

In addition to being one of the best locations to unwind, it has an ambiance that is ideal on Instagram, especially in stories. The atmosphere is really classical, and you can't help but dance to the beats of the music. This vicinity is perfect if you're searching for a spot to chill while on a staycation with your heartbroken friends or if you are celebrating by creating more moments. Imagine staying all night here and talking about life. You won't be bored because the stunning sunflowers outside will mesmerize you!

Asa na ang tagay, bai? Tara diri, feel the cool breeze while naggunit ug gin or beer!

🌏 Exact Location: Daorong, Panglao, Bohol   

A perfect place to chill with live music and for your instagram shots.
