#### SEASALT CAFE    
  
<center><i>"Your relaxing pit stop in Catmon that you shouldn’t miss ☕️💦😯"</i></center>  

****
****
Let's have Coffee and Chill!   
  
After a lengthy drive, this place is the best stop-over spot in Catmon which offers a beachfront-view where you can enjoy your refreshments and fine meals.  
  
📍SeaSalt Cafe, Catmon Northern Cebu  

****
##### 🌏 Exact Location:  
KM49 Catmondaan, Catmon Northern Cebu  
  
##### 🕐 Operating hours:  
6:00 AM - 7:00 PM (DAILY)   

**** 
#### HOW TO GET THERE? 

****
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
From North Bus Terminal, ride a bus or vhire bound to Catmon and tell the driver to drop you off at Grotto @ KM49.  
  
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
