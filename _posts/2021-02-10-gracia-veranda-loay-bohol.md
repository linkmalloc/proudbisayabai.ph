A place where you can free your inner soul and breakaway from tradition!  
at **Gracia Veranda** in Bohol.
Breakfast + Coffee + Mountain + Tent

Quiet time is 11pm onwards.

Exact Location: Loay Central Visayas, 6303, Loay Bohol (About 2km from the National Highway)

**How to get there?**

The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus
or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.

**Rates:**
- P500/tent good for 2 persons, P100 additional per head
- P1500/overlooking air conditioned room max of 4pax
- P1000/air conditioned room max of 4pax
 
If you decide to bring your own tent, you can for P350/2 persons and
still P100 additional per head.

Make it exclusive for P6000/15pax (2 air conditioned room, 5 tents,
and kitchen use.)

They serve breakfast for P150 per head

**Inclusions:**
- Toilet & bath 
- Fire pit with firewoods
- Picnic bench & table
- Twin size air bed (bring your own beddings, linens - for tent use, and
towels)
- Barbecue grill (bring your own charcoal)
- Safe parking area

****
Excluding toiletries and other personal supplies
Liquors are permitted for exclusive occupancy - just drink responsibly

****
> NO pets allowed
