Getting to one of Cebu’s most popular island destinations is now more convenient as a new bus route from Cebu City to Camotes Island has officially opened, offering travelers a more accessible and affordable way to reach the island group.



The initiative, implemented under the Cebu Province Local Public Transport Route Plan, is designed to improve connectivity between mainland Cebu and Camotes Island. The route is expected to benefit tourists, backpackers, and locals by providing a more streamlined travel option to the island’s well-known beaches, caves, and cliff diving spots.



Officials said the new transport link responds to the growing demand for budget-friendly and hassle-free travel options, especially as Camotes continues to attract more domestic and international visitors seeking nature-based and adventure tourism experiences.



With this development, travelers can look forward to a smoother journey combining both land and sea travel, reducing the usual complexities of planning multi-leg trips to the island.



The new route is also seen as part of broader efforts to boost local tourism and support economic activity in island destinations across Cebu Province.



As interest in Camotes Island continues to rise, the improved transport access is expected to make it an even more attractive getaway for weekend trips and longer stays alike.
