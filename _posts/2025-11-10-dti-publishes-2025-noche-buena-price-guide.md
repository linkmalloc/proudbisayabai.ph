The Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) has officially released the 2025 Noche Buena Price Guide, aimed at assisting consumers in planning their holiday purchases amid fluctuating market prices.

The guide features updated prices of traditional Christmas items, including ham, spaghetti, pasta sauce, queso de bola, fruit cocktail, and mayonnaise, among other staple products from various brands.

According to the DTI, a Christmas tree icon indicates products that are priced lower than in 2024, while a star marks items with unchanged prices.
