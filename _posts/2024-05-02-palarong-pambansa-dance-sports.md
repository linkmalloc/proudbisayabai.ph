In a significant milestone for the DanceSport community, the Palarong Pambansa 2024 has officially recognized DanceSport as a legitimate sport. This recognition underscores the athletic and artistic value of DanceSport, which includes Cha-cha, Rumba, Tango, Waltz, and Samba, blending rigorous physical training with intricate choreography and performance.  

The inclusion of DanceSport in this prestigious national event highlights its growing popularity and the dedication of athletes who have tirelessly worked to elevate the sport. There will be 24 medal events that are expected to be awarded to the best DanceSport athletes from both the elementary and secondary levels, marking its official inclusion in the medal tally for this year. Competitors from across the country will now have the opportunity to showcase their skills on a larger stage, promoting greater awareness and appreciation of DanceSport.  

This official acknowledgment by Palarong Pambansa not only legitimizes the efforts of DanceSport athletes but also paves the way for further development and support for the sport in the Philippines.
