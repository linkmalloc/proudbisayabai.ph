HAPIT NATA MAG-SINULOG!  

Mark your calendars and prepare to be dazzled by the vibrant festivities of Sinulog 2024!  

Immerse yourself in the rich cultural heritage of Cebu as we celebrate the city's grandest festival. From the awe-inspiring Sinulog Grand Parade to the electrifying Sinulog Showdown, prepare to be captivated by the colorful spectacle that awaits you.  

Here's a sneak peek into the Sinulog 2024 schedule:  

**January 15**: Sinulog sa Kabataan sa Dakbayan - A vibrant celebration of youth and culture  

**January 16**: Sinulog Fiesta Day Holy Mass Celebration - A solemn tribute to the Santo Niño  

**January 17**: Sinulog 2024 Grand Parade - A mesmerizing spectacle of colors, music, and dance  

**January 18**: Sinulog 2024 Grand Finale & Grand Fireworks - A dazzling display of pyrotechnics to captivate the night sky  

And that's just the beginning! Sinulog 2024 has something for everyone, from cultural performances and street parties to religious processions and devotional activities.  

**Hapit na mag PRITITITS!**
