The vast bulk of the haze is thin swirls with curving edges and stunning strokes of nature's artistry on its blue palette. The recreational amenities of Cebu R Resort Tabuelan are top-notch. You can get near the coastline and a beach adjacent to the resort, where visitors may also wolf down a continental breakfast. It has a terrace, garden, bar, and restaurant.  

The accommodations include complimentary facilities, a desk, a flat-screen TV with satellite channels, a fridge, an electric teapot, a bath, and air conditioning. The area features guestrooms with balconies that have a seating area as well as suites with a closet and a private bathroom. It possesses spa services, an outdoor patio, and a pool for kids. Parking is free, and an airport shuttle is available for a charge that is available 24 hours for everyone. During working periods, helpful personnel provide front-desk assistance and information about the locality.  

Exact Location: Antonio Y De Pio Hwy, Tabuelan, Cebu   

###### How to get there? 
Via commute: From Cebu North Bus terminal, ride any bus going to Tabuelan (preferably the one with “Maravilla” sign board. Tell them the name of the resort and they will just drop you off nearby. You will have to walk for about 3-5 minutes to finally arrive at the resort. 

The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
