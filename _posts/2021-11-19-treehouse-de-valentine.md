##### Treehouse De Valentine

Experience the great outdoors in your own secluded Treehouse, equiped with modern amenities that doesn’t spoil its native vibe.

Treehouse de Valentine is a self-contained retreat that combines nature and rustic comfort in a single location. This dream house, which is unconventional for the right reasons, is the ideal retreat from the hustle and bustle of Cebu City.  

Dine and relax in the center of nature — soak in your own private wooden bathtub, complete a long-overdue book on the balcony, or simply relax on the most comfortable king-sized mattresses in this 3-bedroom house. If your spirit so demands it, take a refreshing dip in the perennially chilly waters of your own little river!

The treehouse can only seat 8 people.

The upper level has two rooms, one with a balcony facing the river. The first room contains two single pull-outs. The top level features a wooden bathtub and complete bathrooms (toilet and shower). The riverfront terrace includes a shower too. Two hammocks are available for use on the riverfront terrace. Only the top shower is heated. Every toilet has a bidet (Japanese-type).

The bottom floor has a large al fresco area with a living room, kitchen, bed, and balcony. Because the bottom floor is open, guests must be used to sharing it.  

More details and Book via [https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/39113140](https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/39113140)  

Exact Location: Hingatmonan Balamban, Cebu, 46 km from Marco Polo Cebu. 

###### How to get there?

🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
