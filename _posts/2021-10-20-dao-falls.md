##### DAO FALLS

\#ChasingWaterfalls: Pabugnaw na bah? It's weekend again, featuring the Majestic Waterfalls of Dao Falls in Samboan, Cebu!

Dao Falls offers you a one-of-a-kind-chasing-waterfall experience. Sa ka mingaw ug ka haruhay sa lugar maka ingon jud ka ug mao ni akong gipangita nga pahuway, Arats Bai! 

🌎 Exact Location: Brgy. Suba, Samboan, South Cebu, Philippines

###### How to get there?  

🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu City South Bus terminal you can ride a bus to Bato/Liloan via Barili/Samboan and get off at Dao Eskina. Estimated Fare (200 - 250 pesos). From the highway, take a tricycle or single motorcycle to the trailhead, fare is P20/person (P60/trike or P40/motorbike). The trek from the trailhead takes at least 30 minutes, passing a wooden bridge and along the river sandwiched by limestone walls, Entrance fee is P20, guide is required and readily available on the site for a tip-based rate.   

🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze or Google Map. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze / Google Map.
