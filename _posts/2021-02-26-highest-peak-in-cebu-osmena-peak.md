#### OSMEÑA PEAK
<center>1013 meters above sea level ⛰🏕😯  </center>

****
Popular destination for hikers because of the breathtaking views it can offer. Situated in Mantalongon, Dalaguete, Osmena Peak is also known as the “Vegetable Basket” of Cebu or “Little Baguio” because of the vast vegetable farms in the area. 

****
##### 🌍 Exact Location:
Mantalongon, Dalaguete, Cebu

****
#### HOW TO GET THERE?   
  
##### Via Commute:  
Gikan sa South Bus Terminal to Dalaguete junction. P127  
 (Pa drop ka skina pa ingon Osmeña Peak) Habal-habal back and forth. P200(One way ra na ang P100 unya 2 ka ulo sa isa ka motor) Mag baktas ka 20-25 minutes.  
  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via   
  
##### 📌 Entrance fee:  
**P30.00**  
  
##### 📌 Other informations:  
Kung first time nimo pwede ka mo kuha og guide. Depende ra nimo og pila imong ihatag pero yaw pud palabi pa gamay kay dugang na sa ilang panginabuhi. Hatag ka pinaka gamay P50. Daog na.  
  
##### 📌 Total Budget 
More or less kung naay guide P407
