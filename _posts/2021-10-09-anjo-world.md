##### Anjo World Theme Park
\#FunVibes: New Normal FUN continues at Anjo World Theme Park!

Experience and enjoy all the fun-filled and exciting activities they have to offer, featuring more than 12 world-class rides and attractions for the family and friends to enjoy. From heart-pounding rides to mouthwatering foods, they got it all for us! But guess what? There's more! You can now enter into a magical wonderland of snow and experience winter in Cebu! Isn't that amazing? Come and visit Cebu's First World-Class Theme Park and begin the fun!

🕑 Operating hours: 11am - 9pm daily

🌎 Exact Location: Belmont one, Upper Calajoan, Minglanilla, Cebu, Philippines

###### How to get there?
🗺 Via Commute: You can ride any bus or mini bus bound for Minglanilla, Cebu and Tell the driver to drop you off and stop at Anjo World, Minglanilla. Estimated Fare is P30-40 (one way).
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: You can navigate Waze or Google Maps and set your destination at Anjo World Minglanilla.
