King Pata, a fast-growing local food business based In Cebu, has opened its fourth branch in Minglanilla on Tuesday, July 28, 2026.  

  

Bringing the delicious experience closer to its patrons in the south, King Pata’s latest expansion marked a new milestone as it continues to win over Cebuano’s hearts and appetites. 

![](https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/2526/129bae1785242256-3.jpeg)

Known for its signature pata that is juicy, tender, and packed with flavor, King Pata has become a go-to dining destination for families and food lovers looking for hearty, Filipino-style comfort food. 

  

The classic Filipino dish that King Pata specializes in is sure to be star in every handaan, with its tender and succulent meat. Crispy on the outside and tender on the inside, each serving is slow-cooked to perfection and generously portioned, making it ideal for sharing with family and friends for celebrations and even ordinary salo-salos. Its rich, savory flavor paired with steaming rice has made it a sought-after dish among Cebuano foodies.

![](https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/2526/8d65ca1785242280-5.jpeg)

The grand opening in Minglanilla brought about exciting festivities, graced by Cebu’s favorite content creators, including Joseph Sabello, Al Moralde, Bite King, Shael, and Kicksrus. The first 100 customers were also treated to a free 1.5-liter bottle of Coke to pair with their orders.

![](https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/2526/d8bc5d1785242336-7.jpeg)

Owners Joseph Sabello and Al Moralde shared that beyond just opening King Pata to different parts of Cebu, they mainly aimed to create more job opportunities for Cebuanos. The homegrown food chain commits to empowering the local community with its expansion, with more branches underway.

![](https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/2526/43a5cb1785242310-6.jpeg)

If you want to have a taste of King Pata’s crowd-favorite pata, now is the perfect time to visit their newest branch in Minglanilla. The pata shop is open daily, from 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. For direct orders, you may message King Pata’s social media pages.
