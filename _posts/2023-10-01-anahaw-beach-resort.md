****
Anahaw Beach Resort is a newly-opened private beach resort located in the South of Cebu, specifically in the town of Barili. It is the perfect destination for family gatherings, team buildings, retreats, and reunions.  

The resort offers very spacious rooms, budget-friendly rates, scenic views, and most importantly, an exclusive resort experience. Guests can enjoy the resort's white sand beach, crystal-clear waters, and lush greenery.  

Anahaw Beach Resort is also home to "Barili's Best Kandingan and Chicken Inasal" in LYLYN'S ROASTED LIEMCHICK, where guests can enjoy delicious Filipino food while enjoying the stunning views.  

###### Location
Candugay road Barili Cebu, Philippines

###### How to get there? 
From Cebu City, ride a bus (Ceres or Lebrando trans) at Cebu South-bus terminal then tell the driver to drop you off at Petron Barili or Shamrock (Approximately 1.5hour travel time) and Fare is P110 regular then P95 for students. From Petron Gasoline station ride a habal - habal going to Anahaw beach resort fare is P20.
