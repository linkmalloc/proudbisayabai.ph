#### Lakeview Le Jardin Garden and Cafe  
  
****
Newest Attraction in Balamban Cebu. Let's you experience the Korean feels botanical garden with a stunning view of the Balamban Mountains and the Malubog Lake. Perfect sa imong plano na chill plus pictorial with your oppas or your chingu. Not to mention their best Barista coffee, draft beer and selction of the best dishes! 💟🌸☕  

You can test your endurance in their huge flower garden that has a hundred steps of stairs to cover or you can simply chill and relax in their restaurant while enjoying the cool breeze and sip a good coffee.

##### HOW TO GET THERE  

🌏 Exact Location: Sitio Inaad, Brgy. Tabunan, Cebu  

🗺 Via Commute: From Ayala Center Cebu PUV Terminal, ride a v-hire going to Balamban. Then tell the driver to drop-off at Lakeview Le Jardin. This is just beside Charlie’s Cup in Brgy. Tabunan (just before welcome to Balamban Sign). Estimated fare is around P120 per head (one-way). UV bus route operates on everyday. Regular schedule hours: 7:00 AM - 10:00 PM.  
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze  
  
##### RATES    
- Adult- P100.00  
- Children Below 8 y.o. - P50.00  

****
Note: They offer free parking.

🕗 Operating Hours: 8AM - 6PM
