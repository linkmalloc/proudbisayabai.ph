The refreshment joint known for its flavorful and affordable drinks, Don Lemon commemorates its second year of serving drinks to every Filipino’s taste buds. 

  

Established in July 16, 2024, Don Lemon launched its first-ever branch in Tisa, Cebu City. The company then expanded its operations to Luzon and Mindanao, offering their signature beverages to a wider Filipino audience. 

  

Don Lemon’s known signature drink is their original blend lemonade, captivating the right zesty flavor perfect for everyone's taste! There are also a variety of bestselling flavors, satisfying every customer, including mango, peach, and green apple. Whether customers prefer something sweet, tangy, or refreshingly fruity, Don Lemon offers a flavor for every taste. Made with 100% pure lemon extract, each drink delivers a refreshing burst of flavor

  

As part of Don Lemon's second anniversary celebration, three Cebu City branches—the USC flagship branch, Urgello, and Tisa—joined the festivities.

  

All three branches rolled out special anniversary promos, which were warmly received by customers who visited the stores during the celebration. While they shared the same milestone and promotional offerings, each branch showcased its own unique character and strengths, contributing to the brand's growing presence in the city.

  

## **Don Lemon USC (P. Del Rosario) Branch**

  

The flagship branch is accessible to everyone, especially students as it is located in downtown Cebu City, near the University of San Carlos Main Campus and Asian College of Technology, allowing students to easily drop by the store. The branch also has a dine-in space and offers different pastries along the Don Lemon drinks.

  

  

## **Don Lemon-Urgello Branch**

  

Like the USC branch, Don Lemon Urgello is conveniently located near Southwestern University, making it an accessible stop for students looking to cool down with a refreshing drink. Situated in one of Cebu City's well-known food hubs, the branch also serves as the perfect destination for customers seeking a refreshing beverage to complement their food trip experience.

  

## **Don Lemon-Tisa Branch**

  

Don Lemon Tisa branch unlike the other branches, offers accessibility to people who visit the famous siomai destination. After every siomai meal, people can immediately grab their Don Lemon drinks!

  

Don Lemon has delighted and quenched the thirst of every Filipino. From its original blended lemonade, different fruit-flavored drinks, to healthy drink options, Don Lemon is surely convenient and definitely appeals to the Filipino panlasa!
