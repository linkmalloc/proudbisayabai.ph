Experience amazing sea diving at Kerikite Exotic Island Dive Resort at Samar, Leyte. Deep blue crystal clear waters and scenic tranquility. 🏝 🌊 🏊🏻‍♂️  

Want to escape from the reality and take a break from work and stress? Worry no more. This place will surely take your breath away. Take your diving experience to the next level.  

Arats na mga bai! Swimming nata!  

**Exact Location**: Kerikite Exotic Island Dive Resort. Samar, Leyte - approx. 3hours away from the City  

##### How to get there?  

**Via Commute**: Take a bus bound for Leyte, Samar. Then, ask the conductor or bus driver to drop you off at Leyte, Samar Proper. Look for a motorbike driver who will take you to the Kerikite Exotic Island Dive Resort. Kerikite Island is an island in the province of Samar situated in the eastern part of the Visayan Sea, located to the east of Almagro Island and Sayon Island, southwest of Camandag Island, and northwest of Santo Niño Island and Pilar Island. Administratively, it is one of the five islands that comprises the fifth class municipality of Almagro. The island is home to two hamlets: Barangay Kerikite and Barangay Magsaysay.  

Kerikite is a tiny island, only about 1 kilometer long and 500 meters wide.  

**Via Private Vehicle**: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
