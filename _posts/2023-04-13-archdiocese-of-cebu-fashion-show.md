**Cebu City – Philippines**, The Archdiocese of Cebu is set to showcase a fashion event that combines modern fashion with traditional Sunday's best, and it's all for a good cause, in the name of "Le Voile". Led by the group, 5 Loaves and 2 Fishes Inc., the event aims to raise funds for their target beneficiary. This fusion of contemporary and traditional fashion promises to be a one-of-a-kind event that will take place on June 03, 2023, at the Waterfront Hotel and Casino. With tickets priced at P6,000 per seat, this event is sure to be a night to remember.  

The fashion show will showcase the talent of local and renowned designers who have been tasked with blending the best of modern fashion with traditional Sunday's best. The event promises to be a celebration of Cebuano culture, style, and elegance. Attendees will have the chance to witness first-hand the blending of these two worlds and see how local designers have managed to create something unique and extraordinary.
Apart from being a showcase of fashion and talent, the event will also serve as a fundraiser for the group 5 Loaves and 2 Fishes Inc.'s beneficiaries. This non-profit organization is dedicated to helping those in need in the local community. With the funds raised from this event, they hope to continue their work and assist more people in need.  

For those who wish to join this event, tickets are priced at P6,000 per seat, and can also be enjoyed by your friends, family, and even barkadas! With their 10-seater table, priced at P60,000. The price may seem steep, but it's important to remember that this is a fundraiser for a good cause. The Archdiocese of Cebu, with the support of His Excellency, Archbishop Jose Palma, himself, and 5 Loaves and 2 Fishes Inc. are committed to making a difference in the lives of those in need in the local community, and this event is just one of the many ways they are doing so.  

This initiative, the Fusion of Modern Sunday's Best fashion show promises to be an unforgettable event that celebrates the best of local fashion and culture. The Archdiocese of Cebu and 5 Loaves and 2 Fish Inc. are inviting everyone to be part of this event and help make a difference in the lives of those in need. Let's come together, celebrate fashion and style, and make a positive impact on the community. Magkita-kita unya ta didto, mga Bai!
