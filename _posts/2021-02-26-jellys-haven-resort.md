#### HOW TO GET THERE?  
  
****
##### Exact Location:  
Sta. Fe, Bantayan Island, Cebu (4 hours away from the city)  

##### 🗺 Via commute:  
From North Bus Terminal Ride a bus going to Hagnaya. Travel time is 3 to 4hours. From Hagnaya take the Express Ferry Boat to Sta. Fe, Bantayan Island then the travel time is 45mins to 1hour ride to Sta. Fe, Bantayan Island. Bus fare is P170 pesos per way then P340 pesos roundtrip na. Naay discount (20% discount for students, just present School ID) Then while sa Ferry &amp; Terminal Fee P110 pesos only per way / P220 pesos roundtrip na.

#### Room rates:   
  
##### Tents:   
- COUPLE TENT: P500 pesos per night, good for 2 person  
- FAMILY TENT: P1,000 pesos per night, good for 4 person  

****
#####  Superior Rooms:   
- Good for 2 person  
- 2 Single Beds / 1 Queen-sized  
- Complimentary breakfast for 2 person  
- P5,200 pesos per night  

****
##### Deluxe Room:   
- Good for 2-4 person  
- Complimentary breakfast for 2 person  
- P6,800 pesos per night  

****
##### Executive Suite:   
- Good for 2-4 person   
- Complimentary breakfast for 2pax  
- P7,500 pesos per night  

****
##### Jelly’s Grand Suite:   
- Good for 2 person
