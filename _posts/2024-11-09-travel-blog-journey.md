*****    

As I accepted the Best Cebu Blogs Award (BCBA) for travel blogging, memories of my journey over the past year rushed back to me. Winning this award for the second consecutive year—first in 2023 and now nominated again in 2024—has been both humbling and empowering. It’s more than just a title; it’s a recognition of my passion, hard work, and the unwavering support of the people around me.  

****
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/bestcebublogawards/p4.jpg)

Last year, when I won my first BCBA, it was a milestone that transformed me. It proved that my blog, which started as a simple way to share the beauty of Cebu and inspire others to explore, had truly made an impact. That first award encouraged me to step out of my comfort zone, to dream even bigger, and to see the potential in myself that I once doubted.  

This year, being nominated again felt like another affirmation of my growth. The recognition reminded me that the work I do continues to resonate with readers, and it has deepened my commitment to my blogging journey. Each trip, every article, and all the moments of discovery I capture are dedicated not just to sharing my love for Cebu, but also to honoring the people who have been by my side—my family, my friends, my colleagues, and my team.  

Knowing that I’ve made them proud is my greatest joy and motivator. Their belief in me has fueled my passion, even during the most challenging times. This second nomination is a promise to keep growing, to keep sharing, and to give back to the community that has given me so much.  

****
![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/bestcebublogawards/p3.jpg)

Winning the BCBA last year boosted my confidence in ways I hadn’t imagined, but this year’s nomination reminded me that the journey is ongoing and that every step counts. I’m excited to continue sharing stories, and with each new post, I’m inspired to make a positive difference—both for myself and for the readers who share this journey with me.
