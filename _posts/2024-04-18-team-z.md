Are you an adventure seeker craving the thrill of the open water?  Then look no further than Team Z, the newest watersports hot spot on the picturesque island of Mactan Cebu!    

A Splash of Excitement for Everyone  

Team Z has assembled a fleet of aquatic excitement ready to make your heart pound. Experience the wind in your hair with exhilarating jet ski tours or soar high above the waves on a breathtaking parasailing adventure. Whether you're looking for solo thrills or fun activities to share with friends and family, Team Z offers:  

**High-Octane Rides**:  Jet boats, speed boats, and the amazing sea breacher.  
**Sky-High Adventures**: Parasailing and flyboarding.  
**Classic Fun**: Banana and tube boats for laughter-filled rides.  
**Yacht Rentals**: For those seeking a touch of luxury.  
**Island Hopping**: Explore the beautiful islands of Cebu

###### Your Next Adventure Starts Here  

Team Z is ready to be your gateway to an unforgettable Cebu experience. Their friendly and experienced staff will ensure your safety and excitement every step of the way.   

Make Team Z a must-visit destination on your next trip to Cebu. Get ready to make a splash and create memories that will last a lifetime!

📍Exact Location :  Datag, Maribago (near Jpark / Wharf Bridge) Lapu-Lapu City
