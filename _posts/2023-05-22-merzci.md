Merzci Bread &amp; Pastries, producer of Bacolod's best food pasalubong, lands in the Queen City of the South - Cebu!   

It opened its second new branch last March 15 in Mandaue, Cebu bringing its yummiest and tastiest products to more Cebuanos. This is the company's 67th branch to be opened since it started in 1995. The company aims to expand and reach more clientele in Negros, Panay, Cebu, and even Mindanao where it has been receiving a lot of inquiries for distribution of the best-tasting Filipino delicacies that it has to offer.
