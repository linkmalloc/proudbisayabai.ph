The hands that feed the community deserve honest governance, not forgotten sacrifices

Behind every fresh vegetable displayed at KADIWA in Lapu-Lapu City are hardworking Cebuanos whose sacrifices and dedication continue to keep communities alive and thriving.

From farmers waking up before sunrise to vendors patiently serving customers throughout the day, these ordinary workers carry extraordinary stories of perseverance. Their wrinkled hands reflect years of honest labor, while every harvest symbolizes hope for a better future for their families.

This initiative, created by the Lapu-Lapu City Government, continues to provide opportunities for local farmers, vendors, and small entrepreneurs to directly connect with the Oponganon community through fresh, affordable, and locally produced goods.

More than just a marketplace, KADIWA at Lapu-Lapu City Hall has become a meaningful space where communities support one another while helping strengthen local livelihoods and small businesses.

Every item sold represents sweat, sacrifice, and the determination of people who continue to work despite economic challenges. These hardworking Cebuanos deserve opportunities, honest governance, and public services free from corruption so that the fruits of their labor truly benefit the communities they serve.

As communities continue to support local products and small businesses, many hope that transparency, accountability, and genuine public service will also continue to grow alongside these local initiatives.
