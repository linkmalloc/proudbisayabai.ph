Situated at the southern tip of Cebu, Samboan is a tranquil coastal destination where adventure, history, and pure island serenity meet. This charming town offers an authentic escape for travelers seeking nature’s beauty, cultural depth, and a slower pace of life.

**A Destination Where Nature Thrives**

From cascading waterfalls to pristine shorelines, Samboan boasts some of Cebu’s most breathtaking natural wonders.

*Aguinid Falls*

A multi-tiered waterfall unlike any other, Aguinid invites visitors to climb through its limestone layers, swim in turquoise pools, and explore lush rainforest paths. It’s an adventure that balances excitement and relaxation.

*Saint Michael the Archangel Church*

Built in the 18th century, this centuries-old church stands proudly as a testament to Samboan’s rich cultural heritage. The stone structure, surrounded by scenic landscapes, offers a glimpse into the region’s deep-rooted history.

*A Town Full of Stories*

In Samboan, every wave whispers tales of adventure and tranquility. Whether you’re trekking waterfalls, discovering heritage sites, or simply soaking in the calm coastal charm, there’s always something new to experience.

**How to Get to Samboan, Cebu — and Approximate Fare**

<u>From Cebu City</u>

By Bus:

&#x9;•	Depart from the South Bus Terminal.

&#x9;•	Buses bound for Samboan typically cost ₱170–₱200 and the journey takes about 5 hours.  ￼

&#x9;•	This is the most straightforward, budget-friendly option.  ￼

By Car / Private Transport:

&#x9;•	Driving south via the coastal highway toward Samboan is faster — expect around 2–3 hours depending on traffic and your starting point. Cost will vary depending on vehicle type, fuel use, etc.

<u>From Nearby Areas (e.g. Moalboal → Samboan)</u>

&#x9;•	Bus from Moalboal to Samboan (via coastal route) — fares are roughly ₱120 for the bus segment.  ￼

&#x9;•	From Samboan town proper, you may need a tricycle or local transport to reach waterfalls, beaches, or accommodations — fares vary locally depending on distance.

**Samboan: Where Every Wave Tells a Story**

Plan your southern escape today and discover why Samboan remains one of Cebu’s most captivating hidden gems. With its mix of natural beauty, rich history, and accessible transport, your next adventure could start sooner than you think.
