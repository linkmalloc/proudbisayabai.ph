Unsay Kwentong TOPS nimo? 

Cebu City's popular spot, 'Tops Lookout'Tops Lookout, better known simply as ‘Tops’ is located on Mt Busay. 
This modernist, fortress like viewing deck provides spectacular views, especially at sunrise or sunset.
Operating Hours: 10AM to 2AM daily 

Entrance fee: ₱100 per person  *No Cottage Fee , No Corkage Fee* 

"Walk-ins available"

Exact Location: Cebu Tops Road, Busay, Cebu City.
How to get there?
Via Commute: Ride a taxi going to Busay and tell the driver to drop you off  Tops Lookout, time of travel is about 30mins.
Via Private Vehicle: If you’re driving your own car, just navigate with Waze or Google Maps and pin your location to “Tops Lookout” or refer to the map below. 
Parking space is available
