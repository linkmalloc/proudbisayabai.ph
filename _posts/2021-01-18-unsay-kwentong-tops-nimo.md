Tops Cebu is undoubtedly Cebu City's most well-known viewpoint, offering panoramic views of the vast metropolitan all the way out to Lapu Lapu and its neighboring islands. It's a popular site for both residents and visitors to watch the sunset, go on dates, and have an inexpensive meal beneath the stars. The best aspect is that it is only a short distance from the city center. 

TOPS is best visited early in the morning or late in the afternoon, and on sunny days.

Ikaw bai, Unsay Kwentong TOPS nimo?  

##### Operating Hours:  
10AM to 2AM daily 

##### Entrance fee:  
₱100 per person 

No Cottage Fee , No Corkage Fee  
"Walk-ins available"

##### Exact Location:  
Cebu Tops Road, Busay, Cebu City.

##### Via Commute:  
Ride a taxi going to Busay and tell the driver to drop you off  Tops Lookout, time of travel is about 30mins.

##### Via Private Vehicle:  
If you’re driving your own car, just navigate with Waze or Google Maps and pin your location to “Tops Lookout” or refer to the map below.
