#### DURANO ECO FARM &amp; SPRING RESORT  
Planning to escape from City life and have a break in awhile? Kanindot diri 😍  

An ecotourism destination offering picnic, spring filled swimming pools and camping facilities. A section of the farm is planted with dragon fruits and another portion has been developed as a ornamental plant nursery.

##### 📌 Rates 

###### Entrance fee:  
– ₱50 Kids( 9 y/p below)  
– ₱100 Adults   


###### Table: 
₱300 per day  

 
###### Day Cottage: 
₱1,000 to ₱1,500 per day
