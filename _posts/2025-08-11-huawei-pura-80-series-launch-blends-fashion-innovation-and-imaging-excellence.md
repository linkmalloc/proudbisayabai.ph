The BGC Arts Center transformed into a runway-meets-tech showcase last August 7 as **HUAWEI**, in collaboration with *Vogue Philippines*, officially introduced the **HUAWEI Pura 80 Series** to the Philippine market. This exclusive affair marked the grand debut of the **HUAWEI Pura 80 Ultra** and **HUAWEI Pura 80 Pro**, now available for pre-order starting at PHP 59,999.

<img height="340" width="647" src="https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/542/6d036a1754935032-5.jpg" />

The launch doubled as a celebration of artistry with the **HUAWEI XMAGE Photography Exhibition**, which featured striking works from top Filipino creatives, proving how mobile photography has evolved into a professional-grade art form. Among the star-studded guests was award-winning actor **Jericho Rosales**, who praised the Pura 80 Series as the perfect companion for both glamorous events and capturing his motorcycle adventures.

The **Pura 80 Ultra** leads the series with the industry’s first Switchable Dual Telephoto Camera, capable of seamless transitions between mid-telephoto and ultra-telephoto shots, backed by a 1-inch Ultra Lighting HDR Camera for flawless images day or night. Meanwhile, the **Pura 80 Pro** excels in close-range shooting with its Ultra Lighting Macro Telephoto Camera, delivering true-to-life details even in challenging light conditions.

Beyond imaging, the Pura 80 Series stands out for its **Grace of Glaze** design, available in elegant finishes like Prestige Gold, Golden Black, Glazed Red, Glazed White, and Glazed Black. Built with second-generation Kunlun Glass for exceptional durability, both devices also offer IP69/IP68 water and dust resistance, AI-powered features, and ultra-fast wired and wireless charging.

Pre-orders for the **Pura 80 Ultra** and **Pura 80 Pro** are open via **Shopee, Lazada, TikTok, the HUAWEI Online Store, and HUAWEI Experience Stores** nationwide, complete with premium care services and exclusive freebies. The **HUAWEI Pura 80** and **HUAWEI MatePad 11.5”** will also be launching in the country soon, giving Filipinos even more options for creative expression.
