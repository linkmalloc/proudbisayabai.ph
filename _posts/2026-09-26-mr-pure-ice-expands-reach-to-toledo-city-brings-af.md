Mr. Pure Ice continues its expansion across Cebu with the opening of its new location in Toledo City, marking another step in the brand’s commitment to making quality, affordable ice more accessible to communities and businesses. 

  

The expansion took place on September 26, 2026, along Diosdado Macapagal Highway, Luray I, Toledo, Cebu, in front of South City Central School. 

  

As part of the expansion activities, the Mr. Pure Ice team also conducted a caravan motorcade along the roads of Toledo, bringing the raising awareness of its arrival in the city. The motorcade serves as an opportunity to introduce Mr. Pure Ice to more residents and businesses while celebrating the brand’s growing presence in Cebu. 

  

![](https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/3031/a028491790398044-2.jpeg)

_With special guest appearance from Randolph._

As Mr. Pure Ice continues to grow, its expansion to Toledo opens new opportunities to serve local communities, businesses, and individuals in need of a reliable ice supply. Through its affordable ice products, the brand aims to support everyday needs while creating opportunities for growth within the communities it reaches. 

Mr. Pure Ice’s expansion to Toledo is another step forward in making quality, affordable ice closer to you. Open daily daily from 8:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.
