#### Palayan Valley

****
LOOK: Are you looking for a place to visit? Tara Let’s #Explore the Breathtaking view of Palayan Valley at Barangay Tipolo, Municipality of Ubay, Bohol ⛰🍃☀️  
  
The world is full of beautiful places that most of us don’t even know about, you’re never short on things to see or do. Explore and mesmerize the view of Palayan Valley  in Ubay. A place of natural beauty of the nature where you can relax and reminisce.   
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: Cebu City to Ubay  by boat, M/V J&amp;N Carrier - 10am Monday-Saturday, 12am Sunday, M/V J&amp;N Ferry - 10pm Sunday-Friday, no Saturday trip. Ubay Port Going to Barangay San Tipolo. Ride Motor-Hired /Habal-Habal going to  Tipolo (sabot lang mo sa Driver) Tipolo Proper Going to Palayan Valley. Ride Habal-Habal (Sabot lang mo sa Driver sa Plete)  
  
🗺 Via Waze: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
