There is a distinct difference between a house made of walls and a home filled with heart. For those looking to cultivate the latter, Au-ra Home + Lifestyle is the newest home depot in town that will help you spruce up and brighten your home.

  

Au-ra Home + Lifestyle celebrated its grand opening on Friday, July 10, 2026, unveiling a massive showroom filled with thoughtfully crafted and eclectic furniture and home pieces. The showroom, located in North Reclamation Area, Subangdaku, Mandaue City, seamlessly blends style and functionality. 

  

The showroom features an array of thoughtfully crafted furniture and creative home pieces designed to inspire comfort, connection, and individuality. To elevate the shopping experience of its clients, the space also houses a cozy, in-house café, offering a wide selection of premium drinks, making it a perfect spot to ponder your next home redecoration. During the grand opening, guests were treated to a unique Bloom Bar experience, crafting their own custom flower arrangements while enjoying the café’s signature brews.

  

Rooted in a longstanding family legacy, the brand was born from the foundation of Emerald Mini Depot, a humble home finishing shop specializing in wall and floor coverings, and other essential home materials. 

  

Growing up within the walls of that business sparked a profound realization for Au-ra’s founders: a true home is never defined solely by its foundation but ultimately what makes it uniquely one’s own. It’s quirks, personality and the messy, beautiful realities of everyday life. Driven by this notion, Au-ra Home + Lifestyle was founded to help Cebuanos create spaces that feel intentional and reflective of the people who inhabit them. It bridges the gap between raw construction and soulful interior design, transforming a structural foundation into a true oasis. More than designing interiors, the brand seeks to help people create homes that tell their stories and carry an aura that is entirely their own.

  

At its heart, Au-ra is built not only on design and aesthetics, but on lived experience, authenticity and the belief that the most beautiful homes are the ones filled with meaning. The showroom is open daily from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, located along F.E. Zuellig Avenue, Mandaue City, Cebu, near Emerald Mini Depot.
