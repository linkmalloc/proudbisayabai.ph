It may sound humorous, but there is some truth to the idea of “letting it breathe.” Going commando, or choosing not to wear underwear, can offer certain benefits for male reproductive health when done appropriately.

**Why Temperature Matters**

The testicles are positioned outside the body to maintain a temperature slightly lower than normal body heat, which is important for healthy sperm production.

Wearing tight, non-breathable underwear, especially those made from synthetic materials, can trap heat. This increases the temperature around the testicles and may negatively affect sperm quality over time. In warm climates like the Philippines, this can also lead to discomfort, irritation, or possible infections.

**When Going Commando Helps**

Going without underwear may be beneficial in situations such as:
&#x9;•	While resting at home
&#x9;•	During sleep
&#x9;•	When wearing loose, breathable clothing

These conditions allow better airflow and help maintain a cooler environment.

**When You Should Still Wear Underwear**

Underwear remains important in many daily situations. It is advisable to wear it when:
&#x9;•	Engaging in physical activities or sports for proper support
&#x9;•	Wearing tight-fitting pants to reduce friction
&#x9;•	Having medical conditions such as a hernia that require added support

**Hygiene Is Important**

Regardless of your choice, proper hygiene should always be observed. Keep the area clean and dry, choose breathable fabrics like cotton when wearing underwear, and change clothes regularly, especially after sweating.

The Bottom Line

Going commando is not necessary at all times, but it can help improve comfort and may support reproductive health in certain situations. Maintaining a balance between comfort, support, and hygiene is key. If there are concerns about fertility or discomfort, consulting a healthcare professional is recommended.
