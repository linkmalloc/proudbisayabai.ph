The Philippine food and beverage industry continues to thrive, offering exciting opportunities for aspiring entrepreneurs looking to invest in trusted and fast-growing brands. Among the country’s emerging success stories are Don Macchiatos Philippines, Don Lemon Philippines, and KUKU Frozen Yogurt—three homegrown brands under the Don Macchiatos Group of Companies (DMGC) that have built a reputation for delivering quality products at affordable prices while empowering thousands of Filipinos through franchising. 

  

Driven by innovation, affordability, and a passion for entrepreneurship, DMGC has grown from a single coffee cart into one of the country’s fastest-growing franchise groups. Today, the company continues to expand nationwide while staying true to its mission of creating business opportunities and making a meaningful difference in the communities it serves.

## Don Macchiatos Philippines: Cebu’s First 39ers Coffee

Launched on September 28, 2022, Don Macchiatos Philippines—Cebu’s First 39ers Coffee—introduced a fresh approach to coffee by making premium-tasting beverages accessible to everyone. With its signature ₱39 Iced Caramel Macchiato, the brand quickly captured the hearts of coffee lovers and changed the perception that great coffee has to come with a premium price.

  

The brand was founded by Bethel Jae San Juan, who is originally from Pinamungajan, Cebu, together with her husband from Inayawan, Cebu City. Their entrepreneurial journey began with the opening of the very first Don Macchiatos coffee cart in Urgello, Cebu City. What started as a humble venture has since grown into one of the country’s fastest-growing coffee brands, now with more than 1,000 branches nationwide.

  

Further strengthening the brand’s nationwide appeal, acclaimed actor Daniel Padilla serves as the official brand ambassador, helping bring Don Macchiatos closer to millions of Filipino coffee lovers while representing the brand’s youthful, dynamic, and Filipino identity.

## Don Lemon Philippines: Refreshingly Affordable

Following the remarkable success of Don Macchiatos, DMGC expanded its beverage portfolio with the launch of Don Lemon Philippines in 2024.

  

Known for its refreshing lemonade drinks at budget-friendly prices, Don Lemon opened its first branch in Tisa, Cebu and has since expanded to more than 200 branches nationwide. The brand continues to attract customers seeking refreshing beverages made for the Philippine tropical climate.

  

One of its featured branches is located along P. Del Rosario Street, beside the Don Macchiatos USC branch near Sto. Rosario Church in Cebu City, together with many other locations across the country.

## KUKU Frozen Yogurt: A Sweet Business Opportunity

  

Completing the DMGC family is KUKU Frozen Yogurt, which also launched in 2024.

  

Inspired by the phrase “Keep Up, Keep Up,” KUKU reflects the company’s commitment to continuous innovation and growth. Offering premium frozen yogurt at affordable prices, the brand has become a favorite dessert destination for families, students, and young professionals.

  

Customers can choose from:

*   Plain – ₱59
*   Combo – ₱79
*   Overload – ₱149

  

As KUKU continues to expand in Cebu and key cities across the Philippines, it offers another exciting opportunity for entrepreneurs looking to invest in a fast-growing dessert brand.

## More Than Business

At the heart of DMGC is a commitment that goes beyond business success.

  

The company believes that growth is most meaningful when it creates a positive impact on people’s lives. Through its various brands, DMGC has generated livelihood opportunities for entrepreneurs, created jobs for thousands of Filipinos, and continuously invested in initiatives that benefit communities.

  

The company has consistently extended assistance during times of crisis by supporting relief operations for communities affected by typhoons, earthquakes, floods, and other natural disasters. It also takes pride in supporting community outreach programs, sponsorships, and charitable initiatives, including assistance for cancer warriors and their families.

  

DMGC is equally committed to promoting diversity, equality, and inclusion. The company actively supports the LGBTQIA+ community through partnerships, sponsorships, and events that celebrate acceptance, respect, and empowerment. These initiatives reflect the company’s belief that true success is measured not only by business growth but also by the lives it touches and the communities it helps build.
