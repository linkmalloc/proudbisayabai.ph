Discover two premium residential projects in Cebu City that offer luxurious and convenient living: One Pacific Residence and 8 Newtown Boulevard.  

Both projects offer a variety of unit types to choose from, including studio, executive studio, and one-bedroom units. They also offer a wide range of amenities, including rooftop infinity pools, fitness centers, children's play areas, game rooms, and more.  

Residents of One Pacific Residence and 8 Newtown Boulevard receive a special tuition discount at The Mactan Newtown School of Excellence. And, with Mactan Newtown just a five-minute walk away, you'll have the best of both worlds: outstanding architecture and stunning beach views.
