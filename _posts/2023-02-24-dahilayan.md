The Philippines is well-known for its stunning landscapes and gorgeous beaches, but it also offers unique experiences that go beyond the typical destinations for tourists. The Dahilayan Forest Park Resort, a paradise for nature lovers in Manolo Fortich, Bukidnon, is one of these undiscovered treasures.  

Nestled in the heart of a lush forest, Dahilayan Forest Park Resort is a sprawling 4.5-hectare property that offers a range of exciting activities and attractions for all ages. From thrilling outdoor adventures to peaceful retreats in the midst of nature, this resort has it all.

Adventure-seekers can indulge in a range of activities such as the 840-meter dual zipline, Asia's longest zipline, which offers a stunning view of the lush forest and mountains. For those who prefer a tamer adventure, the resort also has a 320-meter zipline, rope courses, wall climbing, and an obstacle course.   

Nature enthusiasts can also go on a guided tour of the Dahilayan Forest Park, a unique eco-tourism attraction featuring rare flora and fauna found only in Bukidnon. Visitors can explore the forest and its diverse wildlife on foot, on horseback, or in a 4x4 vehicle.  

Families with children can also enjoy the playgrounds, mini-golf, and Bumper Boat, where they can have a splash and a laugh. The resort also has a petting zoo, which is perfect for animal lovers who want to get up close and personal with farm animals.

Dahilayan Forest Park Resort also has several amenities for those seeking a more unwinding experience, including the Forest Spa, where visitors may indulge in a variety of treatments, and the Forest Café, which serves excellent meals created with organic and locally produced products. In addition, guests have the option of staying in one of the resort's charming cabins or glamping tents, which offer a distinctive camping experience without compromising on comfort.  

Everyone can find something to enjoy at this location. This resort is well a visit whether you're an adventure seeker, a nature lover, or just searching for a quiet getaway. With its breathtaking views, exciting activities, and tranquil ambiance, Dahilayan Forest Park Resort is a must-visit for those who want to experience the natural beauty of the Philippines.
