LOOK: Don’t have time and budget to fly to Europe? Worry no more! Sirao Garden will make you feel like you flew to Amsterdam! Revisit and explore the breathtaking and picturesque flower farm in Sirao that is surely the best spot for your next Instagram Post🌻  

****  
**The best part?**  
You can enjoy the fields and take photos to your heart's content for an affordable entrance fee! The inspirational woman behind the paradise garden is Mrs Maria Elena Sy-Chua, a former flight attendant, was influenced by her visits to Netherlands and decided to create a Mini version of Amsterdam in her Garden. The owner is expanding her project to offer guests more fun activities.  

🧭 Operating Hours : 8am - 6pm  
##### 📌 Entrance Fee :
- Adult - Php 100 /person
- 4-12 yrs old  - Php 50 /person 
- Senior Citizen - Php 80 /person
- Prenup / Photoshoots - Php 2,500  (5 pax free) 
- Cottage - Php 300 for 4 hours  

****
##### 📋 Snacks Available:
- Traditional Biko - Php 15
- Fruit/Milk Shakes - Php 70 
- Siomai - 3 for Php 30
- Chicken Sandwich  - Php 80 
- Puto Sikwate - Php 30 
- Water - Php 20 
- Arroz Caldo - Php 60 
- Chinese Misua - Php 50 (small) - Php 70 (large)
- Coffee / Tea - Php 20

****
🌎 Exact Location: Brgy. Sirao.  The SECOND garden from Ayala heights is THE ORIGINAL ONE.  

**📌 How to get there?**   
- **Via Private Vehicle**: From JY square Lahug to Busay then to Ayala heights. Turn right to Canada drive to Brgy. Sirao.  The Second garden from Ayala heights is the original one. Expected travel time : 40 minutes   
- **Via Commute**: You may ride a motorcycle from JY Square for 50-100 pesos per person.  

>The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorcycle or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
