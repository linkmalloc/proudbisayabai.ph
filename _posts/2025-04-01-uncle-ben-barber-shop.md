Nothing beats a good haircut—it’s more than just a trim; it’s a confidence boost. A great haircut not only enhances our appearance but also makes us feel our best. That’s why choosing the right barbershop matters. It’s about finding skilled and trusted barbers in an environment where you can relax and witness the transformation unfold.  

Uncle Barber Shop, now open at Shopwise Cebu City, promises just that—a top-notch grooming experience in a welcoming and comfortable space. Founded by Jenalyn Co and Zeph Co, the brand has its roots in Pampanga, where their first barbershop, Uncle Ben, was established. The name pays tribute to Zeph’s late father, Ben, who believed in the importance of regular grooming and got a haircut every week— a habit Zeph himself emulated.  

The addition of "Uncle" to the name was intentional, symbolizing warmth, familiarity, and inclusivity. It reflects the barbershop’s mission to create a family-friendly space that caters to all ages, making every customer feel right at home while getting a premium haircut.  

Driven by their commitment to exceptional service, they expanded their business through franchising, bringing the Uncle Ben experience beyond Luzon and into Visayas and Mindanao. With their newest branch in Cebu, they aim to offer more customers a high-quality haircut in a space where they can unwind.  

As Uncle Barber Shop continues to grow, its founders remain dedicated to their vision: providing expert grooming services while making every customer feel like family.
