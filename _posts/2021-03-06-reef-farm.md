#### Reef's Farm

SUNFLOWER GARDEN mga Bai! Experience Reef's Farm in Negros Oriental!🍃😍🌻  
  
📍 Brgy. Luca, Tanjay, Negros Oriental  
  
REEF's FARM  is a showcase for Organic farming, it's main crop is rice, but intercropped with sunflower.  
  
&gt;N O T E : Due to COVID they only accept reservation basis. No Walk-ins allowed.

****
#### 📌 HOW TO GE THERE?
 
****
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
To Tanjay City (79 km from Dumaguete), ride a bus from Dumaguete Ceres Bus Terminal.Traveling time will take only about an hour and will cost approx Php 65.00 for air-conditioned bus rides and Php 55.00 for regular non-air conditioned bus rides. Once arrived in Tanjay City you can ride a pedicab (fare is P20). 6mins ride to Reef’s Farm, Brgy. Luca, Tanjay  

****
##### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
