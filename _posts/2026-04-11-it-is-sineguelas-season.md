When the small fruits start turning bright red on the trees, you already know it’s sineguelas season. Also known as seriguelas or Spanish plum, this beloved fruit is a familiar sign of summer and childhood memories in the Philippines.



In both provinces and cities, you will often see kids and even adults climbing trees or picking fallen fruits from the ground. Sineguelas has a sweet and slightly tangy taste when ripe, offering a simple yet joyful treat straight from nature.



Health Benefits of Sineguelas



Sineguelas is not only delicious but also rich in nutrients:



Rich in Vitamin C helps boost the immune system

Good for the skin contains antioxidants that protect against damage

Aids digestion high in fiber that supports gut health

Natural energy booster provides quick energy from natural sugars

Cooling effect perfect for hot summer days



More than just a fruit



Sineguelas is more than something to eat. It represents simple living, shared moments, and childhood memories.



With every bite, it brings back a time when life felt lighter and full of small joys.
