#### Rañola Beach Resort  
  
A seized day with grip, let's take a drip! Tara Bai! balikan nato diri sa laaganan in northern Cebu during the 90's - the 'Rañola Beach Resort' or KM-46 in Catmon with 50 pesos entrance fee! 💦🌴  
  
##### 🌍 Exact location:  
Brgy. Binongkalan, Catmon, Northern Cebu   
  
####  HOW TO GET THERE?
  
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
From the North Bus Terminal, ride any bus/mini bus to Catmon. Bus fare is ₱85 /head (one-way). Inform the driver to drop you off at the beach entrance of KM-46 or Rañola Beach Resort in Brgy. Binongkalan, Catmon.   
  
##### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
