He is one of the voices heard during the Fiesta Señor, proclaiming readings and rallying devotees to praise the Señor Santo Niño. In January, veteran race director Joel Juarez hopes to bring that same devotion to the streets through a Sinulog-themed night run.

  

The Sinulog Night Run, which Juarez considers his personal “halad” to the Holy Child, will take place on Jan. 16, 2027, at Gatewalk in Mandaue City, featuring a 5-kilometer race under the theme “One Beat, One Dance, One Distance.”

  

Juarez, who serves as a lector or commentator at the Basilica Minore del Santo Niño, proclaims readings and makes announcements during religious activities, including the Fiesta Señor. 

  

He also helps lead devotees in expressing their praise for the Holy Child.

  

He said he had been planning to organize a Sinulog-themed night run since 2013 but is finally making the idea happen in 2027.

  

For Juarez, the event is more than another footrace. He considers it an expression of his faith and devotion, adding that he always prays for the Holy Child to continually grant him good wisdom.

  

He also plans to donate portions of the event's proceeds to the Basilica to support its initiatives. 

  

Coco Running will likewise use the event to help sustain its scholarship beneficiaries.

  

“As a devout Catholic and devotee of the Señor Santo Niño, I wanted to offer this running event as part of my personal devotion and share something different with my fellow Cebuanos and the entire running community. Night runs are not something we often see in Cebu, so I thought this would be a good opportunity to bring back that kind of experience,” Juarez said.

  

Unlike traditional early-morning footraces, the event will give participants an opportunity to run in cooler evening conditions while enjoying music, cultural performances and the festive atmosphere associated with the Sinulog Festival.

  

The race will feature a single 5-kilometer category designed for both experienced runners and beginners. Participants will traverse portions of Gatewalk, a developing business district along the Cebu City-Mandaue City boundary.

  

Organizers are preparing music, Sinulog dancers and rhythmic drumbeats along the route to bring the event's theme to life. 

  

Raffle prizes will also be available to participants.

  

Juarez said he expects international runners to join, particularly visitors who will already be in Cebu to participate in and celebrate the Sinulog Festival.

  

“Through this event, I hope to express my devotion while continuing to provide quality and enjoyable running events for the Cebuano running community. We're also expecting international runners to join us, most of whom will already be in Cebu to participate in and celebrate the Sinulog Festival,” he added.

  

Registration is ongoing at P1,150 per participant through Coco Running's official website.

  

Each registration package includes a race shirt, finisher's medal, race bib, raffle stub and post-race snacks. The specially designed finisher's medal will feature a three-layered design highlighting the meaning and significance of the Sinulog Festival.

  

The first 500 registered runners will also receive free cut-out tops, allowing them to customize their Sinulog-themed race shirts.
