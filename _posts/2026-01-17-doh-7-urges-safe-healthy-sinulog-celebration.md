DOH 7 URGES SAFE, HEALTHY SINULOG CELEBRATION


As Cebu gears up for Sinulog 2026, the Department of Health Central Visayas (DOH 7) has released a set of health advisories to ensure that the annual festivities are enjoyed safely and responsibly.

In a public advisory, DOH 7 emphasized the importance of preparing for both heat and rain during the festival.&#x20;

Revelers were advised to bring protective items such as umbrellas, hats, hand fans, raincoats, or other gear to shield themselves from the elements.&#x20;

These simple measures are designed to prevent heat-related illnesses, sunburn, and discomfort from sudden downpours that are common in January.

Food safety is another key concern during large gatherings, the DOH noted. Festival-goers were encouraged to bring food that is less likely to spoil quickly, reducing the risk of foodborne illnesses.&#x20;

In addition, carrying sufficient drinking water was recommended to maintain proper hydration amid the crowds and under the hot sun.

DOH 7 also advised that certain groups—children, senior citizens, pregnant individuals, and people with existing health conditions—consider staying home if possible.&#x20;

The department stressed that avoiding crowded areas can help protect vulnerable populations from potential health risks during the festival.

Proper hand hygiene was highlighted as a critical precaution. Festival attendees were reminded to regularly sanitize their hands using alcohol-based sanitizers or to wash them with soap and water.&#x20;

This simple practice can help limit the spread of germs, particularly in densely packed areas where close contact with others is inevitable.

For emergencies or further health guidance, DOH 7 made available its 24/7 Regional Patient Navigation and Referral Unit. Globe and TM subscribers can dial #0711, while alternative contact numbers include 0967-221-9120, 0923-196-8249, and the landline (032) 411-6900.&#x20;

These lines are intended to provide immediate assistance and reliable health information throughout the festival period.

Through these advisories, DOH 7 reiterated its call to celebrate responsibly and safely, echoing its official campaign slogan: “Atong isaulog ang Sinulog nga Luwas ug Hapsay!”&#x20;

The department’s guidance aims to balance the joy and cultural significance of the Sinulog festival with practical measures to protect the health and well-being of everyone involved.
