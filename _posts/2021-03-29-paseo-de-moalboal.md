#### PASEO DE MOALBOAL
TRENDING‼️ UNLI Pizza, Pasta and Fries for only P189 and UNLI chicken wings for only P199 😱🤩🤤  
  
Looking for a super sulit ug lamian nga lugar to dine in your Moalboal trip? Never miss PASEO DE MOALBOAL and enjoy the food together with your family and barkada.   
  
🌍 Exact Location: Paseo de Moalboal, Brgy Tunga,Moalboal Cebu  
  
📌 How to get there?  
  
🗺 Via Commute: From South Bus, ride a bus going Moalboal. At Gaisano Grand Mall ride a pedicab or trisikad tell the driver to drop you off heading Paseo de Moalboal.   
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.  
  
 📌 Rates:   
Price ranges P99 - P350 only  
  
⭐️Best sellers:   
- Unlimited Chicken Wings - 199 per head  
- Pancit Canto - P150. 00  
- Pochero - P350.00  
- Cordon Bleu - P190. 00  
- Carbonara - P160. 00  
- Sisig - 130.00  
- Halo-Halo - starts at P120
