#### Payag Sa Babag   
  
What's NEW? EXCLUSIVE to ONE GROUP at a time, spend a day in the cool, relaxed grounds of Payag sa Babag with your favorite people and leave the hussle and stress of city life behind!🍃😍😯  
  
🕓 Open everyday, 9am to 7pm. Location is ALWAYS exclusive to 1 group.  
  
🌎 Exact Location: Babag Uno, Cebu City  
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: From Colon Street ride a Ceres Bus, Beep Bus or any Jeepneys bound for Capitol, Cebu. From there ask some locals were to ride going to Babag Uno, Cebu. Just 35 minutes from the Capitol, master the art of doing nothing as you lounge in our deck, take a nap in the payag, enjoy the swings, grill your baon and stroll around the area all in the safety of your bubble! Google Map ( Payag sa Babag)   
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.  

****
📌 AMENITIES:  
- payag &amp; the entire fenced area  
- wifi (location has signal as well)  
- indoor toilet &amp; bath  
- regular coffee maker  
- water dispenser  
- microwave  
- outdoor toilet &amp; prep/washing area  
- manual grill (charcoal available)  
- tables &amp; chairs/benches  
  
****
☀️ DAY USE:   
 - P3,600 max of 12pax. Kids 8y.o. &amp; above will be counted. Additional pax is P400/head but only up to 16pax.  
- Early entrance or late exit can be arranged at a computed fee depending on time requested.  
- Bring your own food and utensils.  
- We can deliver meals by our inhouse catering once during the day. Message further for details.  
- Please clean as you go. Leave the place as you found it.  
- No drugs or firearms are allowed on the premises. Alcohol can be consumed moderately but pls be careful as there are difficult areas to maneuver.  
- Pls do not leave your children unattended.  

****
NO OVERNIGHT. Kiddie pool available upon request @ P500.  

****
NOTE: Soft opening is on March 20.
