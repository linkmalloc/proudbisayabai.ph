Content creator Joseph Sabello is giving back following the success of his short film Gasolina by offering 2,000 liters of free gasoline, valued at around ₱300,000, to working fathers who are delivery riders or habal-habal drivers.

The initiative is in partnership with FuelTech Philippines CEO Ramon Tomontong Jr.

The fuel giveaway serves as a simple act of gratitude for fathers who help keep communities moving every day.
