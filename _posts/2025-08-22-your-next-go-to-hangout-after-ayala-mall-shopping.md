As Cebu continues to thrive as a lifestyle and entertainment destination, more people are seeking fun, relaxing spots to unwind — especially with their closest friends. Whether you’re fresh from a shopping spree or planning a night out, Bad Boys Wingz at Ayala Center Cebu is the perfect barkada hangout you’ve been looking for.

Centrally located in one of Cebu’s busiest shopping hubs, Bad Boys Wingz delivers not just good food, but a full experience. It’s a place where friends can laugh, sing, eat, and bond over flavorful comfort food and great drinks — all in one cozy, high-energy space.

*Perfect for Barkada Nights*

Whether you’re celebrating a milestone, catching up after a long week, or just out to enjoy each other’s company, Bad Boys Wingz sets the mood right. It’s spacious, vibrant, and built for barkadas who love to have a good time.

*What to Expect:*

* Spacious karaoke rooms – ideal for barkadas who love to sing their hearts out
* Cozy bar and resto vibes – relaxed and welcoming, great for sharing stories over drinks
* Crave-worthy menu – wings, burgers, pizzas, pastas, desserts, and more to satisfy the whole group
* Signature drinks – from Bauhinia Brewery craft beers to fun liquor bundles
* Daily Happy Hour – 4:00 PM–8:00 PM & 12:00 MN–4:00 AM, perfect for after-hours bonding

*Weekly Chicken Promos You Can’t Miss:*

* Mondays: Only ₱99 for a wing + drumette pair – affordable treats for the whole barkada
* Tuesdays: Get 1 FREE local beer with every boneless chicken order – yes, it’s that good!

Location: Level 1, The Terraces, Ayala Center Cebu

(Just beside Gelatissimo – the perfect post-shopping pitstop!)

*Operating Hours:*

* &#x9;Monday: 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM
* &#x9;Tuesday to Sunday: 11:00 AM – 4:00 AM

***So whether it’s a casual night out, a spontaneous catch-up, or a weekend unwind with the squad, Bad Boys Wingz is the ultimate barkada hangout spot in the heart of Cebu. Come for the food, stay for the fun, and leave with memories you’ll be laughing about for weeks.***
