King Aces Travel and Tours is thrilled to announce the official relaunch of direct flights from Cebu City to Bantayan Island bringing paradise closer than ever.

Starting October 16, you can trade the long drive and ferry queues for a quick 45-minute flight straight to one of the Philippines’ most breathtaking island destinations.

Imagine arriving just in time for Bantayan’s iconic golden sunset, feeling the sugar-white sand beneath your feet, diving into crystal-clear waters, and indulging in the freshest island seafood—all with more time to explore and unwind.

Flight Overview

* Flight Start Date: October 16, 2025
* Flight Days: Every Monday and Thursday
* Duration: Approximately 45 minutes

Starting Fare: **₱1,500**

Terminal: 6 General Aviation Road, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu (Arrive at least 1 hour before departure)

*Fare Details*

* Regular Passengers: From ₱1,500
* Children (2+ years): Full fare applies
* Infants (0–2 years): ₱560 (must sit on guardian’s lap)
* Senior Citizens & PWDs: 20% discount on base fare (excludes add-ons)

*Baggage Policy*

Hand-Carry: 7 kg

Prepaid Checked Baggage:

* 10 kg – ₱450
* 20 kg – ₱700
* Excess Baggage: ₱150 per kg (max 40 kg per passenger)
