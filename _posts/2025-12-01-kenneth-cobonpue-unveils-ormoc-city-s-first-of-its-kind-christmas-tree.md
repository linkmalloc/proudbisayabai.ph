Renowned designer Kenneth Cobonpue has arrived in Ormoc City to present his unique Christmas tree creation for the city. In the photo, he is seen with Congressman Richard Gomez and Mayor Lucy Torres-Gomez, who welcomed him as he showcased the bold and extraordinary design. Crafting something this innovative takes courage, and Cobonpue’s work marks a new artistic milestone for Ormoc.



ORMOC CITY SHINES WITH “SKYBOUND SPARKLES AND SEABORNE TALES” PARADE OF LIGHTS



The city glowed with festive brilliance during the 2025 Parade of Lights held on Monday, December 1. This year’s theme, “Skybound Sparkles and Seaborne Tales,” filled the streets with dazzling displays inspired by celestial skies and maritime wonders.



While the illuminated floats captivated the crowd, many residents shared that the true highlight of the celebration was the unity and joy felt throughout the community.
