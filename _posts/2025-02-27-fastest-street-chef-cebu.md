Cebu’s food scene has a new sensation, and it’s none other than the “Pancit Canton Ninja” at Kusina ni Osting! Known as the fastest street chef in the Philippines, this culinary expert is whipping up delicious meals in record time. While his signature Pancit Canton steals the spotlight, Kusina ni Osting also serves up a variety of savory noodle dishes, comforting sabaw (soups), and tasty canned goods, all bursting with authentic Filipino flavors.  

If you’re craving a quick yet satisfying meal, Kusina ni Osting is the place to be. Whether it’s a warm bowl of noodles, a hearty soup, or a can of Filipino comfort food, the Pancit Canton Ninja delivers everything with speed and style. Don’t miss out on the food and fun—swing by and enjoy a truly fast and flavorful experience in Cebu!
