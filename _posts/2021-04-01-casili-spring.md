#### Casili Spring
  
An enchanting hidden lagoon in Balamban. Pabugnaw sa ta bai gikan sa kainit sa panahon ug kakapoy para maka tagamtam sad ka ug ka haruhay sa makadali. Arats! 🏊⛅🍃  
  
🌍 Exact Location: Casili Spring, Maigang, Brgy. Arpili, Balamban  
  
🚶🚘 How to get there?  
  
🗺 Via Commute: From South Bus, ride a bus going Balamban. At Balamban public market ride a habal-habal going barangay Arpili. Tell the driver to drop you off heading Casili Cold Spring.   
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
