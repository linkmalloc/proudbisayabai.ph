##### Sea of Clouds in Alcoy Cebu

Any Goals for 2022? Here’s probably one that you want to experience. SEA OF CLOUDS in Cebu!  

Look up above the intense light of a low sun where streaks of the starry sky can be seen over the cliffs and walk as if you’re kissing the Earth’s terrain. Floating in Cloud 9 would be possible in Mt. Campetra in Barangay Nug-as, Alcoy, Cebu. High above the mountains, behold a fascinating beauty. Nug-as is one of the highland barangays of Alcoy, known for having one of the last forest covers on the island of Cebu.  

It might be magical to think, so good to be true, but you will be able to experience reminiscent of sea waves without riding a plane. If you come here early in the morning, you may see a dazzling sea of clouds swelling up to create mystical scenery. In addition, you can witness a stunning sunset where you can take Instagram-worthy silhouette pictures and pose behind the charm of a paradise.

Are you into camping? Well, di mabalaka bai!  

You can pitch your tent and sit under the stars as if you were reaching for the stars while shooting the breeze with your barkada or perhaps the love of your life. What a fantastic experience it would be!  

Grab a cup of coffee and hang your hammocks! Sail and wander in the sea of clouds. Feel the love, warmth, and coziness of comforts as you climb to the mountain and indulge in the breathtaking view of nature within your reach!
