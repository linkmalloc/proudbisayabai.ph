Do you miss Larsian sa Fuente? Worry no more because the original Larsian vendor is now operating and is continue serving the best BBQ experience to the Cebuanos.  

Despite the closure of the famous Larsian sa Fuente, the original stall **Larsian BBQ sa Cebu- Two Brothers since 1979 in Larsian Fuente** is still committed to serve the cebuanos delicious traditional Barbeque. It was established in 1979 in the original location of Larsian Fuente. No one can stop the 43-year BBQ specialty that du e to public demand, it has opened a stall in a new location.  

What makes **Larsian BBQ sa Cebu- Two Brothers since 1979 in Larsian Fuente** unique is their special Dicer Sauce.  

Exact Location: 
Cogon Pardo, Cebu City. Landmark Three Sixty Pharmacy
