#### The Alch  

A place that gives you a cozy, homey feeling and has everything you need. **The Alch** is a private and safe vacation home suitable for family and barkada getaway. Located in Don Salvador Benedicto, Negros Occidental it offers a beautiful view of the mountains including Mt. Canlaon.

They have a lot of instagrammable corners with serenity Bali feels, a pool side cinema, A-framed Loft Houses and the lovely garden is an extra feature!

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/the_alch/2.jpg)  

Imagine these scenarios: breathing in the cool mountain air while sipping your hot morning coffee, taking a dip on the pool while some of your loved ones grilling barbeques for lunch, and then at night, watching a movie or two on the pool side cinema. That sounds perfect, right ?

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/the_alch/3.jpg)  

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/the_alch/4.jpg)  

The interiors of the A-framed Loft Houses are designed to to make sure guests feel at home. They got relaxing decor and comfortable furniture that matches the vibe.

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/the_alch/5.jpg)  

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/the_alch/6.jpg)  

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/the_alch/7.jpg)  

Their main goal is to be a place of peace, calm, and meditation. At the same time, a place for fun, family, and friends. The owner of the place considers the Alch a second home and hopes for their guests to feel the same. Nindot jud na basta Bisaya! Tara mga bai! 🍃☀😎  

##### Overnight stay rates:   
- 6000 on weekdays (Mon, Tue, Wed or Thurs)   
- 7500 on weekends (Fri, Sat or Sun), up to 7 persons per house.   

****
📌Each private A-frame loft house has 3 double sized beds. Guests have access to own mini pool, kitchenette with fridge &amp; microwave, hot &amp; cold shower, cable TV &amp; cooking station. Big pool, gazebo &amp; poolside including the garden are also accessible.  

> Outside food &amp; drinks allowed, no corkage fees  
NO pets allowed  
For overnight stays by reservation only
