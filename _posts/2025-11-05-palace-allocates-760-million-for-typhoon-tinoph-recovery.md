The Office of the President has officially allocated **₱760 million** in financial assistance to 38 cities and provinces severely affected by Typhoon #TinoPH. This fund is aimed at supporting the **immediate recovery of affected families** and the **rehabilitation of devastated areas** across the Visayas, Mindanao, and Southern Luzon.

The financial breakdown is as follows:

* **₱50,000,000 each:** Cebu, Capiz, Surigao del Norte, Iloilo, Bohol, Negros Occidental
* **₱40,000,000 each:** Eastern Samar, Surigao del Sur, Southern Leyte, Antique, Aklan
* **₱30,000,000 each:** Leyte, Masbate
* **₱20,000,000 each:** Guimaras, Agusan del Norte, Dinagat Islands
* **₱10,000,000 each:** Biliran, Camarines Sur, Sorsogon, Misamis Oriental, Negros Oriental, Palawan
* **₱5,000,000 each:** Albay, Romblon, Batangas, Northern Samar, Siquijor, Quezon, Samar, Agusan del Sur, Laguna, Zamboanga City, City of Manila, Camiguin, Occidental Mindoro, Camarines Norte, Zamboanga del Norte, Iligan City
