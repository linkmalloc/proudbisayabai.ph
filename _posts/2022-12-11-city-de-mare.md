Have you been looking forward to a fun-filled weekend with the family and kids? We got you covered!  

CITY DI MARE- “Come together for Christmas” helps you celebrate the holiday cheer. Bond over delicious eats and fun moments with your family while being serenaded by a live acoustic performance. It is known as a master-planned neighborhood in Cebu City that encourages a full-fledged modern live-work-play lifestyle surrounded by nature.   

After a protracted pandemic, the season wavers a paintbrush, making everything softer and more charming. City di Mare ideally situated community, which is extremely accessible, is expected to become Cebu City's next leading residential, commercial, and economic hub. It is perfectly framed by the eastern sea coast and mountain views. This Christmas, with sacred and melancholy music pouring out of every musical pore, it is time for us to sing pieces of coal in a festive interaction, especially with our friends and loved ones.
