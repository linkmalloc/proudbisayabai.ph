##### Bakoo Caribbean RestoBar  

While sailing has made its mark in the world, Bakoo Caribbean Restobar in Maribago, Lapu-Lapu City, is a traveler’s and diner’s haven waiting to be discovered!  

A place where you may gather with friends and family to savor the true flavors of the Caribbean Menu. Caribbean food is a fascinating, multi-faceted dish of the numerous cultures and heritages that have produced the origin of a dynamic region as vivid and varied as the islands themselves. Here in Bakoo, they will set forth a breathtaking atmosphere that is instagrammable, perfect for your feed, food style and poses!

You won't simply be dazzled by their delicacies, but their cocktails will undoubtedly have you pumped up. It will be so much fun to laugh with your friends while you sit, eat, drink, and toss glasses together. The restobar is reasonably priced, and the food is delectable, so you can indulge while taking in the stylish environment.
Avast! There is more than its name suggests!  

They have an Indonesian Cuisine that will make you say “WAHAHA!” It is an expression used by Indonesians when indulged with anything spicy platters. They also serve a variety of Indonesian favorites, and comforting foods made on-site using only the best family recipes to bring a nostalgic taste of Indonesia to your seat or even to your home. It is a one-stop place that will make you taste different foods in different regions.   

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/bakoo_caribbean_restobar/12.jpg)

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/bakoo_caribbean_restobar/19.jpg)

Every Friday, there is a salsa night. They also have a live band on Saturdays and a Roast Dinner on Sundays. Grabe ka lingaw jud! Hapit na dayon ta diri! 🥂💃 🍖 🎸

They offer delivery and takeaway. Enjoy your favorite Caribbean and Indonesian cuisine here as you will be delighted!  

###### Operating Hours:
1PM -12AM, Tuesdays - Sundays  

###### Exact Location:
Bakoo Caribbean Restobar , Maribagp, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu Ph 🇵🇭
