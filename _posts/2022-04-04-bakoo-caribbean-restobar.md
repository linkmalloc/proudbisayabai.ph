##### Bakoo Caribbean RestoBar  

TRENDING: Heading to Lapu-Lapu City? Here’s one we recommend! Refreshing Caribbean vibe, cocktails to hype you up and a very affordable delicious meal to enjoy.  Plus they have a Salsa night every Friday, live band every Saturday and a Sunday Roast dinner. Grabee ka lingaw jud! Hapit na dayon ta diri!  🥂💃 🍖 🎸

🕙Operating Hours: 1PM -12AM, Tuesdays - Sundays

🌎 Exact Location: Bakoo Caribbean Restobar , Maribagp, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu, Philippines

###### How to get there? 

🗺 Via commute: From SM City, ride a vhire going Gaisano Mactan. Ride a multicab heading Maribago and tell the driver to drop you off at Bagumbayan 2 then sulod sa skina sa Aquaventure Whitetip Dive Supply and loook for Bakoo Carribean Restobar.  
🗺 Via private vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze/ Google Map. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze / G Map: 
📍https://goo.gl/maps/PKaM8kzQ1gNErMNB8
