Cebu’s grandest celebration is just around the corner, and this upcoming Sinulog Festival promises to be one of the most vibrant and most diverse yet. After months of preparation, prayer, and anticipation, organizers have officially released both the Sinulog 2026 schedule of religious and cultural activities and the final list of competing contingents for next year’s festivities.

Bringing together faith, culture, music, and movement, Sinulog 2026 carries the spirit of unity and devotion as Cebuano communities and guest performers nationwide gather to honor Señor Santo Niño.

**SCHEDULE OF ACTIVITIES (as of December 10, 2025)**

January 8, 2026

• Walk with Jesus

January 9, 2026

• Sinulog 2026 Kick-Off and Opening Salvo

January 10, 2026

• Brass Band Competition

• Sinulog sa Kabataan sa Dakbayan

• Concert: Battle of the Bands

January 15, 2026

• Walk with Mary

January 16, 2026

• Sinulog Festival Queen 2026 – Coronation Night

• Sinulog Dance Crew Grand Showdown

January 17, 2026

• Fluvial Procession

• Solemn Veneration of the Miraculous Image of the Santo Niño

• Sinulog Fireworks Competition

January 18, 2026

• Sinulog 2026 Grand Parade and Grand Finale

January 19, 2026

• Sinulog 2026 Awarding Ceremonies

Organizers note that details may still be updated.

**A Festival of Faith, Rhythm, and Cultural Pride**

With an expanded list of contingents and a packed schedule blending solemn devotion and high-energy performances, Sinulog 2026 is shaping up to be a celebration that honors tradition while embracing new creative expressions.

From the sacred Walk with Jesus to the electrifying Grand Parade, Cebu is once again ready to welcome devotees, dancers, and visitors in a celebration that unites every Cebuano under one cry:

Pit Señor! Viva Señor Santo Niño!
