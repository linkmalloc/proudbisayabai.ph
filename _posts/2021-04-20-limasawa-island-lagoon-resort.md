#### Island Lagoon Resort 

Island Lagoon Resort is a must-visit place once you are in Limasawa. It is an absolutely beautiful lagoon with turquoise water surrounded by amazing rock formations.

It’s a very cool place for cliff-diving, swimming, and snorkeling. If you’re lucky, you can see turtles coz they are often seen in these areas. You can witness the stunning sunset by climbing to the boulders. Don’t worry there are stairs (note: need to be a little cautious especially for children and the elderly). 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/pbb1/image/upload/v1618934593/island_lagoon_resort/tcxjw70ulrmjkkcuplfj.jpg)

Cottages and rooms are available for overnight stays. If you want to build a tent, don’t worry, the resort has also space to set up your tents.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/pbb1/image/upload/v1618934607/island_lagoon_resort/kdgv7q23quhwmyfkoept.jpg)

Island Lagoon Resort is more than awesome! But it is best to experience it with your friends or family.  

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/pbb1/image/upload/v1618934599/island_lagoon_resort/gd8ccpsmyar0zij69s5a.jpg)

#### HOW TO GET THERE   

🌎 Located at: Limasawa, Southern Leyte, Philippines  
  
✈️ Via Air: The main jump-off point is the airport in San Jose, Tacloban City. Travel time usually takes about an hour to and from Manila and approx. 45 minutes to and from Cebu. Gikan sa airport sakay ka ug taxi or mini van padung sa exact location. Much better to have your friend or kaila nimo if first time nimo molaag sa lugar to guide you.   
  
🛳 Via Sea: Walay deritso biyahe gikan Cebu City to Leyte. However, you can take the ferry to Ormoc then take the taxi, mini van or van going to Leyte.
