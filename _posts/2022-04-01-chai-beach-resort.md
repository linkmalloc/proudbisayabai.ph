Cebu's adverse weather, busy streets, and mundane lifestyle can wear on your nerves at times. The best
therapy at such times is to simply pack your bags, enlist the help of a few traveling pals, and hit the road.
It won't be a long drive, don’t worry! It's only in Medellin, Cebu, which is known for its nice scenery,
colorful cottages, and unique rock formations.  

Sun’s out, summer is here! Who doesn't look forward to spending time in the water? Definitely not me!
It's one of those magical moments when everything feels at home, calm, and serene. There is no worry for
tomorrow, only joy for today. It will surely make your hearts flutter with astonishment at the sight of
crystal-clear blue seas and the soft lapping of waves against a sandy shore.

Operating hours:
• Check in time - 8AM
• Check out time - 5PM on same day

Exact Location: Purok IPA 2 Mahawak, Medellin North Cebu  

How to get there?
Via Commute: From Cebu City, ride any jeepney, Beep bus, or Ceres bus going to North bus
terminal at SM City. Then tell the driver to drop you off at Medellin public market. From there ride a
habal-habal going to the exact location. Most of the drivers already know or familiar with the place.
Bus estimated fare is P130-150 per way.

Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding motorbike or
your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze: Google Map link: [https://g.co/kgs/9jLxnR](https://g.co/kgs/9jLxnR)
Daily Use -1, 200 PESOS only - good for 4/5 persons
