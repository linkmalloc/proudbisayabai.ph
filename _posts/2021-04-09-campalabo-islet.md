#### Campalabo Islet   
  
YES to DAGAT na Bai! Naka ligo naka diri? The pristine white sand bar of Campalabo Islet in Pinamungajan, Cebu. 🏖💦😯  
  
🌏 Exact Location: Campalabo Islet, Pinamungahan, Cebu  
  
📌 How to get there?  
  
🗺 Via Commute: Ride a bus from Cebu South Bus Terminal going to Pinamungahan. Drop off at Bugsay Public Beach Resort. After, naa ray mga musugat sa Bugsay Beach Resort muask if magpa-Campalabo ba (Public Motor Banca). Fare for Bangka is around 1,500 Pesos good for 12-15 persons back and forth. Ride from Bugsay Beach Resort to Campalabo Islet is approx 30mins.  
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.   
  
📌 FEES:   
- Environmental fee: P50 per head  
- Bangka fee: P1,500 good for 12-15 persons (back and forth)   

****
💡 TIPS:   
- Buy your food and drinks ahead before jumping into the boat, since there is no vendors in the islet.  
- No cottage. You may bring your own tables and chairs.  
- You can’t put tents in there.
