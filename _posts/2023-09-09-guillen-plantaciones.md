\#EmbracingLokal: Embark on a journey to Guillen Plantaciones, an idyllic eco-farm and glamping destination designed for those yearning to escape the fast-paced rhythms of urban living. Perched at an elevation of 784 meters above sea level and a mere 40-minute drive from Cebu City, Guillen Plantaciones offers a warm welcome with its panoramic vistas. Here, you'll be treated to a 360-degree view of lush greenery, surrounded by the beauty of Negros Island, the serene Tanon Strait, and the rugged mountain ridges of Toledo. As you immerse yourself in the misty ambiance, don't miss the chance to witness the spectacular sunset that graces the tranquil haven.  

Picture yourself picking your own vegetables for breakfast, knowing that they were grown right on the property. This resort seamlessly blends sustainable farming with the preservation of its breathtaking natural surroundings. Energy-efficient lighting, water-saving fixtures, and sustainable building materials are just some of the features that contribute to the resort's low carbon footprint. Additionally, they are already working on their upcoming amenities and facilities, such as the club house, wedding garden, archery and firing range, prayer and meditation room, among others. Imagine waking up to the sight of lush, green gardens and the soothing sounds of nature all around you. The resort is situated within a protected area, and every effort is made to maintain the integrity of the local ecosystem. Hiking trails, nature walks, and guided tours allow guests to explore the pristine wilderness and learn about the importance of conservation.

The Guillen Plantaciones is more than just a resort; it's a sanctuary where sustainability, natural beauty, and relaxation harmoniously coexist. By promoting sustainable farming and preserving the environment, they not only provide an unforgettable experience for its guests but also set a shining example of responsible tourism. If you're seeking a retreat that combines beauty with a deep commitment to the planet, a stay at Guillen Plantaciones should be at the top of your travel wish list.  

###### Array of activities:
- Teepee A-shaped cottages 
- Horseback riding
- Dip in our infinity swimming pool
- Trail with our ATV's
- “Pick & Pay” section where you can harvest available vegetables and strawberries 
- Walking & jogging path
- Dine & chill in our newest "Veranda Resto + Bar" 
- Overnight camping and glamping with a bonfire to heat up your freezing evening.
