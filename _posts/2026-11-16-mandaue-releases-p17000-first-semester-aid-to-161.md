Mandaue City has released the first tranche of scholarship assistance to 161 new city scholars, with each student receiving P17,000 for the current semester under the local government's education program.

The beneficiaries are entitled to P34,000 annually, equivalent to P17,000 per semester, to help cover education-related expenses.

The latest distribution also marked a shift in the city's scholarship assistance system, with the local government adopting direct cash payouts to address delays under the previous reimbursement arrangement.

Previously, scholars had to submit receipts and other supporting documents before the city could reimburse their expenses. The revised process aims to give students more direct access to their financial assistance without waiting for reimbursement claims to undergo processing.

Councilor and Committee on Education chairman Atty. Joel Seno assured the beneficiaries that the city would continue supporting their education.

“We will not be in front of you, we have your back, naa mi sa inyong likuran,” Seno said.

Mayor Thadeo “Jonkie” Ouano, who led the distribution with his sister, Cebu's lone district Representative Emmarie “Lolypop” Ouano-Dizon, said education remains a priority of the city government.

“Usa ka importante ug prayoridad sa City of Mandaue ang edukasyon,” Ouano said.

The mayor also recognized the city councilors' role in improving the release process, particularly in addressing difficulties encountered under the reimbursement system.

The turnover ceremony took place at the Ibabao-Estancia Gym on Nov. 9, with scholars from various barangays attending.

Councilors Ben Basiga, Kevin Cabahug, Eugene Andaya, Jennifer Del Mar and Anjong Ouano-Icalina, as well as Ibabao-Estancia Barangay Captain Tingy Echavez, also attended the activity.
