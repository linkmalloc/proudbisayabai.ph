BISAYA EATS: Humba remains one of the most beloved dishes in the Visayas, known for its tender pork slow cooked in a rich, savory sweet sauce that has become a staple in many Filipino homes.



Often served during family gatherings, celebrations, and everyday meals, the dish is best paired with steaming hot rice. Its distinct flavor and melt in your mouth texture have made it a favorite across generations.



For many Cebuanos and Visayans, humba is more than just a dish. It is a taste of home, tradition, and comfort.&#x20;
