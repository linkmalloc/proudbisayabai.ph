Experience the beauty of nature at Pinipisakan Falls in Brgy. Bay Ang, San Jorge, Samar, where crystal-clear waters cascade into turquoise pools surrounded by lush greenery. Every moment here feels like stepping into your own main-character scene, perfect for nature lovers and adventure seekers.



Witness the waterfall in its pure, untouched beauty. Take nothing but photos, leave nothing but footprints, and savor every second of this serene escape.



💰 Entrance Fee: ₱500 per person (minimum group of 4)



Inquiries: San Jorge Municipal Tourism, Culture, Arts and Heritage Office



How to get there:

From Tacloban City, take a bus or van heading to Calbayog City or Catbalogan City, and ask to be dropped off at San Jorge town proper. From the town center, hire a tricycle or habal-habal to Brgy. Bay Ang. A short trek from the drop-off point leads to the falls, so wear comfortable footwear and be ready for a bit of adventure.
