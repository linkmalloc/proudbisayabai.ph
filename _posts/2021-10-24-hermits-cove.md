###### Hermit’s Cove, Aloguisan  

LOOK: Naka ari naka? This is one of the best Family-Friendly destinations in Southern Cebu! With only Php 100 per person you get to avail free cottages and enjoy a fun day at the beach!  

The hidden Gem of Aloguinsan, Cebu. The development of the area into a top tourist destination started a few years ago when the provincial government partnered with several national agencies that eventually lead the locals to transform their main economic livelihood from agricultural to eco - tourism. The area is now managed by former fisher folks and farmers through a cooperative and the supervision of the local government.  

🌎 Exact Location: Brgy. Kantabogon, Aloguinsan, Southern Cebu

###### How to get there?

Google Map Link: Hermit's Cove Kantabogon Rd, Aloguinsan, Cebu [https://g.co/kgs/cphn76](https://g.co/kgs/cphn76)  

Via Commute: Ride a bus/vhire going to South Cebu approx. 2-3hrs travel time from Cebu City. estimated fare is P100.  

The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
