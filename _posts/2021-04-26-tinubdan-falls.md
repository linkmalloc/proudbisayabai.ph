#### Tinubdan Falls    
<center>Gasa sa Kina-iyahan nga angay AMPINGAN 💦🍂😯</center>  

****
Tinubdan falls is one of the natural heritage in Brgy. Tabili Catmon. Cebu. It's 57 kms away from Cebu City and 6 kms away from the main highway. It is a multi-cascade type of waterfalls, the water descends in a series of rock steps and flows into a natural swimming pool. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/pbb1/image/upload/v1619400935/tinubdan_falls/j6ahwccnzpn2svtd0ifi.jpg)  

When going to the falls, expect a little hike for about 10 minutes from the designated parking area. There is no entrace fee but you can give a donation (any amount will do). Tables and chairs are available for rent and there is no corkage fee but make sure not to litter around.  

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/pbb1/image/upload/v1619401044/tinubdan_falls/okwixwz4sccsgqgncfve.jpg)  

It is known that there are a lot of visitors that come here during weekends, so it is best to experience Tinubdan Falls during weekdays.

****
##### HOW TO GET THERE   
  
🗺 Via Commute: From North Bus Terminal (SM City Cebu) ride a Bus bound for Catmon (just advise the driver to stop sa JM Store (Fare) – Php 80.00. From there ride a Habal-Habal (Back&amp;Forth) – Php 100.   
  
🗺 Via Waze: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
