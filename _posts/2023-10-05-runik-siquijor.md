Experience the next level of island vibes at Runik Siquijor, a newly opened cliff destination with a bar spot that offers stunning views of the ocean and sunset.  

For just ₱300, visitors can enjoy all-day access to the beach club and pool, as well as a consumable food and drink credit. The food menu features a variety of delicious international and Filipino dishes, while the beverage menu includes a wide selection of refreshing cocktails and other drinks.  

On select nights, Runik Siquijor also hosts live music and DJ performances, making it the perfect place to dance the night away under the stars.
