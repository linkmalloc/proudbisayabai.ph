There are days when all you need is a quiet beach, a beautiful sunset, and the sound of waves washing away the stress of everyday life. If you’re looking for a quick escape from Cebu City, Maravilla Beach in Tabuelan is one destination worth adding to your travel bucket list.



Nestled along the northwestern coast of Cebu, Maravilla Beach is known for its powdery white sand, clear blue waters, and laid-back atmosphere. Unlike some of the province’s more crowded beach destinations, Maravilla offers a peaceful retreat where visitors can slow down, unwind, and reconnect with nature.



One of the highlights of visiting Maravilla is its breathtaking sunset. As the sun dips below the horizon, the sky transforms into shades of orange, pink, and gold, creating a picture-perfect view that keeps travelers coming back. Whether you’re strolling along the shoreline, swimming in its calm waters, or simply relaxing by the beach, every moment here feels like a mini-vacation.



The beach is ideal for barkada outings, family trips, and even solo adventures. With several beachfront resorts and cottages available, visitors can choose between a day trip or an overnight stay to fully experience the beauty of the area.



**How to get there**



*BY BUS:* Head to the Cebu North Bus Terminal and take a bus bound for Tabuelan. The journey takes around three to four hours. Upon reaching Tabuelan town proper, you can hire a habal-habal or local transport to Maravilla Beach.



*BY PRIVATE VEHICLE:* Drive north from Cebu City via the Cebu North Road toward Tabuelan. Travel time is approximately three to four hours, depending on traffic conditions.



**Travel Tips**



• Visit during late afternoon to catch the famous Maravilla sunset.

• Bring extra cash, as not all establishments accept digital payments.

• Pack light but don’t forget sunscreen, swimwear, and a camera.

• Help preserve the beach by practicing responsible tourism and proper waste disposal.



If you’re searching for a destination that combines natural beauty, tranquility, and accessibility, Maravilla Beach proves that paradise can be found just a few hours from Cebu City.



**Entrance Fees and Expenses**



Visitors are advised to prepare a budget for transportation, food, parking, and cottage or resort fees. Rates may vary depending on the resort or beach area you choose. It is recommended to contact resorts directly for the latest entrance fees, accommodations, and day-use rates. Some public beach areas may have minimal environmental or parking fees.



Note: Publicly available fee information varies by facility and may change, so it’s best to verify current rates with local operators before visiting.
