Authorities will intensify traffic enforcement along the entire Transcentral Highway starting Nov. 23, following reports of illegal road racing and reckless driving that have raised safety concerns among residents and motorists.

  

The Cebu Police Provincial Office (Cebu PPO), Land Transportation Office (LTO) Cebu and concerned local government units agreed to expand the Motorist Discipline Zone beyond the area where authorities reported racing incidents.

  

The move followed the circulation of a video showing motorists allegedly racing along Barangay Gaas in Balamban on Nov. 21.

  

On the same day, the Balamban Municipal Police Station issued Temporary Operator's Permits (TOPs) to 22 motorists apprehended for various traffic violations during an enforcement operation, according to the Cebu PPO.

  

The LTO will also issue show cause orders requiring the apprehended motorists to explain their violations and undergo the corresponding administrative proceedings.

  

Police Col. Abubakar Udang Mangelen Jr., Cebu PPO provincial director, met with LTO Cebu representatives, Gaas barangay officials, local police and residents to discuss stronger enforcement measures.

  

During the meeting, the agencies agreed to cover the entire mountain highway under the discipline zone instead of limiting operations to the area where the alleged road racing took place.

  

Authorities will conduct joint operations against reckless driving, unauthorized road racing, unroadworthy vehicles, overloading and other traffic violations.

  

They also reminded motorists to observe speed limits and traffic regulations, practice road courtesy and ensure their vehicles are roadworthy.

  

Barangay Gaas will likewise strengthen enforcement of its existing ordinances on curfew, noise pollution and speed regulation.

  

Mangelen stressed that motorists share responsibility for keeping the highway safe and warned against using the mountain road for racing or other dangerous driving activities.

  

The Cebu PPO also urged residents who witness reckless driving to report incidents to authorities rather than confront motorists directly, warning that such encounters could lead to altercations and additional risks.

  

Authorities emphasized that the Transcentral Highway is a public road, not a racetrack, and that motorists who violate traffic regulations may face corresponding penalties.
