Residents in parts of Liloan Cebu are issuing desperate appeals for assistance days after Typhoon Tino, reporting that their communities are overwhelmed by debris, dead animals and a worsening stench.

According to residents, the situation has become critical with no clean water and growing concern for children still in the area, highlighting the slow pace of clearing operations and relief distribution in some neighborhoods.

The growing health hazard from decaying animals and possible human casualties has left families urging the local and provincial government for immediate intervention.
