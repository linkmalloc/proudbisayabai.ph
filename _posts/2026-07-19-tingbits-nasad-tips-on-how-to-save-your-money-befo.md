Every time we receive our paycheck, we immediately treat ourselves — food, clothes, activities, you name it all! But sometimes, our shopping sprees get too much that we find our pockets almost empty. We find ourselves wondering why our money ran out so easily. To help you avoid experiencing this situation, here are tips to save your money and to avoid saying "_tingbits nasad."_

  

### **No to impulsive buying.**

  

One of the activities we do once we get paid is to shop. However, the shopping that we usually do does not automatically mean buying important and needed items such as groceries, sometimes we go overboard. We often purchase goods we do not need, resulting in a financial loss. That is why you should weigh if a product is essential before buying it and set aside if it is not necessary.

  

### **Budget before spending.**

  

Before spending your salary, make sure that you have a budget plan for your finances that prioritizes your financial obligations such as your loans, bills, savings and necessary allowances. After budgeting, you can lavishly spend your extra money and treat yourself without worrying about financial issues!

  

### **Reduce smoking expenses.**

  

When you smoke on a daily basis, it can contribute to your financial setbacks. Quitting or cutting back on smoking can not only help maintain good physical health, but also it can help you avoid experiencing financial problems.

  

### **Bring your lunch.**

  

It is not bad to spend on lunch as long as you have a designated budget for it, but if you want to save money or have no food allowances, it is better if you bring your own lunch or _baon_. Aside from being able to save, bringing your own food can also help to prevent overspending. Having your _baon_ can surely help you cut costs and it also gives you the freedom to eat a meal of your own choice.

  

### **Walk if you can.**

  

If your destination is only a short distance away and you want to spend less, do not commute and just go for a walk. Spending on Grab, Moveit or any public transport might be too much for a destination that is located nearby, so walk and at the same time get yourself your needed exercise.

  

### **Make use of your gym memberships.** 

  

Gym memberships are known to be expensive. So to avoid spending on a useless lifestyle subscription, make use of the gym membership you availed. Or if you know you cannot commit to such a lifestyle, do not avail the membership and just try doing home workouts that require you to spend less.

  

### **Do not deprive yourself.**

  

It is understandable that one wants to save their money for more essential financial obligations and responsibilities. However, this does not mean that you have to deprive yourself from things that you enjoy. You just have to learn to budget your salary before spending your money and get yourself a well-deserved treat!

  

  

Money is important to everyone as it helps us access and purchase basic necessities. That is why there is a need to use it wisely in order to fulfill your financial obligations and needs without any worries. And with these tips, you can now ensure that your bills, allowances and savings are prioritized financially, while also giving you the opportunity to pamper yourself.
