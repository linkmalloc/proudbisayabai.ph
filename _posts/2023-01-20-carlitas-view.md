Want to escape from City life? Rekindle your soul by taking in good air in a relaxing place!  

Be stashed away in a highland forest with a view of the sea portraying the ideal excursion for leisure and recharge. Witness the tremendous palm trees, coastlines, depictions of fish and wildlife, and the ocean breeze that await as you take beautiful walks at&nbsp;Carlitas View Beach Resort.

Check every commodity, including having an accessible beachfront address and all the water activities you need to relieve the monotony. It is comforting on one's feet and healthy for the mind, body, and spirit. Additionally, they provide excellent local delicacies and services while you take in stunning views from your balcony or outdoors by the pool. How could you top a tasty dinner outside beneath the stars? Consider adding a few cocktails to your fun-filled evening with your significant other, family, or friends. While you are cuddled in the pristine area of Dalaguete, Cebu, immerse yourself in nature and take in the various natural wonders. Each room is nicely decorated and furnished, ensuring a peaceful atmosphere for everyone.   

Kinahanglan sad ta ug pahuway. Grab the opportunity to slowdown and experience beach vibes at Carlitas View in Dalaguete, Cebu!    

Business hours: 8AM - 5PM daily   
Location: Banhigan, Dalaguete, Southern Cebu

###### How to get there? 
Via Commute: From the South Bus Terminal, take a bus going to South Cebu. Tell the driver that you are going to Carlitas estimated fare (150 pesos). Carlitas is just along the way about 2-3hours travel time from Cebu City, with sign board and very visible to travellers.   

The place can be navigated on Waze. / Google Map Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze/GMap: https://g.co/kgs/zCRpEu  

Day Use Rate is for Php 300 PESOS Only!   
Inclusive of Unlimited Swimming Pool use from 8:00AM to 5:00PM, and Free Cottage Use.
