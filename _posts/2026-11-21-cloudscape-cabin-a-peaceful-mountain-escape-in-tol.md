Looking for a quiet place to unwind and reconnect with nature? Nestled in Pangamihan, Toledo City, Cebu, approximately 700 meters above sea level, Cloudscape Cabin offers a refreshing mountain escape for those who want to slow down, enjoy the cool highland atmosphere, and experience a relaxing camping getaway.

  

Whether you’re planning a weekend adventure with friends, a simple family outing, or a peaceful overnight stay, this destination offers a chance to step away from the busy city and enjoy the beauty of the outdoors.

  

<img src="https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/3506/ffce961795239585-2.JPG" style="width:416">

  

### What to expect

  

Cloudscape Cabin provides several complimentary amenities to make your camping experience more convenient:

  

\* Free parking for guests

\* Free restroom access

\* Free electricity, available near the restroom

\* Free water, excluding drinking water

\* 24-hour stay for campers

\* Optional bonfire for ₱490

\* CCTV surveillance within the premises

  

Guests are encouraged to bring their own tents and camping essentials. It is also advisable to prepare drinking water, warm clothing, flashlights, and personal necessities.

  

### Getting there

  

Cloudscape Cabin is located in Pangamihan, Toledo City, Cebu. Visitors coming from Cebu City may explore routes through the Transcentral Highway or the Naga–Uling Road. It is best to request the exact location pin from the management before travelling.

  

### A reminder before your visit

  

While several amenities are offered for free, the standard camping or entrance rate was not provided. Contact the management directly for updated rates, availability, and reservation arrangements before your trip.

  

For inquiries, updates, and more information, visit the Cloudscape Cabin Facebook page.

  

### Find your next escape

  

Sometimes, the best getaway does not require a long journey or an elaborate itinerary. A peaceful mountain setting, a tent under the open sky, and good company may be all you need.

  

At around 700 meters above sea level, Cloudscape Cabin is worth adding to your list of places to explore in Cebu for your next outdoor adventure.
