##### Paraiso Tropicale - Cabin Beach

\#Siargao Feels: Siargao inspired place in Cebu? Yes to it in Oslob, Cebu! 🌴💦🏄‍♂️
  
It is an intimate escape open for rental for friends nestled between lush green forests and white-sand rocky beach. It is frequented by sea turtles and manta rays, home to various Philippine native trees. It is a perfect space for intimate time for couples, young adults, and small groups who are interested to relax and rejuvenate.  

🌍 Exact Location: Oslob, Southern Cebu, Philippines  

###### How to get there?

🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze or Google Map. Location can be found by pinning Lickey Buns' in Google Maps and Waze, it’s best to navigate via Waze/ GMap. 

###### What to expect? 
****
- Calorie-burning 88-step wooden stair puzzle
- Naturally-ventilated private cabin good for 4 pax (overnight)
- Two-storey cabana good for 4 pax (overnight)  
****
📌The naturally-ventilated cabin has:
- 1 king-sized bed
- 1 pull-out sofa bed
- Toilet and bath  

****
📌 The kitchen has:
- Stove and gas
- Microwave
- Toaster
- Fridge
- Air fryer
- Water boiler  

****
📌 Note:
- Standard check-in at 02:00PM 
- Standard check-out at 12:00NN
- Meal good for your stay (and for your fur babies)
- Mosquito-repelling lotion for your skin preference
- Personalized toiletries, coral-safe sunblock
- Smart, Sun, Dito mobile networks have good reception; Globe have poor accessibility.
- Bring your own towels
****
