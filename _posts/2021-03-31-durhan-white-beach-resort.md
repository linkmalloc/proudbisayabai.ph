#### Durhan White Beach Resort  
  
What are your plans for this weekend? This is for you, a peaceful stay away from the buzzling  city life at  Durhan White Beach Resort in Tabuelan, Cebu! 🏖💦☀️  
  
🌎 Exact Location: Tabuelan, Cebu   
  
👉🏻 Click the full details on how to get there and more about their place: [https://www.facebook.com/durhanwhitebeachresort](https://www.facebook.com/durhanwhitebeachresort)  
  
📌 Room Rates:   
(Rooms Average Rates  \| VIEW)  
- aircon room good for 8 - P5800 Garden  
- aircon room good for 6 - P4600 Garden  
- aircon room good for 4 - P3900 Garden  
- aircon room good for 3 - P2800 Seaview  
- aircon room good for 2 (with hot and cold shower) P2200 Seaview  
- aircon room good for 2 - P2000 Garden  
- aircon room good for 2 - P2000 Seaview  
- non-aircon resthouse good for 8 - P4800 Seaview  
- non-aircon room good for 4 - P1600 Seaview  
- For excess person: aircon with foam P450   
- Non-aircon - P350   
  
****
###### 📌 TENTS:  
 (available only when rooms are fully booked)  
- good for 2 – PHP1000  
- good for 3 – PHP1300  

*Inclusions: foams, blankets and pillows and free breakfast*  

****
###### 📌 DAY USE:   
- Weekdays: PHP349 (with Set Meal)  
- Weekends: PHP399 (with lunch buffet)  
- Kids 5-11 years old are half the price.   
- Food and beverage discount for 10%.  
- Additional day use access: billiards, beach volleyball, beach access and complimentary Wi-Fi.  
 
*****
###### 🧭 Day use starts at 8AM – 6PM  
Prices are subject to 10% service charge.  
  
>‼️ Note: Check in time is 2:00 PM and check out time is 12:00 noon. Prices are subject to 10% service charge. Room rates are subject to change based on market demands.  

****
###### 🍽 LOLA PERTA’S RESTAURANT:   
- Offers variety of food from soup, vegetables and noodles to seafood, pork and chicken main courses.  
- Open for customers who only wanted to dine in.  
- Open hours: 6 AM – 10 PM on weekdays and 6 AM – 12 MN on weekends.  
- In-house guests can avail weekend buffets at only PHP299.  
- Prices are subject to 10% service charge.  

****
###### 🥃 BAR NI JUAN:   
- a chill out bar  
- open from 10 AM – 10 PM on weekdays; 10 AM – 12 MN on Friday to Sunday  
- Prices are subject to 10% service charge.
