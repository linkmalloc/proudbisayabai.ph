For over 12 years, Pharmaplus has established itself as a trusted pharmaceutical distributor in the Visayas, delivering quality healthcare products to a wide range of clients. Known for its competitive pricing and reliable supply chain, the company continues to support the growing needs of pharmacies, hospitals, and medical institutions across the region.

Pharmaplus offers an extensive selection of pharmaceutical products, catering to both wholesale and retail customers. Its services extend to botika stores, hospitals and clinics, birthing centers, diagnostic facilities, and other healthcare providers—ensuring that essential medicines and supplies remain accessible.

In addition to distributing well-known pharmaceutical brands, the company also takes pride in its in-house product lines, including Suremed Medicines and Sureplus Medical Supplies and Equipment. These brands reflect Pharmaplus’ commitment to quality, affordability, and dependable healthcare solutions.

Conveniently located along Mandaue 1 Avenue, Plaridel Street, Barangay Alang-Alang in Mandaue City, Pharmaplus continues to expand its reach and impact in the healthcare industry. Customers may also connect with the company through its active social media platforms on Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok for updates and inquiries.

With its strong track record and customer-focused approach, Pharmaplus remains a key partner in advancing healthcare accessibility in the Visayas.
