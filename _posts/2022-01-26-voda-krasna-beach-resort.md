###### A Seaside of Valor and Beauty: Voda Krasna Beach Resort

Are you yearning for a soothing walk in the hills with a view of the ocean? A scene where everything that the sun has reached has a touch of gold from the vestige sunrise.
Grab a cup of coffee, run excitedly with a toy pail and shovel, and wake up grippingly for much-awaited beach daybreak as Voda Krasna Beach Resort in Alcoy, Cebu re-opens their [#LaagHabagatan](https://www.facebook.com/hashtag/laaghabagatan) adventurous journey!

A sandy and tropical place to make memories. Bask in the sun and swim in the crystalline, bluest waters. The air is fresh and clean. The breeze is refreshing as you stand beside the austere and dark-gray rocks. You can sit and rest in their cozy beach chairs while listening to the whispering waves or swim in their pool and indulge in the view of a beautiful golden sunset that can be magic to your soul. You will find yourself unable to look away from the site’s astonishing palm trees like a typical summer vibe with its unrelenting sun rays and sweat on your brow. Indeed, it gives you a sense of longing and contentment. As you gaze out into the immensity, you will find it as a relaxing spot where your thoughts can wander far and wide.
Worry no more mga Bai! Alcoy beaches are now open to Non-residents! 😱🤿 🇵🇭

###### HOW TO GET THERE  

Exact Location: Voda Krasna, Daanglungsod, Alcoy

🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu South Bus Terminal, ride any bus going to Alcoy. Tell the driver to drop you off at the Voda Krasna, Daanglungsod, Alcoy. It is just along the high way with sign board. Estimated fare is P150-180 (one way). 

🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze / Google Map. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze: [https://www.google.com/travel/hotels/s/thLfh](https://www.google.com/travel/hotels/s/thLfh)
