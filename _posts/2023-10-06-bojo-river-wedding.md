Marvin and Jovenine's elopement wedding at Bojo River in Aloguinsan, Cebu was the very first of its kind in the history of this beautiful river. Inspired by another elopement wedding earlier this year at Mantayupan Falls, Marvin and Jovenine chose Bojo River for its stunning scenery and its unique location at the river mouth.  

The couple exchanged vows on a small platform surrounded by the river and lush greenery. The ceremony was intimate and heartfelt, with only their closest family and friends in attendance. After the ceremony, the couple celebrated with a romantic dinner by the riverbank.  

Marvin and Jovenine's elopement wedding was a truly special event, and it is sure to inspire other couples to consider this unique and intimate way to start their married life together.
