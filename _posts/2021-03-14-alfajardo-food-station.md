AFFORDABLE ug lami na Unli Tuslob buwa naa na sa TUYAN NAGA mga Bai!!! Tara sa Alfajardo Food Station. 🍱🥘🍜🤤❤️  

****
##### Opening hours:  
2:00PM- 10:00PM  
  
##### 🌍 Exact Location:  
Purok Ungol, Kalubihan, Tuyan City of Naga, Cebu.  

****
#### HOW TO GET THERE?
  
##### 🗺 Via commute:  
If you are from Cebu City just ride a bus na pa Tuyan Naga then tell the driver to drop you off unahan sa Simbahan sa Tuyan. Then if you are somewhere sa Minglanilla or Naga lang just ride a Jeep na pa Tuyan (see photos below sa landmark since medyo sodlonon ang place but du’ol ra kaayu sya sa highway).  
  
##### 🗺 Via private vehicle:  
You can use wazed or google map then search “Tuyan Naga” unahan rajud sya sa simbahan Tuyan.  
  

****
#### MENU  
  
- Unli Tuslob buwa (good for 4pax) - 40.00  
- Buffalo Chicken - 125.00  
- Fried Isaw (3pcs) - 25.00  
- Batchoy - 55.00  
- Vegetable Lumpia (3pcs) - 20.00  
- Dynamite (5pcs) - 50.00  
- Ginabot - 25.00  
- Siomai - 9.00  
- Ngohiong - 12.00  

****
##### 📌Silog Meal  
- Longsilong - 50.00  
- Chosilog - 55.00  
- Pancit Canton with egg - 30.00  

****
##### 📌Drinks  
- Coke - 15.00 / 45.00  
- Sprite - 15.00 / 45.00  
- Royal - 45.00  
- Water - 15.00
