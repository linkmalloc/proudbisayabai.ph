#### Whale shark watching in Oslob.

<center><i>A MUST-EXPERIENCE unique, and breathtaking activities! 💦🏖🐬🐳  </i></center>

****
****
##### Open hours:  
6am-12noon

****
##### Exact Location:  
Tan-awa Oslob, Cebu.  

****
****
#### HOW TO GET THERE 

##### Via Commute:  
From South Bus terminal ride a sunrays or ceres bus, Then tell the conductor to drop-off at whaleshark watching in Tan-awan Oslob Cebu. Fare is P275 one way (with aircon).  
  
##### Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
