Digimap, one of the Philippines’ leading Apple Authorized Resellers, welcomed the iPhone 18 Pro with **Say Hello to the iPhone 18 Pro**, a series of midnight launch celebrations across the country, where technology meets imagination and every hello brings something exciting to discover.

  

Headlined by a flagship event at One Ayala, with simultaneous celebrations at Ayala Malls Vermosa and Ayala Center Cebu, the October 15 to 16 festivities brought together Apple enthusiasts, media friends, and special guests for an unforgettable launch event with exclusive offers and exciting freebies.

  

### **A midnight celebration to remember**

  

Hosted by Claire Codilla, the Ayala Center Cebu celebration featured friends from the media and content creators joining the fun, building excitement toward the midnight countdown.

  

“The launch of the iPhone 18 Pro is an exciting moment for the Apple community, and we wanted to make this milestone truly memorable for our customers across the country. Through our simultaneous midnight celebrations, exclusive launch offers, and personalized retail experience, we’re making it easier for Filipinos to experience the latest in Apple innovation. At Digimap, we’re committed to bringing our customers closer to the products they love and making every upgrade more rewarding,” said Laine Vallar, Country Marketing Head for Digimap Philippines.

  

### **More reasons to DigiTrade**

  

Digimap is making the upgrade via its DigiTrade program even more rewarding with trade-in savings of up to ₱82,900, plus trade-in top-ups of up to ₱11,000. Qualified customers can also enjoy installment options of up to 36 months. Terms and conditions apply.

  

### **Experience the iPhone 18 Pro at Digimap**

  

The iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max are available nationwide starting October 16, 2026, through Digimap’s retail channels.

  

Since entering the Philippine market in March 2023, Digimap has continued to bring Apple products closer to Filipino consumers through personalized service, flexible payment options, and engaging retail experiences. It operates under Mapple Philippines Inc., part of MAP Group Asia (Mitra Adiperkasa Tbk).

  

**About digimap**

  

Digimap is an Apple Authorized Reseller in the Philippines operating under Mapple Philippines Inc., part of MAP Group Asia, a leading lifestyle retailer in Indonesia. 

  

Through its retail network and online channels, Digimap offers Apple products with friendly service and flexible purchasing options, allowing tech enthusiasts to live, play, and connect with all their Apple needs in one location.
