##### 📍 Indios Resto Bar  

Want to experience another style of unli restaurant with a reasonable price?  Here it is mga Bai! Come and Indulge your cravings for unlimited Pork, Chicken, Shrimps, Rice, Drinks and other side dish for **ONLY P249**! 

##### 🕑 Operating hours:  
10:00 AM - 8:00 PM 

##### Exact Location:  
189 Don Mariano Cui St. Capitol Site, Cebu City - Philippines

##### 📌 How to get there?

****
##### Via Commute:  
From Fuente Circle just ride a taxi and tell the driver to drop-off at Indios Restobar Capitol site near Chonghua Hospital.

##### Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
