If you’re searching for a tropical escape with pure white sands, clear turquoise waters, and a peaceful atmosphere, Virgin Island in Sta. Fe, Bantayan Island, Cebu is the perfect pick. Known for its untouched beauty, this small private island has become a favorite destination for couples, barkadas, and families looking for a serene getaway.

***A Quick Trip From Bantayan***

Reaching Virgin Island is easy. From Bantayan Island, specifically from Sta. Fe’s beach areas, you can hire a boat for island-hopping trips.

How to Get to Virgin Island

1. From Cebu City:

* Head to the North Bus Terminal and take a bus or van bound for Hagnaya Port in San Remigio (travel time: 3–4 hours).
* From Hagnaya, board a ferry to Sta. Fe Port in Bantayan Island (1–1.5 hours).

1. From Sta. Fe Port:

* Once on Bantayan Island, ride a tricycle to your hotel or directly to the beach jump-off point.
* Arrange a boat through local operators or your resort for the 30-minute ride to Virgin Island.

***What to Expect on the Island***

Virgin Island offers day-use access from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, allowing guests to enjoy its fine sand, crystal-clear waters, and photogenic landscapes. It’s ideal for:

* Swimming in calm, transparent waters
* Sunbathing on wide sandy areas
* Snorkeling near the shoreline
* Relaxing with loved ones in a peaceful environment

With its natural charm and simplicity, the island gives you a raw, refreshing beach experience far from the usual tourist crowds.

***Why It’s a Must-Visit***

Virgin Island remains one of Bantayan’s top attractions because it blends convenience and beauty. Whether you’re on a romantic trip, a barkada adventure, or a family vacation, this island promises a memorable stop in Northern Cebu.
