**Bench Lee Abadilla**, a youth leader, a graduate of the Cebu Institute of Technology-University, and our very own Proud Bisaya Bai Content Writer clinched the Electrical Engineering Licensure Examinations. He is part of 3,339 examinees who passed the said exam in which his university became part of the leading passers. Part of his motivation to conquer the said exam was his heartbreak experience of getting ghosted just because his studies were his priorities. After being Cum Laude in his program and finally passing the licensure examination, proved his girl wrong. He said in his Facebook post, "Some people would only recognize your worth at your greatest," with a screenshot message from the girl apologizing and congratulating him.
