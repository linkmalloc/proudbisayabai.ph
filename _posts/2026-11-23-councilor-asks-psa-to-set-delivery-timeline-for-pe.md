Cebu City residents who have completed National ID registration but are still waiting for their physical cards may soon get clarity on when they can receive them, following a proposed measure seeking an update from the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA).

City Councilor Harold Kendrick Go filed a resolution asking the agency to disclose the number of physical Philippine Identification (PhilID) cards pending for Cebu City registrants and provide an estimated schedule for their production and distribution.

The proposal addresses delays that have left some registrants without their physical identification cards despite completing the registration process.

Go cited disruptions in card printing following the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas' termination of its contract with the card supplier. The PSA had earlier indicated that it would announce the resumption of printing once production schedules were finalized.

Although the Digital National ID and paper-based ePhilID provide alternatives, Go said physical cards remain important because they offer holders a durable and convenient form of identification for face-to-face transactions.

The National ID system was established under Republic Act 11055, or the Philippine Identification System Act, as the government's central identification platform for Filipino citizens and resident aliens.

Under the law and Executive Order 162, series of 2022, the PhilID or PhilSys Number is recognized as sufficient proof of identity and age in transactions with government agencies, local government units, government-owned and controlled corporations, financial institutions and private entities.

The resolution also underscores the National ID's role in helping people access government services, financial transactions, employment opportunities and other essential services.

Aside from requesting the number of pending cards and their estimated delivery dates, Go is urging the Cebu City Government to coordinate with the PSA's national office, Central Visayas regional office and Cebu provincial office to facilitate the issuance and distribution of the physical IDs.
