Misamis Oriental is inviting adventurers, especially those new to the trail, to discover one of its most rewarding and accessible hikes: the **Mt. Anggas–Mt. Kalinaw Traverse**. Straddling the boundaries between the municipalities of Gitagum and Libertad, this scenic route is quickly becoming the region's top spot for a quick, beginner-friendly escape into nature.

The traverse is perfectly positioned for first-time hikers or seasoned trekkers looking for a practice run. With a manageable difficulty rating of **3/9**, the trail focuses more on the journey's peace and stunning vistas than grueling vertical climbs. The twin peaks of Anggas and Kalinaw stand at an elevation of approximately **69 meters above sea level**, making the entire route a short, sweet, and highly doable adventure.

### **The Sunrise Expedition**

To maximize the experience and beat the mid-day tropical heat, hikers are strongly advised to begin their trek around **4:00 AM to 4:30 AM**. This early start offers the spectacular reward of catching the sunrise as they approach the peaks, painting the Misamis Oriental landscape in golden hues. The cool morning air makes the short **1–2 hour trek** feel invigorating rather than exhausting.

The trail itself, often described as an **open grassland range**, offers expansive, unbroken views for a 360-degree panorama of the surrounding mountains and, reportedly, the vast Mindanao Sea. Locally known as the "Mineral Mountain" due to its unique, rock-laden soil, Mt. Anggas’s open terrain means limited tree cover, which is why the early morning start is crucial.

### **Essential Trail Tip: Hydration is Key**

While the Mt. Anggas–Mt. Kalinaw traverse is short, hikers must remember one critical detail: **there are no water sources along the route**. Proper hydration is paramount for an enjoyable and safe hike. Trekkers should ensure they bring enough water, especially during warmer days.

The experience promises to be more than just a hike; it’s a peaceful, rewarding interlude away from the city buzz. It's a testament to Misamis Oriental's growing reputation as a haven for local adventurers, offering spectacular views and a clear path to the joys of mountaineering, all within a comfortable half-day journey.
