#### Anceno Kape Antigo    

***** 
Let's go back in time mga bai! Famous with their vintage look. Never miss this local coffee shop when heading to San Carlos City. A little museum of old bottles from different intl' and local brands. The business is now run by the 2nd generation. Maka Proud Bisaya Bai! ☕🍺🎥  
  
###### 🕗 Open Daily:  
6am to 7pm  
  
###### 🌎 Exact Location: 
Remedios Street Brgy. 3 San Carlos City Negros Occidental.  
  
🚘 How to get there?  

###### 🗺 Via Commute:  
From San Carlos City Terminal, ride a Motorcab and tell the driver to drop you off at Anceno Kape Antigo. Remedios Street Brgy. 3 at the  back of Brgy.hall near Peoples Park  
  
###### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
