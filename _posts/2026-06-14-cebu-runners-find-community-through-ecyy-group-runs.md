What began as a post-pandemic initiative has evolved into a regular gathering for runners across Cebu City, bringing together seasoned athletes and beginners through a shared passion for fitness.



The ECYY Community Run continues to promote an active lifestyle while helping strengthen Cebu's growing running community through weekly and regular group runs.



Established in 2022 in partnership with Cebu-based running group JVPRG, the initiative emerged in the aftermath of the COVID-19 pandemic, when outdoor fitness activities began regaining popularity.



The community run regularly draws participants for training sessions covering distances ranging from 5 kilometers to 10 kilometers along various routes in Cebu City.



Beyond physical fitness, the activities provide runners an opportunity to improve endurance, prepare for upcoming races, and connect with fellow members of the local running scene.



Co-organizer Ritche Ompad said the group's goal is to encourage more people to embrace healthier lifestyles while creating an inclusive environment for runners of all skill levels.



“We aim to inspire people to live healthier lives and provide a platform that helps grow the running community,” Ompad said.



As community runs continue to gain popularity in Cebu, initiatives such as the ECYY Community Run have become part of the city's expanding fitness culture, fostering camaraderie and encouraging more residents to take up running as a sustainable form of exercise.



Organizers said the initiative reflects how grassroots sports communities can help build healthier and more connected neighborhoods while promoting wellness beyond competition.
