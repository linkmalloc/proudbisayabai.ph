For decades, International Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (IPI) has been a trusted name in wellness, known for products that deliver relief, care, and reliability to generations of Filipinos. But at its core, IPI is more than a pharmaceutical company—it is a brand rooted in culture, community, and shared values.

This commitment was on full display during Sinulog 2026, where IPI participated in Cebu’s iconic celebration of faith and heritage. Its champion-winning float, featuring Marian Rivera, Dingdong Dantes, and Golda Reserva, reflected the company’s pride in Cebuano culture and its dedication to engaging with the community in meaningful ways.

<img height="282" width="537" src="https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/990/f98b851769053306-4.jpg" />

“Our participation in Sinulog was a way to honor the spirit of Cebu and the people we serve,” IPI shared.

Beyond celebrations, IPI’s mission extends into real-world impact. Recently, the company directed its Sinulog Grand Prize to support families affected by the Binaliw landfill tragedy, demonstrating that its values go beyond products—they live in action, solidarity, and care for the community.

<img height="408" width="544" src="https://d1rl40o93nnuyl.cloudfront.net/posts/990/4e78551769053392-5.jpg" />

From trusted healthcare solutions to initiatives that uplift and inspire, IPI continues to define itself not just as a brand, but as a partner in wellness and the shared Filipino experience.
