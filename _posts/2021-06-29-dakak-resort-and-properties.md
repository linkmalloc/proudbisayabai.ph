Generously situated in the southern region of the Philippines, in the historical city of Dapitan where our National Hero Jose Rizal was exiled.   

Dakak Resort and Properties prides itself with top of the class amenities and facilities right at its doorstep.   
  
  
##### HOW TO GET THERE?  

🌍 Exact location: Brgy. Taguilon, Dapitan City, Zamboanga del norte  

✈️ Via Air from CEBU: You can book a flight en route to Dipolog city airport where you can arrange a vehicle transfer to and from the resort.   
  
🛳 Via Sea: Book a boat ticket from cebu or via Dumaguete going to either Dapitan or DIpolog port.
