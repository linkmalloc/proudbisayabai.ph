#### PARROT RESORT MOALBOAL
  
If you’re looking for serene place to stay in Southern Cebu, look no further! Enjoy cooling down in the pool with their luxurious pool-side mini bar PLUS you can get up close personally with their variety of adorable PARROTS too! 🦜⛱  
  
>PROMO FOR YOU MGA BAI!   
👍🏻Use PROMO CODE : **ProudBisayaBai** for a 10% discount for a 2nights stay  
  
🕑 FRONT desk hours: 7 AM- 7 PM daily  
  
🌍Exact Location: Parrot Resort Moalboal Panagsama Road Basdiot Molboal  
  
📌 How to get there?  
  
###### 🗺 Via Commute:  
Ride a bus from South Bus Terminal via Moalboal and tell the driver to drop you off at 7/11 Moalboal. That is the corner also going to Panagsama beach. Ride a Tricycle and tell the driver to drop you off at Parrot Resort Moalboal. The resort is only 5 mins. away from the Panagsama beach.  


###### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
