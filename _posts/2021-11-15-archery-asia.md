##### Archery-Asia Nipa Huts Moalboal - Philippines
 
\#LOOK: Planning for your Barkadas next laag? Wanting to experience a Bahay-Kubo inspired get-away? Indulge in an authentic Filipino dishes? And come into contact and acquaint with nature? You will experience all of that in one go! Visit Archery-Asia Nipa Huts in Moalboal and feel the one-of-a-kind Pinoy vibe!

For only 600 PESOS a night good for two persons already!

###### How to get there? 

🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu City South Bus Terminal - Moalboal proper (Bus Fare 200-250, travel time 3-4 hours) Moalboal proper - Archery (Tricycle 30 per person). 

🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze or G Map. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle it's best to navigate Waze / GMap. 

###### N O T E : 
- They have their own resto. And you can also bring your own foods, no corkage. 
- You can book your rooms through their page in Facebook or AirBnb
