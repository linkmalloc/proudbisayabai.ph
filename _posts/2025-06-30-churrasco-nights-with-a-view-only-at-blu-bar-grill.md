**CHURRASCO NIGHTS WITH A VIEW — ONLY AT BLU BAR & GRILL**

If you are looking for a dining experience that is not only flavorful, but is also unforgettable, then Churrasco Nights at Blu Bar & Grill is exactly what you're looking for.

"Churrasco" is a traditional Brazilian-style barbecue that is full of flavor with a wide variety of grilled meats. In Blu Bar & Grill, you can expect a mouthwatering lineup of beef, pork, lamb, chicken, sausages, and seafood, grilled to perfection and served unlimited.

What makes the experience even more satisfying is the array of sauces you can pair with the meat of your choice, to bring out the best in every bite. Be it tangy, sweet, or spicy, there’s a blend that caters every palate.

You can also build your own salad with a mix-and-match salad bar that offers fresh greens, toppings, and dressings so you can customize it just the way you like it. There is also a choice between steamed and flavored rice that pair perfectly with the meat.

Lastly, to complete your meal, you can indulge in their selection of desserts that are perfect for the Filipino taste.

Not just that. All of this comes with a breathtaking panoramic view of Cebu City, which makes it the perfect setting for intimate dinners, group celebrations, or simply treating yourself to a dinner you deserve.

It is available every Thursday to Saturday from 6:00 PM until 9:30 PM. Although their regular price is ₱1,200 per person, you can enjoy a discounted price of ₱1,080 when you book online!

Elevate your dining experience right here in the heart of Cebu City: only at Blu Bar & Grill.
