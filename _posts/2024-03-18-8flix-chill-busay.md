TUSLOB BUWA WITH A VIEW!  

The popular hangout spot, 8Flix & Chill, has a brand new branch in Busay! Experience their signature chill vibes while enjoying Cebu's famous Tuslob Buwa with a breathtaking city view.  

Match Cebu’s Best Tuslob Buwa, awarded by SunStar Best of Cebu 2022, with a live band together with your friends and family! It is definitely, a must-try in the mountains of Busay! 

🕒 Operating Hours:  

Mon-Thurs: 11 AM - 2 AM  
Fri-Sun: 11 AM - 4 AM  

Unlimited Tuslob Buwa for only 220 pesos (good for 2 persons only).  
- 3rd person will add P60.00
- Rate comes with 10pcs puso and unlimited lamas
- Additional puso P7.00 each.  

****
Side dishes: 
- Ginabot
- Siomai
- Dynamite
- Stuffed Egg
- Chicken Skin
- Proven
- Chinese Ngohiong
- Longganisa  

****
Parking areas are available!  

📍Location: Sitio Maaslum, Lower Busay Cebu City. After Mr. A and One Love Resto Bar. Left side of the road going to Gaslamp District.
