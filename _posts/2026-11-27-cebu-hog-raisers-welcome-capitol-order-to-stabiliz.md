The Cebu Hog Raisers Association has welcomed the Cebu Provincial Government's move to stabilize the local hog industry, saying the new executive order offers support to producers facing falling farmgate prices and rising production costs.

  

In a letter addressed to Gov. Pamela Baricuatro, association president Ainah A. Lalican thanked the governor and provincial officials for advancing the measure, which seeks to protect hog raisers and their livelihoods across the province.

  

“Your commitment to protecting the local hog-raising industry gives hope and encouragement to our members and the many families whose livelihoods depend on this industry,” the association said in the letter.

  

The group also recognized Engr. Grecilda “Gigi” Sanchez for her assistance and coordination with the association during discussions leading to the issuance of the executive order.

  

According to the letter, Sanchez's guidance and continued engagement helped advance the initiative and address concerns raised by hog raisers.

  

The association likewise credited Board Member Sarsi Baricuatro for supporting the group and assisting Sanchez in pushing the undertaking forward.

  

The group said the provincial officials' involvement showed their willingness to hear industry stakeholders and work with them toward solutions to the challenges facing local producers.

  

“This achievement is more than just an Executive Order. It represents hope, protection, and recognition for the hard work and sacrifices of our local hog raisers,” the letter stated.

  

The association said cooperation between the provincial government and industry stakeholders could help strengthen Cebu's hog-raising sector and protect the families who depend on it.

  

The letter, signed by Lalican on behalf of the association's members, also conveyed the group's gratitude for the provincial government's support and commitment to the industry's future.
