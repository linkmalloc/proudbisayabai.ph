Ascendra International Corporation has expanded its footprint in the Visayas, establishing a company-owned branch at City Times Square in Mandaue City on Monday, September 14, 2026. This launch highlights the firm's ongoing nationwide momentum.

The move leverages Cebu's position as a premier economic engine outside Metro Manila, driven by rapid commercial progress and a thriving entrepreneurial environment. Establishing a presence in this high-growth market allows the company to reach local individuals pursuing new financial and business opportunities.

“Cebu was never a question of whether, but when,” said Engr. Jurgen Daniel Gonzales, Chief Executive Officer of Ascendra International Corporation. “We see the Visayas as an important part of Ascendra’s future, and opening a company-owned office here reflects how seriously we are investing in that future. We’re not here for a moment. We’re here to build, to compete, and to grow alongside the Cebuano community.”

Built on the principle that ambition requires structure to succeed, Ascendra integrates high-quality products, reliable business systems, modern technology, and targeted education. Its diverse portfolio covers wellness, nutrition, personal care, digital health solutions, and financial protection.

The new location in the Queen City of the South extends this model outside the capital, serving as a permanent regional base rather than just a supply point. It functions as a operational center for product access, practical training, business support, and leadership programs.
