TRENDING: Looking for a place to have fun with your family and friends? Visit Waterworld Cebu! 💦😱   
  
🕐 Operating hours: 10am-5pm  
  
🌎 Exact Location: Marcelo Fernan Bridge Mandaue City  
  
𝐎𝐏𝐄𝐍 𝐄𝐕𝐄𝐑𝐘 𝐖𝐄𝐄𝐊𝐄𝐍𝐃𝐒 &amp; 𝐇𝐎𝐋𝐈𝐃𝐀𝐘𝐒!  
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: From SM Cebu, ride a bus going to Cebu and tell the driver to drop you off at Waterworld Cebu.    
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.   
  
📌 What to Enjoy?   
 #SplashSafe at WaterWorld Cebu's re-opening NOW! 💦 With over 20 slides and rides, 3 restaurants, a giant wave pool, and a whole lot more, experience Central Visayas' 𝐁𝐈𝐆𝐆𝐄𝐒𝐓 and 𝐁𝐄𝐒𝐓 Waterpark! 💙  
  
📌 Entrance  
For just 𝐏𝟓𝟎𝟎, enjoy a whole day of swimming, riding, sliding, and splashing the day away! The possibilities are endless at WaterWorld! 🤩
