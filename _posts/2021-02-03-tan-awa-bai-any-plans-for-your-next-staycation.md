Experience The Fog House - a taste of how home feels like while being away from home  

****
****
##### Front desk hours: 
9AM - 8PM  

##### Exact Location:  
The Fog house, Taptap, Busay  

**How to get there?**

##### Via Commute:  
Ride a v-hire going to Busay, Cebu from the Ayala Center Cebu Terminal. Ask the driver to drop-off at The Fog House Taptap, Busay. Estimated fare is P130.  

##### Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.  

##### Overnight Rates:
- P25,000(mon-thu) inclusive of 20 pax.
- P30,000(fri-sun) inclusive of 20 pax.
- Excess pax is charged P800. Max of 30 pax only.

> Note
> The place is Exclusive to 1 group only.
> They don't offer per room accomodations.

##### Amenities:
- Kitchen grill
- utensils are available
- Karaoke is free to use
- No corkage fee
