Good news for Burger Steak lovers! After years of being phased out, Jollibee has officially brought back the much loved Burgersteak Supreme, bringing excitement to loyal fans who have long been waiting for its return.



Known as an upgraded and more loaded version of the classic Burger Steak, the Burgersteak Supreme features Jollibee’s signature juicy beef patty served with rice and crispy French fries, generously covered with rich mushroom gravy, topped with real mushroom slices, crunchy garlic bits, and served with egg for a more filling and satisfying meal.



This fan favorite is now available in a 1 piece serving starting at ₱70, making it an affordable yet hearty meal option. For those with a bigger appetite, Jollibee is also offering the 2pc Burgersteak Supreme for an even more sulit and pangmalakasang experience.



Jollibee shared that the returning meal now comes with an even saucier sarap taste compared to its previous formulation, giving customers more of the savory flavor they love.



The return of Burgersteak Supreme has sparked excitement online, with many customers sharing nostalgic memories and expressing happiness over finally seeing it back on the menu after so many years.



If you’ve been craving this iconic comfort meal, now’s the perfect time to visit your nearest Jollibee branch and grab yours.
