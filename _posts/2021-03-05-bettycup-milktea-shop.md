****

📍Soon to Open at Pasil Branch, Cebu

#### 📌 How to get there?  
  
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
From Cebu City ride a bus at Southbus Terminal. Then tell the driver to drop-off at Boljoon Church. From there ask some locals where is BettyCup Milktea shop located.   
  
###### 🗺 Via Private vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
