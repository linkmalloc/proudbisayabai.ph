Calling all frozen yogurt and matcha lovers! Don Macchiatos, your go-to spot for coffee fixes, is branching out and venturing into the world of icy treats with Kuku, their brand new frozen matcha yogurt concept.   

Get ready to tantalize your taste buds with a refreshing twist on your classic yogurt experience. Kuku uses real matcha powder to create a vibrant green base packed with flavors! But the fun doesn't stop there! You can customize your Kuku creation to your heart's content:  
 
**Start with a plain Kuku for only ₱39**. Perfect for those who appreciate the pure matcha taste.  

Craving some sweetness? **Add a delicious sauce for ₱59**. Choose between fruity strawberry or decadent Belgian white chocolate.  

Feeling adventurous? Go for the fully loaded option! **For ₱99**, you can choose one sauce and two toppings to create your ultimate frozen yogurt masterpiece.  

Kuku is the perfect way to cool down on a hot day or satisfy your sweet tooth with a healthy twist. So head over to AB Aznar St., Sambag 2, Urgello St, and experience the refreshing taste of Kuku for yourself!
