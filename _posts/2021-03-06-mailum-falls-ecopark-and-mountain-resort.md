#### HOW TO GET THERE   
  
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
An hour drive away from Bacolod City Located at Sitio Pataan Bago City  
 
##### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.   

****
##### 📌 Fees:   
- Entrance Fee: 150 per pax  
- Poolside Tables for P1,200 - 6pax, children 4 years old and below free  
- Overnight Stay P4,500/P5,500 - 4 to 5 pax  
- Extra P500/pax  
- Kitchen Rental P1,000  
- No corkage fee  

****
Reservation basis only.

#### 💡 TRAVEL TIPS 

- Best to visit during summer time while heat is scorching. Sampat gid pabugnaw kag pahulum sa falls.  
-  Went there while theres a typhoon coming so very foggy ang area tho we appreciate it naman despite the weather.  
- Way up is steep make sure ang service nga makataklad pasaka.  
- You can bring your own food grill area is free of charge  
- Very accommodating helpers from arrival til makaplastar kmo sa table nyo.  
- Explore the whole area there's more to see  
- Very cold ang tubig make sure kaya mo.  
- Lastly, enjoy!
