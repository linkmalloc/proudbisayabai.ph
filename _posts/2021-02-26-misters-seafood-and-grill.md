#### Mister's Seafood and Grill
  
<center><i>Want to experience an island food trip yet don’t know where to go?</i></center>   

****
Mister’s Seafood and Grill has it all for you! An UNLI-SEAFOOD AND GRILL  restaurant serving you fresh and the best of quality, mouthwatering seafoods and smokey grill  buffet that would take your breath away with its oozing yumminess variety of foods.  
  
This is totally perfect for all seafood lovers!!! 😍  
  
*Php 399.00 per person only❗️*   
  
##### 🕙 Operating hours:  
Lunch: 11:00 am - 3:00 pm   
Dinner: 5:30 pm - 9:00 pm   
  
##### 🌎 Exact Location:  
Ground Floor, The Rivergate Complex, Maxilom Ave., Kamputhaw, Cebu City.   
   
****
****
#### HOW TO GET THERE?  

****
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
From Colon, you can either take a taxi or a Beep bus. Should you choose a taxi, tell the driver to drop you off at Rivergate near Iglesia ni Cristo.   
  
If taking Beep, look for a signboard that goes to Ramos. It will surely pass the Rivergate. Or you can take any Beep that passess through Fuente Osmena Circle, stop at Robinsons Fuente  and take another Beep from Fuente to Ramos.
