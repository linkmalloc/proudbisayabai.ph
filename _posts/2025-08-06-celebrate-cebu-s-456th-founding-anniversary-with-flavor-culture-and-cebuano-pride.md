Mark your calendars and ready your eco-bags — the much-awaited Tabo sa Kapitolyo 2025 is back, bigger and brighter than ever! From August 4 to 8, 2025, the Cebu Provincial Capitol Grounds will transform into a bustling hub of culture, cuisine, and creativity, proudly featuring the best of Cebu’s local products and traditions.

Whether you’re a foodie, shopaholic, culture buff, or a true-blue supporter of local goods, this week-long agri-fishery trade fair promises something special for everyone. 

What’s in Store?

**51 LGUs Participating**

Explore the rich diversity of Cebu’s cities and municipalities as each showcases their local treasures — from native delicacies and fresh produce to crafts that tell stories of heritage.

**31 Private Exhibitors**

Discover exciting new finds from passionate entrepreneurs and makers, all offering unique, homegrown products you won’t find anywhere else.

**A Taste of Cebuano Flavor**

Indulge in beloved Cebuano comfort food like pinto, budbod kabog, chicharon, and more. Whether you’re craving something sweet, salty, or savory — there’s a dish waiting for you.

**Handmade Crafts & Souvenirs**

Support local artisans and bring home handcrafted items that represent Cebuano artistry and ingenuity.

*EVENT DETAILS:*

* Date: August 4–8, 2025 (Monday to Friday)
* Time: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM
* Location: Cebu Provincial Capitol Grounds

*Why You Shouldn’t Miss This:*

The Tabo sa Kapitolyo isn’t just a trade fair — it’s a celebration of identity and innovation, spotlighting how Cebu continues to thrive through unity, heritage, and local entrepreneurship. It’s also a perfect bonding moment with friends and family as you shop, eat, and explore.

Share Your Experience!

Join the online celebration by tagging your best moments and finds. Don’t forget to use the official hashtag:

Let’s show the world what Cebuano pride looks like vibrant, delicious, and full of heart. Tara na sa Tabo!
