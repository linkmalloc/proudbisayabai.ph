#### Asturias Beach House
  
TAN-AWA: Planning to unwind and relax this weekend? Asturias Beach House, is the place to be! ⛱💦😯  
  
Get to know more about what they have to offer at Asturias Beach House by Blissful Creations.   
  
🌍 Exact Location: Sitio San Juan,Brgy. Owak Asturias, Cebu.  
  
📌 How to get there ?   
  
🗺 Google Map: [https://goo.gl/maps/3M2cyMFyqVncf2Zy8](https://goo.gl/maps/3M2cyMFyqVncf2Zy8)  
  
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.   
  
📌 ENTRANCE FEE :  
P50.00 per head consumable to foods and drinks.   


###### 📌  ROOM RATES:

****
SINGLE BED (Good for 2 pax)  
- With breakfast - P1,750  
- Without breakfast- P1,500  

****
TWIN BED (Good for 4 pax)  
- With breakfast- P2,200  
- Without breakfast- P1,700  
 
****
BARKADA ROOM (Good for 8 pax)  
- With breakfast- P4,000  
- Without breakfast - P3,000  

****  
###### 📌ACTIVITIES:  

###### KAYAKING   
1hour - P350 per head  
30 mins - P200 per head  
  
###### ESTUARY CRUISE  
P150 per head (with boatman and life vest)  
  
###### TRAMPOLINE (for kids)  
1 hour - P90 pesos per head  
30 mins - P50 pesos per head  
10 mins - P30 pesos per head
