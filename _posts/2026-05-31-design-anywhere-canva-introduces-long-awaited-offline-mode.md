Design platform Canva has introduced **Canva Offline**, a highly requested feature that allows users to continue creating and editing designs even without an internet connection.



The new capability was unveiled during Canva Create, alongside several other product updates aimed at making design more accessible and efficient for users worldwide.



According to Canva, the feature addresses one of the biggest barriers faced by many creators, students, educators, and professionals who do not always have access to stable internet connectivity. With Canva Offline, users can open selected designs, perform essential editing tasks, upload and work with local image files, and continue projects while disconnected from the web.



Any changes made offline are automatically synced once an internet connection is restored, ensuring that work is saved and updated seamlessly.



Canva said the feature is particularly beneficial for people in areas with unreliable internet service, those working while traveling or commuting, and communities where mobile data remains costly.



The company described Canva Offline as part of its broader mission to make design and visual communication tools available to everyone, regardless of location or connectivity challenges.



Canva Offline is among several new features announced this year as the platform continues to expand its tools for creators, businesses, educators, and teams around the world.
