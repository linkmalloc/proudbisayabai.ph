High above the clouds, a little angel named Anjo grew restless. Named for the Portuguese word for "angel" and inspired by the founders’ parents, Antonio and Josiefe, Anjo dreamed of finding a playground where joy, music, and warmth intertwined. 

His wings carried him across continents—through Egypt’s ancient rhythms, Russia’s fiery folk dances, America’s pulsing street beats, and Italy’s soaring opera halls. Each land offered wonder, yet something felt incomplete. 

Then, as he soared over the Philippines, a symphony of laughter rose from Cebu’s shores. 

The Anjo Eye Ferris wheel spun like a glittering halo and the Pharaoh Roller Coaster carved thrilling arcs through the tropical air. Curiosity ignited and Anjo descended toward Belmont One, where melodies mingled with the scent of popcorn and adventure.  

Anjo’s feet touched ground in a wonderland of sensory magic. At the 5D Theater, wind whipped through his feathers as birds swooped across screens; in Snow World, he marveled at ice sculptures while children giggled on frosty slides—a surreal winter dream in sun-drenched Cebu.

He danced beside families on the Golden Carousel, shared stories under starlit bonfires and even joined a team-building quest on the Boomerang ride where screams of delight echoed his own joy.

This was no ordinary park—it was a place of global cultures gathered into 15 rides across four themed continents. Here, the community turned strangers into friends and every corner hummed with belonging.

And so, in the heart of Minglanilla, Anjo found his answer. Cebu’s spirit—warm, resilient and radiant became the finale of his odyssey. 

As the park’s new musical show crescendos, Anjo and a troupe of performers ignite the stage with tinkling bamboo clicks, Sinulog drum beats, and contemporary dance moves from across the globe, inviting guests to clap and sing along beneath the Anjo Eye’s glowing orbit. This celebration of "heart, culture and community" mirrors the park’s own legacy: a place where children’s smiles light anniversary events and balikbayans reunite with their heritage.

Don’t miss this kaleidoscope of joy every Saturday, Sunday, and holiday at Anjo World, where music, magic, and the angel who found home awaits. 🎢🎤  

Show Details:

📍 Location: Anjo World Theme Park, Belmont One, Minglanilla, Cebu  

🕒 Time: Weekends & holidays (check park hours: Wed-Sun, 12:00 PM–8:00 PM )  

🎟️ Entry: Use a Day Adventure Pass (₱650 weekdays / ₱700 weekends )—unlimited rides plus the musical extravaganza!
