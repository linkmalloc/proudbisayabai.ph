If you’re dreaming of an exotic getaway with an Indonesia feels vibe, you don’t actually have to leave the Philippines. Tucked away in the serene coastal town of Moalboal, Cebu, Bambusa Glamping Resort offers a unique blend of tropical immersion, rustic elegance, and modern comfort. It is an ideal destination for travelers seeking a peaceful yet memorable escape.

**A Nature-Immersed Glamping Experience**

Surrounded by lush tropical gardens and highlighted by a beautiful natural stone pool, Bambusa Glamping Resort redefines the meaning of glamping. The property features carefully designed bamboo houses that blend seamlessly with the environment, allowing guests to fully immerse themselves in nature while enjoying comfort and privacy.

Each bamboo house is thoughtfully crafted to provide a balance between rustic charm and refined living. Guests can expect spacious interiors, comfortable bedding, and a tranquil atmosphere that reflects the simplicity of provincial life with a touch of luxury.

**Accommodation and Amenities**

Guests are provided with essential amenities to ensure a comfortable stay:

* Electric stand fan
* Electric kettle
* Shower heater
* WiFi access
* Bamboo toothbrush and toothpaste
* Handwash, shampoo, and body wash
* Towels, linens, and blankets
* Refrigerator
* Parking space
* Swimming pool

Every detail supports a relaxing stay while maintaining the resort’s eco-friendly and nature-inspired concept.

**Rates**

* Queen Room Php 2870 per night
* King Room Php 3120 per night

These rates make Bambusa Glamping Resort a practical choice for travelers looking for a unique yet affordable glamping experience in Cebu.

**Location**

Bambusa Glamping Resort is located along the road to Basdako White Beach in Barangay Saavedra, Moalboal, Cebu, Philippines. The location provides convenient access to the beach while maintaining a quiet and peaceful setting.

**How to Get There**

*Via commute*
From the Cebu South Bus Terminal, ride a Ceres bus bound for Moalboal and inform the driver to drop you off at Barangay Saavedra. Upon arrival, you may ask locals for directions to Bambusa Glamping Resort.

*Via private vehicle*
The resort is accessible through navigation apps such as Waze or Google Maps. Travelers using cars or motorbikes can easily locate the destination by searching for Bambusa Glamping Resort.

**Why Visit Bambusa Glamping Resort**

Bambusa Glamping Resort stands out as a hidden gem in Cebu, offering an experience that feels like an international escape without the need for long-distance travel. With its eco-conscious design, peaceful ambiance, and well-curated amenities, it is a suitable destination for those who want to reconnect with nature while enjoying modern comforts.

You do not have to travel far to experience something unique because Cebu continues to offer destinations that combine beauty, comfort, and authenticity.
