Cebu City Councilor Winston Pepito has yet to decide whether to seek a higher position in the 2028 elections, despite growing calls from residents and supporters for him to consider running for mayor or vice mayor.

  

Pepito said he had been receiving suggestions for months from people encouraging him to take on a bigger leadership role in the city.

  

While he appreciates the support, the councilor said he has not settled on any position or made a decision on whether to run in the next elections.

  

“I’m open to any position nga tan-aw nako mas makatabang nako to push my agenda ba sa kaayuhan sa atong syudad,” Pepito said in an interview on Friday.

  

He said he would rather focus on his current responsibilities as a councilor and consult senior party leaders before considering any political move.

  

Pepito also denied reports that he had been organizing gatherings, conducting surveys or holding raffles to promote a possible vice mayoral bid.

  

He said those activities were not initiated by him, although he welcomed the support being expressed by residents.

  

“I'm not actively campaigning for any position sa karon,” he said.

  

According to Pepito, the people encouraging him to seek a higher post come from various sectors, including business groups and workers.

  

He believes some supporters are looking for alternative leadership because they feel several problems in the city remain unresolved.

  

However, Pepito said he was not presenting himself as the solution to these concerns. He said he would do his best to deliver results if entrusted with greater responsibility.
