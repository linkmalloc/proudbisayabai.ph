What started as a simple undertaking to build a business that would provide people with dependable printing services, Chase Printing has grown into a one-stop shop for every printing need, be it for business or personal projects. You can count on Chase Printing to get your colorful ideas out into the world.

  

Owner Marlon Villarino shared that he was inspired to enter the printing industry with a clear goal: to be a colorful canvas that brings people’s projects to life. He shared, “Like many small businesses, we started with limited resources. There were challenges in finding customers, managing expenses, and making sure every project met our standards.”

  

With a vision to build a successful and trusted printing business known for quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction, Chase Printing has now solidified its name in the industry with a roster of esteemed clients under its belt.

  

What sets Chase Printing apart from other printing service shops is its affordable prices while maintaining exceptionally high quality. They pay attention to every detail, providing professional printing services while building trust and long-term relationships with every client.

  

Located at Bontores St., Basak San Nicolas, Cebu City, Chase Printing has every printing need you could possibly ask for! From customized uniforms, IDs, standees, mugs, tote bags, and more, the printing shop is fully equipped to bring your creative vision to life.
