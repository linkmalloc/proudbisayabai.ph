“Odong” A steaming bowl of odong soup continues to be one of the most affordable and comforting meals enjoyed by many Filipinos, especially during rainy evenings or late-night cravings.

  

Known for its soft wheat noodles and quick preparation, odong soup has become a household favorite because it is both filling and easy on the budget. It is commonly cooked with canned sardines, fresh vegetables, eggs, or other simple ingredients readily available at home.

  

Despite its simplicity, the dish has earned a special place on Filipino dining tables. Many families prepare odong soup as a quick meal after a long day, while others enjoy it as a comforting snack before bedtime.

  

Its versatility also makes it a favorite among home cooks. Some prefer the classic sardines-based recipe, while others add shrimp, chicken, pork, or a variety of vegetables to enhance its flavor and nutritional value.

  

Beyond being an affordable meal, odong soup reflects the Filipino tradition of creating delicious dishes from simple ingredients. Its warm broth and satisfying noodles make it a reliable comfort food, especially during cold weather.

  

Whether shared with family or enjoyed alone, odong soup remains a timeless favorite that continues to bring warmth and comfort to Filipino homes.

  

### Food Facts

  

• Odong noodles are made from wheat flour and are similar to Japanese udon noodles, although they are thinner and shorter.

• Odong is especially popular in Mindanao and the Visayas, where it is commonly prepared with canned sardines in tomato sauce.

• The dish is known for being budget-friendly, requiring only a few affordable ingredients while still being filling and flavorful.

• Adding vegetables such as cabbage, pechay, malunggay, or carrots boosts the dish’s fiber, vitamins, and minerals.

• Eggs, shrimp, chicken, or pork are popular additions that increase the soup’s protein content.

• Because it cooks in just a few minutes, odong soup is a favorite choice for busy families and as a comforting late-night meal.
