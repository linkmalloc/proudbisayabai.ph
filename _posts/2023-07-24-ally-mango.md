Last July 21, Ally Mango, the popular mango snack brand, is opening its new branch in Ayala Center Cebu tomorrow!  

To celebrate the grand opening, Ally Mango offered a buy one get one free promo for the first 100 customers.  

They offer a variety of mango products, including mango slices and mango smoothies. They also have a line of mango-flavored drinks and desserts.  

###### Location:  
Near Chowking and Pizza Hut, 3rd Floor, Ayala Center Cebu
