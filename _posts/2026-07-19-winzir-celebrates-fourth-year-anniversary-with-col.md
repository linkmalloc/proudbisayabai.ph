Winzir's Color Run 2026, a commemoration of the brand's fourth anniversary, brought loads of raffle prizes, fun, freebies and colors to City di Mare on Saturday, July 18, 2026, and assembled the Cebu running community by offering three different race categories: 6 km, 12 km and 16 km.

  

Before the gun start of the first race of 16KM, an opening program was held, allowing the runners to warm-up and stretch before their race and reminding them of the routes that need to be taken. Runners also availed of the different booths and stations offering free energizers such as bread, coffee, water and even played Winzir’s booth games before the race started.

  

The 16KM race started at 3:00 a.m., while the 12KM category’s gun start was at 3:30 a.m. and the 6KM category’s send-off was at 3:45 a.m.

  

Throughout the race, different stations were also organized, offering runners more race fuel and hydration supplies such as fruits and water, to successfully finish the race. 

  

The races were also livestreamed in Winzir’s page which helped the audience monitor the runners’ rankings, as well as tracking the current location of the runners.

  

After the run, each participant received after-run meals, helping restore the energy used during the entire race. Podium finishers were then awarded, recognizing their capabilities and talent. After the awarding, the color and foam party followed, doubling up the color run’s fun and helping each runner socialize with their community. During the party, MarinongDJ was invited, adding up to the hype of the event and making it more memorable and entertaining.

  

The Color Run 2026 concluded with the raffle draw where several lucky winners received the different raffle prizes. Lucky runners won Honda Click 125 V4 motorcycles, iPhone 17 and iPad A16 units, Macbook Neo laptops and more minor raffle prizes.

  

Winzir’s Color Run 2026 ended successfully, putting smiles on every runner’s faces. The race did not just help runners to move and exercise, but it also offered them various pre-race and post-race entertainment that leveled up their experience. The recently concluded event of Winzir did not just show a successful fun run event, but it also reflects the company’s triumphs.
