****
Bakoo Caribbean RestoBar offers Cebu a taste of the Caribbean. Located in the heart of the city, Bakoo is the perfect place to escape the hustle and bustle of everyday life. The menu at Bakoo is full of delicious Caribbean dishes, such as jerk chicken, curry goat, and fried plantains. They also have a wide selection of drinks, including rum punch, mojitos, and pina coladas.  

In addition to the food and drinks, Bakoo also has a great atmosphere. The restaurant is decorated in a Caribbean style, with colorful murals and plants. There is Salsa night every Friday, live band every Saturday and a Sunday Roast dinner!  

Whether you are looking for a delicious meal or a fun night out, Bakoo Caribbean RestoBar is the perfect place for you!  

Operating Hours: 1PM -12AM, Tuesdays - Sundays

###### How to get there? 

###### Via commute:  
From SM City, ride a vhire going Gaisano Mactan. Ride a multicab heading Maribago and tell the driver to drop you off at Bagumbayan 2 then sulod sa skina sa Aquaventure Whitetip Dive Supply and loook for Bakoo Carribean Restobar.  
###### Via private vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze/ Google Map. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze
