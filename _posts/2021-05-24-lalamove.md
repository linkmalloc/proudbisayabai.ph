Cebuanos’ most trusted same-day, door-to-door delivery app for fast and reliable deliveries. **Lalamove**, is now made more affordable with their lowest motorcycle delivery base fare promo of **₱1 with a succeeding ₱8 only** per kilometer rate.


The **limited-time offer** covers motorcycle deliveries within Cebu City, Consolacion, Cordova, Lapu-Lapu, Lilo-an, Mandaue, Minglanilla, and Talisay. No promo code needed.

For every promo such as the Piso Base Fare, **Lalamove** always handles paying their partner drivers the full rate as part of their commitment to helping their drivers while at the same time treating their users with the most affordable, fast, and convenient deliveries.

Lalamove’s fleet of motorcycles can deliver small packages weighing up to 20 kgs including food items and documents. It also offers additional features and services from their multi-stop feature that allows up to 20 delivery stops in one booking to the Purchase or Pabili Service for shopping assistance.
 
Everybody wins with Lalamove. Book your on-demand or scheduled deliveries using the Lalamove app, available on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store at this link [http://bit.ly/DeliverNowCEB](http://bit.ly/DeliverNowCEB). The ₱1 base fare + ₱8 per kilometer motorcycle delivery rate is automatically applied upon check out.

**Lalamove** was established in Hong Kong in December 2013. Today, it operates in 21 cities across Asia and Latin America, including Cebu and Manila in the Philippines. It provides different vehicle options that fit according to the needs of the customer, easy customer transactions, allows flexible schedules, and multiple bookings to help businesses of all sizes grow their reach.

For more information, visit [www.lalamove.com](http://www.lalamove.com) and like [@LalamovePH](https://www.facebook.com/LalamovePH) on Facebook.
