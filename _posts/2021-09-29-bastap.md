##### THE BASTAP

\#TRENDING: Looking for a perfect pasalubong kung mo uli sa pamilya? Naa diri inyong gipangita, Dayun ug Hapit na kamo sa BASTAP! 

The BASTAP gives you all that is Cebuano. The name Bastap reflects the way Cebuanos pronounce and spell bus stop. They offer Cebu's Best Traditionally-Made delicacies plus the ambiance and the food assortment are truly an experience worth trying. 

Visit their Website: https://thebastap.com

🕛 Operating hours: 9AM - 6PM daily 

🌍 Exact Location: Piazza Elesia, Cabancalan, Talamban Rd. Cebu City (walking distance from 7/11 Cabancalan) 

######  How to get there?

Via Commute: From Colon St. just ride a bus, 13C jeepneys and beep bus going to Talamban. Then tell the driver to drop you off at Piazza Elesia estimated fare is P20-30. The Bastap is located 2nd floor beside Tattoo. 

Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze or Google Map. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze / G Map. 

 Menu: 
- Otap
- Eggnog 
- Rosquillos
- Huevos
- Galletas
- Patatas
- And many more!  

****
Available via Grab Food, Toktok, Lalamove, Maxim, Take out, Website

Take Note: They have FREE coffee to all customers who will visit their newest open FoodHub in Talamban.
