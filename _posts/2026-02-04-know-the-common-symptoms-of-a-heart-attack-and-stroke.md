According to the World Health Organization (WHO), cardiovascular diseases remain one of the leading causes of death worldwide. Early recognition of symptoms and immediate medical care can save lives and reduce long-term disability.



COMMON SYMPTOMS OF A HEART ATTACK

• Chest pain or discomfort, often described as pressure, squeezing, or fullness

• Pain or discomfort in the arms, back, neck, jaw, or stomach

• Shortness of breath

• Cold sweat, nausea, or lightheadedness



COMMON SYMPTOMS OF A STROKE

Use the F.A.S.T. warning signs:

• F – Face drooping: One side of the face is numb or drooping

• A – Arm weakness: Sudden weakness or numbness in one arm

• S – Speech difficulty: Slurred speech or difficulty speaking

• T – Time to seek help: Call emergency services immediately



Other stroke symptoms may include sudden confusion, trouble seeing, severe headache, dizziness, or loss of balance.



IMPORTANT REMINDER

If you or someone else experiences any of these symptoms, seek medical care immediately. Acting fast can significantly improve survival and recovery outcomes.



Protect your heart. Protect your life.
