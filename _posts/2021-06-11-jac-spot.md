Proud Bisaya Bai presents you with another mouth-watering local restaurant. Get your hands messy with a delicious, crispy, and juicy chicken of **Jac Spot**. The place is located at R. Rabaya St. Nonoc Talisay City, Cebu. **Jac Spot** is known for its chicken tossed with savory, sweet, and spicy flavors.


Enjoy their **Unlimited Chicken Wings** plus Rice for only 229 Pesos!

Support Local mga Bai! Mangaon ta sa Jack Spot!
