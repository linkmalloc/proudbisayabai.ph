#### CAN-UMANTAD WATERFALL  

Can-umantad Falls is one of the most impressive waterfalls you will find on the incredible island of Bohol, Philippines! It is the tallest waterfall on the island with a towering height of 60ft.  

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/canumantad_waterfall/2.jpg)  

The waterfall is a tiered waterfall beneath rice terraces. The water hits a plateau before it finally drops down to the main pool. The main pool is quite deep so be careful swimming there. Beneath the waterfall, there are shallower spots too that you can enjoy.  

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/canumantad_waterfall/6.jpg)  

The waterfall has clean and cold water that will surely make you want to take a dip.

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/canumantad_waterfall/8.jpg)  

♻️🗑LEAVE NO TRACE!   
Make your small contribution to help preserve its natural beauty by collecting trash you find along the way and dispose it at the designated areas.  
( Gasa sa kina-iyahan nga angay ampingan, Matag usa kanato makatabang sa pag amping sa atong kalikopan sama sa pag punit sa basura ug paglabay niini sa saktong labayanan)

![](https://d3hukd8e3cn3kb.cloudfront.net/images/posts/canumantad_waterfall/3.jpg)

##### HOW TO GET THERE  

🗺 By Public Transport – Bus from Tagbilaran city – Brgy. Lungsodaan, Candijay Bohol is (92 kms \| 80.00Php) From Candijay town proper, hire a habal-habal that will take you to Barangay Cadapdapan .  
  
🗺 Van from Tagbilaran City – 130.00 Php. From Candijay town proper, hire a habal-habal that will take you to Barangay Cadapdapan. By private car – we will advise for a 4×4 vehicle
