#### Kampo Uno Mountain Resort  
  
LOOK: Grabeh kanindot Bai! Kampo Uno Mountain Resort in Bukidnon! 💦💆🏻😯  
  
🌎 Exact Location: P-9 Camp 1, Maramag, Bukidnon / Landmark: Green Camp along National highway.  
  
Discover some of the reasons why people want to be here. This small resort is surrounded by lush green vegetation and a stunning view of the morning sun and a river of clouds!   
  
📆 Schedule:  
  
OVERNIGHT STAY ONLY!  
🕑 Check-in starts at 3pm  
🕙 Check-out before 10am  
  
📌 Inclusion:  
- Exclusive Pool use  
- Bon fire area  
- Wall climbing  
- Videoke until 9pm  
- Grill area  
- Smoking area  
- Free welcome snack upon entrance  
- Free breakfast (depending on package chosen)  
- Tables and chairs  
- Available drinking water  
- Bath, shower and comfort room  

****
###### 📌 Room rates:  
🏠 Small Room Package with 1 tent - 4,500 pesos/night  
(With free breakfast for 2 people only)   
Excess fee per person is 150 pesos.   
Maximum of 7 persons including more than 7yrs old.  
  
🏠 Big Room Package with 1 tents -8,000 pesos /night   
(With free breakfast for 8 people only)  
Excess fee per person is 150 pesos   
Maximum of 12 persons including children more than 7yrs old.  
  
🏘 Family Package with use of small and big room for a group of more than 12 persons (With free breakfast for 10 persons only)  
Excess fee per person is 150 pesos  
Maximum of 20 persons including children more than 7 yrs old.  
  
###### They serve:  
- Floating breakfast - 200 pesos  
- Drinks and liquors are available!  
- Dinner is available at Green Camp cafe until 9pm only!  

****
###### Corkage:  
 - Lechon - 300 pesos  
- Rice cooker use - 100 pesos  
- Heater- 50 pesos  
- Tent for rent - 150 pesos only  
- No cooking other than Grilling!  
  
****
###### Notes:  
- RESERVE NOW FOR THE MONTH MARCH 2021!  
- Deposit 2,000 pesos for reservation. Reservation fee is not refundable upon cancellation.  
- All rooms are air-conditioned  
- No room service   
- No bringing of hard liquors   
- Pets not allowed  
- Children are welcome   
- Extra beds not available   
- Cribs is not available   
- You can bring your own food and utensils.  

****
###### 📌Special check-in instructions  
- There's no front desk at this property. This property doesn't offer after-hours check-in.  

****
###### 📌Enhanced cleanliness measures  
- Disinfectant is used to clean the property  
- High-touch surfaces are cleaned and disinfected  
- Sheets and towels are washed  

****
###### 📌Social distancing  
- Contactless check-in and check-out  
- Protective shields in place at main contact areas  
-Guest rooms kept vacant for 24hrs between stays  
- Social distancing measures in place  

****
###### 📌Safety measures  
- Facemask and faceshields  worn by staff  
- Temperature checks given to staff  
- Temperature checks available for guests  
- Hand sanitizer provided  
- Individually wrapped food options available for breakfast and through room service  
- Fire extinguisher  
- First aid kit
