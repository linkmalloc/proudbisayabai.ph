##### BOJO RIVER - ALOGUINSAN, CEBU

\#VisitAloguinsan: Imagine going to a place where you can unwind and recharge after an exhausting work week.💦 😮🌴

Listen to the whisper of gentle cold  breeze as you watch the astonishing view of the river from above. 
A must-see ecotourism destination that features Aloguinsan's magnificent jewel.

🌏 Location: Bojo River, Aloguinsan, Cebu, Philippines

###### How to get there? 

Google Map Link: Aloguinsan, Cebu [https://g.co/kgs/3yJGv3](https://g.co/kgs/3yJGv3)

🗺 Via Commute: Ride a Ceres Bus or V-hire bound for Municipality of Pinamungajan Cebu approx. 2-3hrs travel time. Ask the conductor to drop you in front of The Farmhouse in Aloguinsan. Estimated fare is P100.

🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze or Google Map. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze/ G Map.
