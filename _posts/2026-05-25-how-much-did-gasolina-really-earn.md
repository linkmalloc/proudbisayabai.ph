What started as a viral Holy Week short film has now turned into over a million pesos worth of gasoline assistance for riders across the country.



Content creator Joseph Sabello revealed that his hit film “Gasolina” generated a total of ₱1.05 million for his nationwide free fuel initiative benefiting father-riders in Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.



Sabello shared that around ₱303,000 came from Facebook and YouTube earnings alone, while ₱475,000 was donated by private supporters who backed the project’s advocacy.



The short film, which quickly gained millions of views online, became more than just viral content — it also helped power free gasoline drives for hardworking riders nationwide.
