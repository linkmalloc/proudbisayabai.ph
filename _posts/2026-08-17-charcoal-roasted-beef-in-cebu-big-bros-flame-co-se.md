Looking for a hearty meal with that unmistakable smoky flavor of food cooked over charcoal? Big Bros Flame Co. is bringing charcoal-roasted goodness to the table in Sambag 1, Cebu City, with its tender and flavorful Lechon Baka that is made for sharing.

  

The restaurant’s Lechon Baka is slowly cooked over charcoal, giving the beef a rich roasted flavor, juicy texture, and distinct smokiness that makes every bite satisfying. Good for two to three persons, it is a fitting choice for family gatherings, celebrations, or simply a food trip with friends.

  

Adding more flavor to the roasted beef is Big Bros Flame Co.’s special homemade Baka Sauce, a savory accompaniment designed to complement the richness of the meat. Pair it with rice and you have a filling meal that brings together smoky, tender beef and a flavorful house-made sauce.

  

**Chicken Inasal with a Special Twist**

  

Big Bros Flame Co. also puts its own spin on a Filipino favorite with its Chicken Inasal.

  

One of its highlights is the restaurant’s signature chicken oil made from real chicken, adding extra aroma and flavor to the grilled chicken and rice. Diners can also enjoy the restaurant’s UNLI Rice promo, making it an appealing option for those looking for a satisfying and budget-friendly meal.

  

Whether you are craving roasted beef, grilled chicken, or simply looking for a place to gather over good food, Big Bros Flame Co. offers a menu centered on bold, smoky flavors and generous servings.

  

**Where to Find Big Bros Flame Co.**

  

Big Bros Flame Co. is located at Tron Square, Upper Ground, J. Urgello Street, Sambag 1, Cebu City.

  

The restaurant is open Mondays to Saturdays, from 11:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., giving diners plenty of time to drop by for lunch, dinner, or a late food trip.

  

With its charcoal-roasted Lechon Baka, homemade Baka Sauce, and flavorful Chicken Inasal, Big Bros Flame Co. is a spot worth checking out for anyone who enjoys smoky, grilled goodness.

  

_Smoky. Juicy. Charcoal-roasted. Made for sharing. At Big Bros Flame Co., the flame is part of the flavor._
