Yombig Burger has officially opened its doors at SM City Cebu to serve foodies with bold flavors, big bites, and budget-friendly prices that will make you say, "Hay, ka-sulit!"

If you like your burgers juicy, messy, and flavorful, then stop by Yombig Burger.

If you're having a hard time choosing between satisfying your craving between beef and chicken, then just grab their signature Chi-Be burger that combines chicken and beef in one epic bite!

They also serve an array of other burger combos, tasty rice meals, and crispy fries that are perfect to share with your barkada or finish by yourself.

All items are made to be delicious and filling, making it ideal for students, mall-goers, or anyone seeking comfort food.

So if you're on a snack mission, visit the lower ground floor of SM City Cebu and find Yombig Burger beside SM Supermarket.
