Jollibee Gorordo officially welcomed the holiday season with its annual Christmas lighting ceremony on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, at 6:00 p.m., featuring festive decorations inspired by traditional Filipino Christmas celebrations.

  

The event was led by Restaurant Manager Gavz Orzales Jr., together with the store managers, continuing Jollibee’s tradition of bringing holiday cheer to its customers and the community.

  

Carrying the theme “Sa Bawat Sandali ng Pasko, May Joy,” this year’s celebration highlights how Christmas joy can be found in simple moments, from sharing meals with loved ones to creating meaningful memories with the community.

  

Beyond the festivities, Jollibee Gorordo remains committed to serving its signature Chickenjoy, quality food, and excellent customer service.

  

Through its annual Christmas lighting tradition, Jollibee continues to celebrate Filipino culture and remind everyone that the true spirit of Christmas lies in sharing joy and togetherness.
