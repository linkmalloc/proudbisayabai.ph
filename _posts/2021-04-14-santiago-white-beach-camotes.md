#### Santiago White Beach Camotes

It's Vacation Time! Santiago White beach is a long beach with fine white sand. It is located in the south part of Pacijan island, one of the 3 main islands of Camotes. 

Free admission - yes! no entrance fee. This public beach is managed by Camotes Local Government Unit(LGU) - they keep the beach clean and safe. It is safer for  the kids for it has a long shallow shore, during low tides you can walk on the sand bar as far as you want. Certainly, Santiago White Beach is a perfect place for a summer vacation, every BARKADAS DREAM! 🏝💦

##### HOW TO GET THERE

🌎 Exact Location: Santiago White Beach, Camotes Island  
  
🗺 Via Commute: If you are commuting, from Cebu City you can ride a bus or jeepney going Danao Port and ride a boat to Consuelo Port (fare is Php220/head).  
  
 🗺 Via Private Vehicle: If using a private utility and want to bring it in the Island, you can ride a roro in the same port, fare is around Php680 for motorcycle including the rider. Travel time is around 2 hours.  
  
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.

****
Barge Schedule pls refer to [Jomalia Shipping Lines FB page](https://www.facebook.com/JomaliaShippingCorp).  
  
Ceres bus from Mactan to Danao to Consuelo port vice versa rate. Estimated rate see below.
