Users of Viber can now access ChatGPT directly within the messaging application, marking a significant step in the integration of artificial intelligence into everyday communication.



The new feature allows Viber users to interact with ChatGPT without leaving the app, providing instant access to AI-powered assistance for a variety of personal, educational, and professional needs.



Through simple text-based conversations, users can ask questions, seek recommendations, generate ideas, draft content, and receive support for a wide range of tasks directly within their chats.



The integration is designed to make artificial intelligence more accessible by bringing conversational AI capabilities into one of the world’s widely used messaging platforms. Users can utilize ChatGPT for writing assistance, brainstorming, language support, translations, productivity tasks, and general information inquiries.



According to Viber, the addition of ChatGPT aims to enhance user experience by providing convenient and on-demand assistance in a familiar messaging environment.



ChatGPT is capable of understanding natural language and generating human-like responses across numerous topics. The AI assistant can help users compose messages, summarize information, refine written content, translate text, and explore creative ideas, among other functions.



The launch reflects a broader industry trend as communication platforms increasingly incorporate artificial intelligence to expand services beyond traditional messaging and calling features.



Industry observers note that the growing adoption of AI within messaging applications is transforming how users access information and complete everyday tasks, offering faster and more efficient digital experiences.



By combining Viber’s established messaging ecosystem with ChatGPT’s conversational capabilities, users can benefit from instant, AI-powered support wherever and whenever they need it.



Key Features of ChatGPT on Viber:



\* Instant access to AI-powered conversations within Viber

\* Answers to questions across a broad range of topics

\* Writing, editing, and brainstorming assistance

\* Translation and language support

\* Productivity and research assistance

\* Accessible directly through the Viber application



As artificial intelligence continues to evolve and become integrated into daily digital experiences, the availability of ChatGPT on Viber represents another milestone in making AI tools more widely available to users worldwide.



Users interested in trying the feature can access ChatGPT directly through the Viber platform and explore its AI-powered capabilities.
