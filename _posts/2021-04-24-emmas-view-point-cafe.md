#### EMMA'S VIEW POINT CAFE   
  
Looking for a perfect place to get relax and de-stess away from city life? 😯😍🍃  
  
🕔 Open hours: 6AM - 5PM ( DAYTOUR   
  
🌎 Sitio Uraya, Talaingod, Davao del Norte (5mins away from Uraya's Peak)  
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.  
  
📌 Fees:   
- Entrance fee:  
- P50.00 with FREE Coffee and Puto (day tour)  
- P1500.00 overnight with free breakfast for 2  
  
For more info about their rates and how to get there please visit Emma's ViewPoint Cafe
