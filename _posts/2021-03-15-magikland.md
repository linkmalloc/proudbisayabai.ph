#### MAGIKLAND
  
Great news! Due to high public demand, Magikland is extending its operations from 1PM - 8PM every FRIDAYS TO SUNDAYS of March 2021 🎪🎡🎠  
  
🌎 Magikland is located at Brgy. Guinhalaran, Silay City, Negros Occidental. In front of Carmela Subdivision.  
  
📌 How to get there?  
  
🗺 Via Commute: Take a public utility vehicle from North terminal bound for Silay. Drop off to Bangga Carmela (in Brgy. Guinhalaran, Silay City). You can either walk up to Magikland with an approximately 1KM distance or take a pedicap, E-bike or tricycle.  
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.  
  
##### 📌 Park Rates:   
- P250 - Park Entrance + Shows  
- P520 - Park Entrance + Shows + All You Can Ride  

****  
Balik Saya Promo: (FRIDAYS ONLY)  
- P150 - Park Entrance + Shows  
- P520 - Park Entrance + Shows + All You Can Ride  

****
📝Kids 3ft. below are free to enter. Kids above 3ft. are subject to regular prices.  
Ride height restrictions apply.   
  
🌭Outside foods are not allowed. They sell variety of foods and drinks in Magikland.
