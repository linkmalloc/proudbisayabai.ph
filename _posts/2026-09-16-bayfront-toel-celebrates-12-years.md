For 12 years, Bayfront Hotel Cebu has been part of countless Cebu stories—from family getaways and business trips to celebrations and quiet moments of rest. This year, it marks its 12th anniversary alongside the first anniversary of Bayfront CoLive+, its modern co-living concept for travelers, remote workers, students, and long-stay guests.

To celebrate, both brands are offering anniversary deals that bring more value to stays, gatherings, and dining experiences while giving guests more reasons to connect and make new memories in Cebu.

## **Anniversary room offers**

Guests can enjoy special anniversary rates across Bayfront properties. At **Bayfront Hotel Cebu – North Reclamation Area**, the Superior Room with breakfast for two is available for **P2,700 per night**, while **Bayfront Hotel Cebu – Capitol Site** offers its Classic Room with breakfast for two for **P2,500 per night**.

Meanwhile, **Bayfront CoLive+** offers its Micro Rooms at a special **P1,288 room-only rate**, providing a practical option for business, leisure, and longer stays. Bookings are open **September 1 to October 7, 2026**, with stays valid **September 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027**, through Bayfront’s direct booking channels.

## **Celebrate more with Caja Buffet and Bayfront banquets**

At **Caja Kitchen Cebu**, groups of four paying adults dining at the **P688 Friday/Saturday Dinner or Sunday Lunch Buffet** will be given a **mysterious paper roll** revealing their surprise: they may get **free buffet meals**, applicable to their current dining bill.

For celebrations beyond the dining table, Bayfront Hotel Cebu’s anniversary **Food and Venue Package is available for P650 per person**, with bookings throughout **September 2026** and event dates valid until **November 30, 2027**.

## **More reasons to dine at Caja Kitchen Cebu**

Guests ordering Caja Kitchen Cebu’s signature **Crispy Pata** can enjoy **12% off the total à la carte bill**. For a sweeter celebration, purchase any **whole cake for P990, Monday to Thursday throughout September**, and get an **ensaymada, cinnamon roll, muffin, or cookies for only P12**.

## **Café+ celebrates first anniversary**

As Bayfront CoLive+ turns one, **Café+** offers a treat from **October 1 to 31, 2026**: purchase any cake slice and enjoy **coffee for only Php 1**, available daily from **11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.**

As Bayfront Hotel Cebu enters its 13th year and Bayfront CoLive+ begins another chapter, both brands continue to welcome guests with more opportunities to stay, gather, dine, and celebrate in Cebu.
