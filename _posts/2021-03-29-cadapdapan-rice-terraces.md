#### CADAPDAPAN RICE TERRACES
  
If you’re thinking that you could only see the grandeur of the rice terraces in Banaue, think again for Candijay will prove you wrong! 🍃🌾  
  
🌎 Location: Barangay Cadapdapan, Municipality of Candijay, Bohol  
  
These rice terraces have been carved out of the mountains and are truly spectacular to wander through or view from an aerial perspective.  
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: By Public Transport – Bus from Tagbilaran city – Brgy. Lungsodaan, Candijay Bohol  is (92 kms \| 80.00Php) From Candijay town proper, hire a habal-habal that will take you to Barangay Cadapdapan.   
  
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: Van from Tagbilaran City – 130.00 Php. From Candijay town proper, hire a habal-habal that will take you to Barangay Cadapdapan.  
  
📌 WHAT TO BRING?  
-  Extra T-shirt  
- Towel  
- Drinking water  
- Camera  
- Mobile phones  
- Etc.
