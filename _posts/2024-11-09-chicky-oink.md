Attention, Cebu foodies! If you’re a fan of delicious, crispy Lechon Manok and melt-in-your-mouth Liempo, there’s a new spot in town you won’t want to miss. Chicky’Oink, renowned for its flavorsome dishes in Lapu-Lapu, has opened a new branch in Pit-os, Talamban, bringing its legendary taste even closer to home. Here’s what makes Chicky’Oink a must-try dining experience!  

###### A Menu to Satisfy Every Craving

Chicky’Oink’s newest branch comes with a refreshed menu that offers something for every kind of meat lover. Their specialty?  

Perfectly seasoned, slow-roasted lechon belly that’s as tender as it is flavorful. Here’s a breakdown of their Lechon Belly options:
- Mini Lechon Belly (P1,800): 1.5 to 2 kg, serves 10-15
- Medium Lechon Belly (P2,800): 3 to 3.8 kg, serves 20-25
- Large Lechon Belly (P3,600): 4 to 4.8 kg, serves 30-35  

****
If you’re a fan of spice, you can upgrade your belly with a spicy kick for just a little extra!  

###### Don’t Forget the Add-Ons!

What’s a meal without the extras? Chicky’Oink offers a selection of add-ons that complement their lechon belly perfectly:
- Manok - P280
- Chickyhang 🌶️ (Best Seller!) - P290
- Liempo - P250
- Spicy Liempo 🌶️ - P260

****
These add-ons bring extra depth to the meal, with options that range from the smoky flavor of liempo to the heat-packed Chickyhang, a must-try for spice lovers.  

###### A New Level of Dining Experience  

Chicky’Oink’s branch in Pit-os, Talamban, is located at MCP Building, where the ambiance is designed for comfort. With options for dine-in and convenient takeout, you can enjoy their mouthwatering dishes whether you’re in a hurry or ready to sit back and savor.  


###### How to Order

If you’re eager to try Chicky’Oink’s delicious offerings, placing an order is easy. Just call 0943 462 6867 for inquiries, and they’ll be happy to assist you.  

Whether you’re planning a family feast, a celebration, or simply a craving-filled lunch, Chicky’Oink promises to deliver with unforgettable flavors. Swing by their new Pit-os branch, and let your taste buds experience the finest Lechon Manok and Liempo Cebu has to offer!
