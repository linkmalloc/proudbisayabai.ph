Looking for the perfect spot to host a grand celebration with friends and family or just unwind during the upcoming holidays? Look no further—Katawa is your dream vacation destination!  

Katawa—a playful fusion of “Kaon” (eat), “Tabi” (chitchat), and “Waswas” (splash)—promises an unforgettable experience for food, fun, and relaxation by the sea. With breathtaking seaview bedrooms, a private cliffside beach, and an infinity pool, this exclusive vacation house sets the stage for unforgettable moments.  

This private resort is a family-owned venture, built with the goal of providing guests with a truly restful and unforgettable experience. What sets it apart from the many other resorts in the area is its unbeatable sunrise views, convenient location right by the main road, complete range of modern facilities, and affordable rates that make luxury accessible to all.  

###### OCTOBER PROMO
Book this October and save ₱3,000 on your stay! Rates start at ₱13,000/day during off-season and ₱18,000/day during peak season.  

###### ACCOMMODATIONS:
● 2 Seaview Master Bedrooms
● 4 Seaview Guest Bedrooms with glass walls and a spacious balcony
● Exclusive Beach House Access
● FREE use of kayaks and swimming gears  

###### FACILITIES
● Infinity Pool with a kiddie section  
● Private cliffside beachfront access  
● Entertainment area with Smart TV, billiard table, and karaoke  
● Gym equipment for fitness enthusiasts  
● A fully equipped kitchen and a cozy bar  
● Perfect photo spots for your next viral post—ideal for Facebook, Instagram, YouTube,
and TikTok!  

Complete with outdoor lounging areas, a grilling station, and parking for 6 cars, Katawa is the place to be for any celebration.
