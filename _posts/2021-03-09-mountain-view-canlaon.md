#### Mountain View   
  
Get lost with nature and Immerse yourself in the middle of this beautiful garden with the stunning view of Mt. Canlaon! 😯😍  
  
##### 🌏 Exact Location:  
La Castellana, Negros Occidental 🇵🇭

#### 📌 HOW TO GET THERE?
  
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
From La Castellana ride a bus going to Mountain View. Travel time is around 20 minutes from La Castellana town proper.   
  
#####  🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
By using a private utility, you can pin point Mountain view La Castellana in waze or google map.   
  
📌 Entrance fee: P20 per person  
  
##### 📌 What to enjoy?   
  
- Delicious foods  
- Photoshoot
