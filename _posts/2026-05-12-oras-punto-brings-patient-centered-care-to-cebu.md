**Grand Opening Marks New Chapter in Healthcare**

A new healthcare facility dedicated to accessible, timely, and patient-centered medical services has officially opened in Cebu as Oras Punto Diagnostics formally welcomed the public during its grand opening celebration on May 11, 2026.

The opening ceremony featured a ribbon-cutting and blessing of the laboratory, symbolizing the beginning of the diagnostic center’s commitment to serving individuals and families in Cebu. The event gathered distinguished guests and stakeholders who expressed support for the facility’s vision of expanding healthcare accessibility in the community.

Among the VIPs in attendance were Guest of Honor Mayor Nestor Archival, who graced the event, Mrs. Mia Cabalquinto-Alo, who delivered the opening message and acknowledgment of guests, and Dr. Gracia Niña Bongabong, who gave the closing remarks.

### **A Mission Rooted in Accessibility**

Located at the heart of Banawa, Cebu City, Oras Punto Diagnostics aims to address long-standing barriers to healthcare by making medical services more convenient, affordable, and accessible for patients who often delay consultations due to cost, distance, or inconvenience.

According to the organization, the inspiration behind Oras Punto Diagnostics stemmed from the need to bridge healthcare gaps, particularly for underserved communities and patients who struggle to access clinics and hospitals. ***The name “Oras Punto,” which means exact timing,*** reflects the facility’s mission to provide timely, accurate, and reliable healthcare services when patients need them most.

### **Focus on Early Detection and Diagnostics**

The center places strong emphasis on diagnostic and laboratory services, recognizing early and accurate diagnosis as the foundation of effective healthcare. By prioritizing preventive care and early detection, the facility hopes to improve treatment outcomes while helping reduce long-term healthcare costs for patients and families.

### **Bringing Healthcare Closer Through Mobile Services**

What sets Oras Punto Diagnostics apart is its commitment to both in-clinic and mobile healthcare services. Beyond its laboratory services, the facility offers Mobile Clinic and Mobile X-ray services, bringing diagnostic care directly to communities, workplaces, and homes. These services particularly benefit senior citizens, persons with disabilities, and residents in remote or underserved areas who may face difficulties visiting healthcare facilities.

The diagnostic center also provides home service options, allowing patients to undergo laboratory procedures without leaving their homes. Through this initiative, Oras Punto seeks to make healthcare more convenient and encourage more individuals to prioritize routine medical check-ups.

### **Strengthening Access Through HMO Partnerships**

Strengthening its credibility and accessibility, Oras Punto Diagnostics is accredited by several major HMOs, including InLife (Generali), Intellicare, Avega, Pacific Cross, Icare, and Life & Health. The management emphasized that HMO accreditation reflects compliance with healthcare standards while giving patients confidence in the quality and reliability of services offered.

### **Free Healthcare Through the Yakap Program**

In addition, Oras Punto Diagnostics is a Yakap-accredited facility, offering free check-ups, free medicines, and free laboratory tests to qualified beneficiaries. Through the Yakap Gamot program, eligible patients may receive up to ₱20,000 worth of free medicine annually, a program expected to significantly reduce the financial burden for patients managing chronic illnesses.

### **Preventive Care Saves Lives**

The facility also places strong emphasis on preventive healthcare, particularly cancer screening. Services include mammograms, low-dose chest CT scans, colonoscopy, and liver ultrasound, all aimed at detecting illnesses early before they progress into severe or costly conditions.

### **Prioritizing Mental Health**

Recognizing the growing importance of emotional and psychological well-being, Oras Punto Diagnostics also integrates mental health services into its offerings. Its outpatient mental health package covers conditions such as depression, psychoses, dementia, substance disorders, and other concerns. The facility emphasized that mental health remains an essential component of holistic healthcare and that improving awareness and early intervention remains vital in the Philippines.

### **Supporting Children’s Health and Vision**

Children’s healthcare is likewise among the center’s priorities. Through its optometric services, Oras Punto offers free eye check-ups and eyeglasses for children aged 0–15, underscoring the importance of good vision in learning, confidence, and overall development.

### **Plans for Future Expansion**

Looking ahead, Oras Punto Diagnostics also plans to expand its healthcare services through the launch of dental services, an Animal Bite Center, and TB-DOTS, allowing the facility to address a broader range of community health concerns under one roof.

Despite challenges faced by diagnostic centers today including maintaining affordability, adapting to technological advancements, and ensuring healthcare accessibility—Oras Punto Diagnostics said it remains committed to delivering quality services through efficient operations, strategic partnerships, and compassionate care.

As it opens its doors in Cebu, Oras Punto Diagnostics hopes to empower more families through early detection, preventive healthcare, and quality medical services delivered at the right time.
