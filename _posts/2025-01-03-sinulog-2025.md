<br>
Sinulog 2025 is set to dazzle once more as it returns to its iconic home—the Cebu City Sports Center. This year’s festival is packed with exciting activities that celebrate Cebu’s rich culture, faith, and artistry, making it an event to remember.  

##### Here are the events to look forward:
<h6>Sinulog sa Dakbayan 2025</h6>
<small>January 12, 2:00 PM</small>
<p>Experience traditional dances and performances that kick off the celebration with vibrant energy.</p>

<h6>Festival Queen 2025</h6>
<small>January 17, 7:00 PM</small>
<p>Witness the grace and elegance of this year’s candidates as they compete in a dazzling pageant.</p>  

<h6>Sinulog 2025: Grand Parade</h6>
<small>January 19, 9:00 AM</small>
<p>The festival’s centerpiece event, with colorful floats, creative dance routines, and lively music parading through Cebu City’s streets, ending at the Cebu City Sports Center.</p>  

<h6>Awarding Ceremonies 2025</h6>
<small>January 20, 2:00 PM</small>
<p>Celebrate the winners and outstanding performances of this year’s Sinulog festivities.
With the festival’s return to the Cebu City Sports Center, Sinulog 2025 promises to bring back the authentic and nostalgic spirit of Cebu’s most beloved tradition. Pit Señor!</p>
