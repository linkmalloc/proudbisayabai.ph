Sinulog 2025 is set to dazzle once more as it returns to its iconic home—the Cebu City Sports Center. This year’s festival is packed with exciting activities that celebrate Cebu’s rich culture, faith, and artistry, making it an event to remember.  

##### Here are the events to look forward:
<h6>Sinulog sa Dakbayan 2025</h6>
<small>January 12, 2:00 PM</small>
<p>Experience traditional dances and performances that kick off the celebration with vibrant energy.</p>

<h6>Festival Queen 2025</h6>
<small>January 17, 7:00 PM</small>
<p>Witness the grace and elegance of this year’s candidates as they compete in a dazzling pageant.</p>  

<h6>Sinulog 2025: Grand Parade</h6>
<small>January 19, 9:00 AM</small>
<p>The festival’s centerpiece event, with colorful floats, creative dance routines, and lively music parading through Cebu City’s streets, ending at the Cebu City Sports Center.</p>  

<h6>Awarding Ceremonies 2025</h6>
<small>January 20, 2:00 PM</small>
<p>Celebrate the winners and outstanding performances of this year’s Sinulog festivities.
With the festival’s return to the Cebu City Sports Center, Sinulog 2025 promises to bring back the authentic and nostalgic spirit of Cebu’s most beloved tradition. Pit Señor!</p>

<h5><a href="https://proudbisayabai.ph/story/2025/01/08/walk-with-jesus">WALK WITH JESUS FOR SEÑOR SANTO NIÑO</a></h5>
Devotion and faith illuminated the early morning today as thousands of devotees filled Osmeña Boulevard for the Penitential Walk with Jesus, a tradition marking the start of the 460th Fiesta Señor celebration.  

The procession began at 4:00 AM at Fuente Osmeña, culminating at the Basilica Minore del Santo Niño, where the “Opening Salvo” Mass was celebrated at 5:00 AM. This heartfelt gathering serves as a spiritual prelude to the Fiesta Señor 2025, running from January 9 to 24, with novena masses and religious activities dedicated to the Santo Niño de Cebu.  

The event showcased the enduring faith of the Cebuanos, reinforcing the deep connection between the people and their beloved Señor Santo Niño.

###### LOOK: Highlights from the Opening Salvo of Sinulog 2025!

The program began with vibrant traditional dances performed by high school students from Cebu, showcasing the province’s rich culture. This was followed by a captivating reenactment of the arrival of Señor Sto. Niño, presented by various universities in Cebu.  

Cebu City Mayor Garcia officially opened the grand celebration, joined by city government officials and the candidates of Miss Cebu 2025, making the event even more festive and memorable.
