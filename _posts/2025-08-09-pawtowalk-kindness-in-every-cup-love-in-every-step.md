A Heartwarming Initiative by Don Macchiato Group of Companies

In the bustling streets of Urgello, a new kind of movement is taking shape—one fueled not only by caffeine, but by compassion.

Don Macchiato’s PawtWalk, an outreach initiative under the company’s growing community efforts, is changing the way we connect with the world—by turning ordinary Sundays into extraordinary moments of kindness.

On a heart-filled day at the Urgello KUKU branch, baristas served more than just drinks—they served hope. With bowls of food, gentle hands, and open hearts, volunteers fed and cared for stray dogs and cats, the often-overlooked souls that roam our streets but warm our hearts.

*Brewing a Culture of Care*

PawtWalk’s mission is simple but powerful: to help, rescue, and care for street dogs and cats who deserve love and protection. As part of Don Macchiato Group of Companies’ long-term community vision, this initiative isn’t just a one-time event—it’s a commitment. The goal? To make PawtWalk a consistent act of compassion in every neighborhood they serve.

“Sundays are no longer just for rest,” shares Kobe Go, one of the youth leaders behind the event. “They’re for showing love—to people, to animals, to the community.”

*Powered by People with Purpose*

This weekend’s outreach brought together an inspiring mix of students from different universities, media supporters, local influencers, and kind-hearted volunteers—a community united by purpose.

The event was held in celebration of Proud Bisaya Bai founder Ariel Alegado’s birthday, proving that the best way to celebrate life is by giving back.

*The Bigger Picture*

What started as a small idea over coffee is now growing into a movement of meaningful impact. With future plans already brewing, Don Macchiato envisions PawtWalk as a regular program, reaching more areas, rescuing more animals, and encouraging more people to lead with empathy.

**Because at Don Macchiato, it’s not just about serving coffee It’s about serving kindness.**

<u>*One paw, one step, one heart at a time.* </u>
