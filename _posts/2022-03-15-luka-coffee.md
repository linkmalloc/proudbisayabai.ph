##### Luka Coffee

A coffee shop that will drive you crazy, Luka Coffee, Ma Luka jud ka! Chill and relax on a beach side coffee shop. ☕️🥤🧁

Scenic and picture perfect for your travel memories while enjoying a cup of coffee and indulging on sumptuous cakes feeling the fresh sea breeze leisurely gazing at the islands of hushed Siquijor, a mile distant Sumilon and the bustling Dumaguete. 

🌍 Exact Location: Natalio Bacalso Avenue, Oslob, Cebu, Philippines

🕒 Operating hours: 9:30AM - 8PM daily 

###### How to get there? 

🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu Southbus Terminal, ride a bus going to Oslob Cebu. Then tell the driver to drop you off at Brgy. Luka. About 3-4 hours travel time, with estimated fare of P250-280. Visible Sign Board and it's along the way.  

 🗺 Via Waze/ Google Map: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze / GMap: 📍[https://g.co/kgs/oqsNkf](https://g.co/kgs/oqsNkf)
