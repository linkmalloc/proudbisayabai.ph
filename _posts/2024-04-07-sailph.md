Experience the beauty of the crystal-clear waters and stunning scenery of Cebu aboard a traditional paraw sailboat. SailPH offers private and shared cruises, perfect for a romantic getaway, a fun family outing, or a bonding experience with friends.   

Paraw sailing is an ancient Filipino tradition that dates back to pre-colonial times. It's a unique and authentic way to explore the wonders of Cebu.  

In addition to sailboat cruises, SailPH also offers crystal kayak rentals, allowing you to explore the coastline at your own pace.  

Types of Cruises:  
• Private cruises: Perfect for a romantic getaway or a special occasion.  
• Shared cruises: A great way to meet new people and experience Cebu with others.  

Destinations: Explore the stunning islands and coves around Santa Fe, Cebu, including Naked Island, Sandbar Island, and Hidden Beach.  

Activities: Depending on the cruise you choose, you may have the opportunity to go swimming, snorkeling, fishing, or simply relax and enjoy the scenery.  

So what are you waiting for? Book your SailPH adventure today and create memories that will last a lifetime!
