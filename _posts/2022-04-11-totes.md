##### Trendsetting with a Mission  
A Good Middle Ground on Limiting Carbon Footprint while Keeping the Course of Style with Totes  

Totes is a fusion of love for bags, convenience, and a socially conscious product that
enhances functionality in ordinary living. Carrying a tote is a statement of support for
ecologically responsible commodities and appreciation for the City Ordinance 2343's
plastic prohibition, which is enforced every Wednesday and Saturday.  

When the pandemic struck in March of 2020, sick days had arrived, and fate had revealed
its time for some. The necessity for reusable eco-bags in the supermarket has skyrocketed
to minimize the mountain high waste that could possibly generate a double risk of
contamination. It can be utilized for more than just shopping, all it takes is ingenuity.
When one of these Totes bags has seen the end of its days, the opportunity for a new life
exists. Totes grew encouraged to be a part of the movement, but intended it to be
universal. Totes was born as a result of this.

Totes bags are a convenient and simple approach to reducing your carbon impact, as the
Philippines produces a staggering 2.7 million tons of plastic waste each year, with an
estimated 20% washing up in the ocean. Limiting your carbon footprint is an eco-friendly
lifestyle decision that may appear difficult at first, but is entirely achievable. You don't
have to make grand gestures or go off the grid. Small changes can make a significant
difference, and over time, these small changes can add up to have a massive impact.

or without designs in order to promote a no-plastic policy and an eco-friendly lifestyle in
the Philippines.
