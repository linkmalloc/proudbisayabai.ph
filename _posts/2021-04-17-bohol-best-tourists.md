Bohol is a province in the 

Bohol is a province of the Philippines, in the country’s Central Visayas region. It comprises Bohol Island and numerous smaller surrounding islands. Bohol is known for coral reefs and unusual geological formations, notably the Chocolate Hills. On the main island, near the town of Carmen, these 1,200 or so symmetrical mounds turn cocoa-brown in the dry season, contrasting with the surrounding jungle's greenery

Bohol is the tenth largest island of the Philippines, The province is a popular tourist destination with its beaches and resorts.

Here are some beautiful places in Bohol that every first-time visitor must check out.   
  
- Panglao Island  
- Chocolate Hills  
- Hinagdanan Cave  
- Loboc River  
- Bamboo Hanging Bridge  
- Man-Made Forest  
- Anda Beach  
- Blood Compact Shrine  
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: Fly to Tagbilaran from Manila or a fastcraft ride from (different neighboring provinces in Visayas) Cebu City or Dumaguete City or Siquijor or Southern Leyte (Ubay Port).  
  
There are also regular trips of fastcraft/RORO from Cebu City directly plying to Talibon Port, the closest port. Trinidad is more or less 30 minutes ride from Talibon.
