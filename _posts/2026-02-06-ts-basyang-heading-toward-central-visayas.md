NEWS REPORT: Tropical Storm Basyang (Penha) is now over the Bohol Sea and moving west-northwest toward Central Visayas, according to PAGASA Tropical Cyclone Bulletin No. 16 issued at 8:00 AM, February 6, 2026. The storm packs 65 km/h winds with gusts up to 90 km/h, bringing gale-force winds, heavy rainfall, rough seas, and possible storm surge across affected areas.



TCWS No. 2 remains in effect over parts of Cebu, Bohol, Negros, Leyte, Siquijor, and nearby provinces, while TCWS No. 1 covers surrounding regions. PAGASA warns of flooding, landslides, and dangerous sea conditions, urging residents and local authorities to remain alert and follow safety advisories.



The next bulletin will be issued at 11:00 AM today.



Source: DOST-PAGASA
