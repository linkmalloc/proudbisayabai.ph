A new seaside experience is drawing crowds at IL Corso Filinvest Malls as its latest attraction opens to the public today, April 18, 2025.&#x20;



Blending art, leisure, and breathtaking views, this vibrant destination is The Cebu Lighthouse in partnership with Anjo World Theme Park that is set to become Cebu’s next must-visit seaside hotspot.



For an affordable entrance fee of ₱150, visitors can step into an immersive space featuring large-scale installations and interactive elements designed to capture the beauty and spirit of Cebu. With stunning backdrops perfect for photos and moments worth remembering, every visit feels like a mini escape by the sea.



From relaxing strolls along the baywalk to soaking in panoramic sunset views, the experience offers something for everyone—whether you’re with friends, family, or simply chasing a peaceful view of the horizon.



Come and see where art meets the ocean, and rediscover the charm of Cebu’s coastline like never before!



OPENING HOURS

Weekdays: 3PM - 10PM

Weekends 3PM - 11PM
