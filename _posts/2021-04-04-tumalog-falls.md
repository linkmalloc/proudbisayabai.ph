#### Tumalog Falls
LOOK: Come and visit the bright and scenic Tumalog Falls.🍃😯💦  
  
🌎 Brgy. Luka, Oslob, Cebu City   
  
It's near the famous whaleshark attraction in oslob so if you want to complete your tour, you need to witness this majestic falls that stands at approximately 80 - 100 meters in height.   
  
Have a choice to pay 50 pesos going down and coming back up the falls or walk the 600 meter steep hill.
