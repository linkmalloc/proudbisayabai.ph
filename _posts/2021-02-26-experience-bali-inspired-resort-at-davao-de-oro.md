#### How to get there?   
<hr style="border-color: #c5c5c552 !important;">

##### 🌍 Exact Location:
Brgy. Poblacion, Montevista, Davao de Oro   
  

****
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
From Tagum City ride a Bus at Bus Terminal approx 40mins. travel time. About 2hrs travel time from Davao City.    
  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.   

****
##### 📌 ENTRANCE FEE:  
- 30 pesos (viewing & picture taking only)   
- 80 pesos  

****
