#### JVR Island in the Sky
  
Pabugnaw sata Bai! Na stress ka this week? Arats mo RELAX sa ta! 💨🌼🍃 

It's a good place to relax and think about life. See the beauty of nature at JVR Island in the Sky.

###### Perks!
✔️ Colorful rainbow bridge  
✔️ Overlooking view of Western Cebu  
✔️ Great for Nature Lovers  

  
🌎 Exact Location: Gaas Balamban, Transcentral Highway  
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Kung mo gikan ka sa JY Square Mall mangita raka ug habal-habal, dili raka maglisud kay daghan mamasahero sa palibot but make sure sabota ug tarong ang plite ninyo daan. The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.   
  
📌 Fees:   
  
###### Entrance fee  
- Adult: P55.00  
- Children: P30.00  
- Cottages: P350.00  
- Cable Car is 150 per head (but not available as of now)
