Two graduates of the University of Cebu (UC) landed in the Top 10 of the August 2026 Licensure Examination for Criminologists, according to the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC).

  

Kristine Mae Calipayan Gatmen ranked eighth with a rating of 92.80%, while Ynman Daniel Engbino Usman placed 10th with 92.45%.

  

The two were among 15 examinees listed by the PRC among the 10 highest ranks, with some positions shared by examinees who earned identical ratings.

  

Jeb Jobet Remolleno Gabriel of Polytechnic College of Davao del Sur topped the examination with a rating of 93.70%.

  

Mark Anthony Eufracio Carlos of Nueva Ecija University of Science and Technology-Cabanatuan ranked second with 93.55%, followed by Shiela Mae Garcia Garcia of Saint Michael’s College-Iligan City with 93.40%.

  

The other top examinees were:

  

\* Nicolo Dela Cruz Atienza, Araullo University — 93.25%

\* Eluhim Caranto Machucar, Araullo University-San Jose — 93.05%

\* Johndex Emanuel Soriano Villarico, University of Mindanao-Digos College — 93.05%

\* Sanicolas Ramos Almonia III, Araullo University — 92.95%

\* Janz Reid Mirontos Manlanot, University of Mindanao-Tagum — 92.85%

\* Rhein Ivy Macasero Marga, University of Mindanao-Digos College — 92.80%

\* John Russel Reyes Tayoto, Kolehiya ng Lungsod ng Lipa — 92.80%

\* Roy Saludares Francisco Jr., University of Mindanao-Davao City — 92.75%

\* Shaira Mae Iligan Ibañez, Southern Leyte State University-Sogod — 92.45%

\* Christian Joseph Lozano Para, Asia Pacific College of Advanced Studies — 92.45%.
