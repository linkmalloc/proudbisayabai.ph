#### GG Bakes
Naka ari naka diri? KALAMI! Naa tanan imong gipangita, Bisitaha ni  kan-anan sa Kayda "Dimsum, Street Food, and More" in Carcar, City Cebu! 🍤🥓🍗 🥤  
  
🕑 Operating Hours: 9:00AM - 9:00PM  
  
GG Bakes is located at Cambuntan, Bolinawan, Carcar City - corner of St. Martha's Cemetery beside Tupas Memorial Services.   
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: From Southbus Terminal just ride any bus going to Carcar, Cebu and tell the driver to drop-off at GG Bakes Food Hub. Fare P100 pesos one way.  
  
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a Bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
