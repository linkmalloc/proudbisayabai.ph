Toledo City Hospital will receive P100 million in provincial government assistance to fund improvements after Cebu Gov. Pamela Baricuatro approved a resolution authorizing the allocation.

Baricuatro announced the funding during her visit to Toledo City Hall on Monday, Nov. 23, where she joined city officials for the local government's flag-raising ceremony.

The assistance will be released in two tranches to support upgrades at the city-operated hospital.

Toledo City Hospital operates under the Toledo City Government and is not part of the provincial government's network of provincial and district hospitals.

During her address, Baricuatro also called on local officials to maintain cooperation despite political differences, warning that internal conflicts could hamper development efforts.

“In unity there is strength and in division there is weakness. So kung magkagubot ta, kita ra’y alkansi,” she said.

The provincial government also brought its mobile kitchen to City Hall to serve free hot meals to government employees and deployed a mobile clinic to provide medical services.

The clinic offered mammography, which Baricuatro encouraged women employees to undergo as part of their efforts to monitor their health.

“Please avail mo, sa atong mga kababayen-an nga naa dinhi karon because importante na as we age,” she said.

Mayor Marjorie “Joie” Perales welcomed the governor and thanked the provincial government for extending assistance to the city.

She described Baricuatro as an approachable leader who listens to and cares about the public.

“Ang imong pagka-mapaubsanon, pagkabuotan, pagkamaabi-abihon, makita namo ang usa ka leader nga andam maminaw, daling maduolan ug adunay tinuod nga pagpakabana sa katawhan,” Perales said.

Also present during the ceremony were Vice Mayor Jay Sigue, members of the City Council, Provincial Councilors League Cebu Province president and ex-officio board member Ricardo “Nick” Pepito, Office of the Governor Chief of Staff James Canoy, and Provincial Engineering Office head Engr. Hector Jamora.
