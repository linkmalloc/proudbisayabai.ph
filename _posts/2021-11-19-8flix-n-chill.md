##### 8FLIX &amp; Chill

Unli imong gipangita? Ayaw na ug ka sad ari ta sa 8FLIX &amp; Chill Unlimited Tuslob Buwa Cebu City, Lami jud diri!

Tuslob buwa is a Cebuano street food which originated from the barangays of Pasil and Suba in Cebu City. Sulit ug lingaw kaayo labi na sa mga group of friends nga ganahan maka tipid ug mag bonding. It consists of a mixture of pig brain, soy sauce, and some oil. Gisahon ra nimo ang mga ingredients sa kaha (pan), then if mo buwa na or bubbles pwedi na mo kaon with puso. 

🌏 Exact Location:  Beside Queensland Manor Condominiums Parking Lot Rahman St., Zapatera Cebu City, Philippines

📍 They also have branch 8FLIX Talisay City 

🕑 Operating hours: 11AM - 11PM daily

###### How to get there?

🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated via Waze or Google Map: https://g.co/kgs/5tzTTL  

📌 Main dish:  
- Unlimited Tuslob Buwa for only 200 pesos (good for 1-2 persons only). 
- 3rd person will add P50.00
- Rate comes with 10pcs puso and unlimited lamas
- Additional puso P7.00 each.
