Looking for an exciting outdoor adventure with your barkada? Skip the usual beach trips and explore one of Cebu’s best hiking destinations — Mt. Kapayas in Catmon, Cebu.

  

Standing at approximately 783 meters above sea level, Mt. Kapayas offers an unforgettable trekking experience featuring panoramic mountain views, fresh countryside air, challenging trails, and a rewarding summit climb. Located in northern Cebu, this hidden gem is ideal for hikers, nature lovers, and adventure seekers searching for a peaceful escape away from crowded tourist spots.

  

Whether you are planning a weekend hike, a barkada camping trip, or a nature getaway in Cebu, Mt. Kapayas provides a memorable outdoor experience surrounded by lush landscapes and scenic views.

  

Mt. Kapayas Location in Catmon, Cebu

  

Mt. Kapayas Sitio Kampanya, Brgy. Kabangkaya,

Catmon, Cebu

  

Located in the quiet highlands of Catmon, Mt. Kapayas highlights the natural beauty of northern Cebu beyond its famous beaches and waterfalls. The mountain offers hikers a refreshing journey through green surroundings, cool mountain air, and breathtaking views of the countryside.

  

How to Get to Mt. Kapayas Cebu

  

Via Public Transportation from Cebu City

  

Traveling to Mt. Kapayas from Cebu City is convenient through public transportation:

  

1\. Proceed to the Cebu North Bus Terminal located at SM City Cebu.

2\. Ride a bus bound for Carmen.

3\. Inform the driver that you are heading to Catmon Town Proper.

4\. From Eskina Katambisan, hire a habal-habal going to Mt. Kapayas.

  

The estimated habal-habal fare is around ₱400–₱500 round trip, with possible side trips to nearby attractions such as Tinubdan Falls or Kanggangawan Falls.

  

For Travelers Bringing Their Own Motorcycle

  

Visitors traveling by motorcycle may continue until the designated drop-off point near the hiking area.

  

Travel reminder: Drive carefully, especially during rainy weather. Some sections of the road can be rough, steep, and slippery.

  

Mt. Kapayas Hiking and Trekking Experience

  

The Mt. Kapayas trek begins from the drop-off point, where hikers only need to walk for approximately 20 minutes before reaching the campsite.

  

Depending on your itinerary and hiking pace, you can either continue to the summit on the same day or set up camp first before completing the climb.

  

The trail features uphill sections, natural landscapes, and peaceful mountain surroundings. As you ascend, each step brings you closer to the rewarding summit views that make Mt. Kapayas one of Cebu’s must-visit hiking destinations.

  

Mt. Kapayas Fees, Expenses, and Budget Guide

  

Permit Fee

  

\* ₱250 per group

\* Maximum of 10 persons per group

  

Habal-Habal Transportation

  

\* Around ₱450 per person

\* Round trip transportation with possible falls side trip

  

Guide and Camp Fee

  

\* ₱250 per person

  

Camping at Mt. Kapayas and Local Community Experience

  

One of the highlights of the Mt. Kapayas hiking experience is the warm hospitality of the local community.

  

A water source is available at Nanay’s place near the campsite. Hikers are encouraged to use water responsibly and may provide any amount as appreciation for the service.

  

Visitors can also charge their phones or power banks and request home-cooked meals, including delicious Bisaya-style chicken dishes prepared by local residents.

  

Supporting these community services helps promote sustainable tourism and preserves the natural beauty and hiking experience of Mt. Kapayas.

  

What to Bring for Your Mt. Kapayas Hiking Adventure

  

Before starting your climb, prepare these hiking essentials:

  

\* Comfortable hiking shoes

\* Extra clothes

\* Drinking water and trail snacks

\* Rain protection

\* Flashlight or headlamp

\* Power bank

\* Personal essentials

\* Garbage bag for proper waste disposal

  

Why Mt. Kapayas Should Be Your Next Cebu Hiking Destination

  

Mt. Kapayas is more than just a mountain climb — it is an adventure filled with friendship, nature, and discovery. Whether you are an experienced mountaineer or a barkada looking for a new outdoor activity in Cebu, this hidden mountain destination in Catmon offers a rewarding escape from the busy city.

  

Pack your hiking gear, gather your friends, and experience the breathtaking beauty waiting at the summit of Mt. Kapayas, Catmon, Cebu.
