Throughout its years of tenure, MATLEX has remained committed to bringing quality materials to its clientele’s needs. The brand actively searches, sources, develops and provides solutions that support client projects from start to finish.

  

MATLEX Rubber currently stands as a trusted partner in the construction and industrial sectors, continuously innovating and maintaining relationships that go beyond traditional client-supplier transactions. 

  

The custom rubber provider’s journey began with a simple goal: to provide high-quality, made-to-order rubber products that meet the unique needs of every client. Backed by modern and state-of-the-art facilities, as well as a skilled team, MATLEX supplies and installs durable rubber solutions across different industries, aiding businesses in building safer and long-lasting projects. 

  

Beyond offering top-tier rubber materials, MATLEX values collaborative efforts shared with clients. Its team listens, collaborates, adapts to meet every client’s vision and expectation, delivering what it promises. The values it continuously fosters through its experience with international partners and clients helps the brand build a culture of excellence and continuous improvement. 

  

From essential raw materials to custom-fabricated components MATLEX delivers versatile solutions such as:

  

Construction materials

Rubber supplies

Road safety solutions

Parking Solutions

EPDM Granules

Waterstop

  

As your one-stop partner for rubber supplies, construction materials and projects, MATLEX continues to set the benchmark for affordable, quality rubber products, and a reliable supply chain support across every industry it serves.
