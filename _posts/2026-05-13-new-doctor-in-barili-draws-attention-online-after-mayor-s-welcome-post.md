A newly appointed doctor in Barili is currently gaining attention online not only for his medical credentials but also for the amusing reactions he received from netizens.



John-John Garcia recently welcomed Dr. Kean Louie Llamoso as the new Internal Medicine consultant at MFG Infirmary through a social media post that quickly circulated online.



While many residents expressed excitement over the addition of a new healthcare professional in the municipality, others could not help but notice the doctor’s charming looks, leading to a flood of funny and entertaining comments from netizens.



Among the viral comments were:

“Pwede magpa-IE doc bisan dili buntis? Nangutana akong mega.”&#x20;

“Doc, pwede magpa-inject anti-rabies bisan wa gipaak sa iro?”&#x20;

“Naay gipamati sa lawas ❌ Naay gibati sa doctor ✅”



The lighthearted reactions quickly entertained online users, with many joining the comment section to share playful jokes and supportive messages for the new doctor.



Despite the humorous online buzz, many residents also welcomed the arrival of Dr. Llamoso as a positive development for healthcare services in Barili, especially in strengthening medical support within the municipality.



The post continues to gain reactions and shares online as netizens jokingly ask what kind of “check up” they would like to schedule with the doctor.
