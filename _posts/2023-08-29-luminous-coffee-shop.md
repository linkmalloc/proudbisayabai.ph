Luminous Espresso Industry PH is a coffee shop from Qatar that recently opened its first branch in the Philippines, located at IL Corso Cebu. The shop serves not just freshly brewed coffee, but also a variety of palate-stimulating dishes and desserts, all set to a calming soundscape that brings you closer to the sea.  

The menu features all-day rice meals, pizzas, pastas, cakes and pastries, frappes, hot and cold chocolate, hot and iced coffee, cocktails, and wines. The interior of the shop is stylish and comfortable, with a large outdoor seating area overlooking the sea perfect with your intimate dates and memorable family gatherings. 

Whether you're looking for a quick caffeine fix or a leisurely meal with friends, Luminous Espresso Industry PH is the perfect place to enjoy the best of both worlds: coffee and the seaside.
