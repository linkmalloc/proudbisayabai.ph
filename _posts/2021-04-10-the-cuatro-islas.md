#### The Cuatro Islas
LOOK: Visit the Beautiful group of islands located in Leyte!🏖🏝  
  
Cuatro Islas (Spanish, meaning "Four Islands") is a group of islands belonging to the municipalities of Inopacan and Hindang, in the province of Leyte, Philippines.   
  
###### ⛵️ Digyo Island
The ‘islandhoppers’ because of its white sandbar that protrudes in the southern tip of the island.   

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/proudbisayabaiph2/image/upload/v1618029898/the_cuatro_islas/sfilurwfmnshjrym6ayu.jpg)

###### ⛵️ Mahaba Island
A long stretch of white sugary sand beach.
