Cebu’s skyline just got more exciting as Sky Experience Adventure’s Sky Walk Extreme, the country’s first and only sky walk experience, officially reopens to the public. Located at the Crown Regency Hotel & Towers, this one-of-a-kind attraction returns with upgraded safety features and an even more breathtaking view of the Queen City of the South.  

After being closed for a significant period, the Sky Walk Extreme is finally back—better, safer, and more thrilling than ever. The management proudly announced a complete overhaul of their overhead lifeline system, full-body harness, and shock-absorbing fall system. These improvements now align with global safety standards—the same standards used in skyscraper construction and professional stunt work.  

And what a view it is. Since its last opening, Cebu’s cityscape has grown, now featuring new architectural landmarks and infrastructure projects. Whether it’s your first time or a long-awaited return visit, the panorama from above offers a fresh perspective of Cebu’s vibrant urban landscape.  

The Sky Walk Extreme is now open daily from 2:00 PM to 12:00 midnight, giving visitors plenty of time to catch the city bathed in golden hour light or sparkling under the stars.
