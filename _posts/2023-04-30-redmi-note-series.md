LOOK: Xiaomi recently unveiled its latest budget smartphone lineup, the Redmi Note 12 Series, at the Rocks Grill and Tavern.

The series includes three variants - the Redmi Note 12, Redmi Note 12 Pro, and Redmi Note 12 Pro Max - with a 6.6-inch FHD+ display and a high refresh rate of 120Hz. The devices are equipped with a powerful Qualcomm Snapdragon 870 chipset, up to 8GB of RAM, and 256GB of storage, making them ideal for gaming and streaming. Additionally, the phones feature an impressive camera setup, with the Redmi Note 12 and 12 Pro sporting a 64MP main camera and the 12 Pro Max boasting a 108MP main camera.  

The launch event at the Rocks Grill and Tavern showcased the Redmi Note 12 Series' features and capabilities in an immersive technological environment. Xiaomi has also launched an extensive advertising campaign across various media channels, featuring commercials highlighting the phones' features and capabilities, including their impressive camera setup, high refresh rate displays, and powerful hardware.
