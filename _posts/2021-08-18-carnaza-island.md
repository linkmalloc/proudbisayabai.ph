###### Carnaza Island  
  
A Must-visit paradise in Carnaza Island, Daanbantayan, Northern Cebu!💦🏖 🌴  
  
> Naka pag Carnaza na ba Tanan? Ilado ang maong Isla tungod sa puting balas ug tin aw nga tubig niini. Mingaw ug layo sa lungsod or siyudad maong sakto kaayo para sa mga gustong mag relax ug mo pahuway! 😍  

Carnaza Island is made up of six sitios: Carnaza, Daanbaryo, Linao, Liog-Liog, Candionesio, and Pantao. Each of them has their own special charm. The dazzling seas of the island's northern and eastern portions are excellent for underwater activities to see its spectacular marine life, while the beaches of the island's south and west feature gorgeous beaches with powdered white sand.


###### Carnaza Eco Park  

Carnaza Eco Park is the island's primary resort, and it is owned by the Osmeñas. This resort has white-splashed wooden tents that are perfectly lined along the front and blend in with nature as if they were always supposed to be there. It is indeed a perfect place for glamping.

###### HOW TO GET THERE?  
  
🗺 From Cebu City:  
Go to Cebu North Terminal and get on a bus bound for Maya-Kawit. (P200-250/way). Take the earliest trip at 3am so you can catch the passenger boat to Carnaza Island. Tell the driver that you are alighting in TAPILON PORT, Daanbantayan and not Maya port. Travel time from the city to Tapilon is 3-4 hours (About 129.0 km) Once in Tapilon port, hop on a boat going to Carnaza Island. (P100/way) Travel time takes 2-3 hours on the average. (About 26 kms from Tapilon)  
  
🗺 From NAVAL, BILIRAN:  
Go to Naval, Port, hop on a boat going to Higatangan Island.  (P60/way) Once in Higatangan Island, Naval, Biliran charter a boat to Carnaza Island. P3,500 8,000/boat 15 pax capacity) Travel time takes 1-2 hours on the average. (About 18 kms from Higatangan Island)

- P200 per night, good for 2pax.There are 20 standard rooms (woodshed) of the same size and type.   
- You may bring your own camping tents as well.  
- You can bring foods, and don't forget extra money.
