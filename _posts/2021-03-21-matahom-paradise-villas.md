#### Matahom Paradise Villas 
<center>BUCKET LIST CHECK!  😱🏖🌊  </center>

****

Experience Bali vibes at the newest exclusive resort villa at Matahom Paradise Villas Northern part of Cebu!   
  
✨Matahom is a bisaya word of BEAUTIFUL. Some say it’s alluring, fascinating, magnificent, relaxing, lovely, and marvelous!

🌍 Exact Location: Kawit Medillin, Cebu   
  
📌 How to get there?   
   
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Search "Villa Edar", they are located before that resort, a tarpauline can be seen along the road. Whether you are riding a motorcycle (habal-habal) or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
