Cebu City, Philippines, 10 June 2026 — Marco Polo Plaza Cebu invites guests to indulge in The Lamb Affair, a bold and elevated culinary showcase created exclusively for Cebu Food & Wine Festival 2026. Available from June 6 to 27, 2026, the limited-time offering celebrates premium lamb through contemporary Filipino-inspired flavors masterfully crafted by Chef Juanito Abangan and Chef Jan Jacob.

At the heart of the experience are two signature creations that reinterpret familiar local flavors through refined culinary artistry.

The Tocino Spiced Leg of Lamb Kare-Kare features slow pot-roasted leg of lamb infused with tocino spices,served with burnt eggplant, roasted sweet potato, charred corn, crispy kadaifi, and finished with a rich peanut velouté. Comforting yet sophisticated, the dish delivers layers of sweetness, smokiness, and depth while elevating the essence of the beloved Filipino classic.

Meanwhile, the Leg of Lamb Dinakdakan Samosa offers a vibrant contrast in both texture and flavor. Golden, crisp samosa pastry is filled with charcoal-grilled leg of lamb, ginger, chili, and red onion, accompanied by calamansi aioli, mango pico chutney, and fermented garlic yogurt for a balanced interplay of smoky, tangy, and savory notes.

While both dishes remain central to the experience, each outlet presents The Lamb Affair in a distinct and thoughtfully curated way.

At El Viento and the Lobby Lounge, guests may enjoy The Lamb Affair Duet, a composed sampler experience designed for sharing and relaxed indulgence. Each order beautifully pairs three pieces of each signature creation, inviting diners to savor multiple flavor profiles in one elevated presentation. Set within the warm and social atmosphere of the hotel’s lounge spaces, the duet offers an approachable yet refined dining experience ideal for casual gatherings, cocktails, and leisurely conversations.

At Blu Bar & Grill, the culinary journey takes on a more intimate and contemporary expression. Framed by panoramic city views, each dish is presented individually, allowing diners to fully appreciate the craftsmanship, complexity, and distinct identity of every creation. The refined ambiance of the restaurant enhances the bold flavors and modern presentation, transforming the experience into a sophisticated evening indulgence.

To complete the experience across all participating outlets, guests may pair their selections with a glass of Instant Bécot 2018 Bordeaux, thoughtfully selected to complement the richness and layered flavors of the menu.

As part of Cebu Food & Wine Festival 2026, The Lamb Affair reflects Marco Polo Plaza Cebu’s continuing dedication to innovative dining experiences that celebrate Filipino inspiration through contemporary culinary expression and world-class hospitality.For reservations and inquiries:
