###### PASEO de Porferio  

\#VisitSogod: Murag need nato mo palayo ron dah! Looking for a place na maka relax and chill sa Bukid?  

A Must-Visit Paseo de Porferio in Sogod North Cebu! 
A perfect ground to unwind. A place to relax and enjoy the richness of Mother Earth. This 8000sq meter campground is ideal also for for wedding venue, reunion, birthday, and camping. 

🌏 Exact Location: Binaobao 2, Sogod North Cebu 🇵🇭

🕐 Operating hours :

Check in: 2:00 PM
Check out: 12:00 NOON 

###### Other information: 
- Reservations must be made &requires 50% down payment and balance should be settled upon check out.
- There is no corkage fee
- Pets are allowed in the camp.
-Bonfire area
- Pet- friendly campsite
- Kite spot
- Common open native payag inside the campground 
- Spacious parking area is available inside the campground

****
