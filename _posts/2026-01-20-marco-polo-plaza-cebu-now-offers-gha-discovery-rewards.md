Guests at Marco Polo Plaza Cebu can now enjoy special rewards and benefits through GHA DISCOVERY, a global hotel loyalty program. Members get access to over 950 hotels across more than 40 brands worldwide and can earn points, enjoy perks, and take part in unique local experiences from their very first stay.

With DISCOVERY Dollars (D$), members can pay for rooms, dining, spa treatments, and other hotel services. Members can also earn up to 7% back in D$, plus enjoy free room upgrades, early check-ins, late check-outs, and exclusive member rates.

Marco Polo Plaza Cebu also offers local experiences that showcase Cebuano culture. Guests can join an Adobo Pizza Cooking Class for two or a Dim Sum making session with lunch at Hai Shin Lou Seafood King, combining fun, food, and Filipino flavors.

Joining GHA DISCOVERY is free, and guests can sign up on the hotel’s website to start earning rewards and enjoying special benefits at Marco Polo Plaza Cebu and partner hotels around the world.

Located on top of Nivel Hills, Marco Polo Plaza Cebu is known for luxury rooms, great views, award-winning dining, and warm Cebuano hospitality, making it one of the top destinations in Cebu.
