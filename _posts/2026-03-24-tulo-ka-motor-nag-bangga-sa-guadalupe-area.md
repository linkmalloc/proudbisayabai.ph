Guadalupe, Cebu City – Just after midnight on March 23, 2026, a minor traffic incident involving three motorcycles occurred at the V-Rama corner Bacayo, in front of Villa Fatima Subdivision.



The crash involved a female driver heading to her work duty, a delivery rider, and another motorist who was on his way home to Guadalupe. One of the riders said he was alone among the two motorcycles when the collision happened.



In an interview, the female driver explained that she had already signaled her turn from a distance, but the rider suddenly attempted to make a turn in front of her. “Layo pa ko nag-signal, pero na bangga,” she said.



Fortunately, the incident resulted only in minor injuries, with the female driver sustaining a small wound. Authorities arrived at the scene to investigate the circumstances, while traffic briefly slowed in the area during the cleanup.



Motorists and riders are reminded to stay alert, especially during late-night travel, to prevent similar accidents.
