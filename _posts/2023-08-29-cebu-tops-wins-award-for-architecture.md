Tops Cebu, a popular tourist destination in the city, has won a prestigious international award for its outstanding architecture. The award was given by the Asia Architecture Design Awards (AADA), which is a leading competition that recognizes excellence in architectural design in Asia.  

Tops Cebu was awarded the "Best Commercial Building Architecture Design" award for its redevelopment project. The project was designed by ArchiGlobal Inc., a Cebu-based architectural firm. The new design of Tops Cebu features a three-story building with a modern and chic aesthetic. The building also has a panoramic view of the city, making it a popular spot for tourists and locals alike.
