##### Kota Beach

\#VisitBantayan: Where to next? Asa pa ba, edi sa Kota Beach na ta! Kota Beach is situated in the well-known island of Bantayan, located in the Northernmost tip of Cebu. 💦🏝

Want to wake up to a golden sun and swim in turquoise waters? Want to experience and eat foods like locals? Want to explore the white-sand beach in a twilight afternoon? And relax in a comfy rooms after a tiring escapade? Kota Beach offers you all of that! Check out the details below for more info.

🕑 CHECK-IN | CHECK-OUT: 
- Check-in  2:00 PM
- Check-out 12:00 PM
- Reception open until 8:00 PM
****
🌎 Exact Location: F. Duarte Street, Santa Fe, Bantayan Island, Cebu, Philippines, 6047

###### How to get there?  
A. BY BUS:  
📍North Bus Terminal (now located in SM City Cebu, beside MyBus terminal) to Hagnaya Port
Travel time: approx. 4 hrs
Fare: ₱280  
📍Hagnaya Port to Sta. Fe Port
Travel time: 1 hr
Fare: ₱185  
B. BY PLANE (via AirJuan)  
📍 Mactan Airport to Bantayan Airport  
Travel time: 20 minutes
Fare: ₱1,288 one way; P2,576 roundtrip
Availability: Mondays and Thursdays only
http://www.airjuan.com

###### Room Rates :   
- Cheapest Room is P2,300 good for 2 persons with FREE breakfast.
