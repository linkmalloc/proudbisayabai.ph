Travelers seeking a private beachfront getaway in southern Cebu can find a relaxing retreat at White Beach Oceanfront House, located along the pristine shoreline of White Beach in Barangay Saavedra, Moalboal.

  

Surrounded by crystal-clear waters, powdery white sand, and scenic sunset views, the vacation home offers guests direct access to one of Cebu’s most popular beach destinations. The property is ideal for families, groups of friends, and weekend travelers looking to unwind while staying close to Moalboal’s renowned marine attractions, including the Sardine Run, sea turtle encounters, snorkeling sites, and island-hopping activities.

  

The beachfront house features spacious accommodations suitable for group vacations and private gatherings, allowing guests to enjoy both comfort and convenience in a tranquil seaside setting.

  

Rates start at ₱15,000 per night on weekdays and ₱16,000 on weekends, accommodating up to 20 guests. Reservations may be made directly through the property’s official Facebook page or contact number.

  

How to Get There

  

White Beach Oceanfront House is located in Barangay Saavedra, Moalboal, approximately 95 kilometers southwest of Cebu City, or about a 2½- to 3-hour drive.

  

Visitors may travel by bus from the Cebu South Bus Terminal bound for Moalboal, or by private vehicle via the Natalio Bacalso National Highway. From the Moalboal town proper, White Beach is just a short tricycle ride away. (visitmoalboal.com)
