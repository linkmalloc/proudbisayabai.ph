##### Antonio's Cabin Retreat and Campsite  

LOOK: Newly erected Retreat and Campsite away from the hustle and bustle of Metro Cebu! Perfect for Barkada and Family Outings this Summer!  

Make the best of your time away from the busy streets of Cebu. Relax and connect with nature while staying in a modest Cabin on top of a mountain with a magnificent valley view in Sogod, Cebu, Philippines.  

🌎 Exact location: Sitio Cabadiangan 6007 Sogod, Cebu, Philippines  

###### How to get there?  
 
🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu Northbus Terminal ride a bus going to Sogod (via Catmon) estimated bus fare is more or less, P225. Then tell the driver to drop you off Sogod Proper. From there, ask some local vendors or habal-habal drivers to guide you going to the exact location.  

🗺 Via Waze / Google Map: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze / Google Map.

###### Promo Rates:
- 3000 php/ night good for a family of 2 adults and 2 kids with age 12 years old and below. 
- 4500php/ night good for 4-6 adults
- 2500 php/ night for couples
