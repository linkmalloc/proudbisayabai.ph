Bayfront CoLive+ celebrates its first anniversary this October, marking one year since it opened its doors on October 3, 2025, and offering Cebu residents and visitors a new approach to modern, flexible living.

With 60 rooms designed for convenience and practicality, Bayfront CoLive+ brings together accommodation, co-living spaces and lifestyle amenities within a single building. The property caters particularly to young professionals, students, entrepreneurs, remote workers and long-stay guests looking for a comfortable place to live, work and connect.

Beyond its rooms, Bayfront CoLive+ provides a co-living working space, gaming area and laundry facilities, allowing residents to take care of everyday needs without having to leave the building. These shared facilities are complemented by other amenities intended to create a more connected and convenient living experience.

“Bayfront CoLive+ was developed in response to the changing way people live and work,” said **Charlton C. Cokaliong, Founder and President of Bayfront Hotel Cebu**. “Today’s guests are looking for more than just a room. They want convenience, flexibility and spaces where they can work, relax and connect.”

The first year has provided Bayfront CoLive+ with an opportunity to better understand the needs of this emerging market and refine its offering around the lifestyle of its residents.

The property is part of the growing Bayfront family of hospitality and accommodation brands, which continues to explore new concepts that respond to changing customer preferences.

As it enters its second year, Bayfront CoLive+ remains focused on providing an affordable, practical and community-oriented alternative for those seeking a place to stay, work and live in Cebu.

One year after opening, Bayfront CoLive+ celebrates not only a milestone, but the beginning of a new chapter in flexible urban living.
