Cebu provincial officials are seeking to bring the work of the Provincial Board closer to residents through the province's first Legislative Awareness Month, which opened at the Capitol on Monday.

Vice Governor Glenn Soco urged Cebuanos to pay closer attention to how provincial lawmakers deliberate, listen to public concerns and approve local policies, saying the legislature must remain accountable to the people it serves.

“The people of Cebu are watching and i hope they are. They should see us how we work, how we listen, how we debate, how we've asked hard questions and how we answered them,” Soco said during the opening ceremony.

The monthlong observance aims to help residents better understand the Provincial Board's responsibilities and address the gap between the provincial legislature and the communities affected by its decisions.

Soco said the board's work should be measured not only by the ordinances it passes but also by how these measures improve people's lives.

“We hope that our Cebuano people would understand that our job is not just about merely passing ordinances; it is about making people’s lives better,” he said.

Representing Gov. Pamela Baricuatro, Soco formally opened the celebration during the Capitol's Monday flag-raising ceremony, attended by provincial board members, department heads and government employees.

He said the observance should not be judged by its opening ceremony or scheduled activities alone, but by the quality of work lawmakers deliver during regular sessions.

Soco also emphasized that public concerns should guide the crafting of provincial measures, urging lawmakers to remain attentive to the needs of their constituents.

Aside from explaining the board's legislative responsibilities, the monthlong program will also give residents access to government services.

A free legal aid program is scheduled from Nov. 17 to 27, excluding weekends, at the third-floor Multipurpose Hall of the Cebu Legislative Building.

The service will be available from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. for Cebuanos seeking legal guidance and assistance.
