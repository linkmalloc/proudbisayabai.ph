As Halad sa Pagtuo 2026 brings communities across Cebu together in a celebration of faith, culture, and tradition, organizers have released the official order of procession for the participating carozzas.

  

The procession will feature religious images representing parishes and communities from different parts of Cebu, showcasing the province’s rich and diverse traditions of devotion.

  

The lineup begins with Santa Fe – Señor Santo Niño, followed by Borbon – San Sebastian Martyr, Catmon and Dalaguete – San Guillermo de Aquitania, and Ginatilan – San Gregorio Magno.

  

The procession continues with entries from San Francisco, Medellin, Santander, Pinamungajan, San Fernando, Moalboal, San Remigio, Tabogon, Bogo City, Liloan, Tuburan, Toledo City, Tabuelan, Bantayan, Compostela, Badian, Sogod, Barili, Sibonga, and Asturias.

  

The second part of the lineup features carozzas from Minglanilla, Carmen, Alcoy, Malabuyoc, Ronda, Danao City, Argao, Samboan, Balamban, Dumanjug, City of Naga, Talisay City, Aloguinsan, Consolacion, Poro, Carcar City, Alegria, Pilar, Madridjejos, Oslob, Tudela, Mandaue City, Boljoon, Lapu-Lapu City, and Cebu City.

  

Each participating carozza will carry a patron saint or Marian image associated with its community, making the procession a showcase of the different expressions of Catholic faith and devotion found throughout Cebu.

  

From Señor Santo Niño and Birhen sa Regla to Nuestra Señora de Guadalupe de Cebu, the lineup reflects the many devotions that remain deeply rooted in Cebuano communities.

  

For those planning to witness Halad sa Pagtuo 2026, the official order of procession provides a guide to the sequence of the participating carozzas and the communities they represent.
