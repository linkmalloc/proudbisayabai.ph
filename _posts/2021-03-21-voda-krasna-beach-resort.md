#### Voda Krasna Beach Resort
  
BISDAK LAAGAN CHECK! Naka ari naka? Need to unwind with the Barkadas after this quarantine? Voda Krasna Resort is a must - experience.⛱🏝💦  
  
🌍 Exact Location: Voda Krasna, Daanglungsod, Alcoy, Cebu 2 to 3hrs ang byahe mga Bai gikan Cebu City   
  
📌 How to get there?  
  
🗺 Via commute: From the South Bus Terminal ride any bus padung to Alcoy then tell the driver to drop you off sa  Voda Krasna na Resort (Voda Krasna Daanglungsod, Alcoy). Bus fare is Pl150.00 pesos only (one-way rana sya).  
  
🗺 Via private vehicle: You can search the location sa google map or navigate wazemakita ra.   
  
📌 Day rates  
Entrance fee: None  
- Cottage: P1,700 pesos per day then P2,500 per night only.   
- Tables and chairs: P300 pesos per day, P600 pesos per day.  
- Tent Rents: P600 pesos per day.  
- Own Tent: P300 pesos per day.  

****
📌 Room rates  
- Villa 1 (2 twin bed): P6,000 pesos per night then good for 4pax, max of 4 extra pax  
- Villa 2 (1 king sized bed): P5,000 pesos per night then good for 2pax, max of 2 extra pax
