Cebu City, Philippines — Vietnamese dairy giant Vinamilk officially introduced Saigon Star Philippines to the local market through its debut at the Hotel & Foodservice Suppliers Show Visayas 2026, held from November 26 to 28 at the SMX Convention Center, SM Seaside City Cebu.

  

Leading the brand’s Philippine rollout is Don Macchiatos Group of Companies (DMGC), which has partnered with Vinamilk to bring Saigon Star, a versatile condensed creamer, to Filipino consumers and the food and beverage industry. The product will be exclusively distributed nationwide by Don Macchiatos Franchising Corp.

  

Known for its rich and creamy consistency, Saigon Star is designed for a wide range of culinary applications, from preparing authentic Vietnamese coffee to baking and everyday kitchen creations. Its versatility makes it a valuable ingredient for both household consumers and food service businesses.

  

The partnership marks another milestone in DMGC’s continuing business expansion, strengthening its portfolio through collaborations with internationally recognized brands.

  

Beyond nationwide distribution, DMGC has also begun integrating Saigon Star into its own food and beverage concepts. The condensed creamer is now part of the newly introduced Vietnamese-inspired coffee collection of Don Macchiatos, bringing its beverages closer to the traditional flavors and preparation of Vietnamese coffee.

  

Meanwhile, Padayon Croissants & Coffee has also incorporated Saigon Star into its coffee selections and croissant recipes, demonstrating the product’s versatility across both beverages and food offerings.

  

During the three-day trade show, visitors, industry professionals, and prospective business partners had the opportunity to discover Saigon Star through product presentations, sampling, and demonstrations showcasing its potential applications in the food and beverage sector.

  

With Vinamilk’s entry into the Philippine market, the partnership with Don Macchiatos Group signals a promising new chapter for both companies, combining international product expertise with DMGC’s growing distribution network and presence in the local food and beverage industry.

  

As Saigon Star Philippines begins its journey, both companies look forward to bringing the richness and versatility of Vietnamese-inspired products to more Filipino consumers and business partners nationwide.
