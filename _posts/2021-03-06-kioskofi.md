#### KIOSKOFI
<center><i>A Taste of Vintage in Dalaguete, Southern Cebu</i></center>  

****  
📍Kioskofi - Dalaguete’s Finest Cafe  
  
This one of a kind vintage cafe brings nostalgia and has survived throughout time and kept its beauty. This unique experience awaits to those who pay a visit and enjoy delicious coffee, a selection of sweet and savoury treats ☕️ 😍  
  
It’s sophisticated charm is a perfect spot for a coffee date or stop over with the enchanting view of the sea! Unsa pay gihuwat ninyo bai? Laag na 🤩  

****
##### 🕐 Operating hours: 
9:00 AM - 9:00 PM (DAILY)  
  
##### 🌎 Exact Location:  
Poblacion Dalaguete Cebu (few steps away from San Guillermo Church)  

****  
#### HOW TO GET THERE?   
  
##### 🗺  Via Commute:  
From South Bus Terminal, take a bus going to Dalaguete. Drop off at Dalaguete Poblacion and ride a trisikad going to the Town’s Seawall. The cafe is located just in front of San Guillermo Church.  
  
##### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
