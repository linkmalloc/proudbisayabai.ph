Looking for a dream beach holiday this weekend? South Palms Resort Panglao is the perfect place to escape to.  

Step into this secluded paradise and experience unparalleled pleasure and relaxation. South Palms Resort Panglao offers a beach holiday worth telling, with its stunning white sand beach, crystal-clear waters, and lush tropical gardens.  

The resort is open from 8AM to 10PM daily, and offers a variety of amenities, including:  
  - Comfortable and spacious rooms and villas
  - A rooftop infinity pool
  - A fitness center
  - A restaurant and bar
  - Spa treatments
  - Beach activities  

****
Happy hour is from 12NN to 7PM daily, and while you're dining at the resort, you can also use the beach for free. That's right, no entrance fee!
So what are you waiting for? Book your stay at South Palms Resort Panglao today and experience the best that Bohol has to offer.
