Looking for a perfect place to chill, hang out and have a great lazy time with your buddies? then,  MAAYONG HOSTEL is perfect for you! 😍😱  
 
**** 
Imagine waking up from a good night’s rest, opening your front door and have a coffee on your beautifully peaceful private terrace ☕️🌴  
  
##### 🕑 Operating hours:  
8:00AM - 10:30PM   
  
##### 🌎 Exact Location:  
Panagsama road, 6032 Moalboal, Philippines  

****
#### How to get there?  
  
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
If you go on a bus from the city, tell the driver to drop you off at 7/11 Moalboal. That is the corner also going to Panagsama beach. There will be alot of tricycle and habal2 drivera that would convince you to go with them.  
  
##### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.  

##### 📌AMENITIES:  
- They have a bar and restaurant, a huge open lounge for everybody to share. A Petanqué field and for Mölky if you want to play. Beerpong table, card and board games. Free Wifi around as well.  

****
##### 📌 RATES:   
- Shared dormitory [12beds, aircon with 2 showers(hot and cold) and 2 shared toilets]
	- Php 500.00/person/bed  
- Bungalow (queen size good for 2 pax bed Fan room with its own toilet and bath)
	- Php 1200/ night.
