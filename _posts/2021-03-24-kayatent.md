#### KayaTent
  
Kanindot sad ingun ani Bai! Escape the City life and Camp by the beach. Experience the crystal clear waters of Samboan, Cebu! 💦⛺️🏝  
  
🌎 Exact Location: Tangbo, Kinabonbonan Samboan  
  
📌 How to het there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute / Private Vehicle: KayaTent is situated at Tangbo, Kinabonbonan Samboan. Very accessible and can easily be spotted. Guest with private vehicles can use waze to navigate. Guest can also use the public transportation KayaTent is just 5-10 minutes away from Aguinid Falls  
  
📌 Rates:   
PHP 1,200.00 nett per night good for two persons.   
  
📌 Inclusions:   
- mattress and pillows  
- Utensils will also be provided upon request  
- The area is equipped with a shower and changing room  
- Electricity is also available in the area..
