**TAN-AWA**: Mag relax and unwind with family and friends at Allure Badian Beach in Badian, Cebu 💦⛱🧘 Allure Badian Beach Villa is a private beachfront property for #EXCLUSIVE overnight rental. It's popular with big groups — perfect for #TeamBuilding and #FamilyReunions.  

🌍 Exact Location: Matutinao Badian, Cebu \| Close to #KawasanFalls and #Canyoneering  

📌 **How to get there?**  
The villa is conveniently located along the main road, easily accessible by private vehicle or public transport.

🗺 **Via Private Vehicle:**  
From Cebu City, take the Cebu South Road (Natalio B. Bacalso S National Highway) heading south, passing through Talisay, Minglanilla, Naga and San Fernando. Upon reaching the roundabout in Carcar, turn right into the Carcar-Barili road and keep driving on that main road. From the Carcar roundabout, Badian is approximately 66km. As soon as you reach the main town proper of Badian, you will begin to see multiple road signages along the main highway. These road-side directional signs will lead you to the villa.  

🗺 **Via Commute:**  
Take any south-bound bus that will drive by Badian. Inform the driver you will need to get off at Barangay Matutinao, just before reaching Kawasan Falls.
