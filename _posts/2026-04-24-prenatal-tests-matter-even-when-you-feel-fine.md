Pregnancy can feel smooth and healthy, but some medical conditions may develop without obvious signs or symptoms. That’s why regular prenatal tests are essential for protecting both mother and baby throughout pregnancy.



Prenatal checkups often include an **ultrasound**, which helps monitor your baby’s growth and development. It also allows healthcare providers to check your baby’s position and identify any concerns early.



**Blood and urine tests** are also important because they help detect infections, anemia, and other health conditions that may affect pregnancy if left untreated.



Doctors may also perform **blood pressure and glucose screenings** to help prevent complications such as gestational diabetes, preeclampsia, and other pregnancy-related risks.



Regular **baby monitoring** ensures that your baby is developing properly and helps healthcare providers prepare for a safer delivery.



Every prenatal test plays an important role in supporting a healthy pregnancy. Work closely with your healthcare provider to make sure you receive the right care at the right time—for you and your baby.
