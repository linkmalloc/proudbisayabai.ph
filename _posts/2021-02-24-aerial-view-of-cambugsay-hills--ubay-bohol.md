Enjoy the panoramic view of Malingin Dam at Cambugsay Hills, where the mountains & the water melts, sitting under the tree, breathing the fresh air & temporarily forget the busy life you left behind.  

****
##### 🌍 Exact Location:  
Barangay San Pascual, Municipality of Ubay, Bohol.  

****
##### 📌 How to get there?

****
###### Cebu City to Ubay  by boat:  

###### M/V J&N Carrier  
- 10am Monday-Saturday  
- 12am Sunday 

****
###### M/V J&N Ferry  
- 10pm Sunday-Friday
- no Saturday trip

****
###### Ubay Port Going to Barangay San Pascual:  
Ride Jeepney  to San Pascual Proper Fare (10 pesos) Ride Motor-Hired /Habal-Habal going to  San Pascual  (sabot lang mo sa Driver)

###### San Pascual Proper Going to Cambugsay Hills:  
Ride Habal-Habal (Sabot lang mo sa Driver sa Plete) please leave no trace!
