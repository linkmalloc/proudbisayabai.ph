Government Service Insurance System (GSIS) pensioners will receive a higher Christmas cash gift this year, with the maximum amount increased from P10,000 to P15,000 starting Dec. 1.

  

GSIS President and General Manager Wick Veloso announced the increase in a video message posted on the agency's Facebook page, alongside the early release of December pensions and expanded access to two loan programs.

  

“Bilang pasasalamat sa inyong tapat na paglilingkod sa bayan, itinaas natin ang maximum Christmas Cash Gift mula PHP10,000 tungo sa PHP15,000 (As a gesture of gratitude for your faithful service to the country, we increased the maximum Christmas cash gift from PHP10,000 to PHP15,000),” Veloso said.

  

The cash gift will be credited to qualified pensioners on Dec. 1, providing them with additional funds ahead of the holiday season.

  

GSIS will also release the regular December monthly pensions earlier than scheduled, moving the payout from Dec. 8 to Dec. 4 to help retirees prepare for Christmas expenses.

  

Aside from the cash gift and early pension release, pensioners can now access the GSIS Solar Energy Loan (GSEL) and GSIS E-Mobility Loan (GEML).

  

The two loan programs are intended to help beneficiaries reduce household electricity and transportation expenses.

  

Veloso said the expanded loan access supports President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr.'s directive to ease the financial burden on Filipino families.

  

“Tatlong magandang balita ang hatid ng inyong GSIS ngayong Pasko (Your GSIS brings three pieces of good news this Christmas),” he said.

  

Speaking on behalf of GSIS Chairperson Rick Blancaflor and the Board of Trustees, Veloso expressed hope that the additional benefits would help pensioners enjoy a more comfortable retirement and holiday season.
