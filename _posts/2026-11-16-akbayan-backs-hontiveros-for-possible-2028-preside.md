Akbayan Partylist has declared its readiness to support Sen. Risa Hontiveros in a possible bid for the presidency in 2028, calling on progressive groups and supporters to unite behind her.

On Wednesday, Nov. 11, Akbayan National President Rafaela David said the party was prepared to campaign for Hontiveros and work toward her victory.

“We are Ready for Risa!” David declared.

She cited Hontiveros' record of confronting what she described as difficult political and social battles, saying the senator had stood alongside the public against transnational syndicates, cult leaders, fake mayors, corrupt and violent officials, and even powerful nations.

Addressing questions about Hontiveros' chances of winning, David said victory would require collective effort from the party and its allies rather than simply expecting electoral support to come their way.

She also urged progressive groups to set aside their differences, warning that division could benefit their political opponents.

“Divided, the enemies of progress wins. We need a united and formidable wind to build that progressive wave in 2028,” David said.

David further encouraged the public to support Hontiveros, saying: “Fight for someone who fights for you. Fight with someone who fights with you. Fight for progress, fight for a fair and just future!”

She ended her statement with a campaign call: “Tara na! Risa Na sa 2028! Risa na para sa magandang bukas!”

Meanwhile, former vice president and Naga City Mayor Leni Robredo has ruled out running for president or any other national position in the 2028 elections.

In a Nov. 9 statement, Robredo said her decision was final and that she would not use her mayoralty as a stepping stone to national office. She said she had reached the decision even before running for mayor in 2025 and remained focused on local governance.

Robredo also said she was open to supporting a deserving candidate in the next elections, but would not seek national office herself.
