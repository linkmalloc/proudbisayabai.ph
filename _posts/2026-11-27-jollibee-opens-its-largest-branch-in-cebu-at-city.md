Jollibee officially opened its newest and largest branch in Cebu, Jollibee City di Mare Drive-Thru, on Friday, November 27, following its ribbon-cutting and blessing ceremony held the previous day at City di Mare, South Road Properties (SRP).

  

The opening marks another milestone in the brand’s expansion in Cebu, introducing a next-generation store experience with enhanced dining facilities, Bike & Dine, dual LED party rooms, drive-thru services, and sustainability features.

  

To celebrate its opening, the branch is offering a lineup of exclusive promotions from November 27 to 29, including freebies, special treats, and activities for customers.

  

For its opening day, the first 100 customers who purchase any Value Meal receive a FREE Cheesy Yumburger. Meanwhile, the first dine-in or take-out customer with any food purchase gets a Jollibee Plush Doll and a ₱2,000 gift certificate.

  

Customers can also receive one claw machine token for every ₱500 food purchase for a chance to win a Jollibee Plush Doll. A free photobooth session is available with a minimum single-receipt purchase of ₱300.

  

For drive-thru customers, the first 200 customers with a minimum ₱500 food purchase receive a FREE Coke Phone Charm. Children can also enjoy free Jollibee balloons.

  

Strategically located along the Cebu South Coastal Road, the new branch is accessible to motorists traveling to and from southern Cebu.

  

Jollibee currently operates 1,378 stores nationwide, including 32 in Cebu City, further strengthening its presence in the local market.
