🌎 Exact Location: Zaragosa, Badian, Cebu  

🗺 You may reach the port by navigating thru waze or google maps - Badian Island Wellness Resort Port. This will take you to mainland parking and private port.

##### RATES  

###### Day Trip Promotion
- Adult - P1,500.00
- Kids - P750.00

****

###### Terms and Conditions:
- Starts at 9am-5pm daily 
- Bringing of foods and beverages inside the Resort's premises is strictly prohibited
- 12 years old and below are considered kids. The hotel reserves the right requests proof
- Prior reservations is required

****

###### INCLUSIONS: 
-  Round trip Boat Transfer (scheduled). 
- Complementary High Speed Wifi Connection ( up to 60 Mbps). 
- Consumable of Php1,000 for F&B consumption and Water Sports for adults.
- Consumable of Php500 for F&B consumption and Water Sports for kids (12 yrs. old and below).
- Access to floating Gamehaus (Billiards, Dart, Sungka, Soccer Table and Chess).
- 1x use of Thalasso Waterfalls (30 minutes).
- 1x use Paddle Boat/Board (30 minutes).
- Beach Towel.
