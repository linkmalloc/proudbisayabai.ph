Jamme’s Campsite in Busay, Cebu City offers a serene escape surrounded by misty hills and lush greenery. This exclusive hideaway features a cozy, homey atmosphere that lets you unwind and reconnect with nature—perfect for anyone craving peace and quiet away from the city’s rush.

They are located in Sitio Lupa (Lower Cantipla) Brgy. Sudlon 2, Cebu City. They have one cottage with 1 room and will be exclusively used for your group. They will not accept other guests on that day once your booking is confirmed. They have Superb customer service and very accomodating staff Jamme Mabini Leuman. The place is very accessible, child-friendly and what's more, is you can bring your furbabies.

For commuting campers:

From Ayala Terminal or SM Mabolo Terminal, ride a v-hire going to Strawberry Farm in Brgy. Cantipla.

From Strawberry Farm, take a habal-habal (about 5 minutes).

Sitio Lupa’s Basketball Court serves as the jump-off point.



Check-in time: 2:00 PM

Check-out time: 12:00 NN

##### HOW TO GET THERE?

🌏 Location: Trancentral Highway, mu left turn ka diha sa may strawberry farm, Sitio Lupa, brgy sudlon 2, Cebu City, Just a 40-minute drive from Cebu City.

🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
