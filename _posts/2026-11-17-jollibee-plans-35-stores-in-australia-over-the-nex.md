Philippine fast-food giant Jollibee is set to enter the Australian market, with plans to open 35 stores over the next five years following the signing of two exclusive franchise development agreements.

The Jollibee Group announced the agreements on November 15, 2026, in Manila, with Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese joining the announcement during his visit to a Jollibee restaurant.

Under the agreements, Australian companies Happy Food Group Pty Ltd. and Lucky Bee Australia Pty Ltd. will lead the brand’s expansion in Victoria and parts of New South Wales, respectively.

The planned rollout represents an estimated investment of A$60 million and is expected to generate approximately 900 direct jobs for Australians, particularly in restaurant operations, logistics, and product supply.

Albanese welcomed the investment, describing it as an example of the growing economic relationship between Australia and the Philippines. He also highlighted the potential employment opportunities and wider benefits for local supply chains.

### **Filipino community among potential customers**

Jollibee’s entry into Australia is expected to attract interest from the country’s large Filipino community, which has long been familiar with the brand’s signature offerings.

According to the Australian government, more than 400,000 people living in Australia were born in the Philippines, making Filipinos one of the country’s largest overseas-born communities.

The expansion is also expected to introduce Filipino-style fast food to more Australian consumers, bringing popular menu items such as Chickenjoy, Jolly Spaghetti, and Peach Mango Pie to a new market.

For many Filipino Australians, the arrival of Jollibee could provide greater access to familiar Filipino food and a taste of home.

### **Part of Jollibee’s global expansion**

Jollibee Group Global President and Chief Executive Officer Ernesto Tanmantiong described the Australian expansion as another milestone in the company’s international growth.

He said the company looks forward to bringing its food and dining experiences to Australian communities while building a long-term presence with its local franchise partners.

The group is also seeking additional franchise partners in other parts of Australia beyond the territories covered by the newly signed agreements.

Jollibee has continued to expand its international footprint, with its brand operating in multiple markets across Asia, North America, Europe, and the Middle East.

The Australian expansion marks another step in the Philippine company’s efforts to introduce its brands to more international consumers.

While the five-year development target has been announced, specific opening dates and the complete locations of the planned stores have yet to be disclosed.

Sources: Office of the Prime Minister of Australia; SBS News.
