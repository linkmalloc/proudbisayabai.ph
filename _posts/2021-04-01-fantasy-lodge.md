#### Fantasy Lodge
   
TAN-AWA: Diba mao ni imong gipangita? Tara na sa Fantasy Lodge, Cebu Philippines!  
  
Looking for a place to relax and chill for a family or friend’s getaway from the city’s bustling and dazzling noise? Too tired and weakened for a day’s work? Need to refurbish your energy? 😯💦🥺  
  
🕒 Check in time: 2PM Check out time: 11PM  
   
🌎 Exact Location: Brgy. Dalahikan, Samboan, South of Cebu  
  
###### 📌 INCLUSIONS:   
Rates per night w/ FREE breakfast:  
- Standard AC Room for 2 pax P2,850   
- Superior Couple Room w/ AC &amp; private terrace for 2 pax P3,900  
- Family Room w/ AC for 4 pax P3,250  
- Superior Family Room w/ AC &amp; private terrace for 4 pax P4,600  
- The Dome for 2 pax P4,200.00  
- Talisay Bungalow w/ AC, Bathtub &amp; Sauna for 2 pax P5,00.00  
- Casa Amihan for 6 pax P8,500  

****
###### ROOMS:
Non TV rooms, Fan w/ Air condition, w/ WiFi . 
(NOTE: we dont have tv in our rooms because we want to promote bonding between family and friends by doing outdoor activities)  
  
###### DINING:  
Fusion of French and Filipino Cuisines. Special request can be accomodated too like Vegetarian food.   
  
###### SPA:  
Massage is not allowed for the moment but we have ''KAWA HOT BATH" where you can cook/relax yourself with virgin coco oil and pure essential oils.   
  
###### POOL:  
Our swimming pool is 5-9 ft. Proper swimming attire is a must.   
- They also offer Dinner,  Birthday &amp; Anniversary set up.    

****
> NOTE: All rates are net prices. Non-refundable, with VAT inclusion. No Service Charge.
