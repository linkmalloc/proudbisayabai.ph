If you're on the lookout for a new go-to hangout spot in Cebu, look no further! Tambayan ni Ensoy has just opened in Talamban, and it promises to be the perfect place to satisfy your cravings for delicious, budget-friendly Filipino street food. Whether you're planning a casual meet-up with friends, a special date with your partner, or a fun family gathering, this new tambayan has something for everyone.       
    
###### A Taste of Local Delights      
    
Tambayan ni Ensoy specializes in pungko-pungko and silog dishes that are sure to hit the spot. Known for their savory flavors and hearty servings, these meals are a beloved staple of Cebuano street food culture. Each dish is prepared with care, ensuring that every bite is packed with flavor and satisfaction. Whether you’re indulging in their crispy *Ginabotan* (deep-fried pork intestines) or a classic *Silog* (a portmanteau of sinangag, or garlic fried rice, and itlog, or egg, typically paired with a variety of meats), you’re in for a treat.

But it doesn’t stop there—Tambayan ni Ensoy also offers an a la carte menu, beverages, and a selection of beers and liquors. Pair a cold Red Horse beer with your meal to elevate your dining experience, whether you're celebrating a monthsary, anniversary, or simply winding down after a long day.

As a special treat for their early patrons, Tambayan ni Ensoy is running an exciting raffle promotion. The first 50 customers who visit between August 19 and September 15 will receive a raffle entry, giving them a chance to win one of three cellphones worth PHP 10,000 each! The lucky winners will be announced on September 20, so don't miss out on this fantastic opportunity to enjoy great food and potentially walk away with a brand-new phone.       
    
###### Where to Find Us:       
    
Tambayan ni Ensoy is conveniently located near Metropolis Talamban Cebu, just beside Fusion Gas Station. Whether you're commuting or driving, getting there is a breeze:       

****    
- Via Commute: Hop on a 62B jeepney heading to Talamban Cebu. The destination is easy to spot and well-known in the area.       
- Via Private Vehicle: The place is easily navigable through Waze. Simply input “Fusion Gas Station” or “Metropolis Talamban” as your destination, and you’ll find Tambayan ni Ensoy nearby.
