****
Catmon, a town in Cebu, Philippines, is well-known for its stunning waterfalls and picturesque natural settings. The Areca Palm Hut, a beautiful eco-resort tucked away in the middle of luxuriant foliage, is the most recent attraction to draw both tourists and residents alike.  

The resort is known for its cozy, environmentally friendly bamboo cottages that are modeled after conventional Filipino architecture. The huts are made to fit in with the landscape flawlessly, making it the ideal refuge for anyone looking for a quiet and relaxing break.

###### How to get there?  

Via Google Map/ Waze: You can locate them via Waze and Google Map Apps:   
[https://g.co/kgs/xwyANr](https://g.co/kgs/xwyANr)

Nearest landmark from the highway is Recuerdo Beach Resort. Opposite side there is a road going uphill. It is situated 3km away from the national highway.

What to expect:
- ZERO network which is really good for us, for anybody who wants to disconnect and relax. (They offer wifi for a minimal fee) 
- You can bring your own food, no corkage fees. You can cook or you can directly order from their menu. They also provide basic condiments in the kitchen. 
- It's 3km+ away from the main road (the road itself isn't too convenient, it's narrow, lowered vehicles not advised)
