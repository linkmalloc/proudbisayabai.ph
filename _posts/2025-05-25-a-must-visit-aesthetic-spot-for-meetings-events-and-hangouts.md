Skydeck 360 offers stunning views, great food, and the perfect vibe for every occasion! &#x20;

Cebu Eats: Looking for the ideal venue to meet, unwind, or celebrate with friends? Skydeck 360 is the place to be, offering great food, stunning views, and the perfect ambiance for any occasion! &#x20;

&#x20;Exact Location: Roof Deck of Grand Residences Tower A, Roxas Street, Cebu City / beside 88th Avenue  &#x20;

**Operating Hours:**

&#x20;• Sunday to Thursday: 6am - 11pm

&#x20;• Friday to Saturday: 6am - 1am (with live acoustic music).&#x20;

Unli Breakfast Buffet: ₱549 from 6am - 10am

Whether it’s for business meetings, casual hangouts, or special events, Skydeck 360 is your go-to destination! &#x20;
