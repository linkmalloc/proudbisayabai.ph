Need a break from the city hustle? Escape to the highlands and enjoy the fresh breeze, peaceful trails, and scenic mountain views of Mt. Mago, located in Barangay Santican, Danao City. Whether you’re a seasoned hiker or a first-timer just wanting to breathe fresh air, Mt. Mago offers the perfect quick getaway to reconnect with nature. 

*How to Get There*

From Cebu City:

1. Take a bus or V-hire from SM City Cebu or North Bus Terminal bound for Danao City.
2. Drop off at the Danao Public Market or terminal.
3. Hire a habal-habal (motorbike) to Barangay Santican — just let the driver know you’re heading to Mt. Mago (a known trekking spot).
4. Travel time from Danao Market to the jump-off point is around 45 minutes to 1 hour, depending on road conditions.

*Trek Details*

* Estimated Trek Time: 45 minutes to 1 hour
* Difficulty: Beginner-friendly
* Trail Type: Gentle ascent with scenic landscapes, perfect for sunrise or sunset views
* Highlights: Rolling hills, sea of clouds (if you’re lucky!), and peaceful nature vibes

*Helpful Tips*

* Bring enough water and light snacks
* Wear comfortable hiking shoes or sandals with good grip
* Best time to visit? Early morning or late afternoon to avoid the heat

Most importantly: Leave no trace. Bring your trash with you and help preserve the beauty of Mt. Mago.

***Mt. Mago is proof that you don’t need to travel far for a meaningful escape. It’s a place to breathe, reset, and simply enjoy being outdoors. So call up your barkada, prep your trail gear, and pabukid ta!***

Location: Mt. Mago, Barangay Santican, Danao City, Cebu
