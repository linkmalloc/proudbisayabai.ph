The Mimoy's Grill Cebu is an alfresco dining with a homey atmosphere. The area has some excellent foliage that adds to its tranquil atmosphere. Al fresco tables are available if you wish to eat, relax, or drink outside. It is classy and fashionable and is always a treat. Be overwhelmed with the options on the menu!   

After a lengthy, breathtaking descent, it is always a delight to arrive in this incredibly Instagrammable area. The terrace is usually warm, and later in the season, the sun will surely kiss your skin.   

It might be daylight or at nightfall, dining here is refreshing because of the mellow vibe.  

Tops Road, Brgy. Busay, Cebu City  

They are open Weekdays: 3 PM - 12 AM  and Weekends / Holidays: 12 NN - 12 AM  

###### How to get there? 

Via Commute: Ride any jeepney or bus going to JY Square. Ride a habal-habal then tell the driver to drop you off at Tops or Mimoy’s Grill. The estimated fare is P150 

The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze: [https://goo.gl/maps/ibrkPXjFc42jbFqk9](https://goo.gl/maps/ibrkPXjFc42jbFqk9)
