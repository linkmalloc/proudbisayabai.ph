##### Matahom Paradise Villas

\#VisitMedellin: Experience one of the most beautiful sunsets in Cebu; taste the freshest seafood and other delectable specialties; choose from garden-view and ocean-view villas, or the glass house; or just walk on a white-sandy beach front and get sun-kissed skin. It's definitely paradise. It's certainly Matahom! The newest exclusive Resort Villa in Medellin, Cebu.

🌍 Exact Location: Kawit, Medellin, Cebu

Restaurant is open from 7AM - 9PM daily  
🕓 Check in: 2:00PM  
🕕 Check out: 12:00NN

###### How to get there? 

🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu North Bus Terminal in which located now at SM City Cebu, ride a bus bound for Kawit, Medellin. Then tell the driver to drop you off at Villa Edar Beach Resort. Matahom Paradise Villas is before Villa Edar and a signage or tarp can be seen along the road.

🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Search "Villa Edar", they are located before that resort, a tarpaulin can be seen along the road. Whether you are riding a motorcycle (habal-habal) or your own vehicle, it's best to navigate via Waze.

###### Room Rates:
🌊 Ocean View Villa: 
P12,500 (Fri-Sun) | P10,500 (Mon-Thur)
- good for 5 persons (front villa + 2 rooms)
- free breakfast 
- free of resort amenities
- free use of 1 kayak for 30 minutes

****
🍃 Garden View Villa: 
P12,000 (Fri-Sun) | P10,000 (Mon-Thur)
- good for 2-3 persons (front villa only)
- free breakfast and use of resort amenities

****
🏠 Regular Room: P1,800
- good for 2 persons
- with breakfast
- is only available for rent if you will book either their ‘Ocean View Villa’ or ‘Garden View Villa' Package
