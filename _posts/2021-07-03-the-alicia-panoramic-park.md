##### THE ALICIA PANORAMIC PARK  
  
Add on your travel list 📍Binabaje Hills located in Barangay Cambaol, Alicia, Bohol 😯🌄🍃  
  
Features the Breathtaking Landscape of Binabaje Hills of Barangay Cambaol in Alicia Municipality. The Hills, which rise 400 meters above sea level, are mostly covered in tropical perennial grasses commonly known as “COGON".

##### HOW TO GET THERE  

🌏 Exat Location: Barangay Cambaol Alicia BOHOL - Philippines. The hike itself is about 30-minutes from Alicia town hall along a very steep, muddy and crazy trail.
