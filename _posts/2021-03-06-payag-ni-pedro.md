#### PAYAG NI PEDRO
  
Perfect for those who dream on spending a night under the stars with a panoramic view of the city and experience the feeling of just being alone with nature.⛺️🍃  
  
📍Purok Magtahos Brgy. Minoyan Murcia, Negros Occidental   
 
 ****
##### 📌 HOW TO GET THERE?   
  
Easy trail: 15-20mins.  

****
##### 🗺 Via Commute:  
From Bacolod sakay sa double tire na Mambukal P60, manaog sa Simaguan or bangga Graceland. Sakay ug habal-habal P100-P150 padung sa Purok Magtahos Brgy. Minoyan.   
  
****
##### 🗺 Via Private Vehicle:  
Long walk: From Mambukal resort to Payag ni Pedro 1&amp;30mins-2hrs.   

****
##### 📌 Fees:   
Overnight: P500, (5pax or less) Additional P100 per head. Tent area P150 per tent  
  
****
##### Day Hike:  
P500, (5pax or less) Additional P50 per head.   
 
****
**NO WALK-IN/ EXCLUSIVE for the guests!**
