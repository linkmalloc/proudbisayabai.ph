#### 100 PESOS CHALLENGE  
  
BISDAK LAAGAN CHECK! Thinking of Dagat with Family and friends? Hermits-Cove in Aloguinsan, Cebu is a MUST- EXPERIENCE! 💦🏖 🌊  
  
🌎 Exact Location: Brgy. Kantabogon, Aloguinsan, Cebu  
  
Located in the beautiful southern part of Cebu, Aloguinsan, Hermit’s Cove is definitely one of the top tourist spots Cebu has to offer.   
  
📌 How to get there?  
  
🗺 Via Commute: Ride a bus/vhire going to South Cebu approx. 2-3hrs travel time from Cebu City. estimated fare is P100.  
  
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.   
  
📌 Entrance fee: P100 per person  
  
With short orientation about the history, rules and regulations, Do's and Dont's before getting inside the beach .   
  
👉🏻 Click the Full details here:[https://www.facebook.com/pages/Hermits-Cove-Aloguinsan/326163464260146](https://www.facebook.com/pages/Hermits-Cove-Aloguinsan/326163464260146)  
  
📞 Contact Number: 0921 262 2824
