#### Buho Rock Resort Camotes  
  
<center>Summer in Buho Rock Resort Camotes ☀️🏝💦</center> 

****
Buho Rock Resort is located in Poro, Camotes Islands, Cebu Philippines. It is one of the well-visited place and an important landmark in Camotes Island due to it's unique ship shape. The main attraction here is the cliff jump wherein you can choose between 10ft or 40ft. If you're into rough adventure or cliff jumping. Buho Rock Resort is the place to go. A great sea view, cool rock formations and you get to enjoy the beautiful sunset as well. It also has a lot of beautiful areas to take picture for your social media.   
  
##### Entrance Fee: 
P20 pesos per person   


##### Amenities:  
- Guest House  
- Swimming Pool  
- Cliff Diving  
- Beach Area  
- Rest Rooms  
- Training Center

##### HOW TO GET THERE
  
🗺 Via Commute: If you are commuting, from Cebu City you can ride a bus or jeepney going Danao Port and ride a boat to Consuelo Port (fare is Php220/head).  
  
 🗺 Via Private Vehicle: If using a private utility and want to bring it in the Island, you can ride a roro in the same port, fare is around Php680 for motorcycle including the rider. Travel time is around 2 hours.  
  
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
