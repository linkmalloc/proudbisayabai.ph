SM Seaside City Cebu has joined SM Mall of Asia and SM Megamall in WorldWorthy's 100 Best Shopping Malls 2026, giving SM Supermalls three entries in the global ranking and making it the only mall operator in the Philippines with more than one listed destination.

  

SM Mall of Asia in Pasay City ranked 29th worldwide and first among Philippine malls, followed by SM Megamall in Mandaluyong City at 41st and SM Seaside City Cebu at 94th.

  

The three malls account for three of the Philippines' five entries in the list, which recognizes 100 shopping destinations across 43 countries and six continents.

  

SM Mall of Asia received a WorldWorthy score of 8.98, making it the fifth-highest-ranked mall in Asia. It placed ahead of the United Kingdom's Trafford Centre at 31st, Pavilion Kuala Lumpur at 40th and Seoul's Lotte World Mall at 72nd.

  

SM Megamall followed with a score of 8.97, ranking as the country's second-highest mall and Asia's eighth-highest.

  

SM Seaside City Cebu earned a score of 8.82 and was the only shopping destination outside Metro Manila to represent the Philippines in the ranking, placing Cebu and the Visayas among the world's recognized retail destinations.

  

"Seeing three SM Supermalls among the world's best is a proud moment not just for SM, but for every Filipino shopper and tenant who has made these malls part of their lives," said SM Supermalls Executive Vice President for Marketing Joaquin L. San Agustin.

  

"It shows that Philippine malls can stand alongside the most celebrated shopping destinations in the world."

  

San Agustin said the recognition also reflected the contribution of customers and employees across the three malls.

  

"From SM Mall of Asia to SM Megamall in the heart of Ortigas and SM Seaside City Cebu in the Visayas, this recognition belongs to our customers and our people.

  

We will keep evolving every SM Supermall nationwide to give Filipinos world-class experiences close to home," he added.

  

WorldWorthy compiles consensus-based rankings across various categories, including airports and football stadiums. 

  

For its 2026 shopping mall list, its research team evaluated malls, shopping centers, historic arcades and outlet villages, grouped them by recognition tier and conducted manual verification.

  

The latest edition uses data as of Nov. 4, 2026.

  

The Philippines secured five places in the ranking, tying for the third-highest number of entries among countries. 

  

SM Supermalls accounted for three of those entries.

  

The list is led by The Avenues in Kuwait and also includes Milan's Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II at fifth and Paris' Le Bon Marché at 71st.
