#### Tongo Hill Cottages

****
A peaceful stay away from the buzz of the city at Tongo Hill Cottages in  Moalboal, Cebu! 📖☕️🌴🍃  
  
Tongo Hill Cottages is a characteristic eco stay, nestled in between palm trees and lush nature. It has 4 kubo huts, each with private outdoor shower and balcony. Tongo Hill also has a communal social cottage to be shared by guests. What are your plans sa sunod laag ? Add Tongo Hill Cottages on your list, for sure you won't regret it. 

*****
##### THINGS TO ENJOY!   
- Staying at traditional Bamboo cottages  
- enjoy looking up at the stars while showering surrounded by plants  
- Outdoor cinema movie marathon while the crickets playing a song for you.

##### Rates:  
- Standard Room: ₱1,960 per night (good for 2 pax w/ breakfast)  
- Island Hopping: ₱2,500 per boat (good for 4pax)  
- With life vest and snorkeling gears  
- Without food package  
- If less than 4pax, arrangements must be made at Moalboal Municipality only  
- Volleyball Equipment: Free but with reservation  

****
##### Amenities:  
- Each cottage at Tongo Hill Cottages is fitted with a terrace and a partially open-air bathroom with free toiletries  
- The units also have access to free WiFi.   
  
For any tips on how to get around or what to do in the area, guests can ask at the reception. If you would like to discover the area, diving, snorkeling, or canyoning can be arranged. Horse riding, trekking, and cycling is also possible.

##### HOW TO GET THERE

🌎 Exact location: Tongo Hill, Moalboal, Southern Cebu  

From moalboal Tourist Office to Tongo Hill Cottages it will take you 10 mins to get there Panagsama Road.  
  
🗺 Via Commute: 2-3 hours away from the City, if you'll get there by bus, look for the bus bound to moalboal. Then dropped off at Moalboal Market Place then find Moalboal Tourism office. From there, you can ride a rela going to Tongo Hill Cottages.  
  
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
