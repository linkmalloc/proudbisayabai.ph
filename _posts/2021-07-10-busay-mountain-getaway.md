##### Busay Mountain Getaway
  
#BusayGetaway: Need a quick weekend getaway not far away from the city at all? The NEWLY renovated 'Busay Mountain Getaway' is the perfect spot for you! 😮😍  
  
Getting a quick recharge is all you need! This is the perfect place for you to capture the Sunrise and by night time, it gives you an amazing panoramic view of the Cebu City Skyline 🏙   
  
Deserve jud nato mo pahuway, Arats Bai!  
  
🌎 Exact Location: Tops Rd, Busay (under delice Restaurant) 1711 tops Rd, Busay  
  
📌 How to get there?  
  
🗺 Via Commute: Ride any jeepneys or bus going to JY Square Mall. Then, from JY look for habal-habal (need good negotiation) going to Vue Restaurant beside Balay sa Busay. Estimated Fare (P100-150 one way).   
   
🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.  
  
📌 Amenities:  
- Jacuzzi  
- Billiards and pool  
- Air conditioning  
- Wifi  
- Flatscreen TV  
- Exercise Equipment  
- Essentials
