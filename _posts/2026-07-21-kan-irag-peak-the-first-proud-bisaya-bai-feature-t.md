Just a short drive from the bustling streets of Cebu City lies a mountain escape where cool winds, lush landscapes, and breathtaking panoramic views await. Nestled in the highlands of Sitio Langob, Barangay Sirao, Kan-Irag Peak has become one of Cebu’s most sought-after hiking and camping destinations, offering visitors a peaceful retreat surrounded by nature.

  

Rising above the city, Kan-Irag Peak is known for its refreshing climate, scenic forest trails, and sweeping views of Cebu’s mountains and coastline. Its beginner-friendly hiking route makes it an excellent choice for first-time trekkers, while its serene environment continues to attract seasoned hikers, campers, photographers, and nature lovers looking for a quick weekend escape.

  

Whether you’re planning a day hike or an overnight camping adventure, Kan-Irag Peak offers an unforgettable outdoor experience just 30 to 45 minutes from downtown Cebu.

  

Best Time to Visit

  

The best time to visit Kan-Irag Peak is during the dry season, from November to May, when the trails are generally safer and the skies are clearer. Early morning hikes are highly recommended, giving visitors the chance to witness spectacular sunrise views and, on fortunate days, a mesmerizing sea of clouds blanketing the mountains.

  

How to Get There

  

Travelers can reach Kan-Irag Peak in approximately 30 to 45 minutes from Cebu City.

  

By private vehicle: Drive along the Transcentral Highway toward Barangay Sirao and follow the road leading to Sitio Langob. Parking spaces are available near the jump-off point.

  

By public transportation: Ride a jeepney or bus bound for Busay or Sirao, then hire a habal-habal (motorcycle taxi) to take you directly to the trailhead.

  

What to Bring

  

Since there are no stores within the hiking area, visitors are encouraged to prepare all essentials before starting their trek.

  

Recommended items:

  

\* Drinking water and packed meals

\* Tent, sleeping bag, and camping mat

\* Flashlight or headlamp

\* Extra clothes and rain protection

\* Basic first aid kit

\* Trash bags for proper waste disposal

  

The entrance fee is ₱70, which includes access to the comfort room and designated camping area. Campers are advised to bring their own tents, food, and camping equipment.

  

Visitors are also reminded to follow all site regulations, practice the Leave No Trace principle, dispose of waste responsibly, and avoid lighting open fires to help preserve the natural beauty of Kan-Irag Peak.

  

Did You Know?

  

For Proud Bisaya Bai, Kan-Irag Peak is more than just a travel destination—it is where the journey began.

  

In 2020, just days before the COVID-19 lockdown was declared in the Philippines, Proud Bisaya Bai founder Ariel Alegado went on a camping trip to Kan-Irag Peak with friends. After returning home, he uploaded photos of the adventure on his personal Facebook account.

  

To his surprise, the photos quickly went viral, reaching thousands of people who were captivated by the mountain’s stunning scenery. The overwhelming response revealed a growing interest in discovering Cebu’s hidden destinations and inspired something much bigger than he had imagined.

  

Seeing the positive reception, several of Ariel’s friends encouraged him to create a dedicated Facebook page that would showcase Cebu’s tourist attractions, local culture, hidden gems, and inspiring community stories.

  

That simple suggestion marked the birth of Proud Bisaya Bai.

  

What started with a single camping trip and one viral travel post has since grown into one of Cebu’s leading digital media platforms, reaching millions of people and continuing its mission of promoting tourism, supporting local communities, and sharing stories that celebrate the beauty of Cebu, the Visayas, and the Philippines.

  

Today, every visit to Kan-Irag Peak is more than just another adventure—it is a reminder that great journeys often begin with a single step, a beautiful destination, and the courage to share a story.

  

Note: Leave nothing but footprints, take nothing but memories. Help protect Kan-Irag Peak by practicing responsible tourism and preserving its natural beauty for future generations.
