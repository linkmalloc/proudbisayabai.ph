#### Villa Thera Beach House  😍  
  
Villa Thera beach house offer relaxing accommodation with pool and beach front, 3 bed rooms, and great services for 13,500 per night carrying capacity of 15 persons.  
  
🌍 Exact location: North of cebu Carmen luyang bantigue, Villa thera beach house  
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 Via Commute: Ride a bus/vhire going to North Cebu approx. 3-4 hrs travel time from Cebu City. estimated fare 50 pesos  
  
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.   
  
📌 Please also check their Facebook page [https://www.facebook.com/Villa-Thera-Beach-House-642300102889585/ ](https://www.facebook.com/Villa-Thera-Beach-House-642300102889585/ )
