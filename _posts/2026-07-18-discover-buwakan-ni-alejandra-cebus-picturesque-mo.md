BALAMBAN, Cebu — Perched in the cool highlands of Barangay Gaas along the scenic Transcentral Highway, Buwakan ni Alejandra has become one of Cebu’s most photographed nature destinations. Known for its vibrant flower gardens and panoramic mountain views, the attraction offers visitors a refreshing escape from the city’s hustle and bustle.

  

Spread across rolling hills, the garden features thousands of colorful blooms carefully arranged against the backdrop of the Balamban mountains. Whether you’re a nature lover, photographer, road trip enthusiast, or simply looking for a peaceful retreat, Buwakan ni Alejandra offers a relaxing experience surrounded by fresh mountain air and breathtaking scenery.

  

The spacious grounds are ideal for leisurely walks, family outings, pre-nuptial shoots, content creation, and drone photography, subject to weather conditions and site regulations. Every season brings a different mix of flowers, ensuring that no two visits are exactly the same.

  

Because the attraction is located in the mountains, weather conditions can change quickly. Visitors are encouraged to arrive early in the morning to enjoy cooler temperatures, clearer skies, and the best lighting for photography.

  

Entrance Fees

  

\* Adults: ₱50

\* Environmental Fee: ₱5

\* Children (0–5 years old): Free

\* Children (6–10 years old): ₱20

\* Senior Citizens (60 years old and above): ₱25

\* Persons with Disabilities (PWDs): ₱25

  

Operating Hours

  

Buwakan ni Alejandra is open daily from 7:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.

  

For the protection of the gardens and the safety of visitors, the attraction temporarily closes during storms or severe weather conditions. Expert guides are also available to assist guests during their visit.

  

How to Get There

  

By Private Vehicle

  

From Cebu City, drive along the Transcentral Highway toward Balamban. The journey takes approximately 1.5 to 2 hours, depending on traffic. The scenic mountain drive itself is one of the highlights of the trip, offering several viewpoints along the way.

  

By Public Transportation

  

Ride a bus or V-hire bound for Balamban from Cebu City. Upon reaching Balamban town, hire a habal-habal or other local transportation to Buwakan ni Alejandra.

  

Travel Tips

  

\* Visit early in the morning for cooler weather and fewer visitors.

\* Wear comfortable footwear, as some areas involve walking on sloped pathways.

\* Bring sun protection, drinking water, and a light jacket, as mountain temperatures can be cooler than in the city.

\* Check the weather forecast before your trip, as mountain conditions can change rapidly.

  

Whether you’re planning a weekend road trip, a family outing, or a photography adventure, Buwakan ni Alejandra remains one of Cebu’s must-visit mountain destinations, combining colorful floral landscapes with sweeping views of the Balamban highlands.
