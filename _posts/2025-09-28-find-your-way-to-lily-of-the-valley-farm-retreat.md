Heads up, nature lovers! If you’re planning a peaceful escape to Lily of the Valley Farm Retreat, take note: the main access road is temporarily closed due to ongoing construction. But no worries there’s a scenic alternate route that will still take you to this charming countryside haven.&#x20;

To reach the farm, take the Brgy. Babag route, passing by CTU Mountain Campus and the Birhen sa Guadalupe sa Sugbo Parish Church. Turn right after the church, and continue driving until you reach GAIA Cabin House. From there, simply follow the signages or ask a friendly local and you’ll be at the farm in no time.

Sitio Kangsi Brgy. Pung-ol Sibugay Cebu City

➤ Travel Tip:

• First, pin Mai Pharmacy Tugop as your initial landmark.

• Then, add GAIA Cabin House to your navigation app. Follow signages from there to reach Lily of the Valley.

Known for its affordable accommodations, Lily of the Valley is a cozy farm retreat where guests can relax, recharge, and reconnect with nature. Plus, there’s no corkage for food — except lechon.

At Lily of the Valley Farm Retreat, reconnect with the peace that’s been waiting for you. Escape the noise, unwind under the sky, and create moments that last.
