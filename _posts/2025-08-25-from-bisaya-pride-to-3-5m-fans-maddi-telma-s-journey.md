To the Bisaya community, Jaime Onod — or more famously known as Madi Telma — is more than just a content creator. At 28 years old, she has become our certified happy pill every time we scroll through TikTok or any social media feed. With her witty, hilarious, and oh-so-relatable content, Telma has carved out a special place in every Bisaya heart — one laugh at a time.

**“Madi!”** — A word now forever tied to her brand of humor and charm, echoing not only in Mindanao but across the country and beyond.

Hometown: Bislig City, Surigao del Sur

*More Than Just a Comedian*

Did you know? Behind the viral videos is a dedicated educator. Telma works as an online teacher in Davao City, holding a Bachelor of Science in Secondary Education, major in English — and yes, she proudly graduated Cum Laude. Bright, talented, and effortlessly funny — a rare combo!

*From Skits to Startups*

Thanks to her growing online fame, Telma has also become a successful entrepreneur, founding and owning businesses like:

&#x9;•	Telma Skin

&#x9;•	Don Macchiatos

&#x9;•	Don Lemon

She doesn’t just bring laughter online, but also brings joy in person — with live shows across different municipalities. Today, she’s one of the most in-demand Bisaya entertainers in the country.

*Rooted, Real, and Relatable*

Despite all the fame, Telma remains as humble and grounded as ever. Her kindness, authenticity, and love for her roots make her more than a viral sensation — she’s an inspiration to many.

Teacher. Vlogger. Businesswoman. Bayani sa Katawa. She’s living proof that you can chase your dreams without losing who you are — all with hard work, humor, and heart.

***But who exactly is “Telma”?***

As Jaime Onod shared with her fans:

“Ang Telma kay pangalan sa among silingan sauna na sabaan.”

Every character in her skits has real-life inspiration:

* Telma – the name of a noisy neighbor from her childhood
* Jesselyn – her high school classmate and valedictorian
* Charie  – named after her favorite manicurista

This is what makes her content so genuine — it’s drawn from real Bisaya life, full of heart, humor, and history.

Congratulations, Telma!

3.5 million followers strong and growing!

**Padayon sa pagdala og katawa, gugma, ug garbo sa atong Bisaya community. We’re proud of you!**
