*****    
If you’re searching for the perfect beachside spot to unwind, enjoy great food, and soak up the sea breeze, look no further than BrevStar Tambayan sa Baybay in Catmon! This laid-back haven is not just any ordinary “tambayan” (hangout); it’s a place where you can experience the coastal beauty of Catmon while indulging in mouth-watering food, refreshing drinks, and a relaxed beach vibe.  


###### Getting There

Catmon is a cozy municipality located about 57 kilometers north of Cebu City. If you’re driving, you can reach it in around an hour and a half. Once you arrive, BrevStar is conveniently nestled right by the bay, offering an inviting view of the sea. The peaceful setting is enough to make anyone feel at home, and the coastal air will refresh you from the moment you step out of the car.  

###### The Vibe at BrevStar Tambayan  

BrevStar Tambayan sa Baybay brings a fun, chill ambiance that’s ideal for beach lovers, barkada (friends) hangouts, and even solo travelers looking for a break from city life. With open-air seating and a mix of beach-inspired decor, it’s the ultimate spot to relax with the sounds of the waves as your soundtrack. Grab a cozy seat and let the calming sea breeze set the mood.  

###### What to Try: Food and Drinks

The menu at BrevStar features an exciting blend of local favorites and classic comfort food. Here are some must-tries:

- Seafood Specials: Being right by the bay, BrevStar offers fresh seafood dishes, from sinugbang isda (grilled fish) to shrimp and crabs. Don’t miss out on their seafood platter, perfect for sharing.
- Signature Shakes: A day by the beach isn’t complete without a refreshing drink in hand. BrevStar’s fruit shakes come highly recommended, with flavors ranging from classic mango to tropical mixes like coconut-pineapple. It’s the perfect way to cool off under the sun.
- Local Delights: Try their lechon kawali (crispy fried pork) or kinilaw na isda (fish ceviche) if you’re in the mood for traditional Filipino fare. These dishes capture the essence of local flavors and are perfect companions for the view.

****
###### Why BrevStar Stands Out

Unlike other beachside restaurants, BrevStar Tambayan sa Baybay embraces a true local vibe, making everyone feel like family. The staff is friendly, and they add that personal touch that makes you feel at home. Plus, the view of the sunset over the ocean is breathtaking, so plan your visit around late afternoon to catch the golden hour glow.  

###### Activities by the Bay  

At BrevStar, you can do more than just eat and drink. Consider going for a quick dip in the ocean, exploring the shore, or simply lounging with a book. There are plenty of opportunities to connect with nature here, whether you’re watching the waves or enjoying the serene surroundings.  

****
###### Insider Tips
- Go Early: Weekends can be packed, so try to visit on weekdays or early mornings for a quieter experience.
- Bring Your Camera: The setting is picturesque, especially during sunset!
- Cash and Small Bills: It’s always a good idea to carry cash in small denominations, as not every spot around Catmon accepts credit cards.

****
###### Wrapping Up: A New Hangout by the Sea

BrevStar Tambayan sa Baybay is more than just a dining spot; it’s a hidden gem along Catmon’s coastline, ready to welcome anyone who wants to unwind by the sea. So pack your bag, bring along your friends or family, and head over to BrevStar to enjoy an unforgettable beachside experience!
