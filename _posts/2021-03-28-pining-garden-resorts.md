#### Pining Garden Resorts  
  
Feeling stressed or burnt out, or you just want to get that feeling of inner peace 🍃😍😯 Have a quick break at Pining Garden Resorts in Danao City, Cebu!   
  
🕓 Operating hours: 8AM - 10PM  
  
- Check in time: 2PM  
- Check out time: 11AM   

****
🌎 Located at Purok 2, Centro Barangay Dungga Danao City  
  
📌 How to get there?   
  
🗺 The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze. 14km from Danao City Proper, habal-habal( motor Taxi for 100 pesos way) Parking Space Available. Just search Pining Garden Resorts in Dungga Danao City via Google.   
  
###### 📌 Rates:   
- Entrance fee 30 pesos for adult 15 pesos for kids noted that it can be changed   
  
Pining Garden Resorts offer ako Ride going to Pining Garden Resorts:   
- Van for 18 seaters  
- For 8 seaters and 5 seaters  
*( Transport Fee 200 pesos back and forth)*   

****
###### ⛺️ Pining Garden Resorts Campsite ⛺️  
- To Rent Our Tent for only 200 pesos per tent in Capacity of 4 person  
- Bring your own Tent Bayad Lang 150 pesos  

****
📌 Corkage Fee: P100 if your bringing foods  
  
###### 📌 What to Enjoy?   
- Shower available   
- Restaurant serves Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks And Drink 🍹   
*( value meals,coffe,ramen and more variety’s of food)*   

****  
🎤 Live Acoustic Band every weekend Friday Saturday night.   

##### FREE WIFI   
  
****
>Take NOTE: Please be informed that swimming pool, Rooms are still underconstruction, no cellular signal.   
  
>Note Proper Disinfect, Wearing Mask and applied social dinstancing from other visitors.
