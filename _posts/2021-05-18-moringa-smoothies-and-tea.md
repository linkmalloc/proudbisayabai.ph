##### HOW TO GET THERE?  

🕑 Operating hours: 10AM - 9PM daily

🌍 Exact Location: Brgy. Luz, Level 2 Super Metro entrance (Ayala Mall Center, 6000 Cebu City)


🗺 Via Commute: From Colon St. Cebu City, ride a ceres bus, jeepney or Beep bus going to Talamban. Then tell the driver to drop you off at Ayala Mall, going up at 2nd level look for the entrance of Super Metro.

🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorbike or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
