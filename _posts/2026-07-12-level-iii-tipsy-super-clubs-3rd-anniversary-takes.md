Three years of unforgettable parties, world-class entertainment, and vibrant nightlife come together for one milestone celebration.

  

To celebrate its third anniversary, Tipsy Super Club staged LEVEL III, a two-day event held from July 10 to 11 that brought together internationally acclaimed DJs, celebrity guests, and electrifying performances, delivering an unforgettable nightlife experience in Cebu.

  

The celebration kicked off on July 10, headlined by internationally acclaimed DJ Jimmy Nocon, whose high-energy set kept the crowd dancing late into the night.

  

The second night of celebration was headlined by actress and social media personality Sunshine Guimary and Cebu’s own DJ Charlz Saladaga, delivering another unforgettable evening of music and entertainment.

  

Tipsy Super Club goes beyond what a regular club offers. It houses several other entertainment options, including karaoke rooms, billiards and even virtual golf simulator. 

  

The founders believe that having fun doesn’t simply mean going out for drinks and partying, but spending time, no matter what the activity may be, with the people you cherish the most. At Tipsy Super Club, it’s a one-stop destination where music, dining, games, and nightlife come together under one roof.

  

More than just a nightlife hotspot, the founders also wanted to create meaningful employment opportunities for Cebuanos. As Tipsy Super Club expanded, so did its team. It continuously nurtures its people, providing opportunities for growth and cultivating local talent. 

  

Celebrate the next level of nightlife with an unforgettable atmosphere, top-tier entertainment, and a night made for creating lasting memories. 

  

Tipsy Super Club is located on the third floor of the Sonata Building, Lahug, Cebu City. Music starts at 10:00 p.m. until late, from Tuesday through Sundays.
