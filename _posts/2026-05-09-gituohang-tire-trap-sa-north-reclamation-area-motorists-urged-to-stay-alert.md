A Facebook post by Daniela Calvez is gaining attention online after she shared an incident involving her father on the morning of May 6, 2026, while on the way to work along the North Reclamation Area (NRA).



According to her post, a sharp metal object believed to be designed to puncture vehicle tires was discovered attached to their tire after they safely arrived at the office. The object was described as heavy, extremely pointed, and approximately four inches in size.



Calvez reminded motorists to remain vigilant, especially when experiencing a sudden flat tire in isolated areas. She also advised drivers to avoid immediately stepping out of their vehicles and to prioritize their safety while assessing the situation.



As of writing, authorities have yet to release an official statement regarding the incident, and it remains unconfirmed whether similar cases have been reported in the area.
