The proposed 2027 national budget contains duplicate funding for some flood control projects and at least 30 allegedly nonexistent multipurpose buildings, Sen. Panfilo “Ping” Lacson said Monday, as he called for the removal of questionable allocations from the spending measure.

Lacson said he would seek to eliminate duplicate or multiple appropriations during the Senate's deliberations and, if necessary, during the bicameral conference committee proceedings.

“Meron pa rin kaming nasilip. Double appropriations. Flood control na nga, doble pa yung appropriations,” (We found more items. Double appropriations. They are already flood control projects, yet they have been given duplicate appropriations) Lacson said in an interview on DZMM radio.

He said the duplicate flood control allocations were separate from at least 30 multipurpose buildings (MPBs) that his team had flagged as potentially nonexistent and which he also intended to have removed from the proposed budget.

Over the weekend, Lacson questioned appropriations for 3,950 MPBs, apart from the 1,584 buildings covered by the Local Government Support Fund's Financial Assistance to Local Government Units subprogram from 2022 to 2026.

He cited cases in which MPBs received allocations in previous years, including 2024, 2025 and 2026, but no structures had been built at their designated sites as of this year.

“Ang na-flag ng team namin at least 30 kung saan saang sulok ng Pilipinas,” (Our team flagged at least 30 projects in various parts of the country) he said.

Lacson also said initial findings pointed to alleged commissions in MPB projects, although these were not as large as those reportedly involved in questionable flood control projects.

He said the standard commissions for MPBs could reach 10 percent. Earlier this year, he cited alleged kickbacks of 15 to 30 percent for proponents of anomalous flood control projects.

Lacson said he and his staff were continuing to review the proposed budget to identify questionable allocations and recommend their removal.

“Sa Senate version lang, mayroon pa kaming period of amendments. Pag maipakita naman sa aming mga kasamahan na hindi kailangan kasi doble na yung appropriation sa iisang proyekto, palagay ko, matuwid naman na tanggalin yun hanggang sa bicam,” Lacson said.

He said the review had uncovered more instances of potentially duplicated funding, stressing that excess allocations should be removed regardless of how many times a project appears in the budget.

“Hanggang ngayon, patuloy pa rin yung pagkalkal namin, nadadagdagan yung mga ganong klaseng mga appropriations. Pag doble, dapat tanggalin na talaga yung isa. Quadruple, tanggalin yung tatlo,”he added.
