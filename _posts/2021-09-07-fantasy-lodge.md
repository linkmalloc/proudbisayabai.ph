###### Fantasy Lodge

LOOK: Looking for a place to relax and chill for a family or friend’s getaway from the city’s bustling and dazzling noise? Too tired and weakened for a day’s work? Tara na sa Fantasy Lodge, Cebu Philippines!

Fantasy Lodge has multi-faceted and state-of-the-art amenities such as an oversize bed, bamboo dresser, a quaint ceiling fan and a spacious bathroom elegantly projected in the spectra of colors mirroring the ocean. The presence of the wooden old-fashioned sliding window covered by capiz shells will surely mesmerize you. It also has fully-airconditioned and fanned rooms which are far from City’s over-rated hotels.   Wifi, among others, is also fully operational in each of the rooms stationed thereby. On the onset, “the feeling of exclusivity and privacy appears definitely more authentic than in any conventional hotel” you know in the city.

###### Comfort before style  
Fantasy Lodge definitely will enthrall you as it provides comforts of home. The uniquely-landscaped natural rocks as you trod down to the place’s frontage, you will see paradise as if it was hidden behind the valleys of the mountains. The flowing crystal-clear waters will stun your aquaphilean and thalassophilean nature. Not to mention the white sands, shiny pebbles and craggy, black boulders. What can you ask more?

###### AMENITIES  
ROOMS: Non TV rooms, Fan w/ Air condition, w/ WiFi . (NOTE: we dont have tv in our rooms because we want to promote bonding between family and friends by doing outdoor activities)  

DINING: Fusion of French and Filipino Cuisines. Special request can be accomodated too like Vegetarian food.  

SPA: Massage is not allowed for the moment but we have ''KAWA HOT BATH" where you can cook/relax yourself with virgin coco oil and pure essential oils.  

POOL: Our swimming pool is 5-9 ft. Proper swimming attire is a must.  
  
They also offer Dinner,  Birthday & Anniversary set up.  

All rates are net prices. Non-refundable, with VAT inclusion. No Service Charge.
