##### Tumalog Falls Oslob   

What are your plans for the next weekend? I think we deserve to relax sometimes mga bai, plan now and add Tumalog falls on your travel list.  
Tumalog Falls in Oslob is without a doubt one of Cebu's greatest waterfalls! The sheer size of Tumalog Falls distinguishes it from other waterfalls in Oslob. Its height is between 80 and 100 meters.

It is situated in Barangay Luka, near to the well-known whaleshark attraction in Oslob. In such, it is becoming increasingly usual for whale shark visitors to stop by Tumalog Falls following their whale shark encounter. It just takes around two and a half hours to get close to the area, which is approximately 170 kilometers from Cebu City.

##### HOW TO GET THERE?   

🌍 Exact Location: Brgy Natalio Bacalso Avenue, Oslob, Cebu 🇵🇭  
  
  
🗺 Via Commute: From Cebu City, take a bus going to Bato and tell the driver you want to get off at Tumalog falls. They will drop you off at the turning to the falls from the main road. From there you’ll need to take a habal-habal up to the car park before making your way to the falls.  
  
🗺 Via Waze: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a motorcycle (habal-habal) or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
