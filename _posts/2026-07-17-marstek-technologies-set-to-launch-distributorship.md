In a cutting-edge undertaking meant to inspire Cebuano growth, Don Macchiatos Group of Companies (DMGC) is set to introduce European power brand MARSTEK to the masses as part of its evolving goal of driving innovation in the region.

  

The grand business launch happening on **Sunday, July 19, 2026,** will mark MARSTEK’s entry into the dynamic and bustling Cebuano market, poised to empower micro, small, and medium enterprises with its advanced lineup of power storage capabilities, allowing users to optimize energy use and reduce unnecessary power consumption.

  

With electricity costs continuing to skyrocket and energy demand rapidly increasing nationwide, MARSTEK’s debut in the province comes at an opportune time. MARSTEK combines advanced AI technology with modern energy storage capabilities, allowing users to optimize energy use and reduce unnecessary power consumption. Its cutting-edge systems are designed to provide flexibility, convenience, and smarter control for households and enterprises alike.

  

The launch will highlight the strategic expansion of the European power brand to the Queen City of the South, a bustling and growing metropolis. 

  

### **Empowering Cebuano business and households against rising energy costs**

  

As Cebu continues to position itself as a major economic powerhouse in the Visayas, local enterprises and households face the persistent challenge of skyrocketing electricity costs. MARSTEK’s entry into the region is an opportunity for Cebuanos to explore energy independence whilst directly addressing the pain points of monopolized electricity sources. MARSTEK’s innovative power solutions offer reliable, efficient, and scalable backup power systems.

  

### **Smart, portable, scalable energy technology**

  

MARSTEK Technologies offers a diverse lineup of backup energy systems that include smart power stations, Plug & Play battery networks, and whole-home energy storage solutions for residential, commercial, and enterprise applications.

  

With its strategic network and deep understanding of local needs, MARSTEK is uniquely positioned to bridge the gap between world-class European energy technology and the everyday needs of Cebuano consumers. / **PR**
