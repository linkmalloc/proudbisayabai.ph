#### Parrot Resort Moalboal
  
You deserve a perfect place to chill in Moalboal mga bai! Enjoy cooling down in the pool with their luxurious pool-side mini bar PLUS you can interact with the adorable parrots too! 🦜💦⛱  
  
🚨 PROMO FOR YOU MGA BAI ‼️  
  
👍🏻Use PROMO CODE : “ ProudBisayaBai” for a 10% discount for a 2nights stay  
  
🕑 FRONT desk hours: 7 AM- 7PM daily  
  
🌍Exact Location: Parrot Resort Moalboal Panagsama Road Basdiot Molboal  
  
📌 How to get there?  
  
🗺 Via Commute: Ride a bus from South Bus Terminal going Moalboal and tell the driver to drop you off at 7/11 Moalboal. That is the corner also going to Panagsama beach. Ride a Tricycle and tell the driver to drop you off at Parrot Resort Moalboal. The resort is only 5 mins. away from the Panagsama beach.  
  
 🗺 Via Private Vehicle: The place can be navigated on Waze. Whether you are riding a bus or your own vehicle, it’s best to navigate via Waze.
