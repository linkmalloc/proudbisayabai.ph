##### A Switzerland of Wonder: The Hidden Gem of Alamada Cotobato

Make your “Switzerland” dream come true at Coco's Funville & Nature Park!  

Chyn and Chris' wedding anniversary shoot featured incredibly romantic and cinematic portraiture, just like in the movies, proving that love moves in mysterious ways. Despite the fact that it gave Alamada Cotobato a SWEETzerland vibe, you can feel the serenity as love caresses their souls like a gust of wind. Fate whispers as their hearts speak in every beat. As you breathed in the pure air, the grassland will dance in sync.

Coco's Funville & Nature Park also has a lake where couples can experience a moment of connection as if a pool of water streaming down to a lifetime—an endless bond of two becoming one. You can sail together while the twilight sky darkens and the moon shines down from above, illuminating the vicinity with tiny bulbs, making it more dramatic.

It has a view of a mountainous site that has an infinite beauty tethered to a realm of reality where you can put an end to your imaginations' attempts to reach the sky. On the farm, they have a food stall that you can be fond of. They have other amenities to look forward to that will offer you a warm and fuzzy feeling, and they certainly have trails to wander down while you feel every moment. Indeed, if you're seeking a site to unwind and get away, this is the place for you!
