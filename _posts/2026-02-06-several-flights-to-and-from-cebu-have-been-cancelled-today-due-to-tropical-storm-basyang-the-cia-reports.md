Affected departures include Cebu-bound flights to Dipolog, Pagadian, Dumaguete, Cagayan de Oro, Cotabato, Masbate, and Siquijor, while arrivals from these cities to Cebu are also cancelled.

Passengers are strongly advised to check the status of their flights before heading to the airport and to contact their respective airlines for updates, rebooking options, or other inquiries. Flight information is also available online via the airline’s official channels: Airline Flight Status￼.



Authorities will provide updates as more information becomes available. Passengers are urged to exercise caution and prioritize safety amid adverse weather conditions. | MCIA
