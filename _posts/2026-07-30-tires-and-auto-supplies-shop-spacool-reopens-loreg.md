Spacool Tyres and Auto Supplies officially reopened its Lorega branch in Cebu City on Wednesday, July 29, further strengthening its commitment in providing premium automotive products and services to motorists and car owners in Cebu. Car enthusiasts can now avail updated and top-of-the-line automotive solutions with the shop’s refurbished services.

  

In addition to its premium tire installation and repair, wheel balancing and alignment, underchassis maintenance and tightening, and premium battery retail and servicing, Spacool now offers quick services such as oil changes and brake cleaning and inspection, making it a convenient one-stop automotive destination for its customers. 

Beyond its brand new services, Spacool Cebu aims to strengthen the ties it has built with its loyal customers and the community it serves by continuously improving the quality of its offerings and customer experience.

  

Spacool Tyres is located in Lorega, San Miguel, Cebu City. The tire and automotive service shop is open daily from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., except on Sundays when it closes at 5:00 p.m.
