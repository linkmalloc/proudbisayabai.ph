At Marco Polo Plaza Cebu, every part of the day comes with something worth craving.



Start your morning with Morning Dips at Café Marco — a generous breakfast buffet with pool access for ₱1,000 nett per person, perfect for a slow and refreshing start to the day.



By afternoon, things turn more indulgent at El Viento with Burger-holic, where croissant burgers are priced at ₱700 nett per order — bringing together comfort and flavor in every bite.



As the evening sets in, the Lobby Lounge’s Corner Bakery offers pastries, cakes, and sweet treats — enjoyed with 20% savings on select items during evening hours, making dessert even more tempting.  



From sunrise cravings to night-time bites, it’s all happening in one place at Marco Polo Plaza Cebu.
