Cebu Eats: Craving something sweet? Indulge in exquisite French pastries at Café Mercí in Banilad! Each bite is a heavenly blend of rich, creamy layers and delicate flavors—perfect for satisfying your dessert cravings.

Gather your friends or family and unwind in the cozy ambiance. Sip on your favorite drink, enjoy delicious treats, and make sweet memories together. 

**G/F Streetscape Mall, Paseo Saturnino, Banilad, Cebu City**

*8 AM - 9 PM (subject to change)*

Best Sellers:

* Giant Strawberry Shortcake
* Fresh Fruit Shortcake
* Caramel Macaron
* Fruit Tart
